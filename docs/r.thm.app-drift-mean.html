Theorem SC.2 (Regularized mean)
Under Assumption S3, the integral (SC.3) converges absolutely in Frobenius norm, with \(\norm{\Gtildebar_\ridge}_\F\le C_\Sigma/\sqrt\ridge\), and
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.8} \varepsilon_B\defeq\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\Gtildebar_\ridge}_\F\longrightarrow0 . \end{equation}
Consequently, for every deterministic \(V\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.9} \sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\big|\big\langle V,\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\Gtildebar_\ridge\big\rangle\big|\le\norm V_\F\,\varepsilon_B, \qquad \langle V,W\rangle\defeq\Tr(VW^\T). \end{equation}
Proof of Theorem SC.2.
The candidate integral exists. By Theorem SB.1(ii) the branch is continuous in \(s\), so \(s\mapsto T(s)\) is continuous on \((0,\infty)\), and by (SC.6) and the scalar integral in the proof of Lemma SA.3,
\[ \frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\norm{T(\ridge+u^2)}_\F\,du\le\frac{2C_\Sigma}{\pi}\int_0^\infty\frac{du}{\ridge+u^2}=\frac{C_\Sigma}{\sqrt\ridge}. \]
The expected update is the integral of \(\mathcal H\). By (SA.37),
\[ \EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)=\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\mathcal H(\ridge+u^2)\,du . \]
The difference is split at a cutoff. Fix \(\mathsf R\ge1\) and put \(I_{\mathsf R}\defeq[\ridge_-,\ridge_++\mathsf R^2]\). For \(u\le\mathsf R\) and \(\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\) the parameter \(s=\ridge+u^2\) lies in \(I_{\mathsf R}\); for \(u\ge\mathsf R\) it satisfies \(s\ge u^2\ge1\), where (SC.6) applies. Hence
\[ \begin{aligned} \norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\Gtildebar_\ridge}_\F &\le\frac2\pi\Big[\mathsf R\,\sup_{s\in I_{\mathsf R}}\norm{\mathcal H(s)-T(s)}_\F+(C+C_\Sigma)\int_{\mathsf R}^\infty\frac{du}{u^2}\Big]\\ &=\frac2\pi\Big[\mathsf R\,\sup_{s\in I_{\mathsf R}}\norm{\mathcal H(s)-T(s)}_\F+\frac{C+C_\Sigma}{\mathsf R}\Big], \end{aligned} \]
uniformly in \(\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\). Taking first the limit superior in the dimension at fixed \(\mathsf R\), which kills the first term by (SC.5) applied to the compact interval \(I_{\mathsf R}\), and then \(\mathsf R\to\infty\), proves (SC.8).
The pairing bound (SC.9) is the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for the Frobenius pairing.
□
