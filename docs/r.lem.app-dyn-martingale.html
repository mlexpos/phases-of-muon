Lemma SE.20 (Block martingale)
Let \(b_\star\) be a block index such that whether \(b<b_\star\) is decided by the past \(\cF_{t_b}\), and such that \(\Delta_{t_b}\in\mathcal A\) for \(b<b_\star\); put \(I_b\defeq\mathbf 1_{b<b_\star}\). Let \(\norm f_\infty,\norm g_\infty\le1\) and
\[ \xi_b\defeq X_{f,g}(\Delta_{t_{b+1}})-X_{f,g}(\Delta_{t_b})-\EE_{t_b}\big[X_{f,g}(\Delta_{t_{b+1}})-X_{f,g}(\Delta_{t_b})\big]. \]
Then for every \(T>0\),
\[ \EE\max_{j\le TB/L}\Big|\sum_{b<j}I_b\xi_b\Big|^2\le C(T+1)\Big\{\frac{L\log^2(2B)}{B}+\frac{L^3}{B}+\frac1L\Big\}. \]
Proof.
Fix a block \(b\) with \(I_b=1\) and write \(t=t_b\), \(D=\Dt\), \(m=M^\circ_{t-1}\). There is no stop in the block, by Lemma SE.14(ii), so \(\Delta_{t+L}=D-\lr\Sigma_{\mathsf S}\) with \(\Sigma_{\mathsf S}\defeq\sum_{r<L}{\mathsf S}_{t+r}\), and (SE.26) for the block gives
\[ X_{f,g}(\Delta_{t+L})-X_{f,g}(D)=-2\lr\langle\mathcal WD,S\rangle-2\lr\langle\mathcal WD,\mathcal E\rangle+\lr^2\langle\Sigma_{\mathsf S},\mathcal W\Sigma_{\mathsf S}\rangle, \]
where \(S=\sum_{r<L}V_r\) is the stationary sum of the chains at time \(t\) and \(\mathcal E\defeq\Sigma_{\mathsf S}-S\). The left side does not depend on the auxiliary batches, and conditionally on \(\cF_t\) its centered version is the sum of the centered versions of the three terms. Hence \(\EE_t\xi_b^2\le3\big(4\lr^2\Var_t\langle\mathcal WD,S\rangle+4\lr^2\EE_t\langle\mathcal WD,\mathcal E\rangle^2+\lr^4\EE_t\langle\Sigma_{\mathsf S},\mathcal W\Sigma_{\mathsf S}\rangle^2\big)\).
The three terms are bounded as follows. By Lemma SE.19, conditionally on \(\cF_t\) with \(T=\mathcal WD\) and \(\norm{\mathcal WD}_\F\le\norm D_\F\le C\), the first is at most \(C\lr^2L^2\log^2(2B)\). By Lemma SE.16(iii), the second is at most \(C\lr^2(L^4+B+\norm m_\F^2)\), and \(\EE[I_b\norm m_\F^2]\le CB\) by Lemma SE.15 with \(\tau=\tau^+\), since \(I_b\le\mathbf 1_{t_b\le\tau^+}\). The third is at most \(\lr^4(L^2\gamma_\star B)^2=CL^4/B^2\), because \(\norm{\Sigma_{\mathsf S}}_\F\le L\sqrt{\gamma_\star B}\). Altogether
\[ \EE\big[I_b\xi_b^2\big]\le C\Big\{\frac{L^2\log^2(2B)}{B^2}+\frac{L^4}{B^2}+\frac1B\Big\}. \]
The variables \(I_b\xi_b\) are martingale differences for the pasts at the block starts, so \(\EE(\sum_{b<j}I_b\xi_b)^2=\sum_{b<j}\EE[I_b\xi_b^2]\), and Doob’s \(L^2\) maximal inequality [101, Theorem 14.11] bounds the expected squared maximum over \(j\le TB/L\) by four times the sum over the at most \(TB/L+1\) blocks.
□
