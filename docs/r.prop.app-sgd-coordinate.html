Proposition SJ.3 (Drift of one coordinate)
Let \(B\ge4\) and suppose \(\norm{\bar\Delta}_\F\ge F/2\) for the entry \((i,j)\). With \(s,m_3\) from Lemma SJ.3 and \(C_1,C_2\) from Lemma SJ.5,
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.14} \begin{aligned} \Big|\EE\sign(G^\circ_{ij})-\sqrt{\frac2\pi}\,\mathcal N_B\frac{\Delta_{ij}}F\Big|\le{}&\frac{C_1\abs{m_3}}{s^3\sqrt B}+\frac{C_2}B+\frac{B^{3/2}\abs{\Delta_{ij}}^3}{3\sqrt{2\pi}\,s^3}\\ &+\sqrt{\frac2\pi}\,\frac{\sqrt B\,\abs{\Delta_{ij}}\,(s^2-F^2)}{sF(s+F)}+\sqrt{\frac2\pi}\,\frac{4\abs{\Delta_{ij}}}{F\sqrt B}. \end{aligned} \end{equation}
Proof.
The sign of \(G^\circ_{ij}\) is the sign of a shifted normalized sum. With \(Y_a\) the standardized copies of \(X\),
\[ \sign\big(G^\circ_{ij}\big)=\sign\Big(\frac1{\sqrt B}\sum_aY_a+\frac{\sqrt B\,\Delta_{ij}}s\Big). \]
Lemma SJ.3 and Lemma SJ.4 verify the hypotheses of Lemma SJ.5, with \(\EE Y^3=m_3/s^3\). The comparison at \(x=\sqrt B\Delta_{ij}/s\) produces the first two terms. The Gaussian shift (SJ.6) with \(\sigma=1\) replaces \(\EE\sign(g+x)\) by \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,x\) at the cost of the third term.
The last two terms replace \(\sqrt B/s\) by \(\mathcal N_B/F\). By the triangle inequality and Proposition SJ.1,
\[ \Big|\frac{\sqrt B}s-\frac{\mathcal N_B}F\Big|\le\sqrt B\Big(\frac1F-\frac1s\Big)+\frac{\sqrt B-\mathcal N_B}F\le\frac{\sqrt B\,(s^2-F^2)}{sF(s+F)}+\frac4{F\sqrt B}. \]
Multiplying by \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,\abs{\Delta_{ij}}\) gives the last two terms.
□
