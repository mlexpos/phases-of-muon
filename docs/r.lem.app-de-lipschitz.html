Lemma SB.9 (Nuclear-norm Lipschitz bound)
For \(K,K'\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\) and \(s>0\), \(\norm{R_s(K)-R_s(K')}_*\le2s^{-3/2}\norm{K-K'}_*\).
Proof.
The resolvent \(R_s(K)\) is a block of the resolvent of a symmetric matrix at a point at distance \(\sqrt s\) from the real axis. Let
\[ \mathcal D(K)\defeq\begin{pmatrix}0&K\\K^\T&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathcal Y(K)\defeq\big(\mathcal D(K)-i\sqrt s\,\Id\big)^{-1}. \]
Since \(\mathcal D(K)\) is real symmetric, its spectrum is real, and \(\norm{\mathcal Y(K)}\le s^{-1/2}\). Block inversion with the Schur complement of the lower-right block \(-i\sqrt s\,\Id\) shows that the upper-left \(\Nout\times\Nout\) block of \(\mathcal Y(K)\) is
\[ \Big(-i\sqrt s\,\Id-K\big(-i\sqrt s\big)^{-1}K^\T\Big)^{-1} =\Big(-\frac{i}{\sqrt s}\big(KK^\T+s\Id\big)\Big)^{-1}=i\sqrt s\,R_s(K). \]
The difference of the two dilated resolvents is controlled by the resolvent identity:
\[ \mathcal Y(K)-\mathcal Y(K')=\mathcal Y(K)\,\mathcal D(K'-K)\,\mathcal Y(K'). \]
The nuclear norm is unitarily invariant, so the ideal property \(\norm{XYZ}_*\le\norm X\,\norm Y_*\,\norm Z\) [81, Proposition IV.2.4 and (IV.40)] applies; the singular values of \(\mathcal D(E)\) are those of \(E\), each taken twice, so \(\norm{\mathcal D(E)}_*=2\norm E_*\). Hence
\[ \norm{\mathcal Y(K)-\mathcal Y(K')}_*\le\norm{\mathcal Y(K)}\,\norm{\mathcal D(K'-K)}_*\,\norm{\mathcal Y(K')} \le\frac{2}{s}\,\norm{K-K'}_* . \]
Compression to the upper-left block does not increase the nuclear norm, by the same ideal property with the orthogonal projection \(P\) onto the first \(\Nout\) coordinates in place of \(X\) and \(Z\). Therefore
\[ \sqrt s\,\norm{R_s(K)-R_s(K')}_*=\norm{P\big(\mathcal Y(K)-\mathcal Y(K')\big)P}_*\le\frac2s\,\norm{K-K'}_* , \]
which is the claim after division by \(\sqrt s\).
□
