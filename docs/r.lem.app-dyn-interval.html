Lemma SE.27 (No exit before \(T_\star\))
Let \(b_c\) be as in Lemma SE.24. Then \(\Pr(t_{b_c}\le BT_\star+L)\to0\). Moreover, with probability tending to one, for all \(t\le BT_\star\),
\[ \abs{\norm{\Dt}_\F^2-\norm{\Delta_0}_\F^2}\le\frac14,\qquad \abs{\cR(t)-\cR(0)}\le\frac{\min(r_0,1)}{4}; \]
in particular \(t_{\rm stop}>BT_\star\) and \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\macrotime)\in\mathfrak M\) for \(\macrotime\le T_\star\).
Proof.
Apply Lemma SE.26 with \(T=T_\star+1\) to the two tests \(f=g=1\) and \(f(\mu)=\mu/2\), \(g(\lambda)=\lambda\), whose integrals against \(\chi(\Delta)\) are \(\norm\Delta_\F^2\) and \(\cR(\Delta)\). The integrands are bounded on the stopped interval. For \(\sigma<\macrotime_c\) the measure \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\sigma)\) is within total variation \(CL/\sqrt B\) of the measure \(\chi(\Delta_{t_b})\) of an admissible block start, which lies in \(\mathfrak M\) because \(\mathcal A\) has exactly the bounds of \(\mathfrak M\). By Lemma SE.1(i), and (ii) on the enlarged set,
\[ \big|\mathsf F_B(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\sigma))(\mathbb K)\big|\le C_E+\frac{CL}{\sqrt B},\qquad \frac12\Big|\int\mu\lambda\,\dif\mathsf F_B(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\sigma))\Big|\le C_{\cR}+\frac{CL}{\sqrt B}. \]
Hence there are random variables \(o_B\to0\) in probability such that for all \(\macrotime\le T_\star+1\),
\[ \abs{\norm{\Delta_{\lfloor B(\macrotime\wedge\macrotime_c)\rfloor}}_\F^2-\norm{\Delta_0}_\F^2}\le C_E(\macrotime\wedge\macrotime_c)+o_B,\qquad \abs{\cR(\lfloor B(\macrotime\wedge\macrotime_c)\rfloor)-\cR(0)}\le C_{\cR}(\macrotime\wedge\macrotime_c)+o_B . \]
Suppose that \(t_{b_c}\le BT_\star+L\), so that \(\macrotime_c\le T_\star+L/B\). At \(\macrotime=\macrotime_c\) the state \(\Delta_{t_{b_c}}\) is not in \(\mathcal A\): its energy exceeds \(E_\star+1\), or its risk is below \(r_0/2\) or above \(r_1+\frac12\). Since \(\norm{\Delta_0}_\F^2\le E_\star\) and \(\cR(0)\in[r_0,r_1]\), the energy or the risk then deviates from its initial value by at least \(1\), \(r_0/2\) or \(\frac12\). But by (SE.10) the deviations are at most \(C_E(T_\star+L/B)+o_B\le\frac18+o(1)+o_B\) and \(C_{\cR}(T_\star+L/B)+o_B\le\min(r_0,1)/8+o(1)+o_B\). Hence \(\Pr(t_{b_c}\le BT_\star+L)\le\Pr(o_B>\min(r_0,1)/16)\to0\) for large \(B\).
On the event \(\{t_{b_c}>BT_\star+L\}\cap\{o_B\le\min(r_0,1)/16\}\), whose probability tends to one, the same bounds hold at every \(t\le BT_\star\) with the right sides at most \(\frac14\) and \(\min(r_0,1)/4\). Then \(\cR(t)\ge\frac34r_0>0\), so \(t<t_{\rm stop}\), and \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\macrotime)\in\mathfrak M\).
□
