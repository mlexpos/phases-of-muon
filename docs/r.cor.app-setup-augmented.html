Corollary SA.1 (Recursion of the algorithm)
In Definition SA.2, assume \(\EE\norm{\Delta_0}_\F^2<\infty\). Then for every \(t\ge0\) the identities (SA.20), (SA.21) and (SA.22) hold with \({\mathsf S}=\Gtilde^\circ\), with \(0\le\cV_{ij}(t)\le1\). In particular
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.24} \EE_t\big[\cR(t+1)\big]-\cR(t)=-\lr_t\big\langle\Dt,\EE_t\Gtilde^\circ\big\rangle_\Sigma+\frac{\lr_t^2}2\,\EE_t\norm{\Gtilde^\circ}_\Sigma^2 . \end{equation}
On \(\{t<t_{\mathrm{stop}}\}\) the conditional expectations in (SA.19) and (SA.24) are obtained by integrating the fresh batch of step \(t\) with \((\Dt,M_{t-1}^\circ)\) held fixed, because the batch is independent of the past; on \(\{t\ge t_{\mathrm{stop}}\}\) both sides of (SA.24) vanish.
Proof.
The update satisfies \(\norm{\Gtilde^\circ}\le1\) and \(\norm{\Gtilde^\circ}_\F^2\le\min(\Nout,\Nin)\) by Lemma SA.2(i), so \(\EE\norm{\Dt}_\F^2<\infty\) for every \(t\) and Proposition SA.4 applies at every \(t\). The identity (SA.24) is (SA.22) rewritten with (SA.23) and (SA.4). The bound \(\cV_{ij}(t)\le1\) follows from \(\abs{u_i^\T\Gtilde^\circ v_j}\le\norm{\Gtilde^\circ}\le1\). On \(\{t<t_{\mathrm{stop}}\}\) the update is a function of \((\Dt,M_{t-1}^\circ)\) and of the batch of step \(t\); on \(\{t\ge t_{\mathrm{stop}}\}\) the update vanishes and \(\Delta_{t+1}=\Dt\).
□
