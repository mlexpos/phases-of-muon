Lemma SI.25 (Kernel templates)
In each of the following cases there are constants \(N_{\rm T}\), \(K_f\ge0\), \(K_s>0\), \(\upsilon>1\) and \(\mathsf b\in(0,1)\), depending only on \(\alpha\), such that for \(N\ge N_{\rm T}\) the kernel \(g_j\) satisfies (SI.60) with \(q=q_j\) on \(0\le v\le N^{q_j}-1\):
  1. (i)
    SignSVD, \(0<\alpha<1\): \(K_f=0\), \(K_s=4(1-\frac\alpha2)/(1-\alpha)\), \(\mathsf b=\frac12\);
  2. (ii)
    SignSVD, \(1<\alpha<2\): \(K_f=0\), \(K_s=4(1-\frac\alpha2)E_{\alpha,\alpha/2}\), \(\mathsf b=2-\frac2\alpha\);
  3. (iii)
    SignSGD, \(0<\alpha<\frac12\): \(K_f=0\), \(K_s=2(1-\frac2\pi)/(1-\alpha)+\frac4\pi(1-\alpha)/(1-2\alpha)\), \(\mathsf b=\frac12\);
  4. (iv)
    SignSGD, \(\frac12<\alpha<1\): \(K_f=0\), \(K_s=2(1-\frac2\pi)/(1-\alpha)+\frac4\pi(1-\alpha)E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\), \(\mathsf b=\max\{2-\frac1\alpha,\frac12\}\);
  5. (v)
    SignSGD, \(\alpha>1\): the constants (SI.62), for all \(v\ge0\).
Proof.
On \(0\le v\le N^q-1\) one has \((1+v)/N^q\le1\), so a bound \(K'N^{-q}((1+v)/N^q)^{-\mathsf b'}\) with \(0\le\mathsf b'\le\mathsf b\) implies the same bound with \(\mathsf b\); in particular a constant bound \(K'N^{-q}\) has template form with any \(\mathsf b\). Let \(N_{\rm T}\) be such that \(N^{1-a}\ge2\) for the relevant \(a<1\), so that \(H_N(a)\ge N^{1-a}/(2(1-a))\); also \(H_N(a)\le N^{1-a}/(1-a)\) for \(0<a<1\), by Lemma SI.8.
(i) The kernel is nonincreasing, so
\[ g_{\text{s-SVD}}\le g_{\text{s-SVD}}(0)=\frac{2H_N(\alpha)}{H_N(\alpha/2)}\le\frac{4(1-\alpha/2)}{1-\alpha}\,N^{-\alpha/2} . \]
(ii) By Lemma SI.7(i),
\[ g_{\text{s-SVD}}(v)\le4\Big(1-\frac\alpha2\Big)E_{\alpha,\alpha/2}\,N^{-(1-\alpha/2)}(1+v)^{-(2-2/\alpha)} , \]
which is the template with \(q=\alpha/2\) and \(\mathsf b=2-\frac2\alpha\in(0,1)\), since \(-q+q\mathsf b=\frac\alpha2-1\).
(iii) The first term satisfies
\[ g_1\le\frac{2(1-2/\pi)}N\,H_N(\alpha)\le\frac{2(1-2/\pi)}{1-\alpha}\,N^{-\alpha} . \]
For the second term, the two bounds of Lemma SI.8, with \(\int_1^{N+1}\ge\int_1^N\) in the lower one, give for \(0<a<1\)
\[ (1-a)H_N(a)\ge N^{1-a}-1,\qquad (1-a)H_N(a)\le N^{1-a}-a . \]
Enlarge \(N_{\rm T}\) so that \(N^{-\alpha}\le2\alpha\) for \(N\ge N_{\rm T}\). The upper bound with \(a=2\alpha\) and the lower bound with \(a=\alpha\) then give
\[ (1-2\alpha)H_N(2\alpha)\le N^{1-2\alpha}-N^{-\alpha}=N^{-\alpha}\big(N^{1-\alpha}-1\big)\le(1-\alpha)N^{-\alpha}H_N(\alpha) . \]
Since \(g_2\) is nonincreasing and \(L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(0)=H_N(2\alpha)\),
\[ g_2\le\frac{4H_N(2\alpha)}{\pi H_N(\alpha)}\le\frac{4(1-\alpha)}{\pi(1-2\alpha)}\,N^{-\alpha} . \]
(iv) The term \(g_1\) is bounded as in (iii). Write \(d\defeq d(2\alpha,\alpha)=(2\alpha-1)/\alpha\). The envelope of Lemma SI.7(i) holds with a strictly smaller constant: there is \(E'<E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\), depending only on \(\alpha\), such that
\[ L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le E'(1+v)^{-d}\qquad(N\ge1,\ v\ge0) . \]
To see this, note that the term \(i=1\) of \(L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\) is \(e^{-2v}\). The other terms sum to at most \(Z_{2\alpha}-1\) by the proof of Lemma SI.7. Hence
\[ L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le Z_{2\alpha}-1+e^{-2v} . \]
Since \(Z_{2\alpha}-1+e^{-1}<Z_{2\alpha}\), continuity gives \(r>1\) with \((1+r)^d(Z_{2\alpha}-1+e^{-1})<2^dZ_{2\alpha}\). On \(0\le v\le\frac12\) the last display gives \((1+v)^dL_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le(3/2)^dZ_{2\alpha}\). On \(\frac12\le v\le r\) it gives \((1+v)^dL_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le(1+r)^d(Z_{2\alpha}-1+e^{-1})\). Both bounds are below \(2^dZ_{2\alpha}\le E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\). For \(v\ge r>1\), the display in the proof of Lemma SI.7(i) and \(\Gamma(d)/\alpha+2^{1-1/\alpha}M_{2\alpha,\alpha}\le E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\) give \(L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le E_{2\alpha,\alpha}(2v)^{-d}\), so
\[ (1+v)^dL_{N,2\alpha,\alpha}(v)\le E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\Big(\frac{1+v}{2v}\Big)^d\le E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\Big(\frac{1+r}{2r}\Big)^d<E_{2\alpha,\alpha} . \]
Let \(E'\) be the largest of the three bounds.
Enlarge \(N_{\rm T}\) so that \(N^{1-\alpha}(E_{2\alpha,\alpha}-E')\ge E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\) for \(N\ge N_{\rm T}\). The lower bound \((1-\alpha)H_N(\alpha)\ge N^{1-\alpha}-1\) of (iii) then gives
\[ \frac{E'}{H_N(\alpha)}\le\frac{(1-\alpha)E'}{N^{1-\alpha}-1}\le(1-\alpha)E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\,N^{\alpha-1} . \]
Therefore
\[ g_2(v)\le\frac{4E'}{\pi H_N(\alpha)}(1+v)^{-d} \le\frac4\pi(1-\alpha)E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\,N^{-\alpha}\Big(\frac{1+v}{N^\alpha}\Big)^{-(2\alpha-1)/\alpha}, \]
since \(-\alpha+(2\alpha-1)=\alpha-1\).
(v) This was shown in the proof of Lemma SI.24.
