Theorem SF.2 (Isotropic volatility)
Work under Assumption S3 with \(\Sigmain=\Id_{\Nin}\) and \(\Sigmaout=\Id_{\Nout}\), let \(I=[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\), let \({\mathsf S}_t(\ridge)=\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)\) be the frozen update of (SD.15), and let \(e_B\to0\) be the sequence of Theorem SD.1.
  1. (i)
    (Same time.) For every row \(i\) and every column \(j\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.14} \sup_{\ridge\in I}\Big|\EE\norm{{\mathsf S}_t(\ridge)^\T u_i}^2-\frac{\psi(\ridge)}{\gout}\Big|\le\ridge_+e_B,\qquad \sup_{\ridge\in I}\Big|\EE\norm{{\mathsf S}_t(\ridge)v_j}^2-\frac{\psi(\ridge)}{\gin}\Big|\le\ridge_+e_B', \end{equation}
    where \(e_B'\defeq\sup_{s\in I}\norm{\EE\widetilde R(-s)-\Rtbar(-s)}\to0\), and
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.15} \EE\sup_{\ridge\in I}\Big|\frac1B\norm{{\mathsf S}_t(\ridge)}_\F^2-\psi(\ridge)\Big|^2\longrightarrow0 . \end{equation}
  2. (ii)
    (Integrated.) There is a number \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)\ge0\), depending on the dimension, \(\bmom\) and \(\ridge\), such that the mode coefficients of Proposition SD.3 and the row coefficients of Theorem SD.7 are
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.16} \mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)=\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)}{\Nout\Nin},\qquad \mathfrak v_i(\ridge)=\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)}{\Nout},\qquad \zeta_\ridge=\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)}{B}\,\delta_{(1,1)} , \end{equation}
    for all \(i,j\), and \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)=\sum_{i,j}\mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)=B\,\overline\Gamma(\ridge,\ridge)\) with \(\overline\Gamma=\overline\Gamma[\Id_{\Nout},\Id_{\Nin}]\) of (SD.54).
  3. (iii)
    (Lag expansion.) With the deterministic lag covariances \(\overline C_\ell=\overline C_\ell[\Id_{\Nout},\Id_{\Nin}]\) of Theorem SD.4,
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.17} \mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)=B\,\psi(\ridge)+2B\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_\ell(\ridge,\ridge),\qquad \abs{\overline C_\ell(\ridge,\ridge)}\le\mathsf K_\star\bmom^\ell , \end{equation}
    with \(\mathsf K_\star\) of (SD.39) at \(C_\Sigma=1\). At \(\bmom=0\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.18} \mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)=B\,\psi(\ridge)=B\,\Phi\big(q(-\ridge)\big)=B\big(\gout-\ridge\,\sigma(-\ridge)\big). \end{equation}
  4. (iv)
    (Actual coefficient.) The actual integrated covariance \(\Gamma=\Gamma[\Id_{\Nout},\Id_{\Nin}]\) of (SD.17) satisfies \(\sup_{\ridge\in I}\abs{\Gamma(\ridge,\ridge)-\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)/B}\to0\).
Proof.
Part (i) is Theorem SD.1 at \(\mu_i=1\) and \(\lambda_j=1\). By (SF.5), the same-time coefficient (SD.22) is the same for every row,
\[ \volraw_i(\ridge)=\frac{\tilde s_1(-\ridge)}{\ridge+\tilde s_1(-\ridge)}=\frac{\tilde s_1\sigma}{\gout}=\frac{\psi(\ridge)}{\gout}, \]
and (SD.24) is the first bound of (SF.14). The column bound of (SD.24) (Theorem SD.1(ii)), with the column coefficient
\[ \frac{s_1(-\ridge)}{\ridge+s_1(-\ridge)}=\frac{s_1\tilde\sigma}{\gin}=\frac{\psi(\ridge)}{\gin}, \]
gives the second bound. The candidate total of (SD.22) is
\[ \overline Z(\ridge)=\frac1B\Tr\Id_{\Nout}-\ridge\,\sigma(-\ridge)=\gout-\ridge\,\sigma(-\ridge)=\psi(\ridge), \]
by (SF.6), and \(\norm{\cdot}_\Sigma=\norm{\cdot}_\F\), so (SD.25) is (SF.15).
Part (ii) is Proposition SD.3 and Proposition SD.4 with one eigenvalue on each side. The only atom of the measures of (SD.78) is \((1,1)\), with multiplicities \(r_1=\Nout\) and \(t_1=\Nin\). Put \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)\defeq B\,\zeta_\ridge(\{(1,1)\})\). Then (SD.80) gives the first identity of (SF.16), and Proposition SD.4 gives
\[ \mathfrak v_i(\ridge)=\Nin\,\mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)=\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)}{\Nout}. \]
The atom of \(\zeta_\ridge\) at \((1,1)\) is, by (SD.78) with \(P_1=\Id_{\Nout}\) and \(P_1'=\Id_{\Nin}\), equal to \(\overline\Gamma(\ridge,\ridge)\), and summing the first identity of (SF.16) over the \(\Nout\Nin\) pairs gives \(\sum_{i,j}\mathfrak v_{ij}=\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}\). Nonnegativity is Theorem SD.8(iii).
Part (iii) expands \(\overline\Gamma\). By (SD.54) at equal ridges,
\[ \overline\Gamma(\ridge,\ridge)=\overline C_0(\ridge,\ridge)+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_\ell(\ridge,\ridge), \]
and \(\overline C_0(\ridge,\ridge)=\overline Z(\ridge)=\psi(\ridge)\) by Theorem SD.4(v) and part (i). The bound on the lag terms is Theorem SD.4(ii) with \(\norm F=\norm G=1\). At \(\bmom=0\) the lag terms vanish by the same statement, and the three forms of (SF.18) are (SF.4) and (SF.6).
Part (iv) is Theorem SD.4(v).

theorem thm_app_iso_volatility (cert : Muon.External.GaussianOpNorm)
    (R : Muon.Setup.SetupRegime) {Ω : ℕ → Type} [∀ k, MeasurableSpace (Ω k)]
    (P : ∀ k, Measure (Ω k)) [∀ k, IsProbabilityMeasure (P k)]
    (S : ∀ k, Muon.Setup.FrozenSamples (P k) (R.m k) (R.n k) (R.B k)) (t : ℤ)
    (Sp : ∀ k, Muon.Setup.SpectralData (R.F k).C)
    (hIso : ∀ k, (R.F k).C.Sin = 1 ∧ (R.F k).C.Sout = 1) :
    -- (i) same time: rows and columns, with the explicit dominating sup `e_B`, `e'_B` (universal form),
    -- their existence with limit 0, and the total.
    (∀ eB : ℕ → ℝ,
      (∀ k, ∀ s ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, Muon.opNorm (Muon.DetEquiv.expRes (Muon.emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t s -
          Muon.DetEquiv.Rbar (Muon.DetEquiv.Fw (Muon.emaW R.β)) (Muon.Drift.specOutFM (R.F k))
            (Muon.Drift.specInFM (R.F k)) (R.F k).C s) ≤ eB k) →
      ∀ k, ∀ ϱ ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, ∀ i : Fin (R.m k),
        Integrable (fun ω => dotProduct ((Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω)ᵀ *ᵥ (Sp k).u i)
          ((Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω)ᵀ *ᵥ (Sp k).u i)) (P k) ∧
        |(∫ ω, dotProduct ((Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω)ᵀ *ᵥ (Sp k).u i)
            ((Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω)ᵀ *ᵥ (Sp k).u i) ∂(P k)) -
          psiIso R.β ((R.n k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ((R.m k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ϱ /
            ((R.m k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ))| ≤ R.ϱhi * eB k) ∧
    (∃ eB : ℕ → ℝ, Tendsto eB atTop (𝓝 0) ∧
      ∀ k, ∀ s ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, Muon.opNorm (Muon.DetEquiv.expRes (Muon.emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t s -
          Muon.DetEquiv.Rbar (Muon.DetEquiv.Fw (Muon.emaW R.β)) (Muon.Drift.specOutFM (R.F k))
            (Muon.Drift.specInFM (R.F k)) (R.F k).C s) ≤ eB k) ∧
    (∀ eB' : ℕ → ℝ,
      (∀ k, ∀ s ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, Muon.opNorm (Muon.DetEquiv.expResT (Muon.emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t s -
          Muon.DetEquiv.Rtbar (Muon.DetEquiv.Fw (Muon.emaW R.β)) (Muon.Drift.specOutFM (R.F k))
            (Muon.Drift.specInFM (R.F k)) (R.F k).C s) ≤ eB' k) →
      ∀ k, ∀ ϱ ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, ∀ j : Fin (R.n k),
        Integrable (fun ω => dotProduct (Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω *ᵥ (Sp k).v j)
          (Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω *ᵥ (Sp k).v j)) (P k) ∧
        |(∫ ω, dotProduct (Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω *ᵥ (Sp k).v j)
            (Muon.Setup.frozenUpd (R.F k) (S k) ϱ t ω *ᵥ (Sp k).v j) ∂(P k)) -
          psiIso R.β ((R.n k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ((R.m k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ϱ /
            ((R.n k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ))| ≤ R.ϱhi * eB' k) ∧
    (∃ eB' : ℕ → ℝ, Tendsto eB' atTop (𝓝 0) ∧
      ∀ k, ∀ s ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi, Muon.opNorm (Muon.DetEquiv.expResT (Muon.emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t s -
          Muon.DetEquiv.Rtbar (Muon.DetEquiv.Fw (Muon.emaW R.β)) (Muon.Drift.specOutFM (R.F k))
            (Muon.Drift.specInFM (R.F k)) (R.F k).C s) ≤ eB' k) ∧
    Tendsto (fun k => ∫⁻ ω, ⨆ ϱ ∈ Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi,

          psiIso R.β ((R.n k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ((R.m k : ℝ) / (R.B k : ℝ)) ϱ) ^ 2) ∂(P k))
