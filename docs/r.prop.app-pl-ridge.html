Proposition SG.7 (Negligible ridge)
Assume Assumption S4. Let \(0<\ridge_N\le\bar z_N\) and let \(H_N\ge0\) be integers with
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.38} \frac{\ridge_N\,Q_\gamma}{c_NN^{-\alpha}\gamma^2}\to0,\qquad (1+H_N)\sqrt{D_\star}\,\ridge_N^{1/4}\to0,\qquad E_\star\,\bmom^{H_N/3}\to0 . \end{equation}
Then Theorem SG.1, Theorem SG.2 and Theorem SG.3, Corollary SG.1 and Corollary SG.2 hold with \(\mathfrak d_i(\ridge_N)\), \(\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge_N)\) and \(\mathfrak v_i(\ridge_N)\) in place of the zero-ridge coefficients. Such sequences \((\ridge_N,H_N)\) exist.
Proof of Proposition SG.7.
The drift error is uniform and small relative to the approximant. By (SG.13) and \(Q_\gamma\ge1\),
\[ \max_i\abs{\mathfrak d_i(\ridge_N)-\mathfrak d_i(0)}\le\frac{C}{\gamma}\sqrt{\ridge_NQ_\gamma}=C\,a_N\Big(\frac{\ridge_NQ_\gamma}{c_NN^{-\alpha}\gamma^2}\Big)^{1/2}=o(a_N), \]
while \(\widehat{\mathfrak d}_i\ge c\,a_N\) for all rows (end of Section SG.5). The volatility error is uniformly small: by (SG.21) with \(H=H_N\), \(D_i\le D_\star\) and \(E_i\le E_\star\),
\[ \max_i\abs{\mathfrak v_i(\ridge_N)-\mathfrak v_i(0)}\le2(1+2H_N)\sqrt{D_\star}\,\ridge_N^{1/4}+\frac{4E_\star\bmom^{H_N/3}}{1-\bmom^{1/3}}\longrightarrow0, \]
and the same bound holds for each lag coefficient by Proposition SG.4(i). Since \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\ge1\), the additive errors preserve every relative statement, and they preserve the limits of Theorem SG.3, which are positive or infinite. For existence, choose \(H_N\) with \(E_\star\bmom^{H_N/3}\le1/N\), and then \(\ridge_N\in(0,\bar z_N]\) small enough for the first two quantities of (SG.38) to be at most \(1/N\).
□
