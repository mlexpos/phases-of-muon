Theorem SF.8 (Small-aspect integrated volatility)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\Nin=\Nout=N\) and \(\gamma=N/B\le(1+\bmom^2)/96\). Then for every \(\ell\ge1\)
\[ \big|\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)-\Omega(\bmom^\ell)\big|\le\frac{96\gamma}{1+\bmom^2}\,\Omega(\bmom^\ell), \]
and the zero-ridge volatility of Definition SF.1 satisfies
\[ \Big|\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}}N-V_\bmom\Big|\le\frac{96\gamma}{1+\bmom^2}\,\big(V_\bmom-1\big),\qquad \Big|\frac{\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}}{N\,V_\bmom}-1\Big|\le96\gamma . \]
Proof.
The proof scales the zero-ridge double integral to the linear root and compares the integrands pointwise.
Scaling. Write \(q_s\defeq q(-s)\) and \(\bar\gamma\defeq\sw2\gamma\). For \(\xi>0\) put \(y_\gamma(\xi)\defeq\sw2\,q_{\bar\gamma\xi^2}/\gamma\), and let \(y\) be the root of \(xy=(1-y)^2\), \(y<1\), the case \(r=1\) of the closed form in the proof of Theorem SF.7. The sandwich of that proof with \(\gamma_\wedge=\gamma\), which uses only \(\varepsilon_1\le\frac12\), together with \(q_c(s)=c^{-1}Y_{\gamma,\gamma}(s/c)\) and the monotonicity of \(Y_{\gamma,\gamma}\), gives
\[ y(\xi^2)\le y_\gamma(\xi)\le(1-\varepsilon_1)^{-1}y(\xi^2),\qquad q_s\le\bar q=\frac{2\gamma}{\sw2}, \qquad\varepsilon_1=6\gamma\frac{\sw4}{\sw2^2} . \]
Fix \(\ell\ge1\), put \(r\defeq\bmom^\ell\), and in (SF.52) at \(\ridge=0\) substitute \(x=\sqrt{\bar\gamma}\,\xi\) and \(y=\sqrt{\bar\gamma}\,\xi'\). With \(\mathsf Y\defeq y_\gamma(\xi)y_\gamma(\xi')\), \(\tilde b\defeq b_\ell/(r\sw2)\) and \(\eta\defeq X_\ell\), one has \(q\,q'=\gamma^2\mathsf Y/\sw2^2\) and \(Z_\ell^2=r^2\tilde b^2\mathsf Y\), so that
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(0)=\frac4{\pi^2}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\frac{r\,\tilde b\,\mathsf Y}{(1-\eta)^2-r^2\tilde b^2\mathsf Y}\,\dif\xi\,\dif\xi' . \]
Pointwise bounds. Put \(\delta\defeq2\varepsilon_1=12\gamma\sw4/\sw2^2=12\gamma(1-\bmom^2)/(1+\bmom^2)\). The inequality \(\frac1{(1+a)(1+c)}\ge1-a-c\) for \(a,c\ge0\), inserted in the series (SG.14), gives
\[ b_\ell\ge r\big(\sw2-3\sw4(q+r^2q')\big)\ge r\sw2(1-\delta), \]
so \(\tilde b\in[1-\delta,1]\) by Lemma SF.11(i). By the same lemma and \(q,q'\le\bar q\), \(0\le\eta\le3r^2\sw4\bar q^2/\gamma\le\delta\). With \(\mathsf Y_0\defeq y(\xi^2)y(\xi'^2)\le1\), the sandwich gives \(\mathsf Y_0\le\mathsf Y\le(1-\delta)^{-2}\mathsf Y_0\). The hypothesis on \(\gamma\) is equivalent to \(4\delta\le(1-\bmom^2)/2\). The integrand increases in \(\tilde b\), in \(\mathsf Y\) and in \(\eta\), its denominator being \(\Delta_\ell>0\). Put
\[ \mathfrak f\defeq\frac{r\,\mathsf Y_0}{1-r^2\mathsf Y_0},\qquad 1-r^2\mathsf Y_0\ge1-\bmom^2 . \]
For the upper bound, evaluate at \(\tilde b=1\), \(\mathsf Y=(1-\delta)^{-2}\mathsf Y_0\) and \(\eta=\delta\), and use \((1-\delta)^4\ge1-4\delta\) and \((1-x)^{-1}\le1+2x\) for \(x\le\frac12\):
\[ \text{integrand}\le\frac{r\mathsf Y_0}{(1-\delta)^4-r^2\mathsf Y_0}\le\frac{r\mathsf Y_0}{1-r^2\mathsf Y_0-4\delta} \le\mathfrak f\Big(1+\frac{8\delta}{1-\bmom^2}\Big). \]
For the lower bound, evaluate at \(\tilde b=1-\delta\), \(\mathsf Y=\mathsf Y_0\) and \(\eta=0\), and use \(1-r^2(1-\delta)^2\mathsf Y_0\le1-r^2\mathsf Y_0+2\delta\):
\[ \text{integrand}\ge\frac{r(1-\delta)\mathsf Y_0}{1-r^2(1-\delta)^2\mathsf Y_0} \ge\mathfrak f\,(1-\delta)\Big(1-\frac{2\delta}{1-\bmom^2}\Big)\ge\mathfrak f\Big(1-\frac{3\delta}{1-\bmom^2}\Big). \]
Limit kernel. Expanding \(\mathfrak f=\sum_{n\ge0}r^{2n+1}\mathsf Y_0^{n+1}\) and integrating termwise, with \(\xi=2\sinh\vartheta'\) as in the proof of Theorem SF.7,
\[ \frac4{\pi^2}\iint\mathfrak f\,\dif\xi\,\dif\xi'=\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge0}r^{2n+1}\Big(\int_0^\infty y(\xi^2)^{n+1}\dif\xi\Big)^2, \]
\[ \int_0^\infty y(\xi^2)^{n+1}\dif\xi=\int_0^\infty e^{-2(n+1)\vartheta'}\,2\cosh\vartheta'\,\dif\vartheta'=k_n . \]
The integral of \(\mathfrak f\) is therefore \(\Omega(\bmom^\ell)\), and \(8\delta/(1-\bmom^2)=96\gamma/(1+\bmom^2)\) gives the lag bound.
Summation. By Proposition SF.3(ii), \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}/N=1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\), and \(V_\bmom=1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\Omega(\bmom^\ell)\). Summing the lag bounds gives the first volatility bound, and dividing by \(V_\bmom\ge1\) gives the second.
□
