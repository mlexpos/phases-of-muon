Proposition SF.7 (Spectral form of the constant)
Let \((\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}},\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{in}})\) be the isotropic measure pair at \((\gin,\gout)\) of Proposition SF.6. Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.27} C(\gin,\gout)=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\gin\gout}\int\sqrt\lambda\,\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}(\dif\lambda) =\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\gin\gout}\int\sqrt\lambda\,\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{in}}(\dif\lambda). \end{equation}
For \(b>0\) let \(\widehat{\mathfrak m}_b\) be the image of \(\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}\) under \(\lambda\mapsto\lambda/b\), a measure of mass \(\gout\) with transform \(S_{\widehat{\mathfrak m}_b}(-t)=b\,\sigma(-bt)\), and with density \(b\rho(bx)\) when \(\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}\) has density \(\rho\). Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.28} C(\gin,\gout)=\frac{\sqrt b}{\sqrt2\,\gin\gout}\int\sqrt x\,\widehat{\mathfrak m}_b(\dif x). \end{equation}
In particular, at \(b=\gin\gout\),
\[ C(\gin,\gout)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\gin\gout}}\int\sqrt x\,\widehat{\mathfrak m}_{\gin\gout}(\dif x). \]
Proof.
By (SF.23), Lemma SF.3(i) and the half-moment formula (SF.26),
\[ C(\gin,\gout)=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\pi\,\gin\gout}\int_0^\infty t^{-1/2}\int\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+t}\,\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}(\dif\lambda)\,\dif t =\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\gin\gout}\int\sqrt\lambda\,\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}(\dif\lambda). \]
The second form of (SF.27) holds because the two measures differ by an atom at \(0\) (Lemma SF.3(iii)), where \(\sqrt\lambda\) vanishes. For the rescaled measure,
\[ S_{\widehat{\mathfrak m}_b}(-t)=\int\frac{\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}(\dif\lambda)}{\lambda/b+t}=b\int\frac{\mathfrak m_{\mathrm{out}}(\dif\lambda)}{\lambda+bt}=b\,\sigma(-bt), \]
the density is the change of variables \(x=\lambda/b\), and \(\sqrt\lambda=\sqrt b\,\sqrt{\lambda/b}\) gives (SF.28).
□

    (hp : IsIsoMeasurePair γin γout mo mi) :
    (∀ b : ℝ, 0 < b →
      isoScaledImage b mo univ = ENNReal.ofReal γout ∧
      (∀ t : ℝ, 0 < t →
        isoNegStieltjes (isoScaledImage b mo) t = b * isoNegStieltjes mo (b * t)) ∧
      (∀ ρ : ℝ → ℝ, mo = volume.withDensity (fun l => ENNReal.ofReal (ρ l)) →
        isoScaledImage b mo = volume.withDensity (fun x => ENNReal.ofReal (b * ρ (b * x)))) ∧
      Cbeta 0 γin γout =
        Real.sqrt b / (Real.sqrt 2 * γin * γout) * ∫ x, Real.sqrt x ∂(isoScaledImage b mo)) ∧
