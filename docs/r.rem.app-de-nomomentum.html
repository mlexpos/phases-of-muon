Remark SB.1 (Dictionary to the no-momentum system)
At \(\bmom=0\), with \(z=-s\), the system (SB.2) for the spectral measures (SA.41) is
\[ \begin{gathered} \sigma=\frac1B\Tr\big(\Sigmaout(\tilde s_1\Sigmaout-z\Id)^{-1}\big),\qquad \tilde\sigma=\frac1B\Tr\big(\Sigmain(s_1\Sigmain-z\Id)^{-1}\big),\\ \tilde s_1\sigma=s_1\tilde\sigma=q\Jfun(q),\qquad q=-z\sigma\tilde\sigma . \end{gathered} \]
Put
\[ \xi\defeq\frac{1}{\sqrt{-2z\sigma\tilde\sigma}}=(2q)^{-1/2},\qquad f(\xi)\defeq\EE\frac{V^2}{V^2+2\xi^2}\quad(V\sim\mathcal N(0,1)). \]
By Proposition SB.1,
\[ q\Jfun(q)=f(\xi)=1-\sqrt\pi\,\xi\,e^{\xi^2}\operatorname{erfc}(\xi), \]
which is the third line of the fixed-point system of Section 2; the relation \(s_1=\tilde s_1\sigma/\tilde\sigma\) is its coupling relation. The deterministic equivalents (SB.3) are the ones displayed there. The trace identity
\[ \tilde s_1\sigma-\frac{\Nout}B=z\,\frac1B\Tr\Rbar(z) \]
is the sum over the eigenvalues \(\mu\) of \(\Sigmaout\) of
\[ \frac{\tilde s_1\mu}{\tilde s_1\mu-z}-1=\frac{z}{\tilde s_1\mu-z}. \]
For \(\bmom>0\) the single function \(f\) is replaced by \(qF_{\bmom}(q)\), and the drift section uses \(L_{\bmom}\) in place of \(\Jfun\) in the source factor.
