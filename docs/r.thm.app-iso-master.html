Theorem SF.3 (Master drift estimate)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\gin,\gout>0\) and \(\ridge\ge0\), with the convention \(\psi(0)\defeq\gamma_\wedge\).
  1. (i)
    The drift satisfies
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.31} \Big|\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)-\frac{1}{\sqrt\pi\,\gin\gout}\int_0^{\psi(\ridge)}\mathsf W(m)\,\dif m\Big| \le\frac{\sqrt2\,(\mathsf g_\bmom+\mathsf g'_\bmom/2)}{\pi\sqrt{\gin\gout}} <\frac{2.32}{(1-\bmom)\sqrt{\gin\gout}} . \end{equation}
  2. (ii)
    In the square case, with \(\Xi_0(x)\defeq\pi^{-1/2}\big(x-\tfrac12x^2\big)\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.32} \Big|\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)-\Xi_0\Big(\frac{\psi(\ridge)}\gamma\Big)\Big|<\frac{2.32}{(1-\bmom)\gamma} . \end{equation}
Proof.
For part (i) write \(H\defeq\psi(\ridge)=m_\ridge\) and \(\Lambda_\bmom(Q_m)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,m+k(m)\), so that \(\abs k\le\mathsf g'_\bmom\) by Lemma SF.7(iii) and \(r_\bmom(Q_m)=m\). An integration by parts gives
\[ \int_0^Hm\big(-\mathsf W'(m)\big)\,\dif m+H\,\mathsf W(H)=\int_0^H\mathsf W(m)\,\dif m . \]
By Lemma SF.8, therefore,
\[ \int_0^\infty\mathsf G_\bmom(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u=\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}2\int_0^H\mathsf W(m)\,\dif m+R, \]
\[ R=\int_0^H\big(-\mathsf W'(m)\big)\Big[\sqrt{Q_m}\,\Lw(Q_m)+\frac{k(m)}2\Big]\dif m+\frac12\mathsf W(H)\,k(H)+E_\ridge, \]
where \(0\le E_\ridge\le\mathsf g_\bmom\,\mathsf W(H)\) by (SF.30), whose last term is at most \(\mathsf g_\bmom\,\mathsf W(H)\).
Since \(-\mathsf W'\ge0\) and \(0\le\sqrt{Q_m}\Lw(Q_m)\le\mathsf g_\bmom\),
\[ \abs R\le\Big(\mathsf g_\bmom+\frac{\mathsf g'_\bmom}2\Big)\big(\mathsf W(0)-\mathsf W(H)\big)+\Big(\mathsf g_\bmom+\frac{\mathsf g'_\bmom}2\Big)\mathsf W(H) =\Big(\mathsf g_\bmom+\frac{\mathsf g'_\bmom}2\Big)\sqrt{\gin\gout} . \]
Multiplying by \(\sqrt2/(\pi\gin\gout)\) ((SF.29)) and using \(\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt{2\pi}/(2\pi)=\pi^{-1/2}\) gives the first inequality of (SF.31). By Lemma SF.7(ii),(iii), \(\mathsf g_\bmom+\mathsf g'_\bmom/2<(2+\pi)/(1-\bmom)\), and \(\sqrt2(2+\pi)/\pi=2.3146\ldots\).
Part (ii) is part (i) at \(\gin=\gout=\gamma\), where
\[ \frac1{\gamma^2}\int_0^{\psi}(\gamma-m)\,\dif m=\frac\psi\gamma-\frac12\Big(\frac\psi\gamma\Big)^2 . \]
□
