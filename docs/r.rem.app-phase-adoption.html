Remark SI.1 (Where the adopted coefficients come from)
The power-law arrays of Definition SI.3 are modelled on the coefficients of the main text’s half-anisotropic table in the oversampled regime \(B\ge N\) (Section 4), at zero momentum and zero ridge. For momentum \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), Theorem SG.1 computes the zero-ridge row coefficients in the thin-batch regime \(N/B\to\infty\), and Remark SG.3 inserts them into the row equations as adopted coefficients. The oversampled regime is not covered by that theorem. The resolved-window endpoint of Corollary SG.1(ii),
\[ \sqrt2\,\mathfrak d_i(0)\sim\sqrt{\frac{\mu_i}{\gamma\,\sw2}},\qquad \mathfrak v_i(0)\to\frac1{\sw2}, \]
has the dependence on \(\mu_i\) and \(\gamma\) of the adopted SignSVD array, up to constant factors, and is the evidence for that array. In the oversampled regime itself, for \(B\gg N\) and rows \(i\ll N\) of the power law, the drift coefficient is \(\mathfrak d_i\sim\sqrt{\mu_iB/(2N)}\), the value in the main text’s table; the adopted SignSVD array is larger by the uniform factor \(\sqrt2\). A uniform factor \(c\) in the drift at a fixed volatility rescales the matched-step clock of SignSVD by \(c^{-2}\), so with the factor removed every ratio of first hitting times below is halved; no exponent and no phase boundary changes. The adopted arrays with momentum reduce to it at a rescaled step (Remark SI.5). The SignSGD array is compared with the corrected mode array of the SignSGD section in Remark SI.4. No statement of this section identifies a trajectory of an algorithm.
