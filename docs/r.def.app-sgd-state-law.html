Definition SJ.3 (Haar state law)
Fix \(K\ge1\), \(0<c\le L\), \(0<f_-\le f_+\) and \(C_{\rm col}>0\). Let \(\Nout,\Nin,B\) be positive integers with \(k\defeq\min\{\Nout,\Nin,B\}\ge2\) and \(\max\{\Nout,\Nin,B\}\le Kk\). Let \(\Lambda=\diag(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_{\Nout})\) and \(D\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\) be deterministic with
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.19} c\,\Idout\preceq\Lambda\preceq L\,\Idout,\qquad f_-\le\norm D_\F\le f_+,\qquad \max_j\norm{D_{\cdot j}}^2\le\frac{C_{\rm col}}k . \end{equation}
Let \(U\) be Haar distributed on the orthogonal group \(O(\Nout)\) and independent of the whitened batch \((z_b,w_b)_{b\le B}\). The half-anisotropic Haar model is
\[ \Sigmaout=U\Lambda U^\T,\qquad \Sigmain=\Idin,\qquad \Delta=UD,\qquad \Xout^{(b)}=U\Lambda^{1/2}z_b,\qquad \Xin^{(b)}=w_b . \]
