Lemma SG.21 (Continuum matching below and at one)
For \(0<\alpha\le1\), \(\Gamma_\alpha(x)\sim x^\alpha\Psi_\alpha(x)^2/c_0^2\) as \(x\downarrow0\); with Lemma SG.9 this is the second equivalence of (SG.27).
Proof.
The bulk profile has a positive double-integral form. With \(V_p\defeq V_\alpha(p)\) put
\[ A_x(p)\defeq\sqrt{\frac{f_x(p)(1-f_x(p))(1-p)}p},\qquad t(p,q)\defeq\int_0^1\frac{V_p}{V_p+r^\alpha}\frac{V_q}{V_q+r^\alpha}\,\dif r=\frac{V_qp-V_pq}{V_q-V_p}, \]
the second form by partial fractions in \(r\). For \(0<p<q<1\) the identities \(f_x(q)-f_x(p)=x^\alpha(V_q-V_p)/((V_q+x^\alpha)(V_p+x^\alpha))\) and \(1-t(p,q)/q=V_q(q-p)/(q(V_q-V_p))\) give
\[ \frac{f_x(q)-f_x(p)}{q-p}\sqrt{\frac{U_\alpha(p)}{U_\alpha(q)}}=\frac{A_x(p)A_x(q)}{\Delta_{\mathrm c}(p,q)},\qquad \Delta_{\mathrm c}(p,q)\defeq(1-p)\Big(1-\frac{t(p,q)}q\Big), \]
so that, substituting \(q=p+\tau\) in (SG.24),
\[ \Gamma_\alpha(x)=2\int_{0<p<q<1}\frac{A_x(p)A_x(q)}{\Delta_{\mathrm c}(p,q)}\,\dif p\,\dif q,\qquad F_x\defeq\Big(\int_0^1A_x\Big)^2=\frac{x^\alpha\Psi_\alpha(x)^2}{c_0^2}, \]
the last identity because \(A_x=x^{\alpha/2}\psi_x\) with \(\psi_x\) of the proof of Lemma SG.9. Without the denominator the double integral is \(F_x\). The continuum elasticity gives \(t(p,q)\le q-\mathsf T(V_q)\le q-cq(1-q)\), by Lemma SG.5(ii), so \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\ge c(1-p)(1-q)\); also \(t(p,q)\le p\).
The upper endpoint is of lower order. For fixed \(\eta>0\) and \(p\ge\eta\), \(A_x(p)\le C_\eta x^{\alpha/2}(1-p)\), since \(V_\alpha\asymp(1-p)^{-1}\). Write \(d_p=1-p\ge d_q=1-q\). If \(d_q\ge d_p/2\), then \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\ge cd_p^2\); otherwise \(1-t/q\ge1-p/q\ge d_p/2\), with the same conclusion. So the square \([\eta,1)^2\) contributes \(O_\eta(x^\alpha)\). On \(p<\eta/2<\eta<q\), \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\ge1/4\), and this rectangle contributes at most \(C_\eta x^{\alpha/2}\sqrt{F_x}\); the rest of the crossed region lies in the square with cutoff \(\eta/2\). Both bounds are \(o(F_x)\), because \(\Psi_\alpha(x)\to\infty\).
The interior diagonal strip is of lower order. On \(p<q<\eta<1/2\) with \(q/p\le R\), \(A_x(q)\le C_{\eta,R}A_x(p)\) and \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\ge c\), so the strip contributes at most
\[ C_{\eta,R}\int_0^\eta pA_x(p)^2\,\dif p\le C_{\eta,R}\int_0^\eta f_x(p)(1-f_x(p))\,\dif p . \]
For \(\alpha<1\), \(V_\alpha(p)\asymp p\) bounds this by \(Cx^\alpha\int_0^\eta p(p+x^\alpha)^{-2}\dif p=O(x^\alpha(1+\log(1/x)))\). For \(\alpha=1\), \(V_1(p)\asymp p/\mathcal L(p)\) with \(\mathcal L(p)=1+\log(1/p)\); the bound one below \(p_0=x\log(1/x)\) and \(f_x(1-f_x)\le Cx\mathcal L(p)/p\) above give \(O(x[\log(1/x)]^2)\). Both are \(o(F_x)\).
Off the strip, \(p/q<1/R\) and \(p,q<\eta\) give \(0\le1-\Delta_{\mathrm c}\le\eta+1/R\), and for \(\eta+1/R<1/2\) the exact and flat integrals there differ by at most \(C(\eta+1/R)F_x\). Letting \(x\downarrow0\), then \(\eta\downarrow0\) and \(R\to\infty\), proves \(\Gamma_\alpha/F_x\to1\).
□
