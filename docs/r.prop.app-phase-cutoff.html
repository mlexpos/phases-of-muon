Proposition SI.7 (A family outside the windows)
Fix \(0<\alpha<1\), \(\texp=1\), \(\alpha<\omega<1\) and \(T\ge4\), let \(\epsilon_N\defeq N^{-\omega}\), and let \(B_N\ge N\) be arbitrary integers. Then \(\epsilon_NN\to\infty\), and
\begin{gather*} \frac{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon_N}}{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}T_0^{\text{s-SVD}}\,\epsilon_N^{-1/2}\log(1/\epsilon_N)/(4\sqrt{8q_{\text{s-SVD}}})}\longrightarrow\frac\alpha{\omega}<1,\\ \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon_N}}{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}\,\epsilon_N^{-1}}\asymp N^{-(\omega-\alpha)/2}\longrightarrow0 , \end{gather*}
with constants depending only on \(\alpha\), \(\omega\) and \(T\).
Proof of Proposition SI.7.
Both kernels are finite exponential mixtures with rates \(2i^{-q_j}\) and weights summing to one: for SignSVD the weights are \(i^{-q}/H_N(q)\) with \(q=\alpha/2\), and for SignSGD they are \((1-\frac2\pi)/N\) and \(2i^{-\alpha}/(\pi H_N(\alpha))\). Every forcing and kernel rate is at least \(b_{\min}\defeq2N^{-q_j}\), and \(F_0=\frac12H_N(p)\in[\frac12,\frac12Z_p]\). For \(N>(2T)^{1/\omega}\) one has \(\epsilon_N<1/(2T)\le F_0/T\), so Lemma SI.11 applies, with
\[ H\le N^{q_j}L^*_N,\qquad \mathsf d\le N^{q_j-\omega/2}L^*_N,\qquad L^*_N\defeq\omega\log N+\log Z_p . \]
Write \(\mathsf I_j\defeq\int_0^{\hat u_j}\sqrt{Y_j}\) and \(\delta_1\defeq(\omega-\alpha)/2>0\); by (SI.30), \(t^j_{T\epsilon_N}=\mathcal S_jT_0^j\mathsf I_j/(2\sqrt{\epsilon_N})\).
For SignSVD, \(q=\alpha/2\) and \(p=\alpha+1=2q+1\). Lemma SI.11 gives
\[ J_N(p,q)-\mathsf I\le\sqrt T\,N^{-\delta_1},\qquad \mathsf I-J_N(p,q)\le2\sqrt{N^{-\delta_1}L^*_NJ_N(p,q)}+N^{-\delta_1}L^*_N , \]
and Lemma SI.12(i) gives \(J_N(p,q)=a_qq\log N+O(1)\). Hence \(\mathsf I=a_qq\log N+O(1)\). The ratio in the statement equals \(2\mathsf I/(a_q\log(1/\epsilon_N))\), and \(\log(1/\epsilon_N)=\omega\log N\), so it tends to \(2q/\omega=\alpha/\omega\).
For SignSGD, \(q=\alpha\) and \(p=q+1\). For \(N\ge4\cdot4^{1/\alpha}\), Lemma SI.12(ii) gives \(c_1'N^{\alpha/2}\le J_N(p,\alpha)\le c_2'N^{\alpha/2}\) with \(c_1'=\sqrt A\,4^{-\alpha/2}\) and \(c_2'=3C_{p,\alpha}\). Lemma SI.11 gives
\[ \frac{J_N-\mathsf I}{J_N}\le\frac{\sqrt T}{c_1'}N^{-\delta_1},\qquad \frac{\mathsf I-J_N}{J_N}\le2\sqrt{\frac{N^{-\delta_1}L^*_N}{c_1'}}+\frac{N^{-\delta_1}L^*_N}{c_1'} , \]
so \(\mathsf I/J_N\to1\) and \(\mathsf I\asymp N^{\alpha/2}\). Then
\[ \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon_N}}{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}\epsilon_N^{-1}}=\frac12\sqrt{\epsilon_N}\,\mathsf I\asymp N^{(\alpha-\omega)/2} . \]
Finally \(\epsilon_NN=N^{1-\omega}\to\infty\). None of the bounds involves \(B\).
□
