Corollary SI.2 (Fixed dimension)
For the arrays of Definition SI.3 at fixed \(N\), \(B\), \(\alpha\), \(\texp\) and \(T\ge4\), as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\),
\[ t^j_{T\epsilon}\sim\frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j\,J_N(p,q_j)}{2\sqrt\epsilon},\qquad \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}}{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}}\longrightarrow D_N\,\frac{J_N(p,\alpha)}{J_N(p,\alpha/2)}\ge D_N . \]
If \(\alpha\ge4\), this limit exceeds \(\pi/2\) for every \(N\).
Proof of Corollary SI.2.
The substitution \(s=T_0^ju\) gives \(\int_0^\infty\sqrt{F_j(s)}\,\dif s=T_0^jJ_N(p,q_j)\), and Theorem SI.11 gives the first equivalent. Dividing the two equivalents and using (SI.27) gives the limit of the ratio. Since \(i^{-\alpha}\le i^{-\alpha/2}\), each term of \(f_{N,\alpha}\) is at least the corresponding term of \(f_{N,\alpha/2}\), so \(J_N(p,\alpha)\ge J_N(p,\alpha/2)\). If \(\alpha\ge4\), then \(H_N(\alpha)\ge1\) and \(H_N(\alpha/2)\le\zeta(2)<2\) by Lemma SI.8, so \(D_N>\pi/2\).
□
