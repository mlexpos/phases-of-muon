Lemma SG.2 (Averaging in the participation coordinate)
Put \(\mathcal A(m)\defeq\sqrt{Q_m}\,\Lw(Q_m)/E(m)\) for \(m>0\). For every \(m_0>0\), \(\mathcal A\asymp1\) on \([m_0,\infty)\) with constants depending on \((\bmom,m_0)\), and
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.8} \sup_{t\ge m_0}\Big|\int_{m_0}^t\big(\mathcal A(m)-\sqrt{2\pi}\big)\,\dif m\Big|\le C_{\bmom,m_0}. \end{equation}
Consequently, for every \([a,c]\subset[m_0,\infty)\) and every continuously differentiable \(W\) on \([a,c]\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.9} \Big|\int_a^c\big(\mathcal A(m)-\sqrt{2\pi}\big)W(m)\,\dif m\Big|\le C_{\bmom,m_0}\Big(\abs{W(a)}+\abs{W(c)}+\int_a^c\abs{W'(m)}\,\dif m\Big). \end{equation}
Proof.
The two-sided bound on \(\mathcal A\) is Lemma SG.1(iii) for \(m\ge1\) and continuity and positivity on \([m_0,1]\).
The average is computed in the variable \(q\). Since \(\dif m=r_\bmom'(q)\,\dif q\) at \(m=r_\bmom(q)\) and \(E(m)=qr_\bmom'(q)\),
\[ \int_{m_0}^t\mathcal A(m)\,\dif m=\int_{q_0}^{Q_t}\sqrt q\,\Lw(q)\,\frac{\dif q}{q} =\sum_{j\ge0}\int_{\emaw j^2q_0}^{\emaw j^2Q_t}x^{-1/2}\Jfun(x)\,\dif x,\qquad q_0\defeq Q_{m_0}, \]
by the substitution \(x=\emaw j^2q\) in the \(j\)-th term of \(\Lw\). The full integral of each term is
\[ \int_0^\infty x^{-1/2}\Jfun(x)\,\dif x=\EE\Big[\abs g\int_0^\infty\frac{y^{-1/2}}{1+y}\,\dif y\Big]=\pi\,\EE\abs g=\sqrt{2\pi}, \]
by the substitution \(y=xg^2\) and the Beta integral \(\int_0^\infty y^{-1/2}(1+y)^{-1}\dif y=\pi\) [85, (5.12.3)]. The incomplete terms are controlled by \(x^{-1/2}\Jfun(x)\le\min(x^{-1/2},x^{-3/2})\): for \(j<n(Q_t)\) the missing parts are at most \(2\emaw j\sqrt{q_0}\) and \(2(\emaw j\sqrt{Q_t})^{-1}\), and for \(j\ge n(Q_t)\) the whole term is at most \(2\emaw j\sqrt{Q_t}\). Summing these geometric bounds as in the proof of Lemma SG.1(iii),
\[ \Big|\int_{m_0}^t\mathcal A(m)\,\dif m-\sqrt{2\pi}\,n(Q_t)\Big|\le2\sqrt{q_0}+\frac{4}{1-\bmom}. \]
For \(t\ge1\), Lemma SG.1(iii) replaces \(n(Q_t)\) by \(r_\bmom(Q_t)=t\) up to \(C_\bmom\), and for \(m_0\le t\le1\) both integrals are bounded. This proves (SG.8).
The weighted bound is an integration by parts. With \(\Phi_0(v)\defeq\int_{m_0}^v(\mathcal A-\sqrt{2\pi})\,\dif m\),
\[ \int_a^c\big(\mathcal A-\sqrt{2\pi}\big)W\,\dif m=\Phi_0(c)W(c)-\Phi_0(a)W(a)-\int_a^c\Phi_0W'\,\dif m \]
[92, Theorem 6.22], and \(\abs{\Phi_0}\le C_{\bmom,m_0}\) by (SG.8).
□
