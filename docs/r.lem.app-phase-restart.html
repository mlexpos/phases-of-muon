Lemma SI.21 (Restart)
Assume the setting of Lemma SI.11, with any \(\epsilon>0\). Let \(u_0\ge0\), suppose that \(Y>T\epsilon\) on \([0,u_0]\), and put \(G_0\defeq(g*\sqrt Y)(u_0)\). Then the first \(T\epsilon\) hit \(\hat u\) is attained in \(\big(u_0,u_0+\frac2{b_{\min}}\log\frac{Y(u_0)}\epsilon\big]\), and
\[ \begin{gathered} \int_{u_0}^{\hat u}\sqrt Y\,\dif u\le\big(\tilde{\mathsf J}^{1/2}+\tilde{\mathsf d}^{1/2}\big)^2,\\ \tilde{\mathsf J}\defeq\int_{u_0}^\infty\sqrt f\,\dif u+\frac2{b_{\min}}\,\epsilon^{1/4}G_0^{1/2},\qquad \tilde{\mathsf d}\defeq\frac2{b_{\min}}\sqrt\epsilon\,\log\frac{Y(u_0)}\epsilon . \end{gathered} \]
Proof.
The memory splits exactly at \(u_0\). Put
\[ y_l\defeq c_l\int_0^{u_0}e^{-c_l(u_0-v)}\sqrt{Y(v)}\,\dif v\ge0, \]
so that \(\sum_lw_ly_l=G_0\). For \(\tilde Y(z)\defeq Y(u_0+z)\), splitting the convolution integral at \(v=u_0\) gives
\[ \tilde Y=\tilde f+\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt{\tilde Y},\qquad \tilde f(z)\defeq\sum_ia_ie^{-b_iu_0}e^{-b_iz}+\sqrt\epsilon\sum_lw_ly_le^{-c_lz} . \]
This \(\tilde f\) is a finite exponential mixture with nonnegative coefficients and rates at least \(b_{\min}\), and
\[ \tilde f(0)=f(u_0)+\sqrt\epsilon\,G_0=Y(u_0)>T\epsilon . \]
By Theorem SI.1, applied with the union of the two rate lists and zero coefficients where needed, \(\tilde Y\) is the unique positive continuous solution of its equation. So Lemma SI.11 applies with initial value \(Y(u_0)\) in place of \(F_0\). Since \(Y>T\epsilon\) on \([0,u_0]\), the first hit of \(Y\) is \(u_0\) plus that of \(\tilde Y\), and Lemma SI.11 bounds both, with \(\int_0^\infty\sqrt{\tilde f}\) in place of \(\tilde{\mathsf J}\). Finally \(\tilde f(z)\le f(u_0+z)+\sqrt\epsilon\,G_0\,e^{-b_{\min}z}\) and \(\sqrt{a+b}\le\sqrt a+\sqrt b\) give \(\int_0^\infty\sqrt{\tilde f}\le\tilde{\mathsf J}\), and \(x\mapsto(\sqrt x+\sqrt{\tilde{\mathsf d}})^2\) is increasing.
□
