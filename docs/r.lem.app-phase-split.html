Lemma SI.23 (Kernel with a slow part)
Let \(g\ge0\) be continuous with \(\int_0^\infty g\le1\), and suppose that for \(0\le v\le U_0\)
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.60} g(v)\le K_f(1+v)^{-\upsilon}+K_sN^{-q}\Big(\frac{1+v}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b}, \end{equation}
with \(K_f,K_s\ge0\), \(\upsilon>1\), \(0\le\mathsf b<1\), \(N\ge1\) and \(q>0\). Let \(0<r_0\le\upsilon\), \(D\ge1\), \(C>0\), \(h(u)\defeq C(1+u/D)^{-r_0}\), \(\delta_\circ=1-(2/3)^{1/r_0}\) and \(\mathsf k_f\defeq K_f\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon}D^{1-\upsilon}b(r_0,\upsilon)\), with \(b(r_0,\upsilon)\) of (SI.55). Then for \(0\le u\le U_0\),
\[ (g*h)(u)\le\Big(\frac32+\mathsf k_f\Big)h(u)+K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}N^{-q}\Big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b}C\,D\,I_{r_0}(u/D), \]
and if \(r_0<1\) the last term is at most \(\frac{K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}}{1-r_0}\big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\big)^{1-\mathsf b}h(u)\).
Proof.
Fix \(u\in(0,U_0]\) and split the convolution at \(v=\delta_\circ(D+u)\). For \(0\le v\le\min\{u,\delta_\circ(D+u)\}\) one has \(D+u-v\ge(1-\delta_\circ)(D+u)\), so \(h(u-v)\le(1-\delta_\circ)^{-r_0}h(u)=\frac32h(u)\), and the mass of \(g\) bounds this part by \(\frac32h(u)\). For \(\delta_\circ(D+u)<v\le u\) one has \(1+v>\delta_\circ(D+u)\), so (SI.60) gives
\[ g(v)\le K_f\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon}(D+u)^{-\upsilon}+K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}N^{-q}\Big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b}, \]
while \(\int_0^uh=C\,D\,I_{r_0}(u/D)\). With \(z=u/D\) the fast part is at most
\[ K_f\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon}D^{1-\upsilon}(1+z)^{r_0-\upsilon}I_{r_0}(z)\,h(u)\le\mathsf k_f\,h(u) , \]
by the bound \((1+z)^{r_0-\upsilon}I_{r_0}(z)\le b(r_0,\upsilon)\) of the proof of Lemma SI.14. The slow part is the stated term. For \(r_0<1\), finally,
\[ D\,I_{r_0}(u/D)\le\frac{D(1+u/D)^{1-r_0}}{1-r_0}=\frac{(D+u)(1+u/D)^{-r_0}}{1-r_0} , \]
which gives the last assertion.
□
