Proposition SA.4 (Conditional recursion of the projected risks)
In Definition SA.1, fix \(t\ge0\), let \(\Dt\) be the state at step \(t\) with \(\EE\norm{\Dt}_\F^2<\infty\), let \({\mathsf S}\) be a random matrix in \(\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\) with \(\EE\norm {\mathsf S}_\F^2<\infty\) (the update, determined by the past and the batch of step \(t\)), let \(\lr_t\ge0\) be deterministic, and put \(\Delta_{t+1}\defeq\Dt-\lr_t{\mathsf S}\). Define
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.19} \cD_{ij}(t)\defeq(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)\,\EE_t\big[u_i^\T {\mathsf S}v_j\big],\qquad \cV_{ij}(t)\defeq\EE_t\big[(u_i^\T {\mathsf S}v_j)^2\big]. \end{equation}
Then
\begin{align} \EE_t\big[\CMscr Q_{ij}(\Delta_{t+1})\big]&=\CMscr Q_{ij}(\Dt)-2\lr_t\,\cD_{ij}(t)+\lr_t^2\,\cV_{ij}(t),\tag{SA.20}\\ \EE_t\big[\CMscr Q_{i}(\Delta_{t+1})\big]&=\CMscr Q_{i}(\Dt)-2\lr_t\sum_j\cD_{ij}(t)+\lr_t^2\sum_j\cV_{ij}(t),\tag{SA.21}\\ \EE_t\big[\tfrac12\norm{\Delta_{t+1}}_\Sigma^2\big]&=\tfrac12\norm{\Dt}_\Sigma^2-\lr_t\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\cD_{ij}(t)+\frac{\lr_t^2}2\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\cV_{ij}(t),\tag{SA.22} \end{align}
with the contractions in the last line equal to
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.23} \sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\cD_{ij}(t)=\big\langle\Dt,\EE_t{\mathsf S}\big\rangle_\Sigma,\qquad \sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\cV_{ij}(t)=\EE_t\norm{{\mathsf S}}_\Sigma^2 . \end{equation}
Proof.
Write \(a_{ij}\defeq u_i^\T\Dt v_j\) and \(b_{ij}\defeq u_i^\T {\mathsf S}v_j\); the coefficient \(a_{ij}\) is determined by the past. The mode identity is the expansion of a square. Pointwise,
\[ \CMscr Q_{ij}(\Delta_{t+1})=(a_{ij}-\lr_tb_{ij})^2=a_{ij}^2-2\lr_ta_{ij}b_{ij}+\lr_t^2b_{ij}^2 , \]
and taking \(\EE_t\) term by term gives (SA.20).
The row and total identities are finite weighted sums of the mode identity. Summing (SA.20) over \(j\) with weight \(1\), and over \((i,j)\) with weight \(\mu_i\lambda_j/2\), and applying Lemma SA.1 to \(\Delta_{t+1}\) and to \(\Dt\), gives (SA.21) and (SA.22).
The contraction identities are the eigenexpansion of the pairing. For any \(V,W\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\),
\[ \langle V,W\rangle_\Sigma=\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\,(u_i^\T Vv_j)(u_i^\T Wv_j), \]
by the computation in the proof of Lemma SA.1 with \(V\) and \(W\) in the two slots. Taking \((V,W)=(\Dt,{\mathsf S})\) and \(({\mathsf S},{\mathsf S})\) and applying \(\EE_t\) gives (SA.23).
□
