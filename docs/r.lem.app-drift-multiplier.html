Lemma SC.3 (Deletion with a bounded multiplier)
Let \(s_\circ>0\). In the setting of Lemma SC.2, let \(h\) be a scalar random variable depending on the sample \((j,a)\) and on \(\mathcal G=\mathcal G_{ja}\), with \(\abs h\le D_h\) for a constant \(D_h\). Then for every \(s\ge s_\circ\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.20} \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}\big[h\,(\mathsf X_s(M)-\mathsf X_s(M_0))\big]\big\|_\F\le\frac{C\,D_h\,\varpi}{s\sqrt B}, \end{equation}
with \(C\) depending only on \((s_\circ,C_\Sigma,\gamma_\star)\). In particular, for a sample of age \(j\) and \(s\ge s_\circ\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.21} \norm{\mathcal H(s)-\mathcal H_j(s)}_\F\le\frac{C\,\emaw j}{s\sqrt B},\qquad \mathcal H_j(s)\defeq\EE\,\mathsf X_0^{(j1)} . \end{equation}
Both bounds, with \(s\) replaced by \(x\defeq\Ree z\), hold at every \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\), for \(\mathsf X_z\) in place of \(\mathsf X_s\), for a complex multiplier \(h\) with \(\abs h\le D_h\), and with \(\mathcal H(z)\) and \(\mathcal H_j(z)\defeq\EE\,\mathsf X_z(M_0^{(j1)})\).
Proof.
The conditional moments of the rank-one factors are bounded through Lemma SB.7. For a scalar \(e\) depending on the sample and on \(\mathcal G\), the identities \(B\,\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf u=\Sigmaout^{1/2}(z\tensor z)\Sigmaout^{1/2}\), \(B\,\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf v=\Sigmaout^{1/2}(z\tensor w)\Sigmain^{1/2}\) and \(\EE_{\mathcal G}[(z\tensor z)e]=\EE_{\mathcal G}[(z\tensor z-\Id)e]+\Id\,\EE_{\mathcal G}e\), together with \(\norm{\Id_{\Nout}}_\F=\sqrt{\Nout}\le\sqrt{\gamma_\star B}\) and \(\abs{\EE_{\mathcal G}e}\le\norm e_{2,\mathcal G}\), give
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.22} \begin{gathered} \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[(\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf u)e]\big\|_\F\le\frac{C_\Sigma(\sqrt2+\sqrt{\gamma_\star B})}{B}\norm e_{2,\mathcal G}\le\frac{C\norm e_{2,\mathcal G}}{\sqrt B},\\ \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[(\mathsf v\tensor\mathsf v)e]\big\|_\F\le\frac{C\norm e_{2,\mathcal G}}{\sqrt B},\qquad \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[(\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf v)e]\big\|_\F=\big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[(\mathsf v\tensor\mathsf u)e]\big\|_\F\le\frac{C_\Sigma\norm e_{2,\mathcal G}}{B}. \end{gathered} \end{equation}
The four terms of (SC.18) are bounded one by one. The outer factors \(R_0\), \(\widetilde R_0\) and \(\mathsf X_0\) are functions of \(\mathcal G\) with norms at most \(1/s\), \(1/s\) and \((2\sqrt s)^{-1}\) by Lemma SB.3, and the multiplied coefficients satisfy \(\norm{h\alpha_i}_{2,\mathcal G}\le D_h\norm{\alpha_i}_{2,\mathcal G}\). With (SC.22) and Lemma SC.2 at \(p=2\),
\[ \begin{aligned} \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[h\alpha_3\,R_0(\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf u)\mathsf X_0]\big\|_\F &\le\frac1s\cdot\frac{1}{2\sqrt s}\cdot\frac{C}{\sqrt B}\,D_h\,C\varpi^2\le\frac{CD_h\varpi^2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B},\\ \big\|s\,\EE_{\mathcal G}[h\alpha_1\,R_0(\mathsf u\tensor\mathsf v)\widetilde R_0]\big\|_\F &\le s\cdot\frac1s\cdot\frac1s\cdot\frac{C_\Sigma}{B}\,D_h\,C\varpi\le\frac{CD_h\varpi}{sB},\\ \big\|\EE_{\mathcal G}[h\alpha_1\,\mathsf X_0(\mathsf v\tensor\mathsf u)\mathsf X_0]\big\|_\F &\le\frac{1}{4s}\cdot\frac{C_\Sigma}{B}\,D_h\,C\varpi\le\frac{CD_h\varpi}{sB},\\ \big\|s\,\EE_{\mathcal G}[h\alpha_2\,\mathsf X_0(\mathsf v\tensor\mathsf v)\widetilde R_0]\big\|_\F &\le s\cdot\frac{1}{2\sqrt s}\cdot\frac1s\cdot\frac{C}{\sqrt B}\,D_h\,\frac{C\varpi^2}{s}\le\frac{CD_h\varpi^2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B}. \end{aligned} \]
Since \(\varpi^2\le\varpi\), \(s^{-3/2}\le s_\circ^{-1/2}s^{-1}\) and \(B^{-1}\le B^{-1/2}\), the sum of the four bounds is at most \(CD_h\varpi/(s\sqrt B)\), which proves (SC.20).
The deletion-mean bound is the case \(h=1\). Taking \(M=\Mt\), \(M_0=M_0^{(j1)}\), \(\varpi=\emaw j\) in (SC.20) and then the full expectation gives (SC.21).
At \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\) the proof is the same, with (SC.18) at \(z\), the norms \(1/x\), \(1/x\) and \((2\sqrt x)^{-1}\) of \(R_0\), \(\widetilde R_0\) and \(\mathsf X_0\) from Lemma SB.3, the sector bounds of Lemma SC.2, and (SC.22), which holds for complex \(e\) since Lemma SB.7 does. The factor \(s\) of the second and fourth terms becomes \(z\), with \(\abs z\le\frac{\sqrt5}{2}x\), so the four bounds are the displayed ones with \(s\) replaced by \(x\), the second and fourth multiplied by \(\sqrt5/2\).
□
