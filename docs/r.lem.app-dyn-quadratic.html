Lemma SE.22 (Quadratic block response)
In the setting of Lemma SE.16 with \(\mathcal K=\mathcal A\), let \(S=\sum_{r<L}V_r\), \(\mathcal E=\sum_{r<L}{\mathsf S}_{t+r}-S\) and \(a\defeq1+\norm{M^\circ_{t-1}}_\F/\sqrt B\), and let \(\mathcal W\) be selfadjoint with \(\norm{\mathcal W}_{\F\to\F}\le1\). On \(\{\Dt\in\mathcal A\}\),
\[ \frac{1}{LB}\Big|\EE_t\big[2\langle S,\mathcal W\mathcal E\rangle+\langle\mathcal E,\mathcal W\mathcal E\rangle\big]\Big| \le C\Big\{\frac{a+a^2}{L}+\frac{a}{\sqrt B}+\frac{L^2}{B}+\frac{L^{3/2}}{\sqrt B}+\frac{aL}{\sqrt B}+\frac{L^3}{B}\Big\}. \]
Proof.
Write \(\mathcal E=A+J\) with \(A=\sum_{r<L}(\widehat V_r-V_r)\) and \(J=\sum_{r<L}({\mathsf S}_{t+r}-\widehat V_r)\). Since \(\mathcal W\) is selfadjoint,
\[ 2\langle S,\mathcal W\mathcal E\rangle+\langle\mathcal E,\mathcal W\mathcal E\rangle =2\langle S,\mathcal WA\rangle+\langle A,\mathcal WA\rangle+2\langle S+A,\mathcal WJ\rangle+\langle J,\mathcal WJ\rangle . \]
By Lemma SE.17(ii) applied conditionally, the first term is at most \(Ca(B+L\sqrt B)\). By Lemma SE.16(ii), the second is at most \(Ca^2B\). By Lemma SE.17(i), (SE.15) and Lemma SE.16, \((\EE_t\norm{S+A}_\F^2)^{1/2}\le CL+C\sqrt{BL}+Ca\sqrt B\) and \((\EE_t\norm J_\F^2)^{1/2}\le CL^2\), so the last two terms are at most \(C(L+\sqrt{BL}+a\sqrt B)L^2+CL^4\). Dividing by \(LB\) gives the claim.
□
