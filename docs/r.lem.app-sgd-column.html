Lemma SJ.10 (Column moments)
There are admissible constants \(V_0\), \(A_4\), \(A_8\) such that, for every column \(j\),
\begin{align} \EE X&=m_j,\qquad V_j\defeq\EE[Z\tensor Z]=(2\cR+2C_j)\Lambda+2T_D+3\,m_j\tensor m_j,\tag{SJ.32}\\ \EE[h_j\tensor h_j]&=\frac{2\cR+2C_j}B\,\Lambda+\frac2B\,T_D+\Big(1+\frac3B\Big)m_j\tensor m_j,\tag{SJ.33}\\ \EE[HH^\T]&=\frac{2\cR\,(\Nin+2)}B\,\Lambda+\Big(1+\frac{2\Nin+3}B\Big)T_D,\tag{SJ.34} \end{align}
with \(C_j=D_{\cdot j}^\T\Lambda D_{\cdot j}\), and
\begin{gather} V_j\succeq2\cR\,\Lambda,\qquad \norm{V_j}\le V_0,\qquad \norm{m_j}^2\le\frac{L^2C_{\rm col}}k,\tag{SJ.35}\\ \EE\abs{a^\T Z}^4\le A_4\norm a^4,\quad \EE\abs{a^\T Z}^8\le A_8\norm a^8\quad(a\in\R^{\Nout}),\tag{SJ.36}\\ \EE\norm Z^4\le A_4\Nout^2,\quad \EE\norm Z^8\le A_8\Nout^4 .\tag{SJ.37} \end{gather}
Every column \(h_j\) is nonzero almost surely.
Proof.
Mean and covariance. Put \(y=\Lambda^{1/2}z\). Integrating \(w\) first with the Gaussian identities \(\EE[(y^\T Dw)w_j]=y^\T D_{\cdot j}\) and \(\EE[(y^\T Dw)^2w_j^2]=y^\T DD^\T y+2(y^\T D_{\cdot j})^2\), and then \(z\) with the pairing identity \(\EE[y\tensor y\,(y^\T My)]=\Tr(\Lambda M)\Lambda+2\Lambda M\Lambda\) for symmetric \(M\) [89], gives
\[ \EE X=\Lambda D_{\cdot j},\qquad \EE[X\tensor X]=(2\cR+2C_j)\Lambda+2T_D+4\,m_j\tensor m_j . \]
Subtracting \(m_j\tensor m_j\) gives (SJ.32). Averaging \(B\) independent copies gives (SJ.33), and summing over \(j\) with \(\sum_jC_j=2\cR\) and \(\sum_jm_j\tensor m_j=T_D\) gives (SJ.34).
Bounds. All terms of \(V_j\) are positive semidefinite, so \(V_j\succeq2\cR\Lambda\). With \(C_j\le LC_{\rm col}/k\), \(\norm{m_j}^2\le L^2C_{\rm col}/k\) and \(\norm{T_D}\le\Tr T_D\le H_0\),
\[ \norm{V_j}\le V_0\defeq LP_++2H_0+5L^2C_{\rm col}. \]
Higher moments. Put \(r=z^\T Aw\). Conditionally on \(w\), \(r\) is Gaussian with variance \(\norm{Aw}^2\), and the convexity argument of Lemma SJ.3 gives \(\EE r^{2q}\le((2q-1)!!)^2(2\cR)^q\). For \(p=4,8\), Hölder’s inequality on the three dependent factors of \(a^\T X\) gives
\[ \EE\abs{a^\T X}^p\le\big(\EE\abs r^{3p}\,\EE\abs{w_j}^{3p}\,\EE\abs{a^\T\Lambda^{1/2}z}^{3p}\big)^{1/3}\le\big((3p-1)!!\big)^2P_+^{p/2}L^{p/2}\norm a^p . \]
Centering costs a factor \(2^p\), which gives the directional bounds with \(A_4\defeq16(11!!)^2P_+^2L^2\) and \(A_8\defeq256(23!!)^2P_+^4L^4\). The inequalities \((\sum_lZ_l^2)^2\le\Nout\sum_lZ_l^4\) and \((\sum_lZ_l^2)^4\le\Nout^3\sum_lZ_l^8\) give the vector bounds.
Nonvanishing. Some coordinate of \(h_j\) is a polynomial in the batch that is not identically zero. Indeed choose \(w=e_j\) if \(D_{\cdot j}\ne0\) and \(w=e_j+e_l\) with \(D_{\cdot l}\ne0\) otherwise, so that \(w_j=1\) and \(Aw\ne0\). Taking \(z=Aw\) in the first sample and all other samples zero makes \(h_j\) a nonzero multiple of \(\Lambda^{1/2}Aw\ne0\). The batch has a Gaussian density, so this coordinate is nonzero almost surely [91].
□
