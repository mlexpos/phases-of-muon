Proposition SG.3 (Two-time row response)
For every finite positive spectrum, every \(\ell\ge0\) and all \(s,s'>0\), one has \(\Delta_\ell>0\) and
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.16} \mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(s,s')=\frac{\gamma\,ss'\,\mu_i\,b_\ell}{(s+\tilde s_1\mu_i)(s'+\tilde s_1'\mu_i)(s+s_1)(s'+s_1')\,\Delta_\ell}\ \ge0 . \end{equation}

This statement has no separate proof in the supplement.
