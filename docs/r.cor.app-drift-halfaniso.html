Corollary SC.2 (Half-anisotropic input)
Under Assumption S3, assume \(\Sigmain=\Id_{\Nin}\), let \(\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\), write \(z=\ridge+u^2\), and put \(\mathfrak d_i\defeq\mathfrak d(\mu_i,1)\).
  1. (i)
    For every pair \((i,j)\) the kernel is \(\mathfrak d_{ij}=\mathfrak d_i\), and
    \[ \mathfrak d_i=\frac{\sqrt2\,B}{\pi\,\Nin}\int_0^\infty\frac{z\,\Lw(q(-z))\,\tilde\sigma(-z)\,\mu_i}{z+\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i}\,du . \]
  2. (ii)
    The row drift of (SA.21) is, with \(E_\ridge\) as in Corollary SC.1,
    \[ \sum_j\cD_{ij}(t)=\frac{\mathfrak d_i}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}}\,\CMscr Q_i(t)+\sum_j(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)(E_\ridge)_{ij}. \]
  3. (iii)
    At \(\bmom=0\),
    \[ \mathfrak d_i=\frac{\sqrt2\,B}{\pi\,\Nin}\int_0^\infty\frac{\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i}{z+\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i}\,du . \]
  4. (iv)
    At \(\bmom=0\), if \(\rho_i\) is a probability measure on \([0,\infty)\) with
    \[ \big(z+\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i\big)^{-1}=\int\frac{\rho_i(dx)}{x+z}\qquad(z>0), \]
    then
    \[ \mathfrak d_i=\frac{B}{\sqrt2\,\Nin}\int_{[0,\infty)}\frac{x}{\sqrt{x+\ridge}}\,\rho_i(dx). \]
Proof.
Part (i) follows from the input trace equation. When \(\Sigmain=\Id_{\Nin}\) every \(\lambda_j\) equals \(1\), so \(\mathfrak d_{ij}=\mathfrak d(\mu_i,1)\) for every \(j\). The input trace equation of (SB.2) becomes algebraic,
\[ \tilde\sigma(-s)=\frac{\Nin/B}{s+s_1(-s)},\qquad\text{so that}\qquad \frac{1}{s+s_1(-s)}=\frac{B\,\tilde\sigma(-s)}{\Nin}. \]
Substituting this at \(s=z\) into (SC.4) with \(\lambda=1\) gives the formula of (i).
Part (ii) is the sum over \(j\) of (SC.12). The identity \(\CMscr Q_i=\sum_j\CMscr Q_{ij}\) of Lemma SA.1 collects the first terms, because \(\mathfrak d_{ij}=\mathfrak d_i\) by (i).
Part (iii) is the no-momentum form of the source factor. At \(\bmom=0\) the function \(\Lw\) is \(\Jfun\). By Remark SB.1, the coupling (SB.4) and \(q(-z)=z\sigma(-z)\tilde\sigma(-z)\) for \(z>0\),
\[ z\,\Jfun(q(-z))\,\tilde\sigma(-z)=\frac{q(-z)\Jfun(q(-z))}{\sigma(-z)}=\tilde s_1(-z). \]
Substituting this identity into (i) gives (iii).
Part (iv) integrates the Stieltjes representation in \(u\). By the hypothesis at \(z=\ridge+u^2\) and since \(\rho_i\) has mass one,
\[ \frac{\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i}{z+\tilde s_1(-z)\mu_i}=1-\int\frac{z\,\rho_i(dx)}{x+z}=\int\frac{x\,\rho_i(dx)}{x+z}. \]
The scalar integral of the proof of Lemma SA.3 is
\[ \frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\frac{du}{x+\ridge+u^2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+\ridge}} . \]
Integrating the previous display in \(u\) against \(\frac{\sqrt2\,B}{\pi\,\Nin}\,du=\frac{B}{\sqrt2\,\Nin}\cdot\frac2\pi\,du\) and inserting the scalar integral gives (iv).
□
