Proposition SG.6 (Reference scales)
Assume Assumption S4. For \(\gamma>1\) let \(\resscale\defeq\Lambda_N(1)\) be the unique solution of
\[ \sum_{i=1}^N\frac{\resscale\mu_i}{1+\resscale\mu_i}=B, \]
and put \(i_\#\defeq(c_N\resscale)^{1/\alpha}\). Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.36} \begin{gathered} c_N\resscale\sim\begin{cases}(1-\alpha)BN^{\alpha-1},&\alpha<1,\\ B/\log\gamma,&\alpha=1,\\ (B/K_\alpha)^\alpha,&\alpha>1,\end{cases} \\ \frac{i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak d}}{i_\#}\sim\begin{cases}\big(\pi/(2\sw2\log^2\gamma)\big)^{1/\alpha},&\alpha<1,\\ 9\pi/(8\sw2\log^2\gamma),&\alpha=1,\end{cases} \qquad \frac{i_{\mathrm c}}{i_\#}\to1\ \ (\alpha>1), \end{gathered} \end{equation}
and \(i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak v}/i_\#\sim(\sw2\log^2\gamma)^{-1/\alpha}\) for \(\alpha<1\) and \(\sim9/(4\sw2\log^2\gamma)\) for \(\alpha=1\).
Proof of Proposition SG.6.
At \(m=1\), (SG.42) gives \(V\defeq c_N\resscale N^{-\alpha}=V_N(1/\gamma)\), and Lemma SG.6(i) gives \(S_\alpha(V)\in[\gamma^{-1},\gamma^{-1}+N^{-1}]=\gamma^{-1}[1,1+B^{-1}]\). Hence \(S_\alpha(V)\sim1/\gamma\to0\), and Lemma SG.5(iii) gives
\[ c_N\resscale=N^\alpha V\sim N^\alpha\cdot\begin{cases}(1-\alpha)\gamma^{-1},\\ (\gamma\log\gamma)^{-1},\\ (K_\alpha\gamma)^{-\alpha},\end{cases} \]
which is the first equivalence of (SG.36). The ratios follow from (SG.32) and (SG.33) with \(d^2=c_0^2/(1-\alpha)\) for \(\alpha<1\) and \(d^2=\frac49c_0^2=8/(9\pi)\) for \(\alpha=1\):
\[ \frac{i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak d}^\alpha}{c_N\resscale}\sim\frac{BN^{\alpha-1}}{\sw2d^2\log^2\gamma\cdot(1-\alpha)BN^{\alpha-1}}=\frac{\pi}{2\sw2\log^2\gamma}\quad(\alpha<1),\]
\[ \frac{i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak d}}{i_\#}\sim\frac{B/(\sw2d^2\log^3\gamma)}{B/\log\gamma}=\frac{9\pi}{8\sw2\log^2\gamma}\quad(\alpha=1), \]
\(i_{\mathrm c}=B/K_\alpha\sim i_\#\) for \(\alpha>1\), and \(i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak v}=c_0^{2/\alpha}i_{\mathrm c,\mathfrak d}\) gives the last statement.
□
