Lemma SG.9 (The profile \(\Psi_\alpha\))
\(\Psi_\alpha\) is finite, positive and continuous on \((0,1]\), bounded below by a positive constant, and satisfies the first equivalences of (SG.27).
Proof.
The bounds of Lemma SG.5(iii) control the integrand \(\psi_x(p)\defeq\sqrt{(1-p)/(pV_\alpha)}\,V_\alpha/(V_\alpha+x^\alpha)\). Near \(p=1\), \(V_\alpha\asymp(1-p)^{-1}\) gives \(\psi_x\le C(1-p)\). Near \(p=0\), \(\psi_x\le x^{-\alpha}\sqrt{V_\alpha/p}\), which is bounded for \(\alpha\le1\) up to a logarithm and is \(O(p^{(\alpha-1)/2})\) for \(\alpha>1\). These bounds are locally uniform in \(x\), which gives finiteness and continuity. On \(p\in[1/4,1/2]\), \(\psi_x\ge c\) uniformly in \(x\le1\), which gives the lower bound.
The small-\(x\) equivalents come from small \(p\). For \(\alpha<1\), \(V_\alpha(p)\sim(1-\alpha)p\), so on a fixed small interval \((0,p_0)\)
\[ \psi_x(p)=\frac{1+o(1)}{\sqrt{1-\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{p+x^\alpha/(1-\alpha)},\qquad \int_0^{p_0}\frac{\dif p}{p+x^\alpha/(1-\alpha)}=\log\big(x^{-\alpha}\big)\,(1+o(1)), \]
the error \(o(1)\) being uniform as \(p_0\downarrow0\); the part \(p>p_0\) is bounded. For \(\alpha=1\), \(V_1(p)\sim p/\log(1/p)\) gives \(\psi_x(p)=(1+o(1))\sqrt{\log(1/p)}\,/\,(p+x\log(1/p))\). Put \(p_x\defeq x\log(1/x)\) and \(\omega\defeq\log(1/x)\). On \(p\ge\omega p_x\), \(x\log(1/p)/p\le1/\omega\to0\), so the integrand is \((1+o(1))\sqrt{\log(1/p)}/p\), with integral \(\frac23[\log(1/x)]^{3/2}(1+o(1))\) because \(\log\omega=o(\log(1/x))\). On \([p_x,\omega p_x]\) the integrand is at most \(\sqrt{\log(1/p)}/p\), with integral at most \(\sqrt{\log(1/p_x)}\,\log\omega=o([\log(1/x)]^{3/2})\). Below \(p_x\), the substitution \(p=p_xu\) bounds the contribution by \(C\sqrt{\log(1/x)}\int_0^1u^{-1/2}(1+\abs{\log u})^{1/2}\dif u\). For \(\alpha>1\), substitute \(p=K_\alpha xu\) on a fixed small interval. Since \(V_\alpha(K_\alpha xu)\sim x^\alpha u^\alpha\),
\[ \psi_x(K_\alpha xu)\,K_\alpha x\,\dif u=(1+o(1))\sqrt{K_\alpha}\,x^{-(\alpha-1)/2}\,\frac{u^{(\alpha-1)/2}}{1+u^\alpha}\,\dif u, \]
\[ \int_0^\infty\frac{u^{(\alpha-1)/2}}{1+u^\alpha}\,\dif u=\frac{\pi}{\alpha\cos(\pi/(2\alpha))}, \]
by [21, (5.12.3), (5.5.3)], and the tails are controlled by the bounds \(Cu^{(\alpha-1)/2}\) near \(0\) and \(Cu^{-(\alpha+1)/2}\) near \(\infty\), the latter integrable because \(\alpha>1\). Multiplying by \(c_0\) gives \(d_\alpha\sqrt{K_\alpha}x^{-(\alpha-1)/2}\).
□
