Lemma SC.6 (Exact mixed identity)
In the frozen-state model, for every \(s>0\), and for every \(z\in\mathbb S\) in place of \(s\) with \(R_0^{(j)}=R_z(M_0^{(j)})\), \(\mathsf X_0^{(j)}=\mathsf X_z(M_0^{(j)})\) and the coefficients at \(z\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.30} \mathcal H(s)=B\sum_{j\ge0}\EE\Big[\alpha_{1,j}\,R_0^{(j)}(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)-\alpha_{2,j}\,\mathsf X_0^{(j)}(\mathsf v_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\Big]. \end{equation}
Proof.
By the sample decomposition (SB.26),
\[ R(-s)\Mt=\sum_{j\ge0}\sum_{a=1}^B\big(\mathsf h_{ja}R(-s)\mathsf u_{ja}\big)\tensor\mathsf v_{ja}, \]
and (SC.17), applied to each sample with the deleted buffer \(M_0^{(ja)}\), rewrites each term as \((\alpha_{1,ja}R_0^{(ja)}\mathsf u_{ja}-\alpha_{2,ja}\mathsf X_0^{(ja)}\mathsf v_{ja})\tensor\mathsf v_{ja}\). Taking expectations and using the exchangeability of the samples of one age gives (SC.30). At \(z\in\mathbb S\) the same derivation applies to \(R_z(\Mt)\Mt\), with (SC.17) at \(z\).
□
