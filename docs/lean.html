The Lean formalization

The theorem proofs and their supporting results are formalized in Lean 4 (v4.34.1) with Mathlib and the accompanying foundations libraries. The audit checks that no declaration depends on sorry and that only Lean's standard axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound) occur.
