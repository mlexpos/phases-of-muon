Lemma SG.17 (Upper participation endpoint)
For every \(\eta\in(0,1)\) there is \(C_\eta\) such that, for all large \(N\) and all \(1\le i\le N\),
\[ \mathfrak v_i\big[[\eta\gamma,\gamma),[\eta\gamma,\gamma)\big]\le C_\eta\,\gamma\,(i/N)^\alpha . \]
Proof.
Write \(d_j\defeq\gamma-m_j\) and \(d\defeq\max(d_1,d_2)\). For \(m\ge\eta\gamma\), Lemma SG.6(iii), whose proof gives \(V_N(p)\asymp_\eta(1-p)^{-1}\) for \(p\ge\eta\), and (SG.42) give
\[ \Lambda_N(m)\asymp_\eta\frac{N^\alpha}{c_N}\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-m},\qquad \frac{\sqrt{\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)}}{1+\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)}\le\big(\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)\big)^{-1/2}\le C_\eta\Big(\frac iN\Big)^{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-m}{\gamma}} . \]
With \(\vartheta_N(m)\le C(1+(\gamma-m)^{-1})\) (Lemma SG.7) this gives
\[ W_i(m)\le C_\eta\Big(\frac iN\Big)^{\alpha/2}\frac{\gamma-m+1}{\gamma^{3/2}}\qquad(m\ge\eta\gamma). \]
The determinant is bounded below by powers of the deficits. If \(d\ge1\),
\[ \Delta^N_\ell\ge c_\eta\,d^2/\gamma^2 . \]
Indeed, if the smaller deficit is at least \(d/2\), the last bound of Lemma SG.14 applies; otherwise \(m_+-m_-\ge d/2\), so \(1-\mathsf T_N/m_+\ge1-m_-/m_+\ge d/(2\gamma)\), and the first factor is \(1-m_-/\gamma=d/\gamma\). If \(0<d\le1\), then
\[ \Delta^N_\ell\ge c\,d/\gamma^2 \]
uniformly in the lag. To see this, note first that the affine bound of the proof of Lemma SG.14, evaluated at \(\mathsf M_\ell\) instead of \(m_-\), reads \(\Delta^N_\ell\ge(1-m_-/\gamma)(1-\mathsf T_N/m_+)+(m_--\mathsf M_\ell)\mathsf c\). Second, \(\mathsf T_N=\gamma-d_1-d_2+\mathsf R\) with \(\mathsf R\defeq B^{-1}\sum_j(1-f_j(m_1))(1-f_j(m_2))\), and \(1-f_j(m_k)\le Cd_k/\gamma\) gives \(0\le\mathsf R\le Cd_1d_2/\gamma\); inserting this in \(\mathsf c\) gives \(\mathsf c\ge(d_1+d_2)/(2\gamma^2)\) for large \(\gamma\). Third, \(m_--\mathsf M_\ell\ge c_\bmom\). For this, the ratio \(Q_{m_1}/Q_{m_2}\) lies in a fixed compact subset of \((0,\infty)\), because \(\frac{\dif}{\dif m}\log Q_m=1/E(m)\) is bounded on \(m\ge1\). If \(m_-=m_2\) and \(\ell\ge1\), the fresh age \(0\) of the later buffer contributes \(\EE\,q_2\emaw0^2g^2(1+q_2\emaw0^2g^2)^{-1}\ge1/2\) to \(m_2-\mathsf M_\ell\), since \(q_2\to\infty\). Otherwise choose a shared age \(a\) with \(q_1\emaw a^2\in[1,\bmom^{-2}]\); on \(1\le\abs g\le2\) both \(X\) and \(Y\) are bounded above and \(X\) (or \(Y\)) below, so the term \(X(1+X)^{-1}(1+Y)^{-1}\) (or the one with \(Y\)) contributes at least a constant.
The lag sum is bounded before integration. By Lemma SG.15(i),(iii), \(\sum_\ell\mathsf D_\ell\le C_\bmom\). Outside the unit square \(d\le1\), the integrand of the lag sum is therefore at most
\[ C_\eta\Big(\frac iN\Big)^\alpha\frac{(d_1+1)(d_2+1)}{\gamma\,d^2}, \]
whose integral over \(\{0<d_j<(1-\eta)\gamma,\ d\ge1\}\) is \(O(\gamma(i/N)^\alpha)\): on \(d_1\le d_2\), integrating \(d_1\) first leaves \(\gamma^{-1}\int_1^{\gamma}O(t+1)\,\dif t=O(\gamma)\). On the unit square the integrand is at most \(C(i/N)^\alpha/(\gamma\max(d_1,d_2))\), whose integral is \(O((i/N)^\alpha/\gamma)\).
