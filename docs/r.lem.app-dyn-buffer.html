Lemma SE.15 (Stopped buffer moments)
Let \(\mathcal K=\mathcal K(r_-,r_+,E)\), and let \(\tau\) be a time such that whether \(t<\tau\) is decided by the past \(\cF_t\), and such that \(\Dt\in\mathcal K\) for \(t<\tau\). Then for every \(t\ge0\),
\[ \EE\big[\mathbf 1_{t\le\tau}\norm{M^\circ_{t-1}}_\F^2\big]\le2c_MB+4C_Mr_+B,\qquad \EE\big[\mathbf 1_{t\le\tau}\norm{M^\circ_{t-1}}^2\big]\le2\bmom^{2t}c_MB+C\log^2(2B). \]
Proof.
The stopped-input buffer \(\widehat M_t\defeq\bmom\widehat M_{t-1}+(1-\bmom)\mathbf 1_{t<\tau}G^\circ_{t+1}[\Dt]\), \(\widehat M_{-1}\defeq M^\circ_{-1}\), coincides with \(M^\circ_t\) for \(t<\tau\), so \(\mathbf 1_{t\le\tau}M^\circ_{t-1}=\mathbf 1_{t\le\tau}\widehat M_{t-1}\). Since \(\mathbf 1_{t<\tau}\) and \(\Dt\) are determined by the past, Lemma SE.3(i) applied conditionally gives \(\EE\norm{\mathbf 1_{t<\tau}G^\circ_{t+1}[\Dt]}_\F^2\le C_MB\cdot2r_+\). Minkowski’s inequality for the unrolled recursion gives
\[ \big(\EE\norm{\widehat M_{t-1}}_\F^2\big)^{1/2}\le\bmom^{t}\sqrt{c_MB}+\sum_{j}\emaw j\sqrt{2C_Mr_+B}, \]
and squaring with \((x+y)^2\le2x^2+2y^2\) proves the first bound. The second is the same computation with Lemma SE.3(ii) and \(\EE\norm{M^\circ_{-1}}^2\le\EE\norm{M^\circ_{-1}}_\F^2\).
□
