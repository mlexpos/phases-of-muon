Lemma SG.22 (Localization of the lag kernel)
  1. (i)
    For \(\ell\ge0\), with \(t\defeq\log(\bmom^{2\ell}q_2/q_1)\),
    \[ \mathsf D_\ell(m_1,m_2)=\frac{r_\bmom(q_1e^t)-r_\bmom(q_1)}{2\sinh(t/2)},\]
    \[ \mathsf D_\ell\le C\min(m_1,m_2,1)^{1/2}\big(1+\abs{m_1-m_2+\ell}\big)e^{-\abs{\log\bmom}\abs{m_1-m_2+\ell}} . \]
  2. (ii)
    For every \(\tau_0>0\) there is \(c>0\) such that \(\Delta^N_\ell\ge c\) for all \(\ell\ge\tau_0\gamma\), all \(m_1,m_2\in(0,\gamma)\) and all large \(\gamma\).
  3. (iii)
    Let \(n\to\infty\) with \(\gamma/n\to\rho_0\in(0,\infty]\), and let \(\ell\) be integers with \(\ell/n\to\tau>0\). For every continuous \(\varphi\) with compact support in \((0,\rho_0)^2\),
    \[ \int\!\!\int\varphi\Big(\frac{m_1}n,\frac{m_2}n\Big)\frac{\mathsf D_\ell(m_1,m_2)}{\pi^2n\,E(m_1)E(m_2)}\,\dif m_1\,\dif m_2\longrightarrow\int\varphi(r,r+\tau)\,\dif r . \]
Proof.
For (i), the divided-difference form (SG.15) gives, with \(p\defeq\bmom^{2\ell}q_2=q_1e^t\),
\[ \mathsf D_\ell=\sqrt{q_1q_2}\,\bmom^\ell\frac{r_\bmom(q_1)-r_\bmom(p)}{q_1-p}=\sqrt{q_1p}\,\frac{r_\bmom(p)-r_\bmom(q_1)}{p-q_1} =\frac{r_\bmom(q_1e^t)-r_\bmom(q_1)}{e^{t/2}-e^{-t/2}} . \]
The function \(qr_\bmom'(q)\) is bounded on \((0,\infty)\) (Lemma SG.1), so the numerator is at most \(C\abs t\). For \(m_1,m_2\ge1\), Lemma SG.1(iii) gives \(t=2\abs{\log\bmom}(m_2-m_1-\ell)+O(1)\), which proves the bound. If \(m_1<1\le m_2\), dropping the first denominator gives \(\mathsf D_\ell\le\sqrt{q_1}\,\sqrt p\,\Fw(p)\), and \(\sqrt{q_1}\le C\sqrt{m_1}\), \(\sqrt p\,\Fw(p)\le C\min\{\sqrt p,(1+\log_+p)/\sqrt p\}\) with \(\log p=2\abs{\log\bmom}(m_2-\ell)+O(1)\), where \(\abs{m_2-\ell}\) differs from \(\abs{m_1-m_2+\ell}\) by less than one. The case \(m_2<1\le m_1\) exchanges the roles, and if both \(m_j<1\) then \(\mathsf D_\ell\le\bmom^\ell\sw2\sqrt{q_1q_2}\le C\bmom^\ell\sqrt{m_1m_2}\).
For (ii), let \(\ell\ge\tau_0\gamma\). Since \(\mathsf M_\ell\le\sum_a\EE\,Y(1+Y)^{-1}=r_\bmom(\bmom^{2\ell}q_2)\), two cases arise. If \(m_2\ge\ell/2\), Lemma SG.1(iii) gives \(\mathsf M_\ell\le(m_2-\ell)_++C\), so \(\mathsf M_\ell/m_2\le1-c\tau_0\). If \(m_2<\ell/2\), then \(\mathsf M_\ell\le\sw2\bmom^{2\ell}Q_{m_2}\le Cm_2e^{-\abs{\log\bmom}\ell}\). In both cases \(1-\mathsf T_N\mathsf M_\ell/(m_1m_2)\ge1-\mathsf M_\ell/m_2\ge c\) and \(1-\mathsf M_\ell/\gamma\ge c\), using \(\mathsf T_N\le m_1\). The subtracted term is at most \(\gamma^{-1}\min(m_-,C^2/m_+)\le C/\gamma\), because \(\mathsf D_\ell\le C\), \(\mathsf D_\ell^2\le m_1m_2\) and \(\mathsf T_N\le m_-\).
For (iii), the large-argument behaviour of \(r_\bmom\) is periodic in \(\log q\). Put \(\mathsf f(x)=x\Jfun(x)\) and
\[ \mathcal H(u)\defeq\sum_{k\ge0}\mathsf f\big((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{2k}e^u\big)+\sum_{k<0}\Big(\mathsf f\big((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{2k}e^u\big)-1\Big), \]
which converges by \(\mathsf f(x)\le x\) and \(1-\mathsf f(x)\le\sqrt{\pi/2}\,x^{-1/2}\). Shifting \(u\) by \(2\abs{\log\bmom}\) shifts \(k\) by one, so \(\mathcal H(u+2\abs{\log\bmom})=\mathcal H(u)+1\), and \(\abs{r_\bmom(e^u)-\mathcal H(u)}\le Ce^{-u/2}\). In the coordinates \(a\defeq\log Q_{m_1}\) and \(t\), one has \(\dif m_1/E(m_1)=\dif a\) and \(\dif m_2/E(m_2)=\dif t\) at fixed \(m_1\), so the measure of the statement is \(\pi^{-2}n^{-1}\mathsf D_\ell\,\dif a\,\dif t\). By (i) the region \(\abs{m_2-m_1-\ell}>A\) carries mass at most \(Ce^{-cA}\) per unit of \(m_1\), and is removed first. On the rest, \(\mathcal H\) replaces \(r_\bmom\) with an error that vanishes on the support. For each \(t\),
\[ \int_a^{a+2\abs{\log\bmom}}\big(\mathcal H(a'+t)-\mathcal H(a')\big)\,\dif a'=t, \]
because the derivative in \(t\) of the left side is \(\mathcal H(a+2\abs{\log\bmom}+t)-\mathcal H(a+t)=1\). Hence one period of \(a\), which is one unit of \(m_1\), carries the mass
\[ \frac1{\pi^2n}\int_{\R}\frac{t}{2\sinh(t/2)}\,\dif t=\frac1n, \]
by \(1/\sinh(t/2)=2\sum_{k\ge0}e^{-(k+1/2)t}\) on \(t>0\) and \(\sum_{k\ge0}(k+\frac12)^{-2}=\pi^2/2\). On the support, \(m_2=m_1+\ell+O(A)\), so \(m_2/n=m_1/n+\tau+o(1)\). A continuous compactly supported \(\varphi\) varies by \(o(1)\) over each of the \(O(n)\) periods in its support, which gives the limit; letting then \(A\to\infty\) concludes.
