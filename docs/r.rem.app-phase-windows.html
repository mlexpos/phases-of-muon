Remark SI.3 (The windows cannot be dropped)
Corollary SI.2 shows that at fixed \(N\) the exponent of \(\epsilon\) is \(-\frac12\) in every phase, so the exponents of Theorem SI.7 require \(N\to\infty\) jointly with \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). In the family of Proposition SI.7, \(\texp=1\) and \(\epsilon N\to\infty\). The order \(\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}\epsilon^{-1}\), which Theorem SI.10 gives for SignSGD at \(\texp=1\) inside its window, fails on this family by the factor \(N^{-(\omega-\alpha)/2}\); the SignSVD time keeps its order but not the constant of (SI.37). The family violates the height condition (SI.33): by Proposition SI.5, \(k_{N,\text{s-SVD}}C_{\text{s-SVD}}\log(C_{\text{s-SVD}}/\sqrt{\epsilon_N})/\sqrt{\epsilon_N}\) grows like a constant times \(N^{(\omega-\alpha)/2}\log N\). The cause is the finite cutoff: the hitting clock is governed by the finite forcing integral \(J_N\), while the infinite-forcing accuracy scale lies beyond the cutoff. Within the windows, Proposition SI.6 gives the following map of the ratio \(t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}/t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}\): it tends to \(0\) for \(\alpha<1\) in every phase; it tends to \(\infty\) for \(\alpha>2\) and \(\texp\le\alpha+1\), and to a positive constant for \(\alpha>2\) and \(\texp>\alpha+1\); for \(1<\alpha\le2\) it tends to \(0\) on and above the curve \(\texp=\texp_c(\alpha)\) (for \(\alpha<2\)) and depends on the joint family below it, as it does in the range \(\texp<1\) for \(1\le\alpha\le2\).
