Proposition SG.5 (The profiles)
  1. (i)
    \(\Psi_\alpha\) and \(\Gamma_\alpha\) are finite, positive and continuous on \((0,1]\), \(\Psi_\alpha\) is bounded below by a positive constant there, and as \(x\downarrow0\)
    \begin{equation}\tag{SG.27} \begin{gathered} \Psi_\alpha(x)\sim\begin{cases}\dfrac{c_0}{\sqrt{1-\alpha}}\log(x^{-\alpha}),&\alpha<1,\\[6pt] \dfrac23c_0\big[\log(1/x)\big]^{3/2},&\alpha=1,\\[6pt] d_\alpha\sqrt{K_\alpha}\,x^{-(\alpha-1)/2},&\alpha>1,\end{cases} \\ \Gamma_\alpha(x)\sim\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^\alpha\big[\log(x^{-\alpha})\big]^2}{1-\alpha},&\alpha<1,\\[6pt] \dfrac49x\big[\log(1/x)\big]^3,&\alpha=1,\\[6pt] \dfrac{4K_\alpha^2}{\alpha-1}\,x,&\alpha>1.\end{cases} \end{gathered} \end{equation}
  2. (ii)
    For \(\alpha>1\), \(\Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}\) and \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}\) are finite, positive and continuous on \((0,\infty)\), and
    \begin{equation}\tag{SG.28} \begin{gathered} \Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}(y)\sim\sqrt{y/\sw2}\ (y\to\infty),\quad \Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}(y)\sim d_\alpha\,y^{\frac{\alpha-1}{2\alpha}}\ (y\downarrow0),\\ G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\to\frac1{\sw2}\ (X\downarrow0),\quad G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\sim\frac{4K_\alpha}{\alpha-1}X\ (X\to\infty). \end{gathered} \end{equation}
Part (i) is the small-\(x\) table to the right of Table 1, with its constants: as \(x\downarrow0\) the unresolved drift profile grows only logarithmically in \(1/x\) for \(\alpha\le1\) and like \(x^{-(\alpha-1)/2}\) for \(\alpha>1\), while the unresolved volatility per unit of \(\gamma\) vanishes like \(x^\alpha\) times logarithms for \(\alpha\le1\) and linearly in \(x\) for \(\alpha>1\). Part (ii) gives the two ends of the crossover profiles for \(\alpha>1\); they are the resolved and unresolved laws of Theorem SG.2 and Theorem SG.3, which the profiles join. All statements concern explicit functions and involve no dimension.
Proof sketch.
The drift profile is dominated, as \(x\downarrow0\), by small participation fractions, where the inverse \(V_\alpha\) has the power laws of Section G.5. For \(\alpha<1\), \(V_\alpha(p)\sim(1-\alpha)p\) turns the integrand, without the factor \(c_0\), into \((1-\alpha)^{-1/2}(p+x^\alpha/(1-\alpha))^{-1}\) on a fixed small interval, whose integral is \(\log(x^{-\alpha})(1+o(1))\); at \(\alpha=1\) the extra logarithm of \(V_1(p)\sim p/\log(1/p)\) gives the power \(3/2\). For \(\alpha>1\) the substitution \(p=K_\alpha xu\) isolates a convergent integral (Lemma SG.9),
\[ \Psi_\alpha(x)\sim c_0\sqrt{K_\alpha}\,x^{-(\alpha-1)/2}\int_0^\infty\frac{u^{(\alpha-1)/2}}{1+u^\alpha}\,\dif u =d_\alpha\sqrt{K_\alpha}\,x^{-(\alpha-1)/2}. \]
The volatility profile is a positive double integral, and for \(\alpha\le1\) it is asymptotically the square of a single integral. With \(A_x(p)=\sqrt{f_x(p)(1-f_x(p))(1-p)/p}\), a partial-fraction identity in the variable of the spectrum gives exactly (Lemma SG.21)
\[ \Gamma_\alpha(x)=2\int_{0<p<q<1}\frac{A_x(p)A_x(q)}{\Delta_{\mathrm c}(p,q)}\,\dif p\,\dif q,\qquad \Big(\int_0^1A_x\Big)^2=\frac{x^\alpha\Psi_\alpha(x)^2}{c_0^2}, \]
where \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\le1\) is the continuum determinant of the lag kernel. Without the determinant the double integral is the square on the right. For \(\alpha\le1\) the integral of \(A_x\) is spread over a range of scales of \(p\) whose logarithmic length tends to infinity, so most of the double integral comes from pairs with \(q>Rp\) and \(p,q<\eta\), where \(1-\Delta_{\mathrm c}\le\eta+1/R\); the diagonal strip \(q\le Rp\) and the upper end \(p\to1\) are of lower order. This gives \(\Gamma_\alpha(x)\sim x^\alpha\Psi_\alpha(x)^2/c_0^2\) and the volatility equivalents of (i). For \(\alpha>1\) the mass of \(A_x\) sits at \(p\asymp x\), the determinant matters, and the substitution \(p=K_\alpha xr\) turns \(\Gamma_\alpha(x)/(K_\alpha x)\) into a double integral over the lag and the participation, with value \(4K_\alpha/(\alpha-1)\) (Lemma SG.23).
The ends of the crossover profiles in (ii) come from the two mechanisms of Section G.5. As \(y\to\infty\) only small participations count in \(\Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}(y)\) and the head Lorentzian gives \(\sqrt{y/\sw2}\); as \(y\downarrow0\) the substitution \(m=y^{-1/\alpha}w\) moves it to large participation, where averaging gives the integral above (Lemma SG.13). For \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\) the rescaling \(m=Xr\) sends every lag term to \(\bmom^{\abs\ell}\) as \(X\downarrow0\), with sum \(1/\sw2\), and as \(X\to\infty\) the concentration of the lag kernel on \(m_2=m_1+\ell\) (Lemma SG.22) gives the double integral \(4K_\alpha/(\alpha-1)\) (Lemma SG.23). Finiteness, positivity and continuity follow from majorants that are locally uniform in the argument.
