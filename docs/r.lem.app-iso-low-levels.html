Lemma SF.17 (Low levels)
For \(\eta>0\) let \((y,z)=\Pi(Q_\eta;a,c)\) as in Lemma SF.16, and put \(\sigma\defeq\log\big((1-\bmom)^2Q_\eta\big)\). Then
\[ \mathsf n^-_\bmom(z)\le\frac{(\tau-\sigma)_+}{2\log(1/\bmom)}, \]
and \(\mathsf L_k\defeq\int_0^\infty\EE_\star\big[\mathsf n^-_\bmom(z)^k\big]\,\dif\eta\) satisfies \(\mathsf L_k\le\bar{\mathsf L}_k\) for \(k=1,2\), where
\[ \bar{\mathsf L}_1\defeq\frac1{2\log(1/\bmom)}\Big(\frac{\mathsf M_1+1}{1-\bmom^2}+\frac{\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\mathsf M_2}2\Big),\qquad \bar{\mathsf L}_2\defeq\frac1{4\log^2(1/\bmom)}\Big(\frac{\mathsf M_2+2\mathsf M_1+2}{1-\bmom^2}+\frac{\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\mathsf M_3}3\Big). \]
Proof.
The function \(\mathsf n^-_\bmom\) is nonincreasing and \(z\ge Q_\eta e^{-\tau}\) (Lemma SF.16(iii)), which gives the first claim.
For the moments, change variables from \(\eta\) to \(\sigma\). Since \(\eta=r(Q_\eta)\),
\[ \dif\eta=r'(Q_\eta)\,\dif Q_\eta=\mathsf e_\bmom(Q_\eta)\,\dif\sigma . \]
Two bounds on the slope are used. For \(\sigma<0\), \(\mathsf e_\bmom(s)\le\sw2\,s=e^\sigma/(1-\bmom^2)\), because \(r'\le\sw2\) (Lemma SF.4(i),(ii)) and \(\sw2/(1-\bmom)^2=1/(1-\bmom^2)\). For \(\sigma\ge0\), \(\mathsf e_\bmom\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) (Lemma SF.13). Hence, for \(\tau\ge0\) and \(k=1,2\),
\[ \int_\R(\tau-\sigma)_+^k\,\mathsf e_\bmom\,\dif\sigma\le\frac1{1-\bmom^2}\int_0^\infty(\tau+v)^ke^{-v}\,\dif v+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\,\frac{\tau^{k+1}}{k+1}, \]
with \(\int_0^\infty(\tau+v)e^{-v}\dif v=\tau+1\) and \(\int_0^\infty(\tau+v)^2e^{-v}\dif v=\tau^2+2\tau+2\). Dividing by \((2\log(1/\bmom))^k\) and integrating against \(\nu_\star\) with Lemma SF.16(ii) gives the bounds.
□
