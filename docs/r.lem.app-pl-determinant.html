Lemma SG.14 (Determinant)
For every lag and all \(m_1,m_2\in(0,\gamma)\),
\[ 0\le\mathsf M_\ell\le m_-,\qquad \mathsf D_\ell^2\le(m_1-\mathsf M_\ell)(m_2-\mathsf M_\ell),\]
\[ \Delta^N_\ell\ge\Big(1-\frac{m_-}\gamma\Big)\Big(1-\frac{\mathsf T_N}{m_+}\Big)\ge c_\alpha\Big(1-\frac{m_1}\gamma\Big)\Big(1-\frac{m_2}\gamma\Big), \]
and \(0\le1-\Delta^N_\ell\le m_-/m_++2m_-/\gamma\). If \(m_1,m_2\le\delta\le1\), then \(1-\Delta^N_\ell\le C_\bmom\delta+2\delta/\gamma\).
Proof.
The scalars are integrals over the shared ages. Take \(\ell\ge0\); negative lags exchange the arguments. For an age \(a\) and the shared scalar \(g\) put \(X\defeq q_1\emaw a^2g^2\) and \(Y\defeq\bmom^{2\ell}q_2\emaw a^2g^2\). Then
\[ \mathsf M_\ell=\sum_a\EE\frac{XY}{(1+X)(1+Y)},\qquad \mathsf D_\ell=\sum_a\EE\frac{\sqrt{XY}}{(1+X)(1+Y)},\]
\[ m_1-\mathsf M_\ell=\sum_a\EE\frac{X}{(1+X)(1+Y)}, \]
and \(m_2-\mathsf M_\ell\ge\sum_a\EE\,Y(1+X)^{-1}(1+Y)^{-1}\), since \(m_2=r_\bmom(q_2)\) also contains the \(\ell\) fresh ages of the later buffer, whose weights \(\emaw0,\dots,\emaw{\ell-1}\) complete \(\{\bmom^\ell\emaw a\}=\{\emaw{a+\ell}\}\). This gives the first bound, and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives the second.
The determinant is bounded below by an affine function of \(\mathsf M_\ell\). Substituting the bound on \(\mathsf D_\ell^2\),
\[ \Delta^N_\ell\ge1-\frac{\mathsf T_N}{\gamma}-\mathsf M_\ell\,\mathsf c,\qquad \mathsf c\defeq\frac{m_1m_2+\mathsf T_N(\gamma-m_1-m_2)}{\gamma m_1m_2}>0, \]
where positivity uses \(\mathsf T_N\le m_-\) when \(\gamma<m_1+m_2\), because \(f_j(m_-)\le f_j(m_+)\) gives \(\mathsf T_N\le B^{-1}\sum_jf_j(m_-)=m_-\). Evaluating at \(\mathsf M_\ell=m_-\) gives \((1-m_-/\gamma)(1-\mathsf T_N/m_+)\). For the last factor,
\[ \mathsf T_N\le\frac1B\sum_jf_j(m_+)^2=m_+-\Lambda m_N'(\Lambda)\big|_{\Lambda=\Lambda_N(m_+)}\le m_+-c_\alpha m_+\Big(1-\frac{m_+}\gamma\Big) \]
by Lemma SG.7.
The upper bounds follow from the definition. Put \(\mathsf a\defeq\mathsf T_N\mathsf M_\ell/(m_1m_2)\in[0,m_-/m_+]\) and \(\mathsf g\defeq\mathsf M_\ell/\gamma\in[0,m_-/\gamma]\); then
\[ 1-\Delta^N_\ell=\mathsf a+\mathsf g-\mathsf a\mathsf g+\frac{\mathsf T_N\mathsf D_\ell^2}{\gamma m_1m_2},\qquad 0\le\frac{\mathsf T_N\mathsf D_\ell^2}{\gamma m_1m_2}\le\frac{\mathsf T_N}\gamma\le\frac{m_-}\gamma . \]
For \(m_j\le\delta\le1\), Lemma SG.1(i) gives \(q_j\le Cm_j\) and \(\mathsf M_\ell\le3\sw4q_1q_2\le Cm_1m_2\), so \(\mathsf a\le C\mathsf T_N\le C\delta\).
□
