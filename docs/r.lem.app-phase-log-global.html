Lemma SI.18 (SignSVD kernel, \(\alpha=2\), \(\texp>1\))
Let \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\), so \(q=1\) and \(r\defeq r_{\text{s-SVD}}>1\). Put \(C\defeq C_{p,1}\), \(K\defeq2E_{2,1}\), \(L_N\defeq H_N(1)\) and
\[ \mathsf A_0\defeq\frac{CK}{r-1}\Big(\frac{2^r}e+2\Big),\qquad L_0\defeq\max\Big\{1,\frac{4K}{2-\sqrt2}\Big\}, \]
\[ h(u)\defeq C(1+u)^{-r},\qquad \mathsf w(u)\defeq\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}\,\epsilon^{1/4}L_N^{-1/2}(1+u)^{-1/2} . \]
Let \(N\ge e^{L_0}\), \(B\ge N\) and \(\epsilon>0\). Then \(f_{N,1}\le Y_{\text{s-SVD}}\le(h+\mathsf w+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\) on \([0,\infty)\). If moreover \(T\ge4\) and \(0<\epsilon<\min\{1,C^2,1/(8T)\}\), the first hit satisfies \(0<\hat u\le H\defeq\max\{(2C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r},4\mathsf A_0/(L_N\sqrt\epsilon)\}-1\), and
\begin{multline*} 0\le\mathcal E\le\epsilon^{1/8}\sqrt{C+\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}+1}+\frac C{r-1}\big(1+\epsilon^{-1/8}\big)^{1-r}\\ +2\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}(2C)^{1/(2r)}L_N^{-1/2}\epsilon^{(r-1)/(4r)}+(2C)^{1/r}\epsilon^{(r-1)/(2r)}+\frac{8\mathsf A_0}{L_N} . \end{multline*}
In particular \(\mathcal E\to0\) along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).
Proof.
By Lemma SI.7(i), \(g(v)\le K/(L_N(1+v))\). The convolution with \(h\) is bounded by splitting at \(u/2\). On \([0,u/2]\), \(h(u-v)\le C2^r(1+u)^{-r}\), so this part is at most
\[ \frac{CK2^r}{L_N}(1+u)^{-r}\log(1+u)\le\frac{CK2^r}{e(r-1)L_N(1+u)} , \]
using \(\sup_{x\ge1}x^{-(r-1)}\log x=1/(e(r-1))\). On \([u/2,u]\), \(g(v)\le2K/(L_N(1+u))\) and \(\int h\le C/(r-1)\), so this part is at most \(2CK/((r-1)L_N(1+u))\). Hence
\[ \sqrt\epsilon\,(g*h)(u)\le\frac{\sqrt\epsilon\,\mathsf A_0}{L_N(1+u)}=\mathsf w(u)^2 . \]
The convolution with \(\mathsf w\) is bounded in the same way. On \([0,u/2]\), \(\mathsf w(u-v)\le\sqrt2\,\mathsf w(u)\) and \(\int g=1\). On \([u/2,u]\), \(g(v)\le2K/(L_N(1+u))\) and \(\int_0^{u/2}(1+z)^{-1/2}\dif z\le2\sqrt{1+u}\). Hence
\[ (g*\mathsf w)(u)\le\Big(\sqrt2+\frac{4K}{L_N}\Big)\mathsf w(u)\le2\,\mathsf w(u) , \]
because \(L_N\ge\log(N+1)\ge L_0\). Put \(U\defeq(h+\mathsf w+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\ge\epsilon\). Using \(\int g=1\),
\[ f_{N,1}+\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt U\le h^2+\mathsf w^2+2\sqrt\epsilon\,\mathsf w+\epsilon\le U , \]
and Lemma SI.3 with \(m=\epsilon\) gives \(Y\le U\) on every compact interval.
At \(H\), \(h(H)\le\sqrt\epsilon/2\) and \(\mathsf w(H)^2=\sqrt\epsilon\mathsf A_0/(L_N(1+H))\le\epsilon/4\), so \(Y(H)\le4\epsilon\le T\epsilon\). Since \(Y(0)\ge\frac12>T\epsilon\), the first hit is attained in \((0,H]\) as in Lemma SI.9(ii).
For \(\mathcal E\), the kernel has integral \(1\) and \(h+\mathsf w+\sqrt\epsilon\) is decreasing, so
\[ 0\le Y-f_{N,1}\le\sqrt\epsilon\,\big(C+\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}\epsilon^{1/4}L_N^{-1/2}+\sqrt\epsilon\big)\le\sqrt\epsilon\,\big(C+\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}+1\big) . \]
Let \(R_0\defeq\epsilon^{-1/8}\). On \([0,\min(\hat u,R_0)]\) bound \(\sqrt Y-\sqrt f\) by \(\sqrt{Y-f}\), and on \([R_0,\hat u]\) by \(h+\mathsf w+\sqrt\epsilon\). This gives
\[ \mathcal E\le R_0\,\epsilon^{1/4}\sqrt{C+\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}+1}+\frac C{r-1}(1+R_0)^{1-r}+\int_0^H\mathsf w\,\dif u+\sqrt\epsilon\,H . \]
Integrating \((1+u)^{-1/2}\) and using \(\sqrt{\max(x,y)}\le\sqrt x+\sqrt y\),
\[ \int_0^H\mathsf w\,\dif u\le2\sqrt{\mathsf A_0}(2C)^{1/(2r)}L_N^{-1/2}\epsilon^{(r-1)/(4r)}+\frac{4\mathsf A_0}{L_N},\qquad \sqrt\epsilon\,H\le(2C)^{1/r}\epsilon^{(r-1)/(2r)}+\frac{4\mathsf A_0}{L_N} . \]
This proves the bound. Each term tends to zero, since \(r>1\) and \(L_N\ge\log(N+1)\to\infty\).
□
