Proposition SB.2 (Normalization checks)
Let \((\nu,\tilde\nu)\in\mathcal K\) and \(s>0\).
  1. (i)
    (One weight.) Let \(w=(\varpi,0,0,\dots)\) with \(\varpi>0\), and let \((\sigma_1,\tilde\sigma_1,\tilde s_{1,1},s_{1,1})(-s')\) denote the solution for the unit weight \((1,0,0,\dots)\) at the spectral parameter \(s'=s/\varpi^2\). Then the solution for \(w\) at \(s\) is
    \[ \begin{gathered} \sigma=\varpi^{-2}\sigma_1(-s'),\qquad\tilde\sigma=\varpi^{-2}\tilde\sigma_1(-s'),\qquad q=\varpi^{-2}q_1(-s'),\\ \tilde s_1=\varpi^2\tilde s_{1,1}(-s'),\qquad s_1=\varpi^2s_{1,1}(-s'), \end{gathered} \]
    and \(\Rbar_w(-s)=\varpi^{-2}\Rbar_1(-s')\).
  2. (ii)
    (\(K\) equal weights.) Let \(w_j=1/K\) for \(0\le j<K\) and \(w_j=0\) otherwise. Then \(F_w(q)=K^{-1}\Jfun(q/K^2)\), and the solution for \(w\) at \((\nu,\tilde\nu)\) is related to the solution \((\sigma',\tilde\sigma',\tilde s_1',s_1')\) for the unit weight at the measures \((\nu/K,\tilde\nu/K)\) by
    \[ \sigma=K\sigma',\qquad \tilde\sigma=K\tilde\sigma',\qquad q=K^2q',\qquad \tilde s_1=\tilde s_1',\qquad s_1=s_1'. \]
Proof.
In both parts the displayed tuple is checked against the four equations of (SB.2) and then identified with the solution by the uniqueness in Theorem SB.1(i).
For (i), the weight function is \(F_w(q)=\varpi^2\Jfun(\varpi^2q)\). The first trace equation holds at \(s\) because, for every \(x\ge0\),
\[ \frac{x}{s+\varpi^2\tilde s_{1,1}x}=\varpi^{-2}\,\frac{x}{s'+\tilde s_{1,1}x}, \]
and the second likewise. The equation for \(q\) holds because
\[ s\sigma\tilde\sigma=\varpi^2s'\cdot\varpi^{-4}\sigma_1\tilde\sigma_1=\varpi^{-2}q_1 . \]
The equation for \(\tilde s_1\) holds because
\[ s\tilde\sigma F_w(q)=\varpi^2s'\cdot\varpi^{-2}\tilde\sigma_1\cdot\varpi^2\Jfun(\varpi^2\varpi^{-2}q_1)=\varpi^2\,s'\tilde\sigma_1\Jfun(q_1)=\varpi^2\tilde s_{1,1}, \]
and the equation for \(s_1\) symmetrically. The resolvent relation follows from
\[ \big(s\Id+\varpi^2\tilde s_{1,1}\Sigmaout\big)^{-1}=\varpi^{-2}\big(s'\Id+\tilde s_{1,1}\Sigmaout\big)^{-1}. \]
For (ii), the weight function is
\[ F_w(q)=\sum_{j<K}K^{-2}\Jfun(q/K^2)=K^{-1}\Jfun(q/K^2). \]
The first trace equation holds because
\[ \int\frac{x}{s+\tilde s_1'x}\,\nu(dx)=K\int\frac{x}{s+\tilde s_1'x}\,\frac{\nu(dx)}{K}=K\sigma', \]
and the second likewise. The equation for \(q\) holds because
\[ s\sigma\tilde\sigma=K^2s\sigma'\tilde\sigma'=K^2q', \]
and the equation for \(\tilde s_1\) because
\[ s\tilde\sigma F_w(q)=s\,K\tilde\sigma'\,K^{-1}\Jfun(q')=s\tilde\sigma'\Jfun(q')=\tilde s_1' , \]
with the equation for \(s_1\) symmetric.
□
