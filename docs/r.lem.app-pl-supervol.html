Lemma SG.23 (Supercritical volatility)
The function \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}\) is finite, positive and continuous on \((0,\infty)\) and satisfies the two endpoint laws of (SG.28). Under Assumption S4, for every row sequence \(i\to\infty\): if \(X\to X_0\in(0,\infty)\) then \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\to G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X_0)\); if \(X\to0\) then \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\to1/\sw2\); if \(X\to\infty\) and \(i/N\to0\) then \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\sim\frac{4K_\alpha}{\alpha-1}X\). Moreover \(\Gamma_\alpha(x)\sim\frac{4K_\alpha^2}{\alpha-1}x\) as \(x\downarrow0\).
Proof.
The profile is dominated by a product. By Lemma SG.15(i),(iii), \(\sum_\ell\mathsf D_\ell(u,v)\le C\,\mathsf w(u)\mathsf w(v)\) with \(\mathsf w(u)\defeq\min(\sqrt u,1)\). Since \(t_\alpha(u,v)\le(1-\alpha^{-1})\max(u,v)\), which for \(u\ge v\) is \(\alpha r-r^\alpha\le\alpha-1\) at \(r=v/u\), and \(\mathsf M_\ell\le\min(u,v)\), one has \(\Delta^\infty_\ell\ge1/\alpha\). Hence (SG.26) is at most a constant times \(I(X)^2\), with
\[ I(X)\defeq\int_0^\infty\frac{X^{\alpha/2}h^{(\alpha-1)/2}}{X^\alpha+h^\alpha}\Big(1+\frac1h\Big)\mathsf w(h)\,\dif h<\infty, \]
finite at zero by \(\mathsf w(h)=\sqrt h\) and at infinity because \(\alpha>1\). This gives finiteness, continuity on compact sets of \(X\), and positivity from the zero-lag term.
The finite-dimensional integral is dominated in the same way. On \(m_1,m_2\le\gamma/2\), Lemma SG.14 gives \(\Delta^N_\ell\ge c_\alpha\), and Lemma SG.6(v) gives \(\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)\asymp(m/X)^\alpha\) for \(1/B\le m\le\gamma/2\), so the one-variable factor is bounded by the integrand of \(I(X)\); on \(m<1/B\), \(\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)\asymp Bm\,i^{-\alpha}\) and \(\vartheta_N\le C/m\) bound its integral by \(Ci^{-\alpha/2}\). On \(m\ge\gamma/4\), the bounds of Lemma SG.17 give a one-variable integral at most
\[ C\sqrt\gamma\,(i/N)^{\alpha/2}=C_\alpha X^{\alpha/2}\gamma^{(1-\alpha)/2}. \]
The rectangle \(m_1<\gamma/4\), \(m_2>\gamma/2\) has \(\Delta^N_\ell\ge c\) and is bounded by the product of the two one-variable bounds, and the square \(m_1,m_2\ge\gamma/4\) is \(O(\gamma(i/N)^\alpha)=O(X^\alpha\gamma^{1-\alpha})\) by Lemma SG.17. These bounds hold for the full lag sum.
At \(X\to X_0\) the integrand converges on compact sets. By Lemma SG.6(iv), \(\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)\to(m/X_0)^\alpha\) and \(m\Lambda_N'/\Lambda_N\to\alpha\), so \(f_i(m)\to \zeta_{X_0}(m)\) and \(\vartheta_N\to\vartheta_\infty\). The normalized sum \(\mathsf T_N\) is a Riemann sum in \(X'=K_\alpha j/B\),
\[ \mathsf T_N(u,v)\to\frac1{K_\alpha}\int_0^\infty\frac{u^\alpha}{X'^\alpha+u^\alpha}\frac{v^\alpha}{X'^\alpha+v^\alpha}\,\dif X'=t_\alpha(u,v), \]
by partial fractions and \(\int_0^\infty(X'^\alpha+u^\alpha)^{-1}\dif X'=K_\alpha u^{1-\alpha}\), with the value at \(u=v\) given by \(\int_0^\infty(1+y^\alpha)^{-2}\dif y=(1-\alpha^{-1})K_\alpha\) [21, (5.12.3), (5.5.3)]. Since \(\mathsf M_\ell/\gamma\to0\) and \(\mathsf T_N\mathsf D_\ell^2/(\gamma m_1m_2)\to0\) on compact sets, \(\Delta^N_\ell\to\Delta^\infty_\ell\). On a compact square \(\mathsf D_\ell\le C\bmom^{\abs\ell}\), so the lag series converges uniformly there. The majorants remove the truncations, which proves \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\to G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X_0)\).
At \(X\to0\) with \(i\to\infty\), rescale \(m=Xr\). On compact sets of \(r>0\), \(Bm=K_\alpha ir\to\infty\) and \(m\to0\), so
\[ f_i(Xr)\to\frac{r^\alpha}{1+r^\alpha},\qquad X\vartheta_N(Xr)\to\frac\alpha r,\qquad \mathsf D_\ell(Xr,Xs)\sim\bmom^{\abs\ell}X\sqrt{rs},\qquad\Delta^N_\ell\to1, \]
by Lemma SG.1(i), \(Q_m\sim m/\sw2\) and \(E(m)\sim m\). Each lag term tends to
\[ \bmom^{\abs\ell}\Big[\frac\alpha\pi\int_0^\infty\frac{r^{\alpha/2-1}}{1+r^\alpha}\,\dif r\Big]^2=\bmom^{\abs\ell}, \]
the integral being \(\pi/\alpha\). The contributions of \(m<\delta X\) and \(m>RX\) to \(I\) are \(O(\delta^{\alpha/2})\) and \(O(R^{-\alpha/2}+X^{\alpha/2})\), the dimension errors vanish, and on compact rescaled squares the lag series is dominated by \(C\bmom^{\abs\ell}\). Hence \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\to\sum_\ell\bmom^{\abs\ell}=(1+\bmom)/(1-\bmom)=1/\sw2\). The same computation without dimension errors gives \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\to1/\sw2\) as \(X\to0\).
At \(X\to\infty\) with \(X/\gamma\to0\), rescale \(m=Xr\) and \(\ell/X\to\tau>0\). On the shell \(m_2=m_1+\ell+O(1)\) one has \(\mathsf M_\ell=m_1+O(1)\) and \(\Delta^N_\ell\to1-t_\alpha(r,r+\tau)/(r+\tau)\), and Lemma SG.22(iii) with \(n=X\) gives
\[ \overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\to C^\infty_\alpha(\tau)\defeq\int_0^\infty\frac{\zeta_1(r+\tau)-\zeta_1(r)}{\tau}\Big(\frac r{r+\tau}\Big)^{\frac{\alpha-1}2}\dif r,\qquad \zeta_1(r)=\frac{r^\alpha}{1+r^\alpha}, \]
by the algebra of Lemma SG.21 with \(V_\alpha(p)\) replaced by \(r^\alpha\). The integral \(I\) divided by \(\sqrt X\) is bounded, its parts \(m<\delta X\) and \(m>RX\) are \(O(\delta^{(\alpha+1)/2})+o(1)\) and \(O(R^{-(\alpha-1)/2})\), and the top bounds divided by \(\sqrt X\) and \(X\) tend to zero because \(X/\gamma\to0\). Lags above \(2RX\) are exponentially small on the remaining square by Lemma SG.22(i), and lags below \(\eta X\) contribute at most \(2\eta X+O(1)\). Hence \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\sim2X\int_0^\infty C^\infty_\alpha\), and, with \(\nu\defeq(\alpha-1)/2\) and \(r=ts\),
\[ 2\int_0^\infty C^\infty_\alpha(\tau)\,\dif\tau=2\int_0^1\frac{t^\nu}{1-t}\Big\{\int_0^\infty\big(\zeta_1(s)-\zeta_1(ts)\big)\dif s\Big\}\dif t =2K_\alpha\int_0^1t^{\nu-1}\,\dif t=\frac{4K_\alpha}{\alpha-1}, \]
because \(\int_0^\infty(\zeta_1(s)-\zeta_1(ts))\,\dif s=\int_0^\infty\big((1+t^\alpha s^\alpha)^{-1}-(1+s^\alpha)^{-1}\big)\dif s=K_\alpha(t^{-1}-1)\). The same computation without dimension errors gives \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\sim\frac{4K_\alpha}{\alpha-1}X\).
For the bulk matching, \(V_\alpha(p)\sim(p/K_\alpha)^\alpha\) at zero, and the substitution \(p=K_\alpha xr\), \(q=K_\alpha xs\) in the double-integral form of Lemma SG.21 turns \(\Gamma_\alpha(x)/(K_\alpha x)\) into the double integral just evaluated. On \(p,q\le1/2\) the continuum elasticity gives \(\Delta_{\mathrm c}\ge c_\alpha\), and the one-variable tails of \(\sqrt{f_x(1-f_x)}/\sqrt p\), divided by \(\sqrt x\), are \(O(\delta^{(\alpha+1)/2})\) and \(O(R^{-(\alpha-1)/2})\); the parts with \(p\) or \(q\) above \(1/4\) are \(O(x^\alpha)\) and \(O(x^{(\alpha+1)/2})\), both \(o(x)\).
