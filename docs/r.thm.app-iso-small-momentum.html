Theorem SF.7 (Small aspect ratios with momentum)
Let \(\bmom\in[0,1)\) and \(\gin,\gout>0\), and assume \(\varepsilon_\bmom\defeq6\gamma_\wedge(1-\bmom^2)\le\frac12\).
  1. (i)
    For every \(\ridge\ge0\) there is \(\vartheta\in\big[1-\varepsilon_\bmom,(1-\varepsilon_\bmom)^{-1/2}\big]\) with
    \[ \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gin,\gout)=\vartheta\;\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout;\ridge). \]
  2. (ii)
    With \(r\defeq\gout/\gin\) and \(J_r\) of Theorem SF.4,
    \[ \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)=\frac{r\,J_r}{\sqrt{2\gout}},\qquad \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gamma,\gamma;0)=\frac{8}{3\pi\sqrt{2\gamma}} . \]
    In particular, in the square case \(C_\bmom(\gamma)=8\vartheta/(3\pi\sqrt{2\sw2\gamma})\) with \(\vartheta\) of (i) at \(\ridge=0\).
  3. (iii)
    If \(6\gamma_\wedge\le\frac12\), then for every \(\bmom\in[0,1)\)
    \[ \big(1-6\gamma_\wedge\big)^{3/2}\le\frac{C_\bmom(\gin,\gout)}{\sw2^{-1/2}\,C_0(\gin,\gout)}\le\big(1-6\gamma_\wedge\big)^{-3/2}. \]
  4. (iv)
    For every \(\ridge\ge0\),
    \[ (1-\varepsilon_\bmom)\big(1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge\big)^{1/2}\le \frac{\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gin,\gout)}{\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;0,\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)}\le (1-\varepsilon_\bmom)^{-1/2}\big(1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge\big)^{-1}. \]
Proof.
The proof sandwiches the root between two linear roots and then integrates.
Constants. For \(\bmom>0\) a direct computation from \(\sw k=(1-\bmom)^k/(1-\bmom^k)\) gives
\[ \frac{\sw4}{\sw2^2}=\frac{1-\bmom^2}{1+\bmom^2},\qquad \frac{\sw3}{\sw2}=\frac{1-\bmom^2}{1+\bmom+\bmom^2}, \]
so that \(\varepsilon_1\defeq6\gamma_\wedge\sw4/\sw2^2\) and \(\varepsilon_2\defeq6\gamma_\wedge\sw3/\sw2\) are both at most \(\varepsilon_\bmom\). At \(\bmom=0\) all power sums equal \(1\) and \(\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon_2=\varepsilon_0\).
Expansions. By Lemma SG.1(i) for \(\bmom>0\), and by Lemma SF.1(iii) and \(\Jfun(x)\ge1-3x\) at \(\bmom=0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.49} \sw2q\Big(1-3\frac{\sw4}{\sw2}q\Big)\le r_\bmom(q)\le\sw2q,\qquad 1-3\sw3q\le\Lw(q)\le1\qquad(q\ge0), \end{equation}
where the upper bounds are Lemma SF.4(v). The bound \(\Jfun(x)\ge1-3x\) is \((1+t)^{-1}\ge1-t\) under the expectation.
A priori bound. Put \(\bar q\defeq2\gamma_\wedge/\sw2\). By (SF.49),
\[ r_\bmom(\bar q)\ge2\gamma_\wedge\big(1-\varepsilon_1\big)\ge\gamma_\wedge . \]
Since \(r_\bmom(q(-s))<\gamma_\wedge\) and \(r_\bmom\) is increasing, \(q(-s)<\bar q\) for every \(s>0\).
Sandwich. For \(c>0\) the root of Lemma SF.5 with \(r(q)=cq\) is \(q_c(s)=c^{-1}Y_{\gin,\gout}(s/c)\), because \(Y=cq\) turns the equation into \((s/c)Y=(\gout-Y)(\gin-Y)\). Lemma SF.5(ii) with \(r_1=r_\bmom\), \(r_2(q)=\sw2q\) and \(i=1\) applies by the upper bound in (SF.49), and gives \(q_{\sw2}(s)\le q(-s)\). Next put \(c\defeq\sw2(1-\varepsilon_1)\). By the a priori bound,
\[ c\,q(-s)\le\sw2\,q(-s)\Big(1-3\frac{\sw4}{\sw2}\bar q\Big)\le r_\bmom\big(q(-s)\big), \]
so Lemma SF.5(ii) with \(r_1(q)=cq\), \(r_2=r_\bmom\) and \(i=2\) gives \(q(-s)\le q_c(s)\).
The factor \(\Lw\). By (SF.49) and \(q(-s)<\bar q\),
\[ 1-\varepsilon_2\le1-3\sw3\bar q\le\Lw\big(q(-s)\big)\le1 . \]
Integration. The function \(Y_{\gin,\gout}\) is decreasing and \(c\le\sw2\), so the substitution \(u=\sqrt c\,v\) gives
\[ \int_0^\infty q_c(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u=c^{-1/2}\int_0^\infty Y_{\gin,\gout}\Big(\frac\ridge c+v^2\Big)\dif v \le c^{-1/2}\int_0^\infty Y_{\gin,\gout}\Big(\frac\ridge{\sw2}+v^2\Big)\dif v, \]
and in the same way
\[ \int_0^\infty q_{\sw2}(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u=\sw2^{-1/2}\int_0^\infty Y_{\gin,\gout}\Big(\frac\ridge{\sw2}+v^2\Big)\dif v . \]
The scaling \(Y_{t\gin,t\gout}(ts)=t\,Y_{\gin,\gout}(s)\) and the substitution \(v=\sqrt{\sw2}\,w\) give
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.50} \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout;\ridge)=\sw2^{-1/2}\,\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi\,\gin\gout}\int_0^\infty Y_{\gin,\gout}\Big(\frac\ridge{\sw2}+w^2\Big)\dif w . \end{equation}
Inserting the sandwich and the bounds on \(\Lw\) in (SF.29) (at \(\ridge=0\), in (SF.21)) proves (i) with \(\vartheta\in[1-\varepsilon_2,(1-\varepsilon_1)^{-1/2}]\).
Closed form. Put \(y(x)\defeq Y_{\gin,\gout}(\gout x)/\gout\), so that
\[ x\,y=(1-y)\Big(\frac1r-y\Big),\qquad y<\min\Big(1,\frac1r\Big). \]
Let \(m_r\) be the transform of the law \(\mathsf M_r\) of Lemma SF.9 and \(\tilde y(x)\defeq1-x\,m_r(x)=\int\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+x}\,\mathsf M_r(\dif\lambda)\). Substituting \(m=(1-\tilde y)/x\) in (SF.34) and expanding gives \(x\tilde y=(1-\tilde y)(1/r-\tilde y)\). Moreover \(\tilde y<\mathsf M_r((0,\infty))=1-\omega_r=\min(1,1/r)\). By the uniqueness in Lemma SF.5(i), \(\tilde y=y\). The half-moment formula (SF.26) for \(\mathsf M_r\) then gives
\[ \int_0^\infty y(w^2)\,\dif w=\frac12\int_0^\infty t^{-1/2}\,y(t)\,\dif t=\frac\pi2\,J_r , \qquad \int_0^\infty Y_{\gin,\gout}(v^2)\,\dif v=\gout^{3/2}\int_0^\infty y(w^2)\,\dif w , \]
the second by \(v=\sqrt{\gout}\,w\). Hence \(\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)=\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi\gin\gout}\gout^{3/2}\frac\pi2J_r=rJ_r/\sqrt{2\gout}\). In the square case \(r=1\), and \(y(v^2)=e^{-2\vartheta'}\) at \(v=2\sinh\vartheta'\), since \(4\sinh^2\vartheta'\,e^{-2\vartheta'}=(1-e^{-2\vartheta'})^2\). Therefore
\[ \int_0^\infty y(v^2)\,\dif v=\int_0^\infty e^{-2\vartheta'}\,2\cosh\vartheta'\,\dif\vartheta'=\int_0^\infty\big(e^{-\vartheta'}+e^{-3\vartheta'}\big)\dif\vartheta'=\frac43 , \]
and \(\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gamma,\gamma;0)=\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi\gamma^2}\gamma^{3/2}\frac43=\frac8{3\pi\sqrt{2\gamma}}\). The last claim of (ii) is (i) at \(\ridge=0\) with (SF.50).
Ratios. For (iii), part (i) at \(\ridge=0\) and (SF.50) give \(C_\bmom=\vartheta_\bmom\,\sw2^{-1/2}\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)\) and \(C_0=\vartheta_0\,\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)\), with \(\vartheta_\bmom,\vartheta_0\in[1-6\gamma_\wedge,(1-6\gamma_\wedge)^{-1/2}]\) because \(\varepsilon_\bmom\le6\gamma_\wedge\). Dividing gives (iii). For (iv), part (i) at momentum \(0\) and aspect ratios \((\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)\) gives \(\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;0,\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)=\vartheta'\,\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout;\ridge)\) with \(\vartheta'\in[1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge,(1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge)^{-1/2}]\); its hypothesis holds because \(\sw2\le1-\bmom^2\), so that \(6\sw2\gamma_\wedge\le\varepsilon_\bmom\le\frac12\). Dividing by part (i) at momentum \(\bmom\) gives (iv).
