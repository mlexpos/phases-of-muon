Proposition SC.3 (Response equation)
Under Assumption S3, for \(s\in I\) let \(b_B(s)\defeq\sum_{j\ge0}b_j\ge0\) with \(b_j\) as in (SC.26), and let \(\mathsf Y_B(s)\defeq\Id_{\Nin}+b_B(s)\Sigmain\). Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.35} \mathcal H(s)\,\mathsf Y_B(s)=\Lw\big(q(-s)\big)\,\Rbar(-s)\,\EE\Gt+E_B(s), \end{equation}
where, with \(r_B\) from Lemma SB.13, \(r_B'\) from Lemma SC.8, \(e_B^\star\) from Lemma SC.9 and \(\delta_B(I)\defeq\sup_{s\in I}\norm{\mathcal P(s)-\Rbar(-s)}\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.36} \sup_{s\in I}\norm{E_B(s)}_\F\le C_I\Big[e_B^\star+r_B'+\norm{\Ahat}+\norm{\Ahat}^2+\delta_B(I)+\frac{1}{\sqrt B}\Big]\longrightarrow0 , \end{equation}
and the coefficient \(b_B\) satisfies
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.37} \sup_{s\in I}\abs{b_B(s)-\sigma(-s)\Fw(q(-s))}\le\sw2\Big(e_B^\star+\frac{C_\Sigma\gamma_\star}{s_-}r_B\Big)\longrightarrow0 . \end{equation}
Proof.
Fix \(s\in I\); every bound below is uniform in \(s\in I\). We start from the exact identity (SC.30) and treat its two terms.
The source term. Fix \(j\), write \(\varpi=\emaw j\), \(x=x_j\), \(f=f_x\), \(\mathcal G=\mathcal G_{j1}\), and split \(\alpha_{1,j}=\alpha_{1,j}^\star+(\alpha_{1,j}-\alpha_{1,j}^\star)\). The replacement error is bounded by the Bessel inequality: with \(e\defeq\alpha_{1,j}-\alpha_{1,j}^\star\), the bound (SC.22), \(\norm{R_0^{(j)}}\le1/s_-\), the tower property, Jensen’s inequality and Lemma SC.9 give
\[ \Big\|B\,\EE\big[e\,R_0^{(j)}(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]\Big\|_\F \le\frac{1}{s_-}\,\EE\Big\|B\,\EE_{\mathcal G}[(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)e]\Big\|_\F\le\frac{C_\Sigma}{s_-}\norm e_2\le C_I\varpi\,e_B^\star . \]
The main part is a Gaussian response. Since \(R_0^{(j)}\) is independent of \((z_j,w_j)\) and \(B\,\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j=\Sigmaout^{1/2}(z_j\tensor w_j)\Sigmain^{1/2}\),
\[ B\,\EE\big[\alpha_{1,j}^\star R_0^{(j)}(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]=\varpi\,\mathcal P_j(s)\,\Sigmaout^{1/2}\,\EE[(z\tensor w)f(d)]\,\Sigmain^{1/2}, \]
and (SB.22) with \(A=\Ahat\), \(\norm{\Ahat}_\F=1\) and the bound \(\norm{f'''}_\infty\le36q_I\) of Lemma SC.7 give
\[ \EE[(z\tensor w)f(d)]=\Ahat\,\EE f'(d)+N_j,\qquad\norm{N_j}_\F\le36q_I\norm{\Ahat}^2 . \]
Since \(\Sigmaout^{1/2}\Ahat\Sigmain^{1/2}=\EE\Gt\), writing \(\jmath_j\defeq\EE f_{x_j}'(d)\), which satisfies \(\abs{\jmath_j}\le1\) and \(\abs{\jmath_j-\Jfun(x_j)}\le r_B'\),
\[ \Big\|B\,\EE\big[\alpha_{1,j}^\star R_0^{(j)}(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]-\varpi\,\jmath_j\,\mathcal P_j(s)\,\EE\Gt\Big\|_\F \le\varpi\,\frac{C_\Sigma}{s_-}\,36q_I\norm{\Ahat}^2\le C_I\varpi\norm{\Ahat}^2 . \]
The expected deleted resolvent is replaced by \(\mathcal P\) and then by \(\Rbar\). By (SB.42) and \(\norm{\EE\Gt}_\F\le C_\Sigma\) (Lemma SA.6(iv)),
\[ \norm{\varpi\jmath_j(\mathcal P_j(s)-\mathcal P(s))\EE\Gt}_\F\le\frac{C_I\varpi^2}{B},\qquad \norm{\varpi\jmath_j(\mathcal P(s)-\Rbar(-s))\EE\Gt}_\F\le C_\Sigma\varpi\,\delta_B(I), \]
and \(\abs{\varpi\jmath_j-\varpi\Jfun(x_j)}\norm{\Rbar(-s)\EE\Gt}_\F\le\varpi\,r_B'\,C_\Sigma/s_-\). Summing over \(j\) with (SC.1), and recalling \(\Lw(q)=\sum_j\emaw j\Jfun(\emaw j^2q)\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.38} \Big\|B\sum_{j\ge0}\EE\big[\alpha_{1,j}R_0^{(j)}(\mathsf u_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]-\Lw(q(-s))\,\Rbar(-s)\,\EE\Gt\Big\|_\F \le C_I\Big[e_B^\star+\norm{\Ahat}^2+\frac1B+\delta_B(I)+r_B'\Big]. \end{equation}
The Gram term. Fix \(j\) and write \(\psi=\psi_{x_j}\). Conditioning on \(\mathcal G\) and using \(B\,\mathsf v_j\tensor\mathsf v_j=\Sigmain^{1/2}(w_j\tensor w_j)\Sigmain^{1/2}\),
\[ \begin{gathered} B\,\EE\big[\alpha_{2,j}\mathsf X_0^{(j)}(\mathsf v_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big] =\EE\Big[\mathsf X_0^{(j)}\,\Sigmain^{1/2}\,\EE_{\mathcal G}[(w_j\tensor w_j)\alpha_{2,j}]\,\Sigmain^{1/2}\Big],\\ \EE_{\mathcal G}[(w_j\tensor w_j)\alpha_{2,j}]=\phi_j\,\Id+\Gamma_j , \end{gathered} \]
where \(\phi_j=\EE_{\mathcal G}\alpha_{2,j}\) is the coefficient (SC.26) and \(\Gamma_j\defeq\EE_{\mathcal G}[(w_j\tensor w_j-\Id)\alpha_{2,j}]\). The centered part is small. Splitting \(\alpha_{2,j}=\alpha_{2,j}^\star+(\alpha_{2,j}-\alpha_{2,j}^\star)\), the Bessel bound Lemma SB.7 with Jensen’s inequality and Lemma SC.9 control the second piece, and for the first, which depends only on the sample \((j,1)\), (SB.23) for \((w,z,\Ahat^\T)\) with \(\norm{\psi''}_\infty\le6\) gives
\[ \EE\norm{\Gamma_j}_\F\le\sqrt2\,\norm{\alpha_{2,j}-\alpha_{2,j}^\star}_2+\varpi^2\sigma\,\big\|\EE[(w\tensor w-\Id)\psi(d)]\big\|_\F \le\varpi^2\big(\sqrt2\,e_B^\star+6\sigma\norm{\Ahat}\big). \]
With \(\norm{\mathsf X_0^{(j)}}\le(2\sqrt{s_-})^{-1}\) and \(\sigma\le C_\Sigma\gamma_\star/s_-\),
\[ \Big\|B\,\EE\big[\alpha_{2,j}\mathsf X_0^{(j)}(\mathsf v_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]-\EE\big[\phi_j\mathsf X_0^{(j)}\big]\Sigmain\Big\|_\F \le\frac{C_\Sigma}{2\sqrt{s_-}}\,\EE\norm{\Gamma_j}_\F\le C_I\varpi^2\big(e_B^\star+\norm{\Ahat}\big). \]
The scalar mean is identified. By Lemma SC.9, Lemma SB.13 and \(\EE\alpha_{2,j}^\star=\varpi^2\sigma\,\EE\psi(d)\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.39} \abs{b_j-\varpi^2\sigma\Jfun(x_j)}\le\abs{\EE\alpha_{2,j}-\EE\alpha_{2,j}^\star}+\varpi^2\sigma\abs{\EE\psi(d)-\Jfun(x_j)} \le\varpi^2\big(e_B^\star+\sigma r_B\big), \end{equation}
and summing over \(j\) with \(\Fw(q)=\sum_j\emaw j^2\Jfun(\emaw j^2q)\) gives (SC.37). The product with the deleted resolvent is split into its mean and its covariance:
\[ \EE\big[\phi_j\mathsf X_0^{(j)}\big]=b_j\,\mathcal H_j(s)+\mathcal C_j=b_j\,\mathcal H(s)+b_j\big(\mathcal H_j(s)-\mathcal H(s)\big)+\mathcal C_j . \]
By Lemma SC.2, \(b_j\le\norm{\alpha_{2,j}}_1\le C_I\varpi^2\); by (SC.21), \(\norm{\mathcal H_j-\mathcal H}_\F\le C_I\varpi/\sqrt B\); and by Lemma SC.5, \(\sum_j\norm{\mathcal C_j}_\F\le C_I\sw2/\sqrt B\). Summing over \(j\) with \(\sum_j\emaw j^3\le1\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.40} \Big\|B\sum_{j\ge0}\EE\big[\alpha_{2,j}\mathsf X_0^{(j)}(\mathsf v_j\tensor\mathsf v_j)\big]-b_B(s)\,\mathcal H(s)\,\Sigmain\Big\|_\F \le C_I\Big[e_B^\star+\norm{\Ahat}+\frac{1}{\sqrt B}\Big]. \end{equation}
Assembly. Inserting (SC.38) and (SC.40) into (SC.30) gives \(\mathcal H(s)=\Lw(q)\Rbar(-s)\EE\Gt-b_B(s)\mathcal H(s)\Sigmain+E_B(s)\) with \(E_B\) bounded by the sum of the two right sides, which is (SC.36); moving the second term to the left is (SC.35). The convergence to zero holds because \(e_B^\star\to0\) (Lemma SC.9), \(r_B,r_B'\to0\) (Lemma SB.13 and Lemma SC.8), \(\norm{\Ahat}\to0\) (Assumption S3) and \(\delta_B(I)\to0\) (Theorem SB.2).
□
