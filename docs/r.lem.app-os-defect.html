Lemma SH.10 (Defect bounds)
For every probability measure \(\nu\) and every \(\mu>0\),
\[ \tfrac12\,m^\varpi_\nu(\mu)\le1-\chi_\nu(\mu)\le m^\varpi_\nu(\mu). \]
Proof.
For \(m\in[0,1]\) one has
\[ \frac m2\le1-\sqrt{1-m}\le m, \]
because \(\sqrt{1-m}\ge1-m\) and \((1-\frac m2)^2\ge1-m\). Apply this with \(m=m_\nu(\mu y)\) and integrate against \(\varpi(y)\,\dif y\), using \(\int\varpi=1\) and the definitions (SH.7) and (SH.16).
□
