Proposition SF.3 (Zero-ridge limits with momentum)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\) and \(\gin,\gout>0\), put \(\gamma_\wedge\defeq\min(\gin,\gout)\) and \(\gamma_\vee\defeq\max(\gin,\gout)\), and let \(K_\bmom\) be the unique \(q>0\) with \(q\Fw(q)=\gamma_\wedge\).
  1. (i)
    As \(\ridge\downarrow0\), \(\psi(\ridge)\to\gamma_\wedge\), and for every \(\ridge>0\)
    \[ \gamma_\wedge-\psi(\ridge)\le\frac{\ridge\,K_\bmom}{\gamma_\vee-\gamma_\wedge}\quad(\gin\ne\gout),\qquad \gamma_\wedge-\psi(\ridge)\le\sqrt{\ridge\,K_\bmom}\quad(\gin=\gout). \]
    In particular the same-time row and column coefficients \(\psi(\ridge)/\gout\) and \(\psi(\ridge)/\gin\) of (SF.14) tend to \(\gamma_\wedge/\gout\) and \(\gamma_\wedge/\gin\), and the same-time total \(B\psi(\ridge)\) of (SF.15) tends to \(\min\{\Nin,\Nout\}\).
  2. (ii)
    Let \(\Nin=\Nout=N\) and \(\gamma=N/B\). For \(\ell\ge0\) let \(\overline C_{1,\ell}(\ridge)\) be the row lag coefficient of (SG.2) for the spectrum \(\mu_1=\dots=\mu_N=1\), which is the same for every row. Then \(\overline C_{1,0}(\ridge)=\psi(\ridge)/\gamma\); for every \(\ell\ge0\) the limit \(\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\defeq\lim_{\ridge\downarrow0}\overline C_{1,\ell}(\ridge)\) exists and is nonnegative, with \(\overline C_{1,0}(0)=1\); and the limit of Definition SF.1 exists and equals
    \[ \mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}=N\Big(1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\Big)\ge N . \]
  3. (iii)
    If moreover \(N>B\), then \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}\ge N>B=\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},0}\).
Proof.
For part (i), \(K_\bmom\) is well defined because every EMA weight is positive, so that \(q\mapsto q\Fw(q)\) is an increasing bijection of \([0,\infty)\) by Lemma SB.1(iii). By Proposition SF.1(ii), \(q(-\ridge)\Fw(q(-\ridge))=\psi(\ridge)<\gamma_\wedge\), so \(q(-\ridge)<K_\bmom\), and (SF.7) gives
\[ \big(\gout-\psi(\ridge)\big)\big(\gin-\psi(\ridge)\big)=\ridge\,q(-\ridge)\le\ridge\,K_\bmom . \]
Both factors are positive. For \(\gin\ne\gout\) the larger one is at least \(\gamma_\vee-\gamma_\wedge\), and for \(\gin=\gout\) the two coincide; this gives the two bounds, and \(\psi(\ridge)\to\gamma_\wedge\). The coefficients are those of Theorem SF.2(i), and \(B\gamma_\wedge=\min\{\Nin,\Nout\}\).
Part (ii) is Proposition SG.4 at \(\mu_1=\dots=\mu_N=1\), which is the case \(\Sigmaout=\Sigmain=\Id_N\) of the setup of the power-law section; the kernel (SG.16) depends on the row only through \(\mu_i\), so every row has the same coefficients. The zero-lag coefficient is the raw coefficient \(\volraw_1(\ridge)\) (Theorem SD.7(iv)), which is \(\psi(\ridge)/\gamma\) by the proof of Theorem SF.2(i). By Theorem SF.2(ii) the row coefficient is \(\mathfrak v_1(\ridge)=\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge)/N\), and by Proposition SG.4(ii) it converges to \(1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\ge1\).
Part (iii) combines (ii) with Proposition SF.2, which gives \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},0}=\min\{B,N\}=B\).
□
