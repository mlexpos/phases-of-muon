Lemma SG.10 (Averaged tail)
There is \(C\) such that, for every fixed \(M\ge M_0(\alpha,\bmom)\),
\[ \limsup_{N\to\infty}\sup_{1\le i\le N}\Big|\frac{\sqrt2\,\mathfrak d_i^{>M}}{a_N\Psi_\alpha^{[M/\gamma]}(i/N)}-1\Big|\le\frac CM . \]
Proof.
The tail integrand is \(\mathcal A\mathcal B_i\cdot E\vartheta_N\), and the factor \(E\vartheta_N\) is close to one inside \([M,\gamma-M]\). Indeed
\[ E(m)\vartheta_N(m)-1=E(m)\Big(\frac{\Lambda_N'(m)}{\Lambda_N(m)}-\frac1m+\frac1{\gamma-m}\Big)\ge0, \]
and Lemma SG.7 and Lemma SG.1(iii) bound it by \(C(m^{-1}+(\gamma-m)^{-1})\le2C/M\) there. The function \(\mathcal B_i\) varies slowly there:
\[ \frac{\mathcal B_i'(m)}{\mathcal B_i(m)}=-\frac1{2(\gamma-m)}-\frac1{2m}+\Big(\frac12-f_i(m)\Big)\frac{\Lambda_N'(m)}{\Lambda_N(m)}, \qquad\Big|\frac{\mathcal B_i'}{\mathcal B_i}\Big|\le C\Big(\frac1m+\frac1{\gamma-m}\Big), \]
uniformly in \(i\).
The averaging error is controlled by Lemma SG.2 with \(W=\mathcal B_i\) on \([M,\gamma-M]\) and \(m_0=1\). It is at most
\[ C\Big(\mathcal B_i(M)+\mathcal B_i(\gamma-M)+\int_M^{\gamma-M}\mathcal B_i(m)\Big(\frac1m+\frac1{\gamma-m}\Big)\dif m\Big). \]
The logarithmic-derivative bound gives \(\mathcal B_i(M)\le(C/M)\int_M^{2M}\mathcal B_i\), and the integral term is at most \((2C/M)\int_M^\gamma\mathcal B_i\). Near the top, (SG.45) and Lemma SG.6(iii) give
\[ \mathcal B_i(m)\le C\,a_N\,\frac{\gamma-m}{\gamma}\qquad(\gamma-M<m<\gamma), \]
uniformly in \(i\), and \(E\vartheta_N\le C(1+(\gamma-m)^{-1})\) there, so the boundary value \(\mathcal B_i(\gamma-M)\) and the whole part \(m>\gamma-M\) of the integral contribute \(O(a_N(M+M^2)/\gamma^2)\) after division by \(\pi\gamma\). On the other hand, the part \(\gamma/4<m<\gamma/2\) gives
\[ \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{\pi\gamma}\int_{\gamma/4}^{\gamma/2}\mathcal B_i\,\dif m\ge c\,a_N, \]
because \(V_N(p)/(V_N(p)+x^\alpha)\ge V_N(1/4)/(V_N(1/4)+1)\) there. Hence replacing \(E\vartheta_N\) by one and \(\mathcal A\) by \(\sqrt{2\pi}\) changes the tail by a relative error at most \(C/M+o(1)\), and the tail becomes \(a_N\) times the profile with \(V_N\) in place of \(V_\alpha\) and the cutoff \(p\ge M/\gamma\).
The replacement of \(V_N\) by \(V_\alpha\) is uniform in the row. The integrand of the profile depends on \(V\) through \(\sqrt V/(V+x^\alpha)\), whose logarithmic derivative in \(\log V\) lies in \([-\frac12,\frac12]\), so Lemma SG.6(ii) changes it by a factor \(1+O(1/(BM))\) on \([M/\gamma,1-M/\gamma]\); the interval \(p>1-M/\gamma\) is negligible for both functions by the bound near the top.
□
