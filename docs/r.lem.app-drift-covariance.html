Lemma SC.5 (Covariance of the Gram coefficient and the mixed resolvent)
Let \(s_\circ>0\). For every age \(j\) and every \(s\ge s_\circ\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.27} \norm{\mathcal C_j}_\F\le\frac{C\,\emaw j^2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B},\qquad\text{hence}\qquad \sum_{j\ge0}\norm{\mathcal C_j}_\F\le\frac{C\,\sw2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B}, \end{equation}
with \(C\) depending only on \((s_\circ,C_\Sigma,\gamma_\star)\). The same bounds, with \(s\) replaced by \(x\defeq\Ree z\), hold at every \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\).
Proof.
The covariance is expanded along a martingale over the remaining samples. Enumerate the samples other than \((j,1)\) as \(l=1,2,\dots\), age by age, and let \(\mathcal A_l\) be the \(\sigma\)-field generated by the first \(l\) of them, \(\mathcal A_0\) the trivial one. Put
\[ D_l\defeq\EE[\phi_j\mid\mathcal A_l]-\EE[\phi_j\mid\mathcal A_{l-1}]\qquad(l\ge1). \]
Since \(\phi_j\) is bounded by Lemma SC.2 and is a function of the enumerated samples, Lévy’s upward theorem [101, Theorem 14.2] gives \(\EE[\phi_j\mid\mathcal A_l]\to\phi_j\) almost surely, hence in \(L^2\) by boundedness; that is, \(\phi_j-b_j=\sum_{l\ge1}D_l\) in \(L^2\). At fixed dimension \(\norm{\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}}_\F\le\sqrt{\min(\Nout,\Nin)}/(2\sqrt s)\), so the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality passes the expansion to the covariance:
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.28} \mathcal C_j=\sum_{l\ge1}\EE\big[D_l\,\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}\big]. \end{equation}
Each martingale difference is bounded by the rank sensitivity. Let \(\phi_j^{[l]}\) denote \(\phi_j\) with the sample \(l\) replaced by an independent copy. Conditioning on \(\mathcal A_l\) after this replacement is the same as conditioning on \(\mathcal A_{l-1}\), so \(D_l=\EE[\phi_j-\phi_j^{[l]}\mid\mathcal A_l]\). The replacement changes the minor \(M_0^{(j1)}\) by a matrix of rank at most two, and Lemma SC.4 with \(k=2\) gives, for every value of the samples,
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.29} \abs{D_l}\le\sup\big\{\abs{\phi_{\emaw j}(K)-\phi_{\emaw j}(K')}:\ \operatorname{rank}(K-K')\le2\big\}\le\frac{2C\,\emaw j^2}{\sqrt s\,B}. \end{equation}
Each term of (SC.28) is a deletion term. Let \(\mathsf X_0^{(j1),[-l]}\) be the mixed resolvent of the buffer with both samples \((j,1)\) and \(l\) deleted. Conditionally on all samples other than \(l\), the matrix \(\mathsf X_0^{(j1),[-l]}\) is fixed, while \(D_l\) is a function of the samples up to \(l\) with \(\EE[D_l\mid\mathcal A_{l-1}]=0\); since the sample \(l\) is independent of the others, the conditional mean of \(D_l\) given all samples other than \(l\) is \(\EE[D_l\mid\mathcal A_{l-1}]=0\). Hence \(\EE[D_l\mathsf X_0^{(j1),[-l]}]=0\) and
\[ \EE\big[D_l\,\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}\big]=\EE\big[D_l\big(\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}-\mathsf X_0^{(j1),[-l]}\big)\big]. \]
If the sample \(l\) has age \(k\), this is the situation of Lemma SC.3 with \(M=M_0^{(j1)}\), the minor \(M_0^{(j1),[-l]}\), \(\varpi=\emaw k\) and the multiplier \(h=D_l\), bounded by (SC.29); conditioning on all samples other than \(l\) and then taking expectations gives
\[ \big\|\EE\big[D_l\,\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}\big]\big\|_\F\le\frac{2C\emaw j^2}{\sqrt sB}\cdot\frac{C\emaw k}{s\sqrt B}=\frac{C\,\emaw j^2\,\emaw k}{s^{3/2}B\sqrt B}. \]
The terms are summed over the enumeration. There are at most \(B\) samples of each age \(k\), so
\[ \norm{\mathcal C_j}_\F\le\sum_{k\ge0}B\,\frac{C\emaw j^2\emaw k}{s^{3/2}B\sqrt B}=\frac{C\emaw j^2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B}\sum_{k\ge0}\emaw k\le\frac{C\emaw j^2}{s^{3/2}\sqrt B}, \]
by (SC.1). Summing over \(j\) gives the second bound of (SC.27).
At \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\) the proof is the same with \(s\) replaced by \(x\) in the bounds. The variables \(\phi_j\) and \(D_l\) are complex: Lévy’s theorem is applied to the real and imaginary parts of \(\phi_j\), (SC.29) follows from Lemma SC.4 at \(z\), \(\norm{\mathsf X_0^{(j1)}}_\F\le\sqrt{\min(\Nout,\Nin)}/(2\sqrt x)\), and the deletion terms are bounded by Lemma SC.3 at \(z\) with the complex multiplier \(h=D_l\).
□

      opNorm F.C.Sout ≤ CS → opNorm F.C.Sin ≤ CS →
      (m : ℝ) ≤ γ * B → (n : ℝ) ≤ γ * B →
      ∀ s : ℝ, s0 ≤ s →
        (∀ (j : ℕ) (i : Fin m) (k : Fin n),

              bj (Muon.emaW F.β) F S t j s) *
