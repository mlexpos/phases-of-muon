Lemma SE.25 (Uniformity over diffuse states)
Assume {{(H1)}}. For each dimension let \(e_B\ge0\) be a function on the set \(\mathscr S_B\) of normalized states such that \(e_B(\widehat A_B)\to0\) along every subsequence of dimensions and every choice of \(\widehat A_B\in\mathscr S_B\) along it with \(\norm{\widehat A_B}\to0\). Then for every sequence \(\delta_B\to0\),
\[ \bar e_B\defeq\sup\big\{e_B(\widehat A):\ \widehat A\in\mathscr S_B,\ \norm{\widehat A}\le\delta_B\big\}\longrightarrow0 , \]
with the convention that the supremum of the empty set is zero.
Proof.
Suppose not. Then there are \(\epsilon>0\) and a subsequence of dimensions at which \(\bar e_B>\epsilon\), and at each of them a state \(\widehat A_B\in\mathscr S_B\) with \(\norm{\widehat A_B}\le\delta_B\) and \(e_B(\widehat A_B)>\epsilon/2\). Along this subsequence \(\norm{\widehat A_B}\to0\) while \(e_B(\widehat A_B)\) does not tend to zero, a contradiction.
□
