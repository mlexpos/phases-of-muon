Proposition SI.6 (Limits of the ratio)
Write \(\mathsf R\defeq t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}/t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}\), and consider sequences with \(N\to\infty\), \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) and \(B_N\ge N\) in the indicated windows. The constants in \(\asymp\) and \(O\) depend only on \(\alpha\), \(\texp\) and \(T\).
  1. (i)
    The range \(\texp<1\) (window of Theorem SI.8). With \(\Xi_N\defeq\epsilon N^{p-1}\) and \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}=(p-1)/\alpha\),
    \[ \mathsf R\asymp\begin{cases} \Xi_N^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})},&0<\alpha<1,\\ (\log N)\,\Xi_N^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})},&\alpha=1,\\ N^{\alpha-1}\,\Xi_N^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})},&1<\alpha<2,\\ (N/\log N)\,\Xi_N^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})},&\alpha=2,\\ \epsilon^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})},&\alpha>2. \end{cases} \]
    Hence \(\mathsf R\to0\) for \(0<\alpha<1\) along sequences with \(\Xi_N\to\infty\), and \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) for \(\alpha>2\). For \(\alpha=1\) and \(\epsilon_N=N^{-r_{\text{s-SVD}}}(\log N)^{b}\) with \(b>0\), \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) if \(b<2r_{\text{s-SVD}}\) and \(\mathsf R\to0\) if \(b>2r_{\text{s-SVD}}\). For \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\epsilon_N=N^{-\omega}\) with \(0<\omega<p-1\), put \(\omega_c\defeq r_{\text{s-SVD}}(2-\alpha)\in(0,p-1)\); then \(\mathsf R\to0\) if \(\omega<\omega_c\) and \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) if \(\omega>\omega_c\). For \(\alpha=2\) and \(\epsilon_N=(\log N)^{-\omega}\) with \(\omega>0\), \(\mathsf R\to0\) if \(\omega<2r_{\text{s-SVD}}\) and \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) if \(\omega>2r_{\text{s-SVD}}\). All these sequences lie in the window.
  2. (ii)
    \(\alpha>2\), \(\texp\ge1\) (window of Theorem SI.9). \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) if \(\texp\le\alpha+1\), and \(\mathsf R\to\pi\zeta(\alpha)J(p,\alpha)/(\zeta(\alpha/2)J(p,\alpha/2))\in(0,\infty)\) if \(\texp>\alpha+1\); this limit exceeds \(\pi/2\) if \(\alpha\ge4\).
  3. (iii)
    \(1<\alpha\le2\), \(\texp\ge1\) (window of Theorem SI.9). \(\mathsf R\to0\) if \(\texp>\alpha+1\). If \(\texp=\alpha+1\) and \(1<\alpha<2\), then \(\mathsf R\to0\). If \(\alpha=2\), \(\texp=3\) and \(\log(1/\epsilon)/\log N\to c_\infty\), then \(0\le c_\infty\le1\) and \(\mathsf R\to\pi\zeta(2)c_\infty/(8J(5,1))\).
  4. (iv)
    \(0<\alpha\le1\), \(\texp\ge1\) (low-exponent window). \(\mathsf R\to0\); more precisely \(\mathsf R\) is \(O(\log(1/\epsilon)^{-2})\), \(O(\epsilon^{(\texp-1)/(p-1)})\), \(O(\sqrt\epsilon\log(1/\epsilon))\) and \(O(\sqrt\epsilon)\) for \(\texp=1\), \(1<\texp<\alpha+1\), \(\texp=\alpha+1\) and \(\texp>\alpha+1\) respectively.
  5. (v)
    \(1<\alpha\le2\), \(1\le\texp<\alpha+1\) (window of Theorem SI.9). Put
    \[ e_\texp\defeq\frac{\alpha+1-\texp}{2(p-1)},\qquad \texp_c(\alpha)\defeq\frac{\alpha^2-2\alpha+3}{3-\alpha} , \]
    so that \(1<\texp_c(\alpha)<\alpha+1\) for \(1<\alpha<2\), and \(\texp_c(2)=3\).
    1. (a)
      If \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\texp_c(\alpha)\le\texp<\alpha+1\), then \(\mathsf R\to0\) along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\), \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) and \(\epsilon N\to\infty\).
    2. (b)
      If \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(1\le\texp<\texp_c(\alpha)\), put \(\omega_c\defeq(2-\alpha)(p-1)/(\alpha+1-\texp)\in(0,1)\). For \(\epsilon_N=N^{-\omega}\) with \(0<\omega<1\), \(\mathsf R\to0\) if \(\omega<\omega_c\) and \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) if \(\omega>\omega_c\). For \(\epsilon_N=1/\log N\), \(\mathsf R\to0\).
    3. (c)
      If \(\alpha=2\) and \(1\le\texp<3\), then \(\mathsf R\to\infty\) for \(\epsilon_N=N^{-\omega}\) with \(0<\omega<1\), and \(\mathsf R\to0\) for \(\epsilon_N=1/\log N\).
    All the sequences named in (b) and (c) lie in the window for large \(N\).
The ratio law of Corollary SI.3 is a competition between the dimension factor \(D_N\), which tends to zero for \(\alpha\le2\), and the accuracy factor, which grows as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). This proposition decides it inside the windows: SignSVD wins for \(\alpha>2\) and \(\texp\le\alpha+1\), the row of Phase A in Figure 7, and SignSGD wins for \(\alpha\le1\) and \(\texp\ge1\). Elsewhere the winner depends on how \(N\) and \(\epsilon\) move together, through a critical exponent \(\omega_c\) and the curve \(\texp_c(\alpha)\); these are windowed forms of the accuracy-exponent map of Corollary SI.7.
Proof sketch.
The order of the dimension factor decides each case. Comparing the harmonic sums with integrals (Lemma SI.8), \(D_N\) is of order \(N^{-\alpha/2}\), \(N^{-1/2}\log N\), \(N^{\alpha/2-1}\), \(1/\log N\) for \(\alpha<1\), \(\alpha=1\), \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\alpha=2\), and tends to \(\pi\zeta(\alpha)/\zeta(\alpha/2)\) for \(\alpha>2\) ((SI.31)). For \(\alpha>2\) the accuracy factor tends to infinity unless \(\texp>\alpha+1\), which gives (ii); for \(\alpha\ge4\) the limit exceeds \(\pi/2\) because \(\zeta(\alpha)>1\), \(\zeta(\alpha/2)<2\), and \(J(p,\alpha)\ge J(p,\alpha/2)\) term by term. In (iii), \(D_N\to0\) and \(\log(1/\epsilon)\le\log N\) in the window. For \(\texp<1\), write \(\epsilon=\Xi_NN^{-(p-1)}\); since \((p-1)/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})=\alpha/2\),
\[ \mathsf R\asymp D_N\,\epsilon^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})}=D_N\,N^{\alpha/2}\,\Xi_N^{-1/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})}, \]
and the five rows of (i) are the five orders of \(D_NN^{\alpha/2}\); for \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\epsilon_N=N^{-\omega}\) this is \(N^{(\omega-\omega_c)/(2r_{\text{s-SVD}})}\).
For \(1<\alpha\le2\) and \(1\le\texp<\alpha+1\) the sign of one exponent locates the curve \(\texp_c\). These rows of the ratio law read \(\mathsf R\asymp D_N\epsilon^{-e_\texp}\ell_\epsilon\), with \(\ell_\epsilon=1/\log(1/\epsilon)\) at \(\texp=1\) and \(\ell_\epsilon=1\) otherwise, and ((SI.58))
\[ \frac\alpha2-1+e_\texp=\frac{(3-\alpha)\big(\texp_c(\alpha)-\texp\big)}{2(p-1)} . \]
For \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\mathsf R\asymp(\epsilon N)^{-e_\texp}N^{\alpha/2-1+e_\texp}\), which tends to zero when \(\texp\ge\texp_c(\alpha)\) because \(\epsilon N\to\infty\); this is (v)(a). Below the curve, at \(\epsilon_N=N^{-\omega}\), the exponent \(\frac\alpha2-1+\omega e_\texp\) changes sign at \(\omega_c=(1-\frac\alpha2)/e_\texp<1\), which is (v)(b); (v)(c) is the same with \(D_N\asymp1/\log N\). For \(\alpha\le1\) and \(\texp\ge1\) the window itself decides: \(D_N=\frac\pi2k_{N,\text{s-SVD}}\), and the window condition \(k_{N,\text{s-SVD}}C_{\text{s-SVD}}I_r(H)\le\sqrt\epsilon\) makes this \(O(\sqrt\epsilon)\), or \(O(\sqrt\epsilon/\log(1/\epsilon))\) at \(\texp=1\), which outweighs the accuracy factor and gives (iv).
