Lemma SF.18 (Ridge excess)
Let \(\gamma\ge\gamma^\circ_\bmom\) and \(\ridge>0\). Then
\[ \sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C^{\,\sharp}_{\ell}(\ridge)\le\frac{\mathsf K^\flat_{\rm ex}+\mathsf K^\sharp_{\rm ex}\,\psi(\ridge)}\gamma . \]
Proof.
The ridge factor exceeds one only within a bounded distance of the top level \(\log Q_{\psi(\ridge)}\) in logarithmic scale, and the lag lattice meets that window once per period of length \(2\log(1/\bmom)\); there are about \(\psi(\ridge)\) periods. Write \(\psi=\psi(\ridge)\), \(b\defeq\log(1/\bmom)\), \(U\defeq\log Q_\psi\), \(u\defeq\log y\), \(v\defeq\log z\) and \(v_\ell\defeq v+2b\ell=\log z_\ell\). Then \(D(y,z)\,\dif y\,\dif z=\mathcal Z(y,z)\,\dif u\,\dif v\).
The ridge factor. The closure at \(s=\ridge\) gives \(\ridge\,Q_\psi=(\gamma-\psi)^2\), and \(\mathsf A(y)\ge\gamma-\psi\) for \(y<Q_\psi\). Hence \(\ridge y/\mathsf A(y)^2\le y/Q_\psi=e^{-(U-u)}\), and
\[ \mathsf u_\ridge(e^u)\le1+\varpi(U-u),\qquad \varpi(\zeta)\defeq\big(1-e^{-\zeta}\big)^{-1/2}-1\le\frac1{e^\zeta-1}\qquad(\zeta>0). \]
The substitution \(\sqrt{1-e^{-\zeta}}=t\) gives \(\varpi(\zeta)\,\dif\zeta=2\,\dif t/(1+t)\), so that
\[ \int_0^\infty\varpi=2\log2,\qquad \int_0^\infty\zeta\,\varpi(\zeta)\,\dif\zeta\le\int_0^\infty\frac\zeta{e^\zeta-1}\,\dif\zeta=\frac{\pi^2}6 . \]
Moreover \(\varpi\) is decreasing, and \(\varpi(1)<0.6\).
The other factors. On the support of \(\overline C^{\,\sharp}_\ell\) one has \(z_1\le z_\ell<Q_\psi<Q_\gamma\), so \(\mathsf A_\vee\ge\Phi(1)\) by Lemma SF.14(v). Also \(\mathsf j(y)\le\mathsf A_\vee+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) and \(\mathsf j(z_\ell)\le\mathsf A(z_\ell)+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\le\mathsf A_\vee+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\). The two cases \(\mathsf A_\vee\ge2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) and \(\mathsf A_\vee<2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) of Lemma SF.15(v) give
\[ \frac{\mathsf j(y)\,\mathsf j(z_\ell)}{\gamma^2\,\widehat\Delta(y,z)}\le\mathsf K_6 . \]
The same bound at \(z_\ell=e^{v_\ell}\) gives
\[ \mathsf u_\ridge(y)\,\mathsf u_\ridge(z_\ell)-1\le\varpi(U-u)+\big(1+\varpi(U-u)\big)\,\varpi(U-v_\ell), \]
and summing over \(\ell\) gives
\[ \sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C^{\,\sharp}_{\ell}(\ridge)\le\frac{\mathsf K_6}{\pi^2\gamma}\iint_{u<U}\mathcal Z\,\Big[\varpi(U-u)\,\mathsf n(v)+\big(1+\varpi(U-u)\big)\,\mathsf W_\ridge(v)\Big]\,\dif u\,\dif v, \]
\[ \mathsf n(v)\defeq\#\{\ell\ge1:\ v_\ell<U\}\le\frac{(U-v)_+}{2b},\qquad \mathsf W_\ridge(v)\defeq\sum_{\ell\ge1,\ v_\ell<U}\varpi(U-v_\ell). \]
The gaps \(U-v_\ell\) form an arithmetic progression of step \(2b\) starting at some \(\zeta_0(v)\in(0,2b]\), and \(\varpi\) is decreasing, so that
\[ \mathsf W_\ridge(v)\le\Big[\varpi\big(\zeta_0(v)\big)+\frac{\log2}b\Big]\mathbf 1\{v<U-2b\} . \]
The bracket is a \(2b\)-periodic function of \(v\) whose integral over one period is at most \(\int_0^\infty\varpi+2\log2=4\log2\).
The term with \(\mathsf n\). By Lemma SF.15(ii), \(\mathcal Z\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\,\mathsf h(\abs{u-v})\) with \(\mathsf h(w)\defeq w/(2\sinh(w/2))\le1\), and the computation in the proof of Lemma SF.16(ii) gives \(\int_\R\mathsf h=\pi^2\) and \(\int_\R\abs w\,\mathsf h(w)\,\dif w=28\,\zeta(3)\). With \((U-v)_+\le(U-u)+\abs{u-v}\) for \(u<U\), this term is at most
\[ \frac{\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom}{2b}\Big(\pi^2\int_0^\infty\zeta\varpi(\zeta)\,\dif\zeta+28\,\zeta(3)\int_0^\infty\varpi\Big)\le\frac{\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom}{2b}\Big(\frac{\pi^4}6+56\,\zeta(3)\log2\Big). \]
The term with \(\mathsf W_\ridge\), for \(v\ge V_0\defeq-2\log(1-\bmom)\). Put \(I(v)\defeq\int_{u<U}\mathcal Z\,(1+\varpi(U-u))\,\dif u\). The same bound on \(\mathcal Z\) gives \(I(v)\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(\pi^2+2\log2)\). If \((1-\bmom)^2Q_\psi\ge1\), the lower bound of Lemma SF.6(i) at \(Q_\psi\) gives
\[ U-V_0=\log\big((1-\bmom)^2Q_\psi\big)=2b\,\mathsf n_\bmom(Q_\psi)<2b\,(\psi+\mathsf c_\bmom), \]
so that \([V_0,U-2b]\) is covered by at most \(\psi+\mathsf c_\bmom+1\) periods; otherwise this interval is empty. This part is therefore at most \(4\log2\;\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(\pi^2+2\log2)(\psi+\mathsf c_\bmom+1)\).
The term with \(\mathsf W_\ridge\), for \(v<V_0\). Put \(w_1\defeq V_0-v>0\); we show \(I(v)\le\mathsf K_{\rm J}(1+w_1)e^{-w_1/2}\). On \(u<V_0\), Lemma SF.15(ii) and \(\sw2e^{V_0}=1/(1-\bmom^2)\) give
\[ \mathcal Z\le\sw2e^{(u+v)/2}=\frac{e^{-(V_0-u)/2}\,e^{-w_1/2}}{1-\bmom^2} . \]
Splitting \(1+\varpi\) into \(1\), \(\varpi\) on \(U-u\ge1\) (where \(\varpi<0.6\)) and \(\varpi\) on \(U-u<1\) (where \(e^{-(V_0-u)/2}\le1\) and \(\int\varpi\le2\log2\)), the part \(u<V_0\) of \(I(v)\) is at most \((2+1.2+2\log2)\,e^{-w_1/2}/(1-\bmom^2)\). On \(u\ge V_0\) put \(w\defeq u-v\ge w_1\); then \(\mathcal Z\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(1+w)e^{-w/2}\), and
\[ \int_{w_1}^\infty(1+w)e^{-w/2}\,\dif w=(6+2w_1)\,e^{-w_1/2}\le6(1+w_1)\,e^{-w_1/2} . \]
The same splitting gives at most \((6+3.6)\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(1+w_1)e^{-w_1/2}\) from \(1\) and from \(\varpi\) on \(U-u\ge1\), and at most \(2\log2\;\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\sup_{w\ge w_1}(1+w)e^{-w/2}\) from \(\varpi\) on \(U-u<1\). The function \((1+w)e^{-w/2}\) is at most \(2e^{-1/2}\), is at least \(1\) on \([0,1]\) and decreases on \([1,\infty)\), so this supremum is at most \(2e^{1/2}(1+w_1)e^{-w_1/2}\). Adding the parts gives the bound on \(I(v)\).
The function \(f(w)\defeq(1+w)e^{-w/2}\) is unimodal on \((0,\infty)\), with \(\sup f=2e^{-1/2}<1.214\) and \(\int_0^\infty f=6\). Cut \((-\infty,V_0)\) into consecutive intervals of length \(2b\) ending at \(V_0\). On each, \(\mathsf W_\ridge\) has integral at most \(4\log2\), and \(I\le\mathsf K_{\rm J}\sup f\) over the corresponding interval of \(w_1\). For a unimodal \(f\ge0\) the sum of its suprema over consecutive intervals of length \(h\) is at most \(3\sup f+h^{-1}\int f\): an interval that neither contains the mode nor is adjacent to the interval containing it has its supremum at the end nearest the mode, which is at most \(h^{-1}\) times the integral of \(f\) over the next interval towards the mode, and the three remaining intervals contribute at most \(\sup f\) each. With \(h=2b\) this part is at most \(4\log2\;\mathsf K_{\rm J}\,(3.64+3/b)\).
Adding the three parts and multiplying by \(\mathsf K_6/(\pi^2\gamma)\) gives the lemma.
