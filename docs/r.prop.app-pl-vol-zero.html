Proposition SG.4 (Volatility at zero ridge)
Let \(0<\bar z\le z_1\) and \(E_i\defeq A_N\bar z^{-1/2}+2\sqrt{D_i}\,\bar z^{1/4}\).
  1. (i)
    For every \(\ell\ge0\) the limit \(\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\defeq\lim_{\ridge\downarrow0}\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)\) exists, \(\overline C_{i,0}(0)=1\), and \(\abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le E_i\,\bmom^{\ell/3}\) for \(0\le\ridge\le\bar z\) and \(\ell\ge1\).
  2. (ii)
    The series \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\defeq1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\) converges absolutely, \(\mathfrak v_i(\ridge)\to\mathfrak v_i(0)\) as \(\ridge\downarrow0\), and for every integer \(H\ge0\) and \(0<\ridge\le\bar z\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SG.21} \abs{\mathfrak v_i(\ridge)-\mathfrak v_i(0)}\le2(1+2H)\sqrt{D_i}\,\ridge^{1/4}+\frac{4E_i\,\bmom^{(H+1)/3}}{1-\bmom^{1/3}} . \end{equation}
  3. (iii)
    For every \(\ell\ge0\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SG.22} \overline C_{i,\ell}(0)=\frac4{\pi^2}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(x^2,y^2)\,\dif x\,\dif y =\frac1{\pi^2}\int_0^\gamma\!\!\int_0^\gamma W_i(m_1)W_i(m_2)\,\frac{\mathsf D_\ell}{\Delta_\ell^N}\,\dif m_1\,\dif m_2 , \end{equation}
    all terms are nonnegative, and \(\mathfrak v_i(0)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z}\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\ge1\) with \(\overline C_{i,-\ell}(0)\defeq\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\), which is the integral (SG.22) with the kernels of the lag \(-\ell\).
Proof.
Part (i) combines the two bounds of Lemma SG.3 with the deficit (SG.11). For \(0<\eta\le\ridge\le z_1\),
\[ \abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\eta)-\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le2\sqrt{D_i}\,\ridge^{1/4}, \]
so the family is Cauchy as \(\ridge\downarrow0\), uniformly in \(\ell\), and the limit satisfies \(\abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)-\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le2\sqrt{D_i}\,\ridge^{1/4}\). At \(\ell=0\) the limit is \(\lim_{\ridge\downarrow0}\volraw_i(\ridge)=1\), by (SG.11). For the envelope fix \(\ell\ge1\) and put \(\ridge_\ell\defeq\bar z\,\bmom^{4\ell/3}\). If \(\ridge\le\ridge_\ell\), comparison with \(\ridge_\ell\) and (SG.19) give
\[ \abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le\frac{A_N\bmom^\ell}{\sqrt{\ridge_\ell}}+2\sqrt{D_i}\,\ridge_\ell^{1/4} =\big(A_N\bar z^{-1/2}+2\sqrt{D_i}\,\bar z^{1/4}\big)\bmom^{\ell/3}=E_i\bmom^{\ell/3}. \]
If \(\ridge_\ell<\ridge\le\bar z\), (SG.19) alone gives \(A_N\bmom^\ell\ridge^{-1/2}\le A_N\bmom^\ell\ridge_\ell^{-1/2}\le E_i\bmom^{\ell/3}\).
Part (ii) splits the lag sum at \(H\). The first \(1+2H\) terms of \(\mathfrak v_i(\ridge)-\mathfrak v_i(0)\) are bounded by the comparison of part (i), and the remaining ones by the envelope:
\[ \abs{\mathfrak v_i(\ridge)-\mathfrak v_i(0)}\le(1+2H)\,2\sqrt{D_i}\,\ridge^{1/4}+4E_i\sum_{\ell>H}\bmom^{\ell/3}, \]
which is (SG.21). Letting \(\ridge\downarrow0\) and then \(H\to\infty\) gives the convergence.
Part (iii) compares the regularized filter with a truncated resolvent filter at zero ridge. For \(0<d<T\) let \(f_{d,T}(x)\defeq\frac2\pi\int_d^T\iota_{y^2}(x)\,\dif y\), an odd function with \(0\le f_{d,T}\le1\) on \([0,\infty)\) and Lipschitz constant at most \(2/(\pi d)\). For \(x>0\),
\[ \begin{gathered} 0\le1-f_{d,T}(x)=\frac2\pi\arctan\frac dx+\frac2\pi\arctan\frac xT\le\frac2\pi\arctan\frac dx+\frac{2x}{\pi T},\\ \Big(\frac2\pi\arctan\frac dx\Big)^2\le\frac{2d^2}{x^2+d^2}=2\big(1-\omega_{d^2}(x)^2\big), \end{gathered} \]
the last inequality by the bound \(1\) when \(x\le d\) and \(\arctan(d/x)\le d/x\) when \(x>d\). Both \(\omega_\eta\) and \(f_{d,T}\) vanish at \(x=0\), and on \(x>0\), \(\abs{\omega_\eta-f_{d,T}}\le(1-\omega_\eta)+(1-f_{d,T})\). Squaring and using \((1-\omega_\eta)^2\le1-\omega_\eta^2\), functional calculus gives
\[ \begin{aligned} &\big(\Qridge\eta(K)-f_{d,T}^\sharp(K)\big)\big(\Qridge\eta(K)-f_{d,T}^\sharp(K)\big)^\T\\ &\qquad\preceq2\big(\Id-\Qridge\eta(K)\Qridge\eta(K)^\T\big)+8\big(\Id-\Qridge{d^2}(K)\Qridge{d^2}(K)^\T\big)+\frac{16}{\pi^2T^2}KK^\T . \end{aligned} \]
The argument of Lemma SG.3 applies to the pair of filters \((\omega_\eta,f_{d,T})\): the closure (SD.52) holds for the fixed odd bounded Lipschitz filter \(f_{d,T}\) by Remark SD.2, and the independent model has the exact row moment \(\EE\,(M^{\mathrm g}_tM^{\mathrm g\,\T}_t)_{ii}=\gamma\sw2\mu_i\). In the limit,
\[ \Big|\overline C_{i,\ell}(\eta)-B\,\mathfrak R_\ell[u_i\tensor u_i,\Id](f_{d,T},f_{d,T})\Big| \le2\Big(2\big(1-\volraw_i(\eta)\big)+8\big(1-\volraw_i(d^2)\big)+\frac{16\gamma\sw2\mu_i}{\pi^2T^2}\Big)^{1/2}, \]
where \(\mathfrak R_\ell\) is the response of (SD.49). By the bilinearity and the sup-norm bound of the response (Theorem SD.3), and the uniform continuity of \(y\mapsto\iota_{y^2}\) in the supremum norm on \([d,T]\), the response of \(f_{d,T}\) is the integral of the responses of the resolvent filters,
\[ B\,\mathfrak R_\ell[u_i\tensor u_i,\Id](f_{d,T},f_{d,T})=\frac4{\pi^2}\int_d^T\!\!\int_d^T\mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(x^2,y^2)\,\dif x\,\dif y . \]
Let \(\eta\downarrow0\), then \(d\downarrow0\) and \(T\to\infty\). The right side of the error bound tends to zero by (SG.11), the left side tends to \(\overline C_{i,\ell}(0)\) by (i), and the double integrals of the nonnegative kernel (Proposition SG.3) increase to the integral over \((0,\infty)^2\). This proves the first equality of (SG.22).
The second equality is the change of variables \(x^2=s_N(m_1)\), \(y^2=s_N(m_2)\). By Proposition SG.1(i), \(\dif x=\frac12\sqrt{s_N(m_1)}\,\vartheta_N(m_1)\,\dif m_1\) in absolute value, so
\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac4{\pi^2}\int\!\!\int\mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(x^2,y^2)\,\dif x\,\dif y\\ &\qquad=\frac1{\pi^2}\int_0^\gamma\!\!\int_0^\gamma\mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(s_1',s_2')\sqrt{s_1's_2'}\,\vartheta_N(m_1)\vartheta_N(m_2)\,\dif m_1\,\dif m_2,\qquad s_k'\defeq s_N(m_k). \end{aligned} \]
In the coordinates of (SG.6) at \(s=s_N(m)\), writing \(\Lambda=\Lambda_N(m)\) and using \(\tilde s_1=s\Lambda=(\gamma-m)m/Q_m\),
\[ \frac{s}{s+s_1}=\frac{\gamma-m}{\gamma},\qquad \frac{\sqrt{\mu_i}\,\sqrt s}{s+\tilde s_1\mu_i}=\frac{\sqrt{\mu_i}}{\sqrt s\,(1+\Lambda\mu_i)} =\sqrt{\frac{Q_m}{\gamma-m}}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{\mu_i\Lambda}}{\sqrt m\,(1+\Lambda\mu_i)} . \]
Inserting these in (SG.16), multiplied by \(\sqrt{ss'}\), gives
\[ \mathfrak k_{i,\ell}(s_1',s_2')\sqrt{s_1's_2'}\,\vartheta_N(m_1)\vartheta_N(m_2)=W_i(m_1)W_i(m_2)\,\frac{\sqrt{q_1q_2}\,b_\ell}{\Delta_\ell}. \]
The scalars of \(\Delta_\ell\) transform in the same way. Since \(\mu_j/(s+\tilde s_1\mu_j)=f_j(m)/\tilde s_1\), \(s\tilde\sigma=\gamma-m\) and \(\sigma=m/\tilde s_1=Q_m/(\gamma-m)\),
\[ ss'\,\mathsf U\tilde\sigma\tilde\sigma'=\frac{\mathsf T_N\,q_1q_2}{m_1m_2},\qquad ss'\,\mathsf V\sigma\sigma'=\frac{q_1q_2}{\gamma},\qquad ss'\,\mathsf U\mathsf V=\frac{\mathsf T_N\,q_1q_2}{\gamma\,m_1m_2}. \]
With \(\mathsf D_\ell=\sqrt{q_1q_2}\,b_\ell\) and \(\mathsf M_\ell=q_1q_2e_\ell\) this gives \(\Delta_\ell=\Delta_\ell^N\), which proves the second equality.
For the lag sum, the negative lags are defined by the transposed kernels, whose integrals over the symmetric square equal those of the positive lags. Absolute convergence and the bound \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\ge\overline C_{i,0}(0)=1\) follow from (i) and the nonnegativity of every term.
