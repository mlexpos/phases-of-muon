Lemma SG.7 (Elasticity)
There is \(c_\alpha>0\), independent of \(N\), \(B\), \(c_N\) and \(\Lambda\), such that
\[ c_\alpha\,m_N(\Lambda)\Big(1-\frac{m_N(\Lambda)}{\gamma}\Big)\le\Lambda m_N'(\Lambda)\le m_N(\Lambda),\qquad \frac1m\le\frac{\Lambda_N'(m)}{\Lambda_N(m)}\le\frac1{c_\alpha}\Big(\frac1m+\frac1{\gamma-m}\Big). \]
Consequently \(\vartheta_N(m)\le C\big(1+m^{-1}+(\gamma-m)^{-1}\big)\), and \(E(m)\vartheta_N(m)\ge1\).
Proof.
Write \(n\defeq(c_N\Lambda)^{1/\alpha}\) and \(u_j\defeq(n/j)^\alpha=\Lambda\mu_j\), so that
\[ Bm_N(\Lambda)=\sum_j\frac{u_j}{1+u_j},\qquad B\Lambda m_N'(\Lambda)=\sum_j\frac{u_j}{(1+u_j)^2}. \]
The upper bound holds term by term. For the lower bound put
\[ S\defeq\sum_j\frac{u_j}{1+u_j}=Bm_N,\qquad T\defeq\sum_j\frac1{1+u_j}=N-S,\qquad F\defeq\sum_j\frac{u_j}{(1+u_j)^2}=B\Lambda m_N', \]
so that \(ST/N=Bm_N(1-m_N/\gamma)\) and the lower bound reads \(c_\alpha ST/N\le F\). Let \(K\defeq\#\{j:u_j>1\}\). Since \(u_j\) decreases in \(j\), these are the indices \(j\le K\). A term with \(u_j\le1\) has \(u_j/(1+u_j)\le2u_j/(1+u_j)^2\), and a term with \(u_j\ge1\) has \(1/(1+u_j)\le2u_j/(1+u_j)^2\). Bounding the remaining terms by one gives
\[ S\le2F+K,\qquad T\le2F+N-K . \]
Since \(S,T\le N\), it follows that \(ST/N\le\min(S,T)\le2F+\min(K,N-K)\).
A window count bounds \(\min(K,N-K)\) by \(F\). Put \(\delta\defeq2^\alpha/(1+2^\alpha)^2\). For \(2^{-\alpha}\le u\le2^\alpha\),
\[ \frac u{(1+u)^2}-\delta=\frac{(2^\alpha-u)(2^\alpha u-1)}{(1+u)^2(1+2^\alpha)^2}\ge0, \]
so \(F\ge\delta\,\#\{j:2^{-\alpha}\le u_j\le2^\alpha\}\). If \(2K\le N\), then \(u_{K+1}\le1\) gives \(n\le K+1\). The at least \(K/2\) indices \((K+1)/2\le j\le K\) then have \(1<u_j\le((K+1)/j)^\alpha\le2^\alpha\), so \(K\le2F/\delta\). If \(2K>N\), then \(u_K>1\) gives \(n>K\). The \(N-K\) indices \(K<j\le N\) then have \(2^{-\alpha}<(K/j)^\alpha<u_j\le1\), because \(j\le N<2K\), so \(N-K\le F/\delta\). In both cases \(\min(K,N-K)\le2F/\delta\), and therefore
\[ \frac{ST}N\le\Big(2+\frac2\delta\Big)F . \]
This is the lower bound with \(c_\alpha\defeq\delta/(2\delta+2)\).
Inverting gives the second display, whose lower bound is the monotonicity of \(\Lambda_N(m)/m\) shown in the proof of Proposition SG.1; with \(1/(m(1-m/\gamma))=1/m+1/(\gamma-m)\). The bound on \(\vartheta_N\) follows with \(E(m)\asymp\min(m,1)\) (Lemma SG.1(iii)), and \(E\vartheta_N\ge1\) because \(\Lambda_N'/\Lambda_N\ge1/m\).
□
