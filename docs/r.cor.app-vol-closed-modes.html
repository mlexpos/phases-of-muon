Corollary SD.3 (Mode volatility)
Let \(\ridge>0\), and let \(\mu,\lambda\) be eigenvalues of \(\Sigmaout,\Sigmain\).
  1. (i)
    For all \(\ell\ge0\) and \(s,u>0\), \(\mathfrak h_\ell(\mu,\lambda;s,u)\ge0\) and
    \[ \mathfrak k_\ell[P_\mu,P'_\lambda](s,u)=\frac{r_\mu t_\lambda}{B^2}\,\mathfrak h_\ell(\mu,\lambda;s,u),\qquad \overline C_\ell[P_\mu,P'_\lambda](\ridge,\ridge)=\frac{r_\mu t_\lambda}{B^2}\,\mathfrak c_\ell(\mu,\lambda;\ridge). \]
  2. (ii)
    For all \(\ell\ge0\),
    \[ 0\le\mathfrak c_\ell(\mu,\lambda;\ridge)\le\frac{\mu\lambda\,\sw2\,(1+\kappa_\Sigma/\ridge)^2}{\ridge}\,\bmom^\ell . \]
  3. (iii)
    The mode coefficient (SD.80) is, for every \(i,j\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SD.63} \begin{gathered} \mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)=\frac1B\Big(\mathfrak c_0(\mu_i,\lambda_j;\ridge)+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\mathfrak c_\ell(\mu_i,\lambda_j;\ridge)\Big),\\ 0\le\mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)\le\frac{\mu_i\lambda_j\,\sw2\,(1+\kappa_\Sigma/\ridge)^2}{B\,\ridge}\cdot\frac{1+\bmom}{1-\bmom}, \end{gathered} \end{equation}
    the series converging absolutely with nonnegative terms.
Proof of Corollary SD.3.
For (i),
\[ \mathcal T^{\mathrm{out}}[P_\mu]=\frac{r_\mu}B\,\frac{\mu}{(s+\tilde s_1\mu)(u+\tilde s_1'\mu)},\qquad \mathcal T^{\mathrm{in}}[P'_\lambda]=\frac{t_\lambda}B\,\frac{\lambda}{(s+s_1\lambda)(u+s_1'\lambda)} . \]
Inserting these into (SD.58) gives the factorization of the kernel. Then \(\mathfrak h_\ell\ge0\) because \(b_\ell\ge0\) and \(\Delta_\ell>0\). The factorization of \(\overline C_\ell[P_\mu,P'_\lambda](\ridge,\ridge)\) follows from Theorem SD.6 and the definition (SD.62).
For (ii), every denominator factor in (SD.61) other than \(\Delta_\ell\) is at least \(s\) or \(u\), so
\[ \mathfrak h_\ell(\mu,\lambda;s,u)\le\frac{\mu\lambda\,b_\ell}{su\,\Delta_\ell}\le\frac{\mu\lambda\,\sw2\,\bmom^\ell}{su\,\theta_\Sigma(\min(s,u))^2}, \]
with \(\min(s,u)\ge\ridge\) at \((\ridge+x^2,\ridge+y^2)\). Integrating as in the previous proof gives the bound.
For (iii), at equal ridges (SD.54) reads \(\overline\Gamma[P_\mu,P'_\lambda](\ridge,\ridge)=\sum_\ell c_\ell\,\overline C_\ell[P_\mu,P'_\lambda](\ridge,\ridge)\), which converges absolutely by (ii). By (SD.78) and (SD.80) and part (i),
\[ \mathfrak v_{ij}(\ridge)=\frac{B\,\overline\Gamma[P_{\mu_i},P'_{\lambda_j}](\ridge,\ridge)}{r_{\mu_i}t_{\lambda_j}} =\frac B{r_{\mu_i}t_{\lambda_j}}\cdot\frac{r_{\mu_i}t_{\lambda_j}}{B^2}\sum_{\ell\ge0}c_\ell\,\mathfrak c_\ell(\mu_i,\lambda_j;\ridge), \]
which is (SD.63). The bound follows from (ii) and \(\sum_\ell c_\ell\bmom^\ell=\frac{1+\bmom}{1-\bmom}\).
□
