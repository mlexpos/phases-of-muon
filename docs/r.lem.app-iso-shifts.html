Lemma SF.14 (Shifts of the participation level)
Let \(Q,y,z>0\) and let \(k\ge0\) be an integer.
  1. (i)
    \(\displaystyle r(\bmom^{-2k}Q)-r(Q)=\sum_{i=1}^k\Phi\big((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{-2i}Q\big)\in[0,k]\).
  2. (ii)
    \(r(\bmom^{-2k}Q)\ge\mathsf n_\bmom(Q)+k-\mathsf c_\bmom\).
  3. (iii)
    \(r(Q)\le\mathsf n_\bmom(Q)+\mathsf c'_\bmom+\mathsf n^-_\bmom(Q)\).
  4. (iv)
    \(\abs{r(y)-r(z)}\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\,\abs{\log(y/z)}\).
  5. (v)
    If \(\gamma\ge\gamma^\circ_\bmom\) and \(r(\bmom^{-2}z)<\gamma\), then \(\mathsf A(z)>\Phi(1)\).
Proof.
For (i), reindexing the sum of Lemma SF.4(i) gives
\[ r(\bmom^{-2k}Q)=\sum_{j\ge0}\Phi\big((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{2(j-k)}Q\big), \]
and the \(k\) terms with \(j<k\) are the extra terms. Each lies in \([0,1]\).
For (ii), suppose first that \((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{-2k}Q\ge1\). Then Lemma SF.6(i) at \(\bmom^{-2k}Q\) gives
\[ r(\bmom^{-2k}Q)>\mathsf n_\bmom(\bmom^{-2k}Q)-\mathsf c_\bmom=\mathsf n_\bmom(Q)+k-\mathsf c_\bmom . \]
Otherwise \(\mathsf n_\bmom(Q)+k=\mathsf n_\bmom(\bmom^{-2k}Q)<0\le r(\bmom^{-2k}Q)\).
For (iii), if \((1-\bmom)^2Q\ge1\) the bound is the upper bound of Lemma SF.6(i), with \(\mathsf n^-_\bmom(Q)=0\). Otherwise \(\mathsf n_\bmom(Q)+\mathsf n^-_\bmom(Q)=0\), and \(r(Q)\le r((1-\bmom)^{-2})\le\mathsf c'_\bmom\) by the same lemma.
Part (iv) holds because \(\dif r/\dif\log y=\mathsf e_\bmom(y)\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) by Lemma SF.13.
For (v), part (i) with \(k=1\) gives
\[ \mathsf A(z)=\gamma-r(z)>r(\bmom^{-2}z)-r(z)=\Phi\big((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{-2}z\big). \]
If \((1-\bmom)^2\bmom^{-2}z\ge1\), the right side is at least \(\Phi(1)\), because \(\Phi\) is increasing. Otherwise \(z<(1-\bmom)^{-2}\), so that \(r(z)\le\mathsf c'_\bmom\) by Lemma SF.6(i), and \(\mathsf A(z)\ge\gamma-\mathsf c'_\bmom\ge1>\Phi(1)\).
□
