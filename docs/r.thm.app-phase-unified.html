Theorem SI.14 (Unified clock)
Fix \(\alpha>0\), \(\texp\) with \(\alpha+\texp>1\), \(T\ge4\) and \(j\in\{\text{s-SVD},\text{s-SGD}\}\).
  1. (i)
    There are \(N_0\), \(\epsilon_0>0\) and \(c_->0\) such that for all \(N\ge N_0\), \(B\ge N\) and \(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0\) the first hit \(\hat u_j\) is attained and \(\mathsf I_j\ge c_-\Upsilon_j(N,\epsilon)\).
  2. (ii)
    If \((\alpha,\texp,j)\in\mathcal U\), there are \(N_0\), \(\epsilon_0>0\) and \(c_+\) such that for all \(N\ge N_0\), \(B\ge N\) and \(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0\), \(\mathsf I_j\le c_+\Upsilon_j(N,\epsilon)\).
In particular, for \((\alpha,\texp,j)\in\mathcal U\), \(t^j_{T\epsilon}\asymp\mathcal S_jT_0^j\,\epsilon^{-1/2}\,\Upsilon_j(N,\epsilon)\) for all \(N\ge N_0\), \(B\ge N\) and \(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0\).
Proof of Theorem SI.14(i).
Write \(q=q_j\), \(r=r_j\), \(f=f_{N,q}\) and \(A=A_p\), and let \(\epsilon<\epsilon_0(p,q,T)\). Attainment is (SI.59), since \(\epsilon_0\le1/(8T)\). Since \(Y_j\ge f\), the first hit is not before \(\inf\{u:\ f(u)\le T\epsilon\}\), so \(\mathsf I_j\) is at least the integral of \(\sqrt f\) up to that time. For \(N\ge4\) the lower envelope of (SI.23) gives \(f>T\epsilon\) on \([0,m)\), where
\[ m\defeq\min\{\sigma_*,V\},\qquad\sigma_*\defeq\Big(\frac A{T\epsilon}\Big)^{1/(2r)}-1,\qquad V\defeq(N/4)^q , \]
so \(\mathsf I_j\ge\sqrt A\,I_r(m)\).
If \(r<1\): the last entry of \(\epsilon_0\) gives \((1+\sigma_*)^{1-r}\ge2\), and for \((N/4)^{q(1-r)}\ge2\) also \(V^{1-r}\ge2\). Hence
\[ \mathsf I_j\ge\frac{\sqrt A}{2(1-r)}\min\Big\{\Big(\frac AT\Big)^{(1-r)/(2r)}\epsilon^{-(1-r)/(2r)},\ 4^{-q(1-r)}N^{q(1-r)}\Big\} . \]
If \(r=1\): \(\epsilon<(A/T)^2\) gives \(\log(1+\sigma_*)=\frac12\log\frac A{T\epsilon}\ge\frac14\log\frac1\epsilon\), and \(N\ge16\) gives \(\log(1+V)\ge\frac q2\log N\). If \(r>1\): \(f(u)\ge\frac12e^{-2u}\), so the first hit is at least \(\frac12\log\frac1{2T\epsilon}>\log2\), and
\[ \mathsf I_j\ge\int_0^{\log2}\frac{e^{-u}}{\sqrt2}\,\dif u=\frac1{2\sqrt2} . \]
In each case \(\mathsf I_j\ge c_-\Upsilon_j(N,\epsilon)\).
□
Proof of Theorem SI.14(ii) when \(r_j<1\).
Use the phase-one data \((D,\tau,N^\circ)\) and \(u_0=\tau N^q\), with \(q=q_j\), \(r=r_j\), \(C=C_{p,q}\) and \(h=C(1+u/D)^{-r}\). Put
\[ E_*\defeq\Big(\frac{DC^{1/r}}\tau\Big)^{2r},\qquad\Pi\defeq\epsilon^{-(1-r)/(2r)} . \]
By (SI.40), \(N^{q(1-r)}=x^{(1-r)/(2r)}\,\Pi\), which separates two cases.
Window \(x\ge E_*\). Let \(H\defeq D[(C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r}-1]\), which is positive for \(\epsilon<C^2\). Since \(\epsilon^{-1/(2r)}=x^{-1/(2r)}N^q\),
\[ H\le DC^{1/r}\epsilon^{-1/(2r)}=DC^{1/r}x^{-1/(2r)}N^q\le\tau N^q=u_0 , \]
and \(h(H)=\sqrt\epsilon\). Lemma SI.9, under its hypothesis (a) on \([0,H]\), gives \(\hat u_j\le H\) and \(Y_j\le(h+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\) there, so
\[ \mathsf I_j\le\int_0^H\big(h+\sqrt\epsilon\big)\,\dif u\le DC^{1/r}\Big[\frac1{1-r}+1\Big]\Pi . \]
Since \(x\ge E_*\), \(N^{q(1-r)}\ge E_*^{(1-r)/(2r)}\Pi\), and therefore \(\Upsilon_j\ge\min\{1,E_*^{(1-r)/(2r)}\}\,\Pi\).
Band \(x<E_*\). The bound (SI.64) was derived for every \(x>0\), and \(a_1(x)\), \(a_2(x)\) and \(\tilde{\mathsf d}/N^{\mathsf e_r}\) are bounded on \((0,E_*]\). With Proposition SI.8(i) this gives \(\mathsf I_j\le c(E_*)N^{q(1-r)}\). Here \(N^{q(1-r)}<E_*^{(1-r)/(2r)}\Pi\), so \(\Upsilon_j\ge\min\{1,E_*^{-(1-r)/(2r)}\}\,N^{q(1-r)}\).

Proof of Theorem SI.14(ii) for the templated models with \(r_j\ge1\).
These are SignSVD for \(0<\alpha<1\) and \(1<\alpha<2\), and SignSGD for \(\alpha\in(0,\frac12)\cup(\frac12,1)\cup(1,\infty)\), with the constants of Lemma SI.25; put \(\upsilon\defeq2\) if \(K_f=0\). Write \(q=q_j\), \(r=r_j\), \(C=C_{p,q}\), \(\mathsf y=\mathsf y_j\), \(J_N=J_N(p,q)\) and \(\mathsf I=\mathsf I_j\).
Set-up. Let \(r_0\defeq1\) if \(r=1\) and \(r_0\defeq\min\{r,(1+\upsilon)/2\}\) if \(r>1\), so that \(r_0\le r\) and \(r_0\le\upsilon\). Let \(\delta_\circ\defeq1-(2/3)^{1/r_0}\), \(\delta_w\defeq1-(2/3)^{2/\mathsf b}\),
\[ \begin{gathered} D\defeq\max\Big\{1,\big[4K_f\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon}b(r_0,\upsilon)\big]^{\frac1{\upsilon-1}}, \big[4K_f\delta_w^{-\upsilon}(1-\tfrac{\mathsf b}2)^{-1}\big]^{\frac1{\upsilon-1}}\Big\},\\ \tau_w\defeq\min\Big\{\frac12,\Big(\frac{(1-\mathsf b/2)\delta_w^{\mathsf b}}{4K_s}\Big)^{\frac1{1-\mathsf b}}\Big\}, \end{gathered} \]
\(u_0\defeq\tau_wN^q-D\), which is positive and at most \(N^q-1\) for large \(N\), and \(h\defeq C(1+u/D)^{-r_0}\). Put \(\mathsf L\defeq1/(r_0-1)\) if \(r>1\) and \(\mathsf L\defeq\Lambda_0\defeq\log(1+u_0/D)\) if \(r=1\), \(A_e\defeq K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}CD\mathsf L\), and
\[ w(u)\defeq\epsilon^{1/4}A_e^{1/2}N^{-q/2}\Big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b/2},\qquad \frac{w(u)}{\sqrt\epsilon}=A_e^{1/2}\,\mathsf y^{-1/4}\Big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b/2}. \]
Hypotheses of Lemma SI.22. The upper envelope of (SI.23), \(D\ge1\) and \(r_0\le r\) give \(f\le h^2\). Lemma SI.23 for \(h\) has \(\mathsf k_f\le\frac14\). Its remainder is at most
\[ K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}N^{-q}\Big(\frac{D+u}{N^q}\Big)^{-\mathsf b}CD\mathsf L=\frac{w^2}{\sqrt\epsilon}\qquad\text{on }[0,u_0], \]
because \(I_{r_0}\le1/(r_0-1)\) for \(r_0>1\) and \(I_1(u/D)\le\Lambda_0\); so \(g*h\le2h+w^2/\sqrt\epsilon\). Lemma SI.23 for \(w\), an envelope of the same form with exponent \(\mathsf b/2<1\), gives on \([0,u_0]\)
\[ g*w\le\frac74w+\frac{K_s\delta_w^{-\mathsf b}}{1-\mathsf b/2}\,\tau_w^{1-\mathsf b}\,w\le2w , \]
since \(D+u\le\tau_wN^q\) there.
Elementary integrals and levels. For \(0\le z\le u_0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.65} \int_0^zw\,\dif u\le\frac{A_e^{1/2}\mathsf y^{1/4}}{1-\mathsf b/2}\Big(\frac{D+z}{N^q}\Big)^{1-\mathsf b/2},\qquad \sqrt\epsilon\,z\le\sqrt{\mathsf y}\,\frac{D+z}{N^q} . \end{equation}
Further, \(h(u)\le\sqrt\epsilon/2\) for \(u\ge H_h\defeq D[(2C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r_0}-1]\), and \(w(u)\le\sqrt\epsilon/2\) once \(D+u\ge N^q(4A_e)^{1/\mathsf b}\mathsf y^{-1/(2\mathsf b)}\); let \(H_w\ge0\) be the least such \(u\) and \(H\defeq\max\{H_h,H_w\}\). If \(H\le u_0\), Lemma SI.22(a),(b) give \(\hat u\le H\) and \(\mathsf I\le\int_0^H(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)\). In every case Lemma SI.22(c) and \(\int_{u_0}^\infty\sqrt f\le J_N\) give
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.66} \mathsf I\le\int_0^{u_0}\big(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon\big)\,\dif u+\big(\tilde{\mathsf J}^{1/2}+\tilde{\mathsf d}^{1/2}\big)^2,\qquad \tilde{\mathsf J}\le J_N+\sqrt{\mathsf y}\,\Theta_0,\qquad\tilde{\mathsf d}\le2\sqrt{\mathsf y}\,\log\Theta_0 . \end{equation}
Saturated case \(r>1\). Here \(J_N\le C/(r-1)\), \(\int_0^\infty h=CD/(r_0-1)\) and \(\Upsilon_j=1\). Let
\[ Y_*\defeq\max\Big\{1,\ (2C)^2\Big(\frac D{\tau_w}\Big)^{2r_0},\ (4A_e)^2\tau_w^{-2\mathsf b}\Big\} . \]
(a) Suppose \(\mathsf y\ge Y_*\). Since \(r_0>1\) and \(\epsilon=\mathsf yN^{-2q}\),
\[ D+H_h=D(2C)^{1/r_0}\mathsf y^{-1/(2r_0)}N^{q/r_0}\le D(2C)^{1/r_0}\mathsf y^{-1/(2r_0)}N^q\le\tau_wN^q , \]
and similarly \(D+H_w\le\tau_wN^q\) when \(H_w>0\); so \(H\le u_0\). Then \(\sqrt\epsilon\,H_h\le D(2C)^{1/r_0}\) because \(\epsilon\le1\), and \(\sqrt\epsilon\,H_w\le(4A_e)^{1/\mathsf b}\mathsf y^{-(1-\mathsf b)/(2\mathsf b)}\le(4A_e)^{1/\mathsf b}\). By (SI.65), \(\int_0^{H_w}w\le c\,A_e^{1/\mathsf b}\mathsf y^{-(1-\mathsf b)/(2\mathsf b)}\le c\), and
\[ \int_0^{H_h}w\,\dif u\le c\,\mathsf y^{\eta}N^{-q(1-1/r_0)(1-\mathsf b/2)},\qquad\eta\defeq\frac14-\frac{1-\mathsf b/2}{2r_0} . \]
If \(\eta\le0\) this is at most \(c\) because \(\mathsf y\ge1\). If \(\eta>0\), the bound \(\mathsf y\le N^{2q}\) bounds it by \(cN^{2q\eta-q(1-1/r_0)(1-\mathsf b/2)}=cN^{-q(1-\mathsf b)/2}\le c\).
(b) Suppose \(\mathsf y\le N^{-q(r_0-1)}\). Then (SI.59) applies directly, with
\[ \mathsf d=\sqrt{\mathsf y}\Big[\log F_0+2q\log N+\log\frac1{\mathsf y}\Big]\le N^{-q(r_0-1)/2}\Big(\Big|\log\frac{\zeta(p)}2\Big|+2q\log N\Big)+\frac2e\le c , \]
so \(\mathsf I\le(\sqrt{C/(r-1)}+\sqrt c)^2\).
(c) Suppose \(N^{-q(r_0-1)}<\mathsf y<Y_*\). Now
\[ \frac{h(u_0)}{\sqrt\epsilon}=CD^{r_0}\tau_w^{-r_0}\,\mathsf y^{-1/2}N^{-q(r_0-1)}\le CD^{r_0}\tau_w^{-r_0}N^{-q(r_0-1)/2}\le1 \]
for large \(N\), and \(w(u_0)/\sqrt\epsilon=A_e^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}\mathsf y^{-1/4}\), so \(\Theta_0\le2+A_e^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}\mathsf y^{-1/4}\). By (SI.65) the first phase contributes at most \(CD/(r_0-1)+cY_*^{1/4}+\tau_wY_*^{1/2}\). In (SI.66), \(\log(2+t)\le\log3+t\) gives
\[ \tilde{\mathsf d}\le2\sqrt{Y_*}\log3+2A_e^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}Y_*^{1/4},\qquad \tilde{\mathsf J}\le\frac C{r-1}+2\sqrt{Y_*}+A_e^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}Y_*^{1/4} : \]
the factor \(\mathsf y^{-1/4}\) in \(\Theta_0\) always meets a factor \(\sqrt{\mathsf y}\). In all three cases \(\mathsf I\) is bounded by a constant \(c_+\).
Critical case \(r=1\). Here \(r_0=1\), \(p-1=2q\), \(A_e=c_e\Lambda_0\) with \(c_e\defeq K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}CD\), \(\Lambda_0\le q\log N+c\), and \(\Upsilon_j\le\log N\). Let
\[ Y_c\defeq\max\Big\{\Big(\frac{2CD}{\tau_w}\Big)^2,\ (4c_e\Lambda_0)^2\tau_w^{-2\mathsf b}\Big\}, \]
so that \(Y_c^{1/2}\le c\Lambda_0\) for large \(N\).
(a) Suppose \(\mathsf y<1\). Then \(\log(1/\epsilon)>2q\log N\), so \(\Upsilon_j\ge\min\{1,2q\}\log N\). By (SI.59), \(\mathsf d\le|\log(\zeta(p)/2)|+2q\log N+2/e\), and \(J_N\le\sqrt{q/8}\log N+c\) by Lemma SI.12(i). So \(\mathsf I\le2J_N+2\mathsf d\le c\log N\).
(b) Suppose \(1\le\mathsf y<Y_c\). Then \(\log(1/\epsilon)=2q\log N-\log\mathsf y\ge q\log N\) for large \(N\), so \(\Upsilon_j\ge\min\{1,q\}\log N\). The first phase contributes
\[ \int_0^{u_0}\big(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon\big)\,\dif u\le CD\Lambda_0+c\,(c_e\Lambda_0)^{1/2}\mathsf y^{1/4}+\tau_w\mathsf y^{1/2}\le c\log N . \]
For the restart, \(h(u_0)/\sqrt\epsilon=(CD/\tau_w)\mathsf y^{-1/2}\le CD/\tau_w\), so
\[ \Theta_0\le1+CD/\tau_w+(c_e\Lambda_0)^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}\mathsf y^{-1/4}. \]
With \(\log(a+t)\le\log a+t\) for \(a\ge1\),
\[ \tilde{\mathsf d}\le2\sqrt{\mathsf y}\log\Big(1+\frac{CD}{\tau_w}\Big)+2(c_e\Lambda_0)^{1/2}\tau_w^{-\mathsf b/2}\mathsf y^{1/4}\le c\log N , \]
and \(\tilde{\mathsf J}\le J_N+c\log N\). Hence \(\mathsf I\le c\log N\).
(c) Suppose \(\mathsf y\ge Y_c\). Then \(H_h,H_w\le u_0\) exactly as in the saturated case (a), so \(\hat u\le H\) and \(\mathsf I\le\int_0^H(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)\). Now \(\int_0^Hh=CD\log(1+H/D)\), with \(\log(1+H_h/D)=\log(2C)+\frac12\log(1/\epsilon)\) and, writing \(\mathsf y=\epsilon N^{2q}\),
\[ \log(1+H_w/D)\le\log2+\frac1{2\mathsf b}\log\frac1\epsilon +\Big[\frac1{\mathsf b}\log(4c_e\Lambda_0)-\frac{q(1-\mathsf b)}{\mathsf b}\log N-\log D\Big]_+ , \]
whose bracket vanishes for large \(N\). Also \(\sqrt\epsilon\,H_h\le2CD\),
\[ \begin{gathered} \sqrt\epsilon\,H_w+\int_0^{H_w}w\le c\,(c_e\Lambda_0)^{1/\mathsf b}\mathsf y^{-(1-\mathsf b)/(2\mathsf b)},\\ \int_0^{H_h}w\le c\,(c_e\Lambda_0)^{1/2}\mathsf y^{-(1-\mathsf b)/4}. \end{gathered} \]
If \(\mathsf y\le N^q\), then \(\log(1/\epsilon)\ge q\log N\ge c\Lambda_0\), and \(\mathsf y\ge Y_c\) bounds the last two terms by \(c\Lambda_0\) and \(c\Lambda_0^{1/2}\). If \(\mathsf y>N^q\), they are at most \(c(\log N)^{1/\mathsf b}N^{-q(1-\mathsf b)/(2\mathsf b)}+c(\log N)^{1/2}N^{-q(1-\mathsf b)/4}\le c\). In both cases \(\mathsf I\le c\log(1/\epsilon)+c\le c'\log(1/\epsilon)\), because \(\log(1/\epsilon)\ge\log(1/\epsilon_0)>0\). Finally \(\mathsf y\ge1\) gives \(\log(1/\epsilon)\le2q\log N\), so \(\mathsf I\le c''\Upsilon_j\).

Proof of Theorem SI.14(ii) for SignSVD at \(\alpha\ge2\), \(\texp\ge1\).
Write \(q=\alpha/2\), \(s=r_{\text{s-SVD}}\ge1\), \(C=C_{p,q}\), \(g=g_{\text{s-SVD}}\), \(\mathsf y=\mathsf y_{\text{s-SVD}}\) and \(\mathsf I=\mathsf I_{\text{s-SVD}}\).
Case \(\alpha>2\). By Lemma SI.7(i) and \(H_N(\alpha/2)\ge1\), \(g(v)\le2E_{\alpha,\alpha/2}(1+v)^{-\upsilon_{\rm V}}\) for all \(v\ge0\), with \(\upsilon_{\rm V}=2-\frac2\alpha>1\). Take \(r_0=1\) if \(s=1\) and \(r_0=\min\{s,(1+\upsilon_{\rm V})/2\}\) if \(s>1\), and \(D=D(2E_{\alpha,\alpha/2},r_0,\upsilon_{\rm V})\) of (SI.55). Lemma SI.14 gives \(g*h\le2h\) on \([0,\infty)\) for \(h=C(1+u/D)^{-r_0}\). Lemma SI.9(i),(ii), under its hypothesis (a) at \(H=D[(C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r_0}-1]\), give \(\hat u\le H\) and
\[ \mathsf I\le\int_0^H\big(h+\sqrt\epsilon\big)\,\dif u=CD\,I_{r_0}(H/D)+\sqrt\epsilon\,H . \]
For \(s>1\) this is at most \(CD/(r_0-1)+DC^{1/r_0}\). For \(s=1\) it is at most \(CD\log(C/\sqrt\epsilon)+DC\). For \(s=1\) and \(\mathsf y\ge1\), \(\log(1/\epsilon)\le\alpha\log N\), so \(\mathsf I\le c\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}\); for \(s=1\) and \(\mathsf y<1\), case (a) of the critical case above applies verbatim, since it uses only (SI.59) and Lemma SI.12(i).
Case \(\alpha=2\), \(\texp>1\). By Lemma SI.18, for \(N\ge e^{L_0}\) and \(\epsilon<\epsilon_0\) the hit is attained and the noise error \(\mathcal E\) there is bounded by a constant, since each of its five terms is. Hence \(\mathsf I\le J_N(p,1)+\mathcal E\le J(p,1)+c\).
Case \(\alpha=2\), \(\texp=1\). Here \(q=1\); take \(D=1\) and \(h=C/(1+u)\). Lemma SI.17(iii) gives, for \(N\ge e^{64}\),
\[ g*h\le\frac{2\log(1+u)+64}{H_N(1)}\,h\le2h\qquad\text{on }[0,u_0],\qquad u_0\defeq e^{-32}(N+1)-1, \]
because \(H_N(1)\ge\log(N+1)\ge\log(1+u)+32\) there. If \(\mathsf y<1\), case (a) of the critical case applies. If \(\mathsf y\ge1\) and \(H\defeq C/\sqrt\epsilon-1\le u_0\), Lemma SI.9 gives \(\hat u\le H\) and
\[ \mathsf I\le C\log(C/\sqrt\epsilon)+C\le c\log(1/\epsilon)\le c\,\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}} . \]
If \(\mathsf y\ge1\) and \(H>u_0\), then \(C/\sqrt\epsilon>e^{-32}N\), so \(\mathsf y<C^2e^{64}\) and \(\log(1/\epsilon)\ge2\log N-c\). The first phase contributes at most \(C\log(N+1)+2e^{-32}\sqrt{\mathsf y}\), and \(\Theta_0\le1+Ce^{32}\mathsf y^{-1/2}\le1+Ce^{32}\) in Lemma SI.22(c), with \(w=0\), gives \(\tilde{\mathsf d}\le c\) and \(\tilde{\mathsf J}\le J_N+c\). By Lemma SI.12(i), \(\mathsf I\le c\log N\le c'\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}\).
