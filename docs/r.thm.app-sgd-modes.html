Theorem SJ.4 (Corrected row volatility)
In the Haar model of Definition SJ.3, define the raw annealed row volatility of the update and the corrected array
\begin{align} \volraw_i&\defeq\EE\norm{S^\T u_i}^2,\tag{SJ.43}\\ \mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}&\defeq\Nin\Big(1-\frac2\pi\Big)+\frac2\pi\,\Nout\Big[\frac{(\Nin+2)\,\mu_i}{\Tr\Lambda}+\frac{(B+2\Nin+3)\,(T_D)_{ii}}{2\cR\,\Tr\Lambda}\Big].\tag{SJ.44} \end{align}
There is an admissible constant \(C_{\rm row}\) such that, for every \(i\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.45} \abs{\volraw_i-\mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}}\le C_{\rm row}\,\frac{\Nin}{\sqrt k},\qquad \Big|\frac{\volraw_i}{\mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}}-1\Big|\le\frac{C_{\rm row}}{(1-2/\pi)\sqrt k}. \end{equation}
The actual total is exact, \(\sum_i\volraw_i=\Nout\Nin\), while
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.46} \sum_i\mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}-\Nout\Nin=\frac2\pi\,\Nout\Big[2+\frac{(B+2\Nin+3)\Tr T_D}{2\cR\,\Tr\Lambda}\Big]\in\Big(0,\ \frac2\pi\Nout\big[2+(3K+3)L/c\big]\Big]. \end{equation}
The corrected array exceeds the main text’s array row by row:
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.47} \mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}-\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=\frac2\pi\,\Nout\Big[\frac{2\mu_i}{\Tr\Sigmaout}+\frac{(B+2\Nin+3)\,(\Lambda DD^\T\Lambda)_{ii}}{2\cR\,\Tr\Sigmaout}\Big]\ge0 . \end{equation}
Proof.
The row volatility. By Lemma SJ.11,
\[ \volraw_i=\Nin\Big(1-\frac2\pi\Big)+\frac2\pi\,\Nout\sum_{j=1}^{\Nin}\EE\frac{(h_j)_i^2}{\norm{h_j}^2}. \]
Replacing each \(\norm{h_j}^2\) by \(x_0\) costs, by Lemma SJ.13 and \(\Nout\le Kk\), at most
\[ \frac2\pi\,\Nout\Nin\Big(\frac{C_{\rm good}}{k^{3/2}}+\frac{C_{\rm bad}}{k^2}\Big)\le C_{\rm row}\frac{\Nin}{\sqrt k},\qquad C_{\rm row}\defeq\frac2\pi K(C_{\rm good}+C_{\rm bad}). \]
The replaced sum is \(\sum_j\EE(h_j)_i^2/x_0=(\EE HH^\T)_{ii}/x_0\), and (SJ.34) with \(x_0=2\cR\Tr\Lambda/B\) identifies it:
\[ \frac{(\EE HH^\T)_{ii}}{x_0}=\frac{(\Nin+2)\mu_i}{\Tr\Lambda}+\frac{(B+2\Nin+3)(T_D)_{ii}}{2\cR\Tr\Lambda}. \]
This proves the first bound in (SJ.45). The relative bound follows from \(\mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}\ge\Nin(1-2/\pi)\).
Totals. By Lemma SJ.11, \(\norm S_\F^2=\Nout\Nin\), and \(\sum_i\norm{S^\T u_i}^2=\norm S_\F^2\) by orthogonality. Summing (SJ.44) over \(i\) gives the identity in (SJ.46). Its bracket is positive, and \(\Tr T_D\le2\cR L\), \(\Tr\Lambda\ge c\Nout\) and \(B+2\Nin+3\le(3K+3)\Nout\) give the upper bound.
The difference. The main text’s array is \(\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=\Nin(1-\frac2\pi)+\frac2\pi\Nout\Nin\mu_i/\Tr\Lambda\). Subtracting it from (SJ.44) gives (SJ.47), with \(\Tr\Sigmaout=\Tr\Lambda\) and \(T_D=\Lambda DD^\T\Lambda\). Both terms are nonnegative.
□
