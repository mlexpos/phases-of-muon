Lemma SG.11 (Head)
For every fixed \(M>0\) and all large \(N\), \(\sqrt2\,\mathfrak d_i^{\le M}\le C_M\sqrt{\mu_i/\gamma}\) for every \(i\). If a row sequence satisfies \(m_N(1/\mu_i)\to0\), then \(\sqrt2\,\mathfrak d_i^{\le M}\sim\sqrt{\mu_i/(\gamma\sw2)}\).
Proof.
In the variable \(u\) of (SG.12), the head is the part where the participation is at most \(M\), and there \(\Lw/\Fw\le C_M\) and, by (SG.6),
\[ c_M\gamma\le\tilde s_1=(\gamma-m)\Fw(Q_m)\le\sw2\gamma\qquad(\gamma>2M). \]
Hence the integrand is at most \(C_M\gamma\mu_i/(u^2+c_M\gamma\mu_i)\), whose integral is \(C_M'\sqrt{\gamma\mu_i}\), and the factor \(\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2/(\pi\gamma)\) gives the bound.
Assume now \(m_N(1/\mu_i)\to0\) and fix \(\delta\in(0,M)\). Then \(\Lambda_N(\delta)\mu_i\to\infty\): otherwise, along a subsequence \(\Lambda_N(\delta)\mu_i\le L_0\) with \(L_0\ge1\), and since \(m_N(\Lambda/L_0)\ge m_N(\Lambda)/L_0\) by the concavity of \(m_N\),
\[ m_N(1/\mu_i)\ge m_N\big(\Lambda_N(\delta)/L_0\big)\ge\delta/L_0 . \]
Consequently \(s_N(\delta)/(\gamma\mu_i)\le C_\delta/(\Lambda_N(\delta)\mu_i)\to0\). On the range \(m\le\delta\), which is \(u^2\ge s_N(\delta)\), Lemma SG.1(i) gives
\[ \frac{\tilde s_1}{\gamma\sw2}=1+O(\delta)+o(1),\qquad\frac{\Lw}{\Fw}=\frac{1+O(\delta)}{\sw2}, \]
so that part of the integral is
\[ \frac{1+O(\delta)}{\sw2}\int_{\sqrt{s_N(\delta)}}^\infty\frac{\gamma\sw2\mu_i(1+O(\delta))}{u^2+\gamma\sw2\mu_i(1+O(\delta))}\,\dif u =\frac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma\mu_i}{\sw2}}\,\big(1+O(\delta)+o(1)\big), \]
the missing interval \(u<\sqrt{s_N(\delta)}\) having relative size \(O(\sqrt{s_N(\delta)/(\gamma\mu_i)})=o(1)\). The range \(\delta<m\le M\) has \(u\le\sqrt{s_N(\delta)}\) and a bounded integrand, so it contributes \(o(\sqrt{\gamma\mu_i})\). Multiplying by \(2/(\pi\gamma)\) and letting \(\delta\downarrow0\) proves the claim.
□
