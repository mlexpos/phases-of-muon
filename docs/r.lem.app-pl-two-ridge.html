Lemma SG.3 (Two ridges and the lag envelope)
For \(0<\eta\le\ridge\) and every \(\ell\ge0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.18} \abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\eta)-\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le2\sqrt{1-\volraw_i(\ridge)},\qquad \abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le1, \end{equation}
and for every \(\ell\ge1\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.19} \abs{\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)}\le\frac{A_N\,\bmom^\ell}{\sqrt\ridge}. \end{equation}
Proof.
All three bounds are read from the independent model along the replications. At the \(n\)-fold replication of Definition SD.3, let \(M^{\mathrm g}_t\) be the buffer of the independent model of Definition SD.2, and for the \(n\) copies \(u_i\tensor e_k\) of the row \(u_i\) put
\[ a_k(\ridge)\defeq\Qridge\ridge(M^{\mathrm g}_t)^\T(u_i\tensor e_k),\qquad b_k(\ridge)\defeq\Qridge\ridge(M^{\mathrm g}_{t+\ell})^\T(u_i\tensor e_k),\]
\[ \mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge')\defeq\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^n\EE\langle a_k(\ridge),b_k(\ridge')\rangle . \]
The independent updates have mean zero, so \(\mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge')\) is \(B\) times the independent covariance \(C^{\mathrm g}_\ell[F,\Id]\) at order \(n\), for the replicated test \(F=(u_i\tensor u_i)\tensor\Id_n\) of norm one. By (SD.52) and the replication invariance of Theorem SD.3,
\[ \mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge)\longrightarrow\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge)\qquad(n\to\infty), \]
and at \(\ell=0\) the limit is \(\overline C_{i,0}(\ridge)=\volraw_i(\ridge)\).
Each row of a regularized update has norm at most one, because \(\Qridge\ridge(K)\Qridge\ridge(K)^\T\preceq\Id\); this gives the second bound of (SG.18). For the first, \(0\le\omega_\ridge\le\omega_\eta\le1\) on \([0,\infty)\) for the filters of (SD.2), so
\[ (\omega_\eta-\omega_\ridge)^2\le(1-\omega_\ridge)^2\le1-\omega_\ridge^2 , \]
and functional calculus gives \((\Qridge\eta-\Qridge\ridge)(\Qridge\eta-\Qridge\ridge)^\T(K)\preceq\Id-\Qridge\ridge(K)\Qridge\ridge(K)^\T\). Hence \(\EE\norm{a_k(\eta)-a_k(\ridge)}^2\le1-\EE\norm{a_k(\ridge)}^2\), and the same holds for \(b_k\), which has the same law. Writing \(\langle a(\eta),b(\eta)\rangle-\langle a(\ridge),b(\ridge)\rangle=\langle a(\eta)-a(\ridge),b(\eta)\rangle+\langle a(\ridge),b(\eta)-b(\ridge)\rangle\) and applying the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality to the average over \(k\),
\[ \abs{\mathsf a^{(n)}(\eta,\eta)-\mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge)}\le2\Big(1-\frac1n\sum_k\EE\norm{a_k(\ridge)}^2\Big)^{1/2}. \]
The average in the bracket is \(\mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge)\) at \(\ell=0\), which tends to \(\volraw_i(\ridge)\), and the first bound follows as \(n\to\infty\).
The envelope is the coupling of the proof of Theorem SD.2(ii). Let \(M^*\) be built from an independent past, so that \(M^*\) is independent of \(M^{\mathrm g}_t\), has the law of \(M^{\mathrm g}_{t+\ell}\), and \(M^{\mathrm g}_{t+\ell}-M^*=\bmom^\ell(M^{\mathrm g}_t-M'_t)\) with \(M'_t\) an independent copy of \(M^{\mathrm g}_t\). The term with \(M^*\) has zero mean, so the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality over the copies and (SA.13) give
\[ \abs{\mathsf a^{(n)}(\ridge,\ridge)}\le\frac1n\Big(n\,\EE\norm{\Qridge\ridge(M^{\mathrm g}_{t+\ell})-\Qridge\ridge(M^*)}_\F^2\Big)^{1/2} \le\frac{\bmom^\ell}{\sqrt{n\ridge}}\Big(\EE\norm{M^{\mathrm g}_t-M'_t}_\F^2\Big)^{1/2}. \]
At order \(n\) the last expectation is \(2\sw2(nB)^{-1}\Tr(\Sigmaout\tensor\Id_n)\,nN=2n\gamma\sw2\sum_j\mu_j=nA_N^2\), which gives (SG.19) in the limit.
□
