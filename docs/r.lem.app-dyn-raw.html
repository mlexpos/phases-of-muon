Lemma SE.11 (Gaussian response of the derivative functional)
Let \(\norm{\widehat A}_\F=1\), \(\delta=\norm{\widehat A}\), and let \(E\) be deterministic with \(C_E=\Sigmaout^{1/2}E\Sigmain^{1/2}\) and \(e=z^\T C_Ew\). For \(x,x'\ge0\), \(\abs{b(x)}\le2\), \(\abs{b(x)-b(x')}\le12\abs{x-x'}\) and \(\norm{N_x}\le24x\), and
\begin{equation}\tag{SE.23} \EE\big[e\,\psi_x'(d)\big]=\langle\widehat A,C_E\rangle\,b(x)+\big\langle C_E,\widehat AN_x\widehat A^\T\widehat A\big\rangle,\qquad \big|\big\langle C_E,\widehat AN_x\widehat A^\T\widehat A\big\rangle\big|\le24x\,\norm{C_E}\,\delta . \end{equation}
Proof.
The scalar bounds are read from
\[ \psi_x''(d)=\frac{2(1-3xd^2)}{(1+xd^2)^3},\qquad \partial_x\psi_x''(d)=-\frac{12d^2(1-xd^2)}{(1+xd^2)^4},\qquad \psi_x(d)=\frac1x\Big(1-\frac1{1+xd^2}\Big). \]
The first gives \(\abs{\psi_x''}\le2\); the second gives \(\abs{\partial_x\psi_x''(d)}\le12d^2\), hence the Lipschitz bound with \(\EE d^2=1\); and the third gives \(\psi_x''''(d)=-x\,\mathsf g''''(\sqrt x\,d)\) with \(\mathsf g(y)=(1+y^2)^{-1}\), whose fourth derivative is bounded by its value \(24\) at \(y=0\). The bound on \(N_x\) is the argument for the matrix \(N\) in the proof of Lemma SB.8.
The identity is (SB.22) with \(f=\psi_x'\), paired with \(C_E\), since \(\EE[e\,\psi_x'(d)]=\langle C_E,\EE[(z\tensor w)\psi_x'(d)]\rangle\). The bound on the second term is trace duality and the ideal property: \(\norm{\widehat AN_x\widehat A^\T\widehat A}_*\le\norm{\widehat A}\norm{N_x}\norm{\widehat A^\T\widehat A}_*=\delta\norm{N_x}\norm{\widehat A}_\F^2\).
□
