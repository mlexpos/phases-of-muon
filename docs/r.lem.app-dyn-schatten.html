Lemma SE.4 (Schatten sensitivity of the filter)
Let \(K,K'\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\), \(s>0\), \(\ridge'>0\) and \(p\in\{1,\infty\}\). Then
  1. (i)
    \(\norm{\mathsf X_s(K)-\mathsf X_s(K')}_{S_p}\le s^{-1}\norm{K-K'}_{S_p}\);
  2. (ii)
    \(\norm{\Qridge{\ridge'}(K)-\Qridge{\ridge'}(K')}_{S_p}\le\ridge'^{-1/2}\norm{K-K'}_{S_p}\);
  3. (iii)
    \(\norm{\partial_{\ridge'}\Qridge{\ridge'}(K)-\partial_{\ridge'}\Qridge{\ridge'}(K')}_*\le\frac12\ridge'^{-3/2}\norm{K-K'}_*\);
  4. (iv)
    if \(\ridge_1,\ridge_2\ge\ridge_\circ>0\), then \(\norm{\Qridge{\ridge_1}(K)-\Qridge{\ridge_2}(K)}\le\abs{\ridge_1-\ridge_2}/(2\ridge_\circ)\) and the same bound multiplied by \(\sqrt{\min(\Nout,\Nin)}\) holds in Frobenius norm.
Proof.
The mixed resolvent is a block of a resolvent of the dilation. For \(c>0\), \(\mathcal D(K)^2=\diag(KK^\T,K^\T K)\), and by Lemma SB.3
\[ \mathcal D(K)\big(\mathcal D(K)^2+c^2\Id\big)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&\mathsf X_{c^2}(K)\\ \mathsf X_{c^2}(K)^\T&0\end{pmatrix}. \]
For a real symmetric \(T\) the left side is a sum of two resolvents,
\[ T(T^2+c^2\Id)^{-1}=\frac12\Big[(T-ic\Id)^{-1}+(T+ic\Id)^{-1}\Big], \]
each of operator norm at most \(c^{-1}\). The resolvent identity \((T-ic)^{-1}-(S-ic)^{-1}=(T-ic)^{-1}(S-T)(S-ic)^{-1}\) and the ideal property \(\norm{XYZ}_{S_p}\le\norm X\norm Y_{S_p}\norm Z\) [8, Proposition IV.2.4 and (IV.40)] give, for symmetric \(T,S\),
\[ \norm{T(T^2+c^2)^{-1}-S(S^2+c^2)^{-1}}_{S_p}\le c^{-2}\norm{T-S}_{S_p}. \]
A matrix \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&Y\\Y^\T&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) has the singular values of \(Y\), each twice, so its \(S_p\) norm is \(2^{1/p}\norm Y_{S_p}\), with \(2^{1/\infty}=1\). Applying the last display with \(T=\mathcal D(K)\), \(S=\mathcal D(K')\) and \(c^2=s\), the factor \(2^{1/p}\) appears on both sides and cancels, which proves (i).
Part (ii) integrates (i). By Lemma SA.3, \(\Qridge{\ridge'}(K)=\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\mathsf X_{\ridge'+u^2}(K)\,\dif u\), so
\[ \norm{\Qridge{\ridge'}(K)-\Qridge{\ridge'}(K')}_{S_p}\le\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\frac{\dif u}{\ridge'+u^2}\,\norm{K-K'}_{S_p}=\ridge'^{-1/2}\norm{K-K'}_{S_p}. \]
Part (iii) is the same argument for the derivative in the ridge. For a symmetric \(T\), partial fractions give
\[ -T(T^2+c^2)^{-2}=\frac{i}{4c}\Big[(T-ic)^{-2}-(T+ic)^{-2}\Big], \]
and \((T-ic)^{-2}-(S-ic)^{-2}=(T-ic)^{-1}\big[(T-ic)^{-1}-(S-ic)^{-1}\big]+\big[(T-ic)^{-1}-(S-ic)^{-1}\big](S-ic)^{-1}\) has nuclear norm at most \(2c^{-3}\norm{T-S}_*\). Hence the difference of \(-T(T^2+c^2)^{-2}\) at \(T\) and \(S\) has nuclear norm at most \(c^{-4}\norm{T-S}_*\). The derivative of the integrand of Lemma SA.3 in the ridge is the upper right block of \(-\mathcal D(K)(\mathcal D(K)^2+(\ridge'+u^2))^{-2}\); with the block factor cancelled as before,
\[ \norm{\partial_{\ridge'}\Qridge{\ridge'}(K)-\partial_{\ridge'}\Qridge{\ridge'}(K')}_*\le\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\frac{\dif u}{(\ridge'+u^2)^2}\,\norm{K-K'}_*=\frac{1}{2\ridge'^{3/2}}\norm{K-K'}_*. \]
Part (iv) is (SD.6) for the filter \(\omega_\ridge\) of (SD.2) together with the first two bounds of (SD.4).
□
