Corollary SI.3 (Ratio of the hitting times)
In the windows of Theorem SI.8 (\(\texp<1\)), Theorem SI.9 (\(\alpha>1\), \(\texp\ge1\)) and Theorem SI.10 (\(\alpha\le1\), \(\texp\ge1\)), with constants depending only on \(\alpha\), \(\texp\) and \(T\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.38} \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}}{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}}\asymp D_N\cdot\begin{cases} \epsilon^{-\alpha/[2(p-1)]},&\texp<1,\\ \epsilon^{-1/2}/\log(1/\epsilon),&\texp=1,\\ \epsilon^{-(\alpha+1-\texp)/[2(p-1)]},&1<\texp<\alpha+1,\\ \log(1/\epsilon),&\texp=\alpha+1,\\ 1,&\texp>\alpha+1. \end{cases} \end{equation}
Along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) in the window of Theorem SI.9 or Theorem SI.10,
\[ \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}}{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}}\sim\frac{D_N\log(1/\epsilon)}{2\sqrt{8\alpha}\,J(p,\alpha/2)}\quad(\texp=\alpha+1),\qquad \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}}{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}}\sim D_N\,\frac{J(p,\alpha)}{J(p,\alpha/2)}\quad(\texp>\alpha+1). \]
Proof of Corollary SI.3.
In each window both theorems apply to both models at once. Dividing the two orders and using (SI.27) gives a factor \(D_N\) times \(\Pi_{r_{\text{s-SGD}}}(\epsilon)/\Pi_{r_{\text{s-SVD}}}(\epsilon)\). For \(\texp<1\) this is \(\epsilon^{-\alpha/(p-1)+\alpha/(2(p-1))}\). For \(\texp=1\), \(p-1=\alpha\) and it is \(\epsilon^{-1}/(\epsilon^{-1/2}\log(1/\epsilon))\). For \(1<\texp<\alpha+1\) it is \(\epsilon^{-\alpha/(p-1)+1/2}=\epsilon^{-(\alpha+1-\texp)/(2(p-1))}\). For \(\texp=\alpha+1\) it is \(\log(1/\epsilon)\), and for \(\texp>\alpha+1\) it is \(1\). At \(\texp=\alpha+1\) the SignSVD model is saturated, since \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}=2\), and dividing the equivalents (SI.37) gives
\[ \frac{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}\,\epsilon^{-1/2}\log(1/\epsilon)/(4\sqrt{8\alpha})}{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}T_0^{\text{s-SVD}}J(p,\alpha/2)/(2\sqrt\epsilon)}=\frac{D_N\log(1/\epsilon)}{2\sqrt{8\alpha}\,J(p,\alpha/2)} . \]
For \(\texp>\alpha+1\) both are saturated and the quotient is \(D_NJ(p,\alpha)/J(p,\alpha/2)\).
□
