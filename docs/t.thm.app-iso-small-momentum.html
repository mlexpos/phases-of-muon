Theorem SF.7 (Small aspect ratios with momentum)
Let \(\bmom\in[0,1)\) and \(\gin,\gout>0\), and assume \(\varepsilon_\bmom\defeq6\gamma_\wedge(1-\bmom^2)\le\frac12\).
  1. (i)
    For every \(\ridge\ge0\) there is \(\vartheta\in\big[1-\varepsilon_\bmom,(1-\varepsilon_\bmom)^{-1/2}\big]\) with
    \[ \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gin,\gout)=\vartheta\;\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout;\ridge). \]
  2. (ii)
    With \(r\defeq\gout/\gin\) and \(J_r\) of Theorem SF.4,
    \[ \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)=\frac{r\,J_r}{\sqrt{2\gout}},\qquad \mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gamma,\gamma;0)=\frac{8}{3\pi\sqrt{2\gamma}} . \]
    In particular, in the square case \(C_\bmom(\gamma)=8\vartheta/(3\pi\sqrt{2\sw2\gamma})\) with \(\vartheta\) of (i) at \(\ridge=0\).
  3. (iii)
    If \(6\gamma_\wedge\le\frac12\), then for every \(\bmom\in[0,1)\)
    \[ \big(1-6\gamma_\wedge\big)^{3/2}\le\frac{C_\bmom(\gin,\gout)}{\sw2^{-1/2}\,C_0(\gin,\gout)}\le\big(1-6\gamma_\wedge\big)^{-3/2}. \]
  4. (iv)
    For every \(\ridge\ge0\),
    \[ (1-\varepsilon_\bmom)\big(1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge\big)^{1/2}\le \frac{\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gin,\gout)}{\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;0,\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)}\le (1-\varepsilon_\bmom)^{-1/2}\big(1-6\sw2\gamma_\wedge\big)^{-1}. \]
At large batch, momentum acts on the drift as the effective batch \(B_{\rm eff}=B/\sw2=B(1+\bmom)/(1-\bmom)\) of the main text: up to the factor \(\vartheta\), the drift is that of the linearized closure at the aspect ratios \((\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)=(\Nin/B_{\rm eff},\Nout/B_{\rm eff})\). In the square case \(C_\bmom(\gamma)\approx\frac8{3\pi}\sqrt{B_{\rm eff}/(2N)}\), the large-batch form of \(\mathfrak d_\bmom\) in (9). The small parameter is \(\gamma_\wedge(1-\bmom^2)\), of the order of \(N/B_{\rm eff}\), so the theorem also covers a fixed aspect ratio as \(\bmom\uparrow1\).
Proof sketch.
At small aspect ratio the root stays where the participation count is linear, with slope \(\sw2=\sum_j\emaw j^2\). Expanding each term \(\Phi(\emaw j^2q)\) to second order gives, for all \(q\ge0\) ((SF.49)),
\[ \sw2q\Big(1-3\frac{\sw4}{\sw2}\,q\Big)\le r_\bmom(q)\le\sw2q,\qquad 1-3\sw3q\le\Lw(q)\le1 . \]
The level \(r_\bmom(q)\) stays below \(\gamma_\wedge\), and \(r_\bmom(2\gamma_\wedge/\sw2)\ge\gamma_\wedge\) once \(\varepsilon_\bmom\le\frac12\), so the root stays below \(\bar q=2\gamma_\wedge/\sw2\). At such roots the two expansions are accurate to relative errors \(6\gamma_\wedge\sw4/\sw2^2\) and \(6\gamma_\wedge\sw3/\sw2\), both at most \(\varepsilon_\bmom\), because \(\sw4/\sw2^2=(1-\bmom^2)/(1+\bmom^2)\).
A larger participation count produces a smaller root, so the root is squeezed between two linear roots. The right side \((\gout-r)(\gin-r)\) of the scalar equation decreases in the count \(r\), and comparing the two sides shows that raising the count lowers the root (Lemma SF.5). For the linear count \(r(q)=cq\) the root is \(c^{-1}Y(s/c)\), where \(Y(s)\) is the explicit root of \(sY=(\gout-Y)(\gin-Y)\). The two expansions give
\[ \frac1{\sw2}\,Y\Big(\frac s{\sw2}\Big)\le q(-s)\le\frac1c\,Y\Big(\frac sc\Big),\qquad c=\sw2\Big(1-6\gamma_\wedge\frac{\sw4}{\sw2^2}\Big). \]
Inserting this sandwich and the bounds on \(\Lw\) in the drift integral (SF.10), and rescaling \(u=\sqrt{\sw2}\,v\), gives (i): the factor \(\sw2\) in the count is absorbed by multiplying both aspect ratios by \(\sw2\), because \(Y_{t\gin,t\gout}(ts)=t\,Y_{\gin,\gout}(s)\) ((SF.50)).
The linear drift at zero ridge is a half-moment of the Marchenko–Pastur law, as in Theorem SF.4. The rescaled linear root \(y(x)=Y(\gout x)/\gout\) solves \(xy=(1-y)(\frac1r-y)\), which is the equation satisfied by \(1-x\,m_r(x)\) for the transform \(m_r\) of \(\mathsf M_r\), and the half-moment formula gives \(\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{lin}}(\gin,\gout;0)=rJ_r/\sqrt{2\gout}\). In the square case \(y(v^2)=e^{-2\theta}\) at \(v=2\sinh\theta\), so \(\int_0^\infty y(v^2)\,\dif v=\int_0^\infty(e^{-\theta}+e^{-3\theta})\,\dif\theta=\frac43\), which gives \(8/(3\pi\sqrt{2\gamma})\). This is (ii). Parts (iii) and (iv) divide (i) at momentum \(\bmom\) by (i) at momentum zero, at the aspect ratios \((\gin,\gout)\) and \((\sw2\gin,\sw2\gout)\) respectively.
