Remark SG.1 (No momentum)
At \(\bmom=0\) the weights are \(\emaw0=1\), so \(\Fw=\Lw=\Jfun\) and \(r_0(q)=q\Jfun(q)\) increases from \(0\) to \(1\), not to \(\infty\); the participation is confined to \(m<1\), and at zero ridge \(m(s)\to1\) when \(\gamma>1\). The computation of Proposition SG.1(ii), which uses only the equations of the branch, then gives \(\Lambda_N(m(s))\to\resscale\), the root of Proposition SG.6, instead of infinity, and by (SG.7)
\[ \lim_{\ridge\downarrow0}\volraw_i(\ridge)=\frac{\resscale\mu_i}{1+\resscale\mu_i}=\frac1{1+(i/i_\#)^\alpha},\qquad \sum_i\lim_{\ridge\downarrow0}\volraw_i(\ridge)=B . \]
All lag coefficients with \(\ell\ge1\) vanish (Theorem SD.7(iv)), so \(\mathfrak v_i=\volraw_i\), and the volatility crossover at \(\bmom=0\) is this exact profile, with critical index \(i_\#\) and the equivalents (SG.36). The drift at \(\bmom=0\) is the single integral (SG.7) with \(\Lw/\Fw=1\); its two endpoint laws, on either side of \(i_\#\), are Proposition SG.8 below. These no-momentum profiles are obtained by setting \(\bmom=0\) before the ridge and dimension limits. For every fixed \(\bmom>0\), \(r_\bmom\) is unbounded, \(\volraw_i(0)=1\) for every row (Proposition SG.2), and the profiles of Theorem SG.1 hold; neither family is a limit of the other under an exchange of \(\bmom\downarrow0\) with the ridge and dimension limits.
This remark explains the no-momentum row of Table 1 and why it differs from the rows with momentum. Without momentum the buffer is a single gradient, whose participation cannot exceed one, so at zero ridge the update whitens only the directions above the level \(1/\resscale\): the zero-lag volatility of row \(i\) is the soft indicator \(1/(1+(i/i_\#)^\alpha)\), the coefficients sum to \(B\), and there are no lag terms. With any fixed momentum the participation is unbounded, every row has zero-lag coefficient one at zero ridge, and the crossover moves to the indices of Theorem SG.2 and Theorem SG.3. The two families are not limits of each other, because the limit \(\bmom\downarrow0\) does not commute with the ridge and dimension limits.
