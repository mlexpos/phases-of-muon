Corollary SI.6 (Ratio at every accuracy)
Fix \(\alpha>0\), \(\texp\) with \(\alpha+\texp>1\), and \(T\ge4\), and let \(N_0\) and \(\epsilon_0\) be thresholds of Theorem SI.14 valid for both models. If \((\alpha,\texp,\text{s-SVD})\) and \((\alpha,\texp,\text{s-SGD})\) both lie in \(\mathcal U\), then for \(N\ge N_0\), \(B\ge N\) and \(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.49} \frac{t^{\text{s-SGD}}_{T\epsilon}}{t^{\text{s-SVD}}_{T\epsilon}}\asymp D_N\,\frac{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SGD}}(N,\epsilon)}{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}(N,\epsilon)} , \end{equation}
and if moreover \(\texp\notin\{1,\alpha+1\}\), the right side equals \(D_N\max\{\epsilon,\epsilon_N\}^{-\chi}\). If only \((\alpha,\texp,\text{s-SGD})\in\mathcal U\), the upper half of (SI.49) holds; if only \((\alpha,\texp,\text{s-SVD})\in\mathcal U\), the lower half holds. For \(\epsilon=N^{-\omega}\) with fixed \(\omega>0\),
\[ D_N\,\frac{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SGD}}(N,\epsilon)}{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}(N,\epsilon)}\asymp N^{e_D+\chi\min\{\omega,p-1\}}\,(\log N)^{L}, \]
with constants depending also on \(\omega\).
Proof of Corollary SI.6.
Divide the bounds of Theorem SI.14, using (SI.41); the lower bounds hold for every model. If \(r_j<1\), then \(1/(2r_j)>\frac12\), so \(e_j=1/(2r_j)\) and \((1-r_j)/(2r_j)=e_j-\frac12\); by (SI.46), \(\Upsilon_j=\max\{\epsilon,\epsilon_N\}^{-(e_j-1/2)}\). If \(r_j>1\), then \(e_j=\frac12\) and \(\Upsilon_j=1\), which is the same formula. For \(\texp\notin\{1,\alpha+1\}\) neither \(r_j\) equals \(1\), so
\[ \frac{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SGD}}}{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}}=\max\{\epsilon,\epsilon_N\}^{-(e_{\text{s-SGD}}-e_{\text{s-SVD}})}=\max\{\epsilon,\epsilon_N\}^{-\chi} . \]
For \(\epsilon=N^{-\omega}\) and \(r_j<1\),
\[ \Upsilon_j=N^{\min\{\omega,p-1\}(1-r_j)/(2r_j)}, \]
because \(q_j(1-r_j)=(p-1)(1-r_j)/(2r_j)\). For \(r_j\ge1\), \(\Upsilon_j\asymp(\log N)^{[r_j=1]}\), with constants depending on \(\omega\). Hence
\[ \frac{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SGD}}}{\Upsilon_{\text{s-SVD}}}\asymp N^{\chi\min\{\omega,p-1\}}(\log N)^{[\texp=\alpha+1]-[\texp=1]} , \]
and multiplying by \(D_N\asymp N^{e_D}(\log N)^{[\alpha=1]-[\alpha=2]}\) gives the last display.
□
