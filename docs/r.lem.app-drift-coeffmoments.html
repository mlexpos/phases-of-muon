Lemma SC.2 (Conditional moments of the coefficients)
Let \(s_\circ>0\) and \(p\ge1\) be an integer. In the setting of Lemma SB.4, let \(M_0\) be a function of the samples other than \((j,a)\), let \((\mathsf h,\mathsf u,\mathsf v)=(\varpi d,\mathsf u_{ja},\mathsf v_{ja})\) with \(0\le\varpi\le1\) and \(d=d_{ja}\), and let \(\mathcal G=\mathcal G_{ja}\). Then for every \(s\ge s_\circ\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.19} \norm{\alpha_1}_{p,\mathcal G}\le C\varpi,\qquad \norm{\alpha_3}_{p,\mathcal G}\le C\varpi^2,\qquad \norm{\alpha_2}_{p,\mathcal G}\le\frac{C\varpi^2}{s}, \end{equation}
with \(C\) depending only on \((p,s_\circ,C_\Sigma,\gamma_\star)\), and \(\alpha_2\ge0\). The same bounds, with \(s\) replaced by \(x\defeq\Ree z\), hold for the coefficients of Lemma SB.4 at every \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\), and there \(\Ree\alpha_2\ge0\).
Proof.
The pair \((d,\mathsf N)\) with \(\mathsf N=\norm{\mathsf u}^2+\norm{\mathsf v}^2\) is a function of the sample \((j,a)\) alone, which is independent of \(\mathcal G\); the conditional norms are therefore the unconditional norms of the bounds below.
The bounds on \(\alpha_1\) and \(\alpha_3\) are those of Lemma SB.5. For \(s\ge s_\circ\) apply that lemma with the compact interval \(I=[s_\circ,s]\), whose constant \(C_I=\max(1,s_\circ^{-1})(1+\tfrac{3}{4\sqrt{s_\circ}})^3\) depends only on \(s_\circ\):
\[ \abs{\alpha_1}\le C_I\varpi(1+\abs d)^4(1+\mathsf N)^3,\qquad \abs{\alpha_3}\le C_I\varpi^2(1+\abs d)^4(1+\mathsf N)^3 . \]
The bound on \(\alpha_2\) carries the factor \(1/s\) because \(\mathsf U\) does. By Lemma SB.3, \(0\le\mathsf U\le\norm{\mathsf u}^2/s\le\mathsf N/s\), and by (SB.16) and \(\abs{\mathsf h}\le\abs d\),
\[ \alpha_2=\frac{\mathsf h^2\mathsf U}{\Lambda}\le\varpi^2d^2\,\frac{\mathsf N}{s}\Big(1+\frac{3\abs d\mathsf N}{4\sqrt{s_\circ}}\Big)^2 \le\frac{\varpi^2}{s}\Big(1+\frac{3}{4\sqrt{s_\circ}}\Big)^2(1+\abs d)^4(1+\mathsf N)^3 , \]
the last step by the elementary inequality (SB.20). Positivity of \(\alpha_2\) holds because \(\mathsf U\ge0\) and \(\Lambda>0\).
At \(z\in\mathbb S\) with \(x\ge s_\circ\) the coefficients are complex, and the pointwise bounds are obtained from the sector bounds. Put \(\kappa\defeq s_\circ^{-1/2}\) and \(\mathsf Z\defeq(1+\abs d)^4(1+\mathsf N)^3\). By Lemma SB.3 at \(z\), \(\abs{\mathsf U}\le\mathsf N/x\), \(\abs{z\mathsf V}\le\frac{\sqrt5}{2}\mathsf N\) and \(\abs{\mathsf C}\le\mathsf N/(4\sqrt x)\); by (SB.17) and \(\abs{\mathsf h}\le\abs d\), \(\abs\Lambda^{-1}\le(1+\kappa\abs d\mathsf N)^2\). With (SB.20) for \(c=\kappa\) these give
\[ \abs{\alpha_1}\le(1+\kappa)^3\varpi\,\mathsf Z,\qquad \abs{\alpha_2}\le\frac{(1+\kappa)^2\varpi^2}{x}\,\mathsf Z,\qquad \abs{\alpha_3}\le2(1+\kappa)^2\varpi^2\,\mathsf Z . \]
The sign of \(\alpha_2\) is replaced by that of its real part. Multiplying (SC.17) at \(z\) by \(\mathsf u^\T\) gives \(\mathsf h\,\mathsf u^\T R_z(M)\mathsf u=\alpha_1\mathsf U-\alpha_2\mathsf C=\mathsf h\mathsf U/\Lambda\), so \(\alpha_2=\mathsf h^2\,\mathsf u^\T R_z(M)\mathsf u\). The matrix \(R_z(M)\) has real orthonormal eigenvectors and eigenvalues \((s_k^2+z)^{-1}\) and \(z^{-1}\), all with positive real part, so \(\Ree\alpha_2\ge0\).
The moments of the common factor are bounded by Hölder’s inequality and Lemma SB.6:
\[ \big\|(1+\abs d)^4(1+\mathsf N)^3\big\|_p\le\norm{1+\abs d}_{8p}^4\,\norm{1+\mathsf N}_{6p}^3 \le\big(1+\norm d_{8p}\big)^4\big(1+\norm{\mathsf N}_{6p}\big)^3 , \]
and the right side depends only on \((p,C_\Sigma,\gamma_\star)\).
□
