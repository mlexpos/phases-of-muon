Corollary SG.2 (Nonnegative weighted sums)
Assume Assumption S4. Let \(I_N\subset\{n_N,\dots,N\}\) with \(n_N\to\infty\), and let \(a_{i,N}\ge0\), \(i\in I_N\), be deterministic weights with \(\sum_{i\in I_N}a_{i,N}>0\) for each \(N\). Then
\[ \frac{\sum_{i\in I_N}a_{i,N}\,\mathfrak d_i(0)}{\sum_{i\in I_N}a_{i,N}\,\widehat{\mathfrak d}_i}\longrightarrow1,\qquad \frac{\sum_{i\in I_N}a_{i,N}\,\mathfrak v_i(0)}{\sum_{i\in I_N}a_{i,N}\,\widehat{\mathfrak v}_i}\longrightarrow1 . \]
Proof of Corollary SG.2.
Let \(\epsilon_N\) be the supremum in (SG.31) over \(i\ge n_N\). Then \((1-\epsilon_N)\widehat{\mathfrak d}_i\le\mathfrak d_i(0)\le(1+\epsilon_N)\widehat{\mathfrak d}_i\) for \(i\in I_N\); multiplying by \(a_{i,N}\ge0\) and summing bounds the first ratio between \(1-\epsilon_N\) and \(1+\epsilon_N\). The second ratio is the same with \(\mathfrak v_i(0)\) and \(\widehat{\mathfrak v}_i\).
□
