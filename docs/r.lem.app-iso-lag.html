Lemma SF.11 (Isotropic lag response)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\Nin=\Nout=N\) and \(\gamma=N/B\). For \(s,s'>0\) put \(q\defeq q(-s)\) and \(q'\defeq q(-s')\), let \(b_\ell\) and \(e_\ell\) be the numbers of (SG.14) for these roots, and put
\[ X_\ell\defeq\frac{q\,q'\,e_\ell}\gamma,\qquad Z_\ell\defeq\frac{\sqrt{qq'}\,b_\ell}\gamma,\qquad \Delta_\ell\defeq(1-X_\ell)^2-Z_\ell^2\qquad(\ell\ge0). \]
  1. (i)
    \(0\le b_\ell\le\bmom^\ell\sw2\), \(0\le e_\ell\le3\bmom^{2\ell}\sw4\), \(0\le X_\ell<1\) and \(0<\Delta_\ell\le1\).
  2. (ii)
    The row lag kernel (SG.1) at \(\mu_1=\dots=\mu_N=1\) is the same for every row and equals
    \[ \mathfrak k_{1,\ell}(s,s')=\frac{b_\ell\,q\,q'}{\gamma^3\,\Delta_\ell}\ \ge\ \frac{b_\ell\,q\,q'}{\gamma^3}, \]
    and for every \(\ridge\ge0\) the row lag coefficient is
    \begin{equation}\tag{SF.52} \overline C_{1,\ell}(\ridge)=\frac4{\pi^2}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\mathfrak k_{1,\ell}(\ridge+x^2,\ridge+y^2)\,\dif x\,\dif y , \end{equation}
    where \(\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\) is the limit of Proposition SF.3(ii).
Proof.
Part (ii) is Proposition SG.3 at \(\mu_i=1\). In the square case the two anchors of (SF.6) give \(\sigma=\tilde\sigma\) and \(\tilde s_1=s_1\), and \(q=s\sigma^2\), so that \(s+\tilde s_1=s+s_1=\gamma/\sigma\). The scalars of (SG.14) become \(\mathsf U=\mathsf V=\sigma\sigma'/\gamma\), and
\[ ss'\,\mathsf U\tilde\sigma\tilde\sigma'=ss'\,\mathsf V\sigma\sigma'=\frac{ss'\sigma^2\sigma'^2}\gamma=\frac{qq'}\gamma,\qquad ss'\,\mathsf U\mathsf V=\frac{qq'}{\gamma^2} . \]
Hence the determinant of (SG.14) is \(\Delta_\ell\), and (SG.16) reads
\[ \mathfrak k_{1,\ell}(s,s')=\frac{\gamma\,ss'\,b_\ell}{(\gamma/\sigma)^2(\gamma/\sigma')^2\,\Delta_\ell}=\frac{b_\ell\,qq'}{\gamma^3\Delta_\ell}. \]
The integral (SF.52) is (SG.2) for \(\ridge>0\), where the kernel is nonnegative so that the limit \(T\to\infty\) is the integral over \((0,\infty)^2\), and (SG.22) at \(\ridge=0\). The lower bound on the kernel is \(\Delta_\ell\le1\) from part (i).
For part (i), every factor \((1+\cdots)^{-1}\) in the series (SG.14) lies in \((0,1]\), so
\[ 0\le b_\ell\le\bmom^\ell\sum_j\emaw j^2\,\EE g^2=\bmom^\ell\sw2,\qquad 0\le e_\ell\le\bmom^{2\ell}\sum_j\emaw j^4\,\EE g^4=3\bmom^{2\ell}\sw4 . \]
For \(X_\ell\) put \(T(q)\defeq-\Fw'(q)=\sum_j\emaw j^4\,\EE[g^4(1+\emaw j^2qg^2)^{-2}]\). The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality over \(j\) and \(g\) gives
\[ e_\ell\le\sqrt{T(q)}\,\sqrt{\bmom^{4\ell}T(\bmom^{2\ell}q')} , \]
and since \(\bmom^\ell\emaw j=\emaw{j+\ell}\),
\[ \bmom^{4\ell}T(\bmom^{2\ell}q')=\sum_{j\ge0}\emaw{j+\ell}^4\,\EE\frac{g^4}{(1+\emaw{j+\ell}^2q'g^2)^2}\le T(q') . \]
Hence \(X_\ell\le\sqrt{X(q)X(q')}\) with \(X(q)\defeq q^2T(q)/\gamma\). Since \(r_\bmom'=\Fw-qT>0\) (Lemma SF.4(i)),
\[ X(q)=\frac{r_\bmom(q)-q\,r_\bmom'(q)}\gamma<\frac{r_\bmom(q)}\gamma=\frac{\psi(s)}\gamma<1, \]
by Proposition SF.1(ii). Thus \(0\le X_\ell<1\), and \(\Delta_\ell\le(1-X_\ell)^2\le1\). Positivity of \(\Delta_\ell\) is Proposition SG.3.
□
