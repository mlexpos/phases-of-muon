The isotropic case · cited in the main text
Proved in LeanTheorem numbering in the paper: Lemma K.1
For \(n\ge0\), \(0\le z\le1\) and \(\bmom\in(0,1)\) put
\[ k_n\defeq\frac1{2n+1}+\frac1{2n+3},\qquad \Omega(z)\defeq\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge0}k_n^2\,z^{2n+1},\qquad V_\bmom\defeq1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\Omega(\bmom^\ell). \]
Lemma SF.10 (The volatility factor)
\(\Omega(1)=1\), and for \(\bmom\in(0,1)\)
\[ V_\bmom=\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge0}k_n^2\,\frac{1+\bmom^{2n+1}}{1-\bmom^{2n+1}},\qquad 1<V_\bmom<\frac1{\sw2}=\frac{1+\bmom}{1-\bmom}, \]
\[ \lim_{\bmom\uparrow1}\sw2V_\bmom=\frac14+\frac1{\pi^2}+\frac{7\zeta(3)}{2\pi^2}=0.77759\ldots . \]
\(V_\bmom\) is the factor by which momentum multiplies the zero-ridge volatility at large batch: \(\mathfrak v_{\mathrm{iso},\bmom}\approx NV_\bmom\) (Theorem SF.8), where \(\Omega(\bmom^\ell)\) is, at large batch, the correlation of two updates \(\ell\) steps apart. The lemma places \(V_\bmom\) strictly between \(1\), the value without correlations, and \(1/\sw2=B_{\rm eff}/B\), the value if successive updates were correlated like successive buffers, by \(\bmom^\ell\). The main text writes \(c_\bmom=\sw2V_\bmom\), so that \(\mathfrak v_\bmom\approx c_\bmom NB_{\rm eff}/B\) in (9); the lemma gives \(c_\bmom<1\) and the limit \(0.7776\ldots\) as \(\bmom\uparrow1\).
Proof sketch.
The coefficients of \(\Omega\) form a probability distribution on the odd powers. With \(x=2n+1\), the square \(k_n^2=x^{-2}+2/(x(x+2))+(x+2)^{-2}\) sums over odd \(x\) to \(\frac{\pi^2}8+1+(\frac{\pi^2}8-1)=\frac{\pi^2}4\), by \(\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}x^{-2}=\pi^2/8\) and a telescoping sum, so \(\Omega(1)=1\). Summing the geometric series in the lag, \(1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\bmom^{\ell x}=(1+\bmom^x)/(1-\bmom^x)\), shows that \(V_\bmom\) is the average of \((1+\bmom^x)/(1-\bmom^x)\) over odd \(x\) under these weights. Each value lies in \((1,(1+\bmom)/(1-\bmom)]\), with equality on the right only at \(x=1\), which gives the two bounds.
The limit is an average of \(1/x\). As \(\bmom\uparrow1\), \(\sw2(1+\bmom^x)/(1-\bmom^x)\to1/x\), with the bound \(2\), so dominated convergence gives \(\lim\sw2V_\bmom=\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_nk_n^2/(2n+1)\). Partial fractions and \(\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}x^{-3}=\frac78\zeta(3)\) evaluate the sum as \(\frac78\zeta(3)+\frac{\pi^2}{16}+\frac14\), and multiplying by \(4/\pi^2\) gives \(\frac14+\frac1{\pi^2}+\frac{7\zeta(3)}{2\pi^2}\).
