Proposition SI.4 (Noise constants, kernels and clocks)
The arrays of Definition SI.3 satisfy (M1), with \(F_0=\frac12H_N(p)\), and the following hold.
  1. (i)
    The noise constants, matched steps and time-scale products are
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.26} \begin{aligned} \mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}&=\tfrac12\sqrt\gamma\,H_N(\alpha/2),&\quad \lr^\star_{\text{s-SVD}}&=\frac{4\sqrt\epsilon}{\sqrt\gamma\,H_N(\alpha/2)},\\ \mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}&=\frac{N^2}2\sqrt{\frac{\pi\bar\mu}B},&\quad \lr^\star_{\text{s-SGD}}&=\frac{4\sqrt\epsilon}{N^2}\sqrt{\frac B{\pi\bar\mu}},\\ \mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}T_0^{\text{s-SVD}}&=\tfrac\gamma2H_N(\alpha/2),&\quad \mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}&=\frac{\pi N^2\bar\mu}{2B}=\tfrac{\pi\gamma}2H_N(\alpha), \end{aligned} \end{equation}
    and the dimension factor is
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.27} D_N\defeq\frac{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}T_0^{\text{s-SGD}}}{\mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}T_0^{\text{s-SVD}}}=\frac{\pi H_N(\alpha)}{H_N(\alpha/2)} . \end{equation}
  2. (ii)
    The dimensionless risks solve \(Y_j=f_{N,q_j}+\sqrt\epsilon\,g_j*\sqrt{Y_j}\), with \(f_{N,q}\) of (SI.23) and
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.28} g_{\text{s-SVD}}=\frac2{H_N(\alpha/2)}L_{N,\alpha,\alpha/2},\qquad g_{\text{s-SGD}}=\frac{2(1-2/\pi)}N\,L_{N,\alpha,\alpha}+\frac4{\pi H_N(\alpha)}\,L_{N,2\alpha,\alpha} . \end{equation}
    Both kernels are positive, continuous and decreasing. The kernel \(g_{\text{s-SVD}}\) has integral \(1\), and the two terms of \(g_{\text{s-SGD}}\) have integrals \(1-2/\pi\) and \(2/\pi\). Their maxima are
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.29} \begin{gathered} k_{N,\text{s-SVD}}\defeq g_{\text{s-SVD}}(0)=\frac{2H_N(\alpha)}{H_N(\alpha/2)},\\ k_{N,\text{s-SGD}}\defeq g_{\text{s-SGD}}(0)=\frac{2(1-2/\pi)H_N(\alpha)}N+\frac{4H_N(2\alpha)}{\pi H_N(\alpha)} . \end{gathered} \end{equation}
  3. (iii)
    If \(\hat u_j<\infty\), then
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.30} t^j_{T\epsilon}=\frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j}{2\sqrt\epsilon}\int_0^{\hat u_j}\sqrt{Y_j(u)}\,\dif u . \end{equation}
  4. (iv)
    As \(N\to\infty\) at fixed \(\alpha\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SI.31} D_N\sim\begin{cases} \pi\,\dfrac{1-\alpha/2}{1-\alpha}\,N^{-\alpha/2},&0<\alpha<1,\\[4pt] \dfrac\pi2N^{-1/2}\log N,&\alpha=1,\\[4pt] \pi(1-\alpha/2)\,\zeta(\alpha)\,N^{\alpha/2-1},&1<\alpha<2,\\[4pt] \pi\zeta(2)/\log N,&\alpha=2,\\[4pt] \pi\zeta(\alpha)/\zeta(\alpha/2),&\alpha>2. \end{cases} \end{equation}
Proof.
All coefficients are positive, since \(\mu_i/\bar\mu=Ni^{-\alpha}/H_N(\alpha)>0\) and \(2/\pi<1\), and the sums in (SI.1) are finite. For SignSVD,
\[ \mathcal S_{\text{s-SVD}}=\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{i^{-\alpha}}{2\gamma^{-1/2}i^{-\alpha/2}}=\frac{\sqrt\gamma}2H_N(\alpha/2) . \]
For SignSGD the factor \(\mu_i\) cancels and \(\sum_i\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=N^2\), so
\[ \mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}=\frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi\bar\mu}B}\sum_{i=1}^N\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=\frac{N^2}2\sqrt{\frac{\pi\bar\mu}B} . \]
The steps and the products follow, using \(N\bar\mu=H_N(\alpha)\) and \(N/B=\gamma\). This proves (i).
For (ii), substituting \(v=T_0^jw\) in the convolution gives
\[ Y_j(u)=F_j(T_0^ju)+T_0^j\int_0^uk_j\big(T_0^j(u-w)\big)\sqrt{Y_j(w)}\,\dif w=F_j(T_0^ju)+\sqrt\epsilon\,(g_j*\sqrt{Y_j})(u). \]
Since \(\mathfrak d_i^jT_0^j=i^{-q_j}\), the forcing is \(F_j(T_0^ju)=\frac12\sum_ii^{-p}e^{-2ui^{-q_j}}=f_{N,q_j}(u)\). At the matched step,
\[ g_j(u)=\frac{T_0^j}{\mathcal S_j}\sum_{i=1}^N\mu_i\mathfrak v_i^je^{-2ui^{-q_j}} , \]
and inserting (i) and the arrays gives (SI.28). Each exponential satisfies \(\int_0^\infty e^{-2ui^{-q}}\dif u=i^q/2\), which gives the three integrals. Each kernel is a positive combination of decreasing exponentials, and its value at \(0\) is (SI.29).
For (iii), Theorem SI.3(i) gives \(t^j_{T\epsilon}=t(H_Q(T\epsilon))\) with \(H_Q(T\epsilon)=T_0^j\hat u_j\), and the change of variables \(v=T_0^ju\) in the clock gives
\[ t(T_0^j\hat u_j)=\frac{T_0^j}{\lr^\star_j}\int_0^{\hat u_j}\sqrt{Y_j(u)}\,\dif u , \]
which is (SI.30). Part (iv) follows from (SI.27) and Lemma SI.8.
□
