Corollary SH.4 (Acceleration of a slow mode)
Let \(\nu\) be a probability measure with \(\nu([0,\mu_+])=1\) for some \(\mu_+>0\), and let \(0<\mu\le\mu_+/2\).
  1. (i)
    One has
    \[ \chi_\nu(\mu)\sqrt\varkappa\le\mathcal A\le\frac{\sqrt2}{C_*}\,\chi_\nu(\mu)\sqrt\varkappa . \]
  2. (ii)
    One has
    \[ \frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\pi}\log\varkappa\le\mathcal A\le\frac{\sqrt2}{C_*}\sqrt\varkappa . \]
  3. (iii)
    For every \(K>0\),
    \[ C_*\sqrt{\frac{\nu([0,K\mu])}{1+K}}\,\sqrt\varkappa\le\mathcal A \le\frac{\sqrt2}{C_*}\Big[\sqrt{\nu([0,K\mu])}+\frac{2(\log(1+K)+2)}{\pi\sqrt K}\Big]\sqrt\varkappa . \]
  4. (iv)
    If \(\nu(\{0\})=0\) and \(\mu_+\mathcal I_\nu\le\Gamma<\infty\), then
    \[ \mathcal A\le\frac{2\sqrt2}{\pi C_*}\sqrt\Gamma\,\big[\log(1+\varkappa)+2\big]. \]
  5. (v)
    For the two-level spectrum of Corollary SH.3, with \(\mu_+=1\) and \(\mu=1/\varkappa\),
    \[ \frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\pi}\max\big\{\sqrt{p\varkappa},\ \log\varkappa\big\}\le\mathcal A\le c_{\rm hi}\max\big\{\sqrt{p\varkappa},\ \log\varkappa\big\}. \]
  6. (vi)
    Along sequences \((\nu_N,\mu_{+,N},\mu_N)\) satisfying the hypotheses, with acceleration \(\mathcal A_N\) and ratio \(\varkappa_N\), one has \(\liminf_N\mathcal A_N/\sqrt{\varkappa_N}>0\) if and only if \(\liminf_N\nu_N([0,K\mu_N])>0\) for some \(K>0\).
Proof.
The top mode is pinned by Theorem SH.2(i). Since \(x\le\mu_+\) on the support, \(1-m_\nu(\mu_+)\ge\frac12\), so
\[ \frac{C_*}2\sqrt{\mu_+}\le\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu_+)\le\sqrt{\frac{\mu_+}2}. \]
By Lemma SH.8, \(\mathcal A=\varkappa\,\chi_\nu(\mu)\sqrt{\mu/2}/\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu_+)\), and the two bounds give (i).
Part (ii) uses Proposition SH.2(v) with \(X=\mu_+\) for the lower bound,
\[ \mathcal A\ge\varkappa\,\frac{\frac1{2\pi}\,\mu\,\mu_+^{-1/2}\log\varkappa}{\sqrt{\mu_+/2}}=\frac{\log\varkappa}{\sqrt2\,\pi}, \]
and \(\chi_\nu\le1\) in (i) for the upper bound. Part (iii) is (i) with Proposition SH.2(iv).
For part (iv), \(\mathcal I_\nu\le\Gamma/\mu_+\), and \(\mathcal I_\nu\ge1/\mu_+\) because the support lies in \((0,\mu_+]\), so \(1/(\mu\mathcal I_\nu)\le\varkappa\). Proposition SH.2(ii) and the lower bound for \(\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu_+)\) give
\[ \mathcal A\le\varkappa\,\frac{\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\sqrt{\Gamma/\mu_+}\;\mu\,\big[\log(1+\varkappa)+2\big]}{\frac{C_*}2\sqrt{\mu_+}} =\frac{2\sqrt2}{\pi C_*}\sqrt\Gamma\,\big[\log(1+\varkappa)+2\big]. \]
Part (v) is Corollary SH.3 multiplied by \(\varkappa\). Part (vi) is (i) together with Proposition SH.2(vi).
□
