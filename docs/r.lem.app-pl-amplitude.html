Lemma SG.4 (Spectral amplitude)
Write \(\mathfrak d_i(\ridge;\mu)\), \(\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge;\mu)\) and \(\mathfrak v_i(\ridge;\mu)\) for the coefficients of the spectrum \(\mu=(\mu_j)\). For every \(c>0\) and \(\ridge\ge0\),
\[ \mathfrak d_i(\ridge;c\mu)=\sqrt c\,\mathfrak d_i(\ridge/c;\mu),\qquad \overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge;c\mu)=\overline C_{i,\ell}(\ridge/c;\mu),\qquad \mathfrak v_i(\ridge;c\mu)=\mathfrak v_i(\ridge/c;\mu). \]
Proof.
For the drift, \(m_N(\Lambda;c\mu)=m_N(c\Lambda;\mu)\), so \(\Lambda_N(m;c\mu)=\Lambda_N(m;\mu)/c\), the functions \(f_i\) are unchanged, and \(s_N(m;c\mu)=c\,s_N(m;\mu)\). In (SG.7) for \(c\mu\) the participation at \(\ridge+u^2\) is therefore the participation for \(\mu\) at \(\ridge/c+(u/\sqrt c)^2\), and the substitution \(u=\sqrt c\,u'\) gives the first identity; the case \(\ridge=0\) is the same computation in (SG.12). For the volatility, the buffer of the independent model with covariance \(c\Sigmaout\) has the law of \(\sqrt c\,M^{\mathrm g}_t\), and \(\Qridge\ridge(\sqrt cK)=\Qridge{\ridge/c}(K)\) because \(\sqrt cx/\sqrt{cx^2+\ridge}=x/\sqrt{x^2+\ridge/c}\). The independent row covariances of the two spectra therefore coincide at every order of replication, and so do their limits \(\overline C_{i,\ell}\) (Theorem SD.3). The integrated and zero-ridge statements follow by summation and by the limits of Proposition SG.4.
□
