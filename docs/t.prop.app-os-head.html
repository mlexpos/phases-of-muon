Proposition SH.1 (Head modes)
  1. (i)
    For every probability measure \(\nu\), every \(\mu>0\) and every \(\delta>0\),
    \[ \frac{\delta}{2(1+\sqrt\delta)^2}\,\nu\big([\delta\mu,\infty)\big)\le1-\chi_\nu(\mu)\le\delta+\nu\big([\delta\mu,\infty)\big). \]
  2. (ii)
    For every sequence of probability measures \(\nu_N\) and numbers \(\mu_N>0\), one has \(\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu_N}(\mu_N)/\sqrt{\mu_N/2}\to1\) if and only if \(\nu_N([\delta\mu_N,\infty))\to0\) for every \(\delta>0\).
This proposition supports the statement of Section 4.1 that for modes high in the spectrum the constant of the square-root law tends to one. By the representation of Section G.5, \(\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu)/\sqrt{\mu/2}\) is the efficiency \(\chi_\nu(\mu)\), and part (i) bounds its defect by the mass of the spectrum above a fraction \(\delta\) of the mode. Part (ii) says that the efficiency tends to one exactly for head modes, which lie above all but a vanishing fraction of the spectrum.
Proof sketch.
The defect \(1-\chi_\nu(\mu)\) is within a factor two of an exact average. The average of the soft count against \(\varpi\) has a closed form, by partial fractions in \(y=w^2\) (Lemma SH.9):
\[ \int_0^\infty\varpi(y)\,\frac{x}{\mu y+x}\,\dif y=\frac{x}{(\sqrt\mu+\sqrt x)^2},\quad m^\varpi_\nu(\mu)\defeq\int_0^\infty\varpi(y)\,m_\nu(\mu y)\,\dif y=\int\frac{x\,\nu(\dif x)}{(\sqrt\mu+\sqrt x)^2}. \]
The elementary bounds \(m/2\le1-\sqrt{1-m}\le m\) on \([0,1]\), integrated against \(\varpi\), give \(\frac12m^\varpi_\nu(\mu)\le1-\chi_\nu(\mu)\le m^\varpi_\nu(\mu)\) (Lemma SH.10).
The averaged count is then bounded by splitting the spectrum at \(\delta\mu\). Its integrand \(x/(\sqrt\mu+\sqrt x)^2\) is nondecreasing in \(x\), smaller than \(\delta\) below \(\delta\mu\), at most one above, and at least \(\delta/(1+\sqrt\delta)^2\) on \([\delta\mu,\infty)\), which gives (i). Part (ii) follows by letting \(N\to\infty\) and then \(\delta\downarrow0\) in the upper bound, and from the lower bound at each fixed \(\delta\) for the converse.
