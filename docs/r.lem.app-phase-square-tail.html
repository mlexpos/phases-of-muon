Lemma SI.20 (Square supersolution with a tail term)
Let \(g\ge0\) be continuous with \(\int_0^\infty g\le1\), let \(\epsilon>0\) and \(u_0>0\), and let \(f,h,w\ge0\) be continuous on \([0,u_0]\) with
\[ f\le h^2,\qquad g*h\le2h+\frac{w^2}{\sqrt\epsilon},\qquad g*w\le2w\qquad\text{on }[0,u_0]. \]
If \(Y>0\) is continuous and \(Y=f+\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt Y\) on \([0,u_0]\), then on \([0,u_0]\)
\[ Y\le(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\qquad\text{and}\qquad g*\sqrt Y\le G\defeq2h+\frac{w^2}{\sqrt\epsilon}+2w+\sqrt\epsilon . \]
Proof.
The candidate \(U\defeq(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\) is a supersolution. Indeed, by the three hypotheses and \(\int g\le1\),
\[ f+\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt U\le h^2+\sqrt\epsilon\Big(2h+\frac{w^2}{\sqrt\epsilon}+2w+\sqrt\epsilon\Big) =h^2+w^2+\epsilon+2\sqrt\epsilon\,(h+w)\le U . \]
Since \(U\ge\epsilon\), Lemma SI.3 with kernel \(\sqrt\epsilon\,g\), \(G=f\) and \(m=\epsilon\) gives \(Y\le U\) on \([0,u_0]\). Then \(g*\sqrt Y\le g*(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)\le G\) by the hypotheses and \(\int g\le1\).
□
