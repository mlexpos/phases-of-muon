Corollary SC.1 (The drift kernel)
Under Assumption S3, let \(\Gtilde=\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)\) be the frozen-state update and put \((E_\ridge)_{ij}\defeq u_i^\T\big(\EE\Gtilde-\Gtildebar_\ridge\big)v_j\).
  1. (i)
    For every pair \((i,j)\) and every \(\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SC.11} \EE\big[u_i^\T\Gtilde v_j\big]=\frac{\sqrt2\,\mathfrak d(\mu_i,\lambda_j)}{\sqrt{2\cR(t)}}\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)+(E_\ridge)_{ij} =\frac{\mathfrak d(\mu_i,\lambda_j)}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}}\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)+(E_\ridge)_{ij}, \end{equation}
    and \(\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\big(\sum_{i,j}(E_\ridge)_{ij}^2\big)^{1/2}=\varepsilon_B\to0\). Consequently the frozen-state drift (SA.19) of the update \(\Gtilde\) is
    \begin{equation}\tag{SC.12} \cD_{ij}(t)=\frac{\mathfrak d_{ij}}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}}\,\CMscr Q_{ij}(t)+(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)(E_\ridge)_{ij}, \qquad \mathfrak d_{ij}\defeq\mathfrak d(\mu_i,\lambda_j), \end{equation}
    and for every bounded function \(\phi\) on \([0,\infty)^2\),
    \begin{equation}\tag{SC.13} \Big|\sum_{i,j}\phi(\mu_i,\lambda_j)\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)\,(E_\ridge)_{ij}\Big|\le\norm\phi_\infty\,\norm{\Dt}_\F\,\varepsilon_B . \end{equation}
  2. (ii)
    In the frozen-state model with \(\max(\Nout,\Nin)\le\gamma_\star B\) and \(\norm{\Sigmaout},\norm{\Sigmain}\le C_\Sigma\), at every finite dimension and for every \(\ridge>0\), the function \(\mathfrak d\) is continuous on \([0,\infty)^2\), vanishes when \(\mu=0\) or \(\lambda=0\), and satisfies
    \begin{equation}\tag{SC.14} 0\le\mathfrak d(\mu,\lambda)\le\frac{\mu\lambda}{\sqrt{2\ridge}},\qquad \abs{\partial_\mu\mathfrak d(\mu,\lambda)}\le\frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{2\ridge}},\qquad \abs{\partial_\lambda\mathfrak d(\mu,\lambda)}\le\frac{\mu}{\sqrt{2\ridge}}, \end{equation}
    with one-sided derivatives at \(0\); in particular, for \(\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]\), \(\mathfrak d\) is Lipschitz on \([0,C_\Sigma]^2\) with constant \(C_\Sigma/\sqrt{2\ridge_-}\) in each variable.
Proof of Corollary SC.1.
The candidate is diagonal in the eigenbases. For \(s>0\), since \(\Sigmaout u_i=\mu_iu_i\) and \(\Sigmain v_j=\lambda_jv_j\),
\[ \begin{gathered} u_i^\T\Rbar(-s)=\frac{u_i^\T}{s+\tilde s_1(-s)\mu_i},\qquad \Rtbar(-s)v_j=\frac{v_j}{s+s_1(-s)\lambda_j},\\ u_i^\T\,\Sigmaout\Dt\Sigmain\,v_j=\mu_i\lambda_j\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j), \end{gathered} \]
so that, by (SC.2),
\[ u_i^\T T(s)v_j=\frac{s\,\Lw(q(-s))\,\mu_i\lambda_j}{\big(s+\tilde s_1(-s)\mu_i\big)\big(s+s_1(-s)\lambda_j\big)}\cdot\frac{u_i^\T\Dt v_j}{\sqrt{2\cR(t)}} . \]
Integrating against \(\frac2\pi\,du\) at \(s=\ridge+u^2\) and comparing with (SC.4), in which the prefactor is \(\sqrt2/\pi=(2/\pi)/\sqrt2\),
\[ u_i^\T\,\Gtildebar_\ridge\,v_j=\frac{\sqrt2\,\mathfrak d(\mu_i,\lambda_j)}{\sqrt{2\cR(t)}}\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)=\frac{\mathfrak d(\mu_i,\lambda_j)}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}}\,(u_i^\T\Dt v_j). \]
Adding \((E_\ridge)_{ij}\) gives (SC.11), and the Frobenius norm is computed in the orthonormal bases: \(\sum_{i,j}(E_\ridge)_{ij}^2=\norm{\EE\Gtilde-\Gtildebar_\ridge}_\F^2\), whose supremum over the ridge is \(\varepsilon_B^2\) by Theorem SC.2.
The drift is the projection times the projected state. In the frozen-state model the state is deterministic and the update is \(\Gtilde\), so the drift (SA.19) is \(\cD_{ij}(t)=(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)\,\EE[u_i^\T\Gtilde v_j]\); multiplying (SC.11) by \(u_i^\T\Dt v_j\) and using \(\CMscr Q_{ij}(t)=(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)^2\) gives (SC.12). The tested bound (SC.13) is the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality in \(\ell^2\) over the pairs \((i,j)\), with \(\sum_{i,j}(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)^2=\norm{\Dt}_\F^2\).
The kernel bounds follow from the integrand. Since \(\tilde s_1,s_1\ge0\), \(0\le\Lw\le1\) and \(z\ge\ridge\), the integrand of (SC.4) lies between \(0\) and \(\mu\lambda/z\), and
\[ \frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\int_0^\infty\frac{du}{\ridge+u^2}=\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt\ridge}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\ridge}}, \]
which gives the first bound of (SC.14); the integrand vanishes when \(\mu=0\) or \(\lambda=0\). Differentiating the integrand in \(\mu\),
\[ \partial_\mu\frac{z\,\Lw\,\mu\lambda}{(z+\tilde s_1\mu)(z+s_1\lambda)}=\frac{z^2\,\Lw\,\lambda}{(z+\tilde s_1\mu)^2(z+s_1\lambda)}\in\Big[0,\frac{\lambda}{z}\Big], \]
and integrating gives the bound on \(\partial_\mu\mathfrak d\); the bound on \(\partial_\lambda\mathfrak d\) is symmetric. Continuity on \([0,\infty)^2\) and the one-sided derivatives at the boundary follow from the same bounds, which are uniform in \((\mu,\lambda)\) on bounded sets. The Lipschitz constant on \([0,C_\Sigma]^2\) is the maximum of the derivative bounds there.
□
