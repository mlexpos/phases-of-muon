Lemma SB.7 (Bessel bounds)
Let \(z\sim\mathcal N(0,\Id_{\Nout})\) and \(w\sim\mathcal N(0,\Id_{\Nin})\) be independent of each other and of a \(\sigma\)-field \(\mathcal G\), and let \(e\) be a real or complex random variable depending on \(z\), \(w\) and \(\mathcal G\). Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SB.21} \begin{gathered} \norm{\EE_{\mathcal G}[(z\tensor w)\,e]}_\F\le\norm{e}_{2,\mathcal G},\qquad \norm{\EE_{\mathcal G}[(z\tensor z-\Id)\,e]}_\F\le\sqrt2\,\norm{e}_{2,\mathcal G},\\ \norm{\EE_{\mathcal G}[(w\tensor w-\Id)\,e]}_\F\le\sqrt2\,\norm{e}_{2,\mathcal G}. \end{gathered} \end{equation}
Proof.
Since \((z,w)\) is independent of \(\mathcal G\), it suffices to prove the inequalities with \(\EE\) in place of \(\EE_{\mathcal G}\), in the \(L^2\) space of \((z,w)\).
The inequalities are Bessel’s inequality for two orthonormal families. By the Gaussian fourth-moment identity (SA.30) and the independence of \(z\) and \(w\), the products \(z_iw_l\) (\(i\le\Nout\), \(l\le\Nin\)) form an orthonormal family in \(L^2\), and the variables \((z_i^2-1)/\sqrt2\) (\(i\le\Nout\)) and \(z_iz_k\) (\(i<k\)) form another orthonormal family.
Bessel’s inequality for the first family gives the first bound:
\[ \norm{\EE[(z\tensor w)\,e]}_\F^2=\sum_{i,l}\abs{\EE[z_iw_le]}^2\le\EE\abs e^2 . \]
Bessel’s inequality for the second family gives
\[ \sum_{i<k}\abs{\EE[z_iz_ke]}^2+\sum_i\frac{\abs{\EE[(z_i^2-1)e]}^2}{2}\le\EE\abs e^2 . \]
Each off-diagonal entry of the symmetric matrix \(\EE[(z\tensor z-\Id)\,e]\) occurs twice in its squared Frobenius norm, so
\[ \norm{\EE[(z\tensor z-\Id)\,e]}_\F^2=2\sum_{i<k}\abs{\EE[z_iz_ke]}^2+\sum_i\abs{\EE[(z_i^2-1)e]}^2\le2\,\EE\abs e^2 , \]
which is the second bound. The third bound is the second with \(w\) in place of \(z\).
□
