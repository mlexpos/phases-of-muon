Proposition SF.10 (Lag coefficients in participation coordinates)
Let \(\ridge\ge0\) and let \(\ell\ge1\) be an integer. Then \(\widehat\Delta(y,z)>0\) whenever \(y<Q_{\psi(\ridge)}\) and \(z_\ell<Q_{\psi(\ridge)}\), and
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.55} \overline C_{1,\ell}(\ridge)=\frac1{\pi^2\gamma^3}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty D(y,z)\, \mathbf 1\{y<Q_{\psi(\ridge)},\ z_\ell<Q_{\psi(\ridge)}\}\, \frac{\mathsf j(y)\,\mathsf u_\ridge(y)\;\mathsf j(z_\ell)\,\mathsf u_\ridge(z_\ell)}{\widehat\Delta(y,z)}\,\dif y\,\dif z . \end{equation}
Proof.
The proof writes the two scalars of the isotropic lag kernel as divided differences of \(r\) at the pair \((y,z)\), and then changes variables from the spectral parameters to the roots.
The kernel at a pair of roots. By Lemma SF.11(ii), the lag kernel at \((s,s')\) is \(b_\ell\,qq'/(\gamma^3\Delta_\ell)\) with \(q=q(-s)\) and \(q'=q(-s')\). Put \(y\defeq q\) and \(z\defeq\bmom^{2\ell}q'\). The divided-difference forms (SG.15) and \(\Fw(x)=r(x)/x\) give
\[ \sqrt{qq'}\,b_\ell=\sqrt{yz}\,\frac{r(y)-r(z)}{y-z}=\mathcal Z(y,z),\qquad qq'\,e_\ell=yz\,\frac{\Fw(y)-\Fw(z)}{z-y}=\mathcal X(y,z). \]
The determinant of Lemma SF.11 is therefore \(\Delta_\ell=\widehat\Delta(y,z)\), which is positive by Lemma SF.11(i).
Change of variables. In the square case the closure (SF.7) inverts as \(s=\mathsf s(q)\defeq\mathsf A(q)^2/q\). The map \(\mathsf s\) is continuously differentiable and strictly decreasing from \((0,Q_\gamma)\) onto \((0,\infty)\), with
\[ \mathsf s'(y)=\frac{-2y\,r'(y)\,\mathsf A(y)-\mathsf A(y)^2}{y^2}=-\frac{\mathsf A(y)\,\mathsf j(y)}{y^2}, \]
and \(q(-\mathsf s(y))=y\) by the uniqueness of Proposition SF.1(ii). For \(\ridge>0\) one has \(\mathsf s(Q_{\psi(\ridge)})=\ridge\), by the closure at \(s=\ridge\). Hence \(x=\sqrt{\mathsf s(y)-\ridge}\) is a decreasing bijection of \((0,Q_{\psi(\ridge)})\) onto \((0,\infty)\) for every \(\ridge\ge0\), with
\[ \Big|\frac{\dif x}{\dif y}\Big|=\frac{\mathsf A(y)\,\mathsf j(y)}{2y^2\sqrt{\mathsf A(y)^2/y-\ridge}}=\frac{\mathsf j(y)\,\mathsf u_\ridge(y)}{2y^{3/2}} . \]
Apply this substitution in both variables of (SF.52), with \(y=q(-\ridge-x^2)\) and \(y'=q(-\ridge-x'^2)\). Since the kernel is \(b_\ell\,yy'/(\gamma^3\widehat\Delta)\),
\[ \begin{aligned} \overline C_{1,\ell}(\ridge)&=\frac4{\pi^2}\iint_{y,y'<Q_{\psi(\ridge)}}\frac{b_\ell\,yy'}{\gamma^3\,\widehat\Delta}\cdot \frac{\mathsf j(y)\mathsf u_\ridge(y)\,\mathsf j(y')\mathsf u_\ridge(y')}{4\,(yy')^{3/2}}\,\dif y\,\dif y'\\ &=\frac1{\pi^2\gamma^3}\iint_{y,y'<Q_{\psi(\ridge)}}\frac{b_\ell\,\mathsf j(y)\mathsf u_\ridge(y)\,\mathsf j(y')\mathsf u_\ridge(y')}{\widehat\Delta\,\sqrt{yy'}}\,\dif y\,\dif y' . \end{aligned} \]
The lag shift. Substitute \(y'=\bmom^{-2\ell}z=z_\ell\). Then
\[ \frac{b_\ell\,\dif y'}{\sqrt{yy'}}=\bmom^\ell\,\frac{r(y)-r(z)}{y-z}\cdot\frac{\bmom^{-2\ell}\,\dif z}{\bmom^{-\ell}\sqrt{yz}}=D(y,z)\,\dif z , \]
which is (SF.55).
□
