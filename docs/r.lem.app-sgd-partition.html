Lemma SJ.12 (Fourth moments of sums)
Let \(Z\in\R^{\Nout}\) be centered with covariance \(V\) and satisfy (SJ.36) and (SJ.37), let \(Z_1,\dots,Z_B\) be independent copies, and put \(S_s\defeq\sum_{b\le s}Z_b\),
\[ \mathcal W\defeq\sum_{b\ne b'}\langle Z_b,Z_{b'}\rangle,\qquad \mathcal D_B\defeq\sum_{b=1}^B\big(\norm{Z_b}^2-\Tr V\big). \]
Then, for \(0\le s\le B\) and \(a\in\R^{\Nout}\),
\begin{gather*} \EE\norm{S_s}^4\le3A_4s^2\Nout^2,\qquad \EE(a^\T S_s)^4\le3A_4s^2\norm a^4,\\ \EE\mathcal W^4\le144A_4^2B^4\Nout^2,\qquad \EE\mathcal D_B^4\le(16A_8+3A_4^2)B^2\Nout^4 . \end{gather*}
Proof.
Sums. In the expansion of \(\norm{S_s}^4\), a term in which some sample index occurs once has mean zero. The remaining terms give
\[ \EE\norm{S_s}^4=s\,\EE\norm Z^4+s(s-1)\big[(\Tr V)^2+2\Tr V^2\big]. \]
With \(\Tr V\le\Nout\sqrt{A_4}\) and \(\Tr V^2\le\Nout A_4\), which follow from \(a^\T Va\le(\EE\abs{a^\T Z}^4)^{1/2}\), this is at most \(3A_4s^2\Nout^2\). The scalar case is \(s\,\EE(a^\T Z)^4+3s(s-1)(a^\T Va)^2\le3A_4s^2\norm a^4\).
The off-diagonal sum. A random partition splits \(\mathcal W\) into independent sums. Let \(\epsilon_1,\dots,\epsilon_B\) be independent fair Bernoulli selectors, independent of the \(Z_b\). For fixed vectors,
\[ \mathcal W=4\,\EE_\epsilon\Big\langle\sum_b\epsilon_bZ_b,\ \sum_{b'}(1-\epsilon_{b'})Z_{b'}\Big\rangle , \]
because diagonal coefficients vanish and each ordered off-diagonal pair has coefficient \(\frac14\) in mean. Jensen’s inequality gives a factor \(4^4\). For a fixed partition into \(s\) and \(s'\) samples the two sums are independent; conditioning on the second and applying the two bounds above gives
\[ \EE\abs{\langle S_s,S'_{s'}\rangle}^4\le3A_4s^2\,\EE\norm{S'_{s'}}^4\le9A_4^2s^2s'^2\Nout^2 . \]
With \(ss'\le B^2/4\), the constant is \(4^4\cdot9/16=144\).
The diagonal sum. For \(Y\defeq\norm Z^2-\Tr V\), Jensen’s inequality gives \(\EE Y^4\le16\,\EE\norm Z^8\le16A_8\Nout^4\) and \(\EE Y^2\le\EE\norm Z^4\le A_4\Nout^2\), and for independent centered summands \(\EE\mathcal D_B^4=B\,\EE Y^4+3B(B-1)(\EE Y^2)^2\).
□
