Lemma SG.15 (Age factorization)
For real \(g\) and \(q>0\) put \(\mathcal Z_g(q)\defeq\sqrt q\sum_{a\ge0}\emaw ag\,(1+\emaw a^2qg^2)^{-1}\).
  1. (i)
    \(\abs{\mathcal Z_g(q)}\le C_\bmom\) and \(\EE\,\mathcal Z_g(q)^2\le q\), for \(g\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\).
  2. (ii)
    For \(m_0>0\) and \(\mathcal A_g(m)\defeq\mathcal Z_g(Q_m)/E(m)\),
    \[ \sup_{t\ge m_0}\Big|\int_{m_0}^t\big(\mathcal A_g(m)-\pi\sign(g)\big)\,\dif m\Big|\le C_{\bmom,m_0}\big(1+\abs{\log\abs g}\big)\qquad(g\ne0). \]
  3. (iii)
    \(\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z}\mathsf D_\ell(m_1,m_2)=\EE\big[\mathcal Z_g(Q_{m_1})\mathcal Z_g(Q_{m_2})\big]\).
  4. (iv)
    The flat coefficient \(\mathfrak v^{\mathrm{flat}}_i\defeq\EE\big[\big(\frac1\pi\int_0^\gamma W_i(m)\mathcal Z_g(Q_m)\,\dif m\big)^2\big]\) satisfies \(0\le\mathfrak v^{\mathrm{flat}}_i\le\mathfrak v_i(0)\), and it is the integral of (SG.22) summed over the lags with every \(\Delta^N_\ell\) replaced by one.
Proof.
With \(t\defeq(1-\bmom)\sqrt q\abs g\) and \(\phi(t)\defeq t/(1+t^2)\), one has \(\mathcal Z_g(q)=\sign(g)\sum_a\phi(t\bmom^a)\). Splitting at the last \(a\) with \(t\bmom^a\ge1\) and using \(\phi(t)\le\min(t,t^{-1})\) bounds the sum by two geometric series, uniformly in \(t\). The second bound of (i) is \(\abs{\mathcal Z_g(q)}\le\sqrt q\abs g\sum_a\emaw a=\sqrt q\abs g\).
For (ii), since \(\dif m/E(m)=\dif q/q\) at \(q=Q_m\) and \(\int\phi(\tau)\,2\dif\tau/\tau=2\arctan\tau\),
\[ \int_{m_0}^t\mathcal A_g(m)\,\dif m=2\sign(g)\sum_{a\ge0}\Big[\arctan\big(\emaw a\abs g\sqrt{Q_t}\big)-\arctan\big(\emaw a\abs g\sqrt{Q_{m_0}}\big)\Big]. \]
The same splitting, with \(\arctan\tau\le\tau\) and \(\pi/2-\arctan\tau\le\tau^{-1}\), gives \(2\sum_a\arctan(\tau\bmom^a)=\pi\log_+\tau/\abs{\log\bmom}+O_\bmom(1)\) uniformly in \(\tau>0\), and Lemma SG.1(iii) gives \(t=r_\bmom(Q_t)=\log_+((1-\bmom)\sqrt{Q_t})/\abs{\log\bmom}+O_{\bmom,m_0}(1)\) for \(t\ge m_0\). Since \(\abs{\log_+(\abs g\tau)-\log_+\tau}\le\abs{\log\abs g}\), subtraction proves (ii).
For (iii), expand \(\mathcal Z_g(q_1)\mathcal Z_g(q_2)\) over ordered pairs of ages \((a,b)\). A pair with \(b=a+\ell\ge a\) has \(\emaw b=\bmom^\ell\emaw a\), and its term is the \(a\)-th term of \(\mathsf D_\ell(m_1,m_2)\); a pair with \(b<a\) gives the term of the negative lag. The scalar \(g\) is the same in each pair, and all terms are nonnegative.
For (iv), (iii) identifies the flat integral, and \(0<\Delta^N_\ell\le1\) (Lemma SG.14) with nonnegative terms gives the inequality.
□
