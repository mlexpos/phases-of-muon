Proposition SC.1 (History truncation)
Under Assumption S3, for \(J\ge J_w\defeq\min\{j:\emaw j>0\}\) let \(\emaw j^{(J)}\defeq\emaw j\mathbf 1_{j\le J}\), let \(M^{(J)}=\sum_{j\le J}\emaw jG_{t+1-j}\) be the truncated buffer of Lemma SA.6, and let \(T^{(J)}\) and \(\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge\) be the candidates (SC.2) and (SC.3) built from the solution of the system (SB.2) for the weights \(\emaw{}^{(J)}\).
  1. (i)
    For every fixed \(J\), \(\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(M^{(J)})-\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge}_\F\to0\) as the dimension grows.
  2. (ii)
    \(\lim_{J\to\infty}\sup_B\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\norm{\Gtildebar_\ridge-\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge}_\F=0\), where \(\sup_B\) runs over the whole sequence of dimensions.
  3. (iii)
    Consequently
    \begin{equation}\tag{SC.10} \lim_{J\to\infty}\limsup_{B\to\infty}\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]} \norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(M^{(J)})}_\F=0 . \end{equation}
Proof of Proposition SC.1.
Part (i) is Theorem SC.2 for the weights \(\emaw{}^{(J)}\), which belong to the class (SC.1) for \(J\ge J_w\); the buffer \(M^{(J)}\) is the frozen-state buffer with these weights.
Part (ii) is the stability of the branch. Fix a compact \(I\subset(0,\infty)\) and let \((\sigma^J,\tilde\sigma^J,a^J,c^J)(-s)\) be the solution of (SB.2) for \(\emaw{}^{(J)}\) at \(z=-s\), with \(q^J=s\sigma^J\tilde\sigma^J\). It is an approximate solution of the system for the full weights. Its first two residuals vanish, because the trace equations do not involve the weights; its third residual is
\[ \abs{a^J-s\tilde\sigma^JF_{\emaw{}}(q^J)}=s\tilde\sigma^J\,\abs{F_{\emaw{}^{(J)}}(q^J)-F_{\emaw{}}(q^J)} =s\tilde\sigma^J\sum_{j>J}\emaw j^2\Jfun(\emaw j^2q^J)\le C_\Sigma\gamma_\star\sum_{j>J}\emaw j^2 , \]
by \(\Jfun\le1\) and the box (SB.6), and the fourth residual has the same bound. The tuple lies in the box of Theorem SB.1(iii) for the full weights, since \(\sw2^{(J)}\le\sw2\). Given \(\epsilon>0\), that theorem provides \(\delta>0\), and \(J_0\) with \(C_\Sigma\gamma_\star\sum_{j>J_0}\emaw j^2\le\delta\), such that for all \(J\ge J_0\), all \(s\in I\) and all spectral measures in \(\mathcal K\), hence uniformly in the dimension,
\[ \abs{\sigma^J-\sigma}+\abs{\tilde\sigma^J-\tilde\sigma}+\abs{a^J-\tilde s_1}+\abs{c^J-s_1}\le\epsilon . \]
The candidates are then close. The resolvent identity gives \(\norm{\Rbar^{(J)}(-s)-\Rbar(-s)}\le C_\Sigma\epsilon/s_-^2\) and the same for \(\Rtbar\), and the source factors satisfy
\[ \abs{L_{\emaw{}^{(J)}}(q^J)-\Lw(q)}\le\sum_{j>J}\emaw j+\abs{\Lw(q^J)-\Lw(q)}\le\sum_{j>J}\emaw j+3\sw3\,\abs{q^J-q}, \]
by Lemma SB.1(ii), with \(\abs{q^J-q}\le s\sigma^J\abs{\tilde\sigma^J-\tilde\sigma}+s\tilde\sigma\abs{\sigma^J-\sigma}\le C_\Sigma\gamma_\star\epsilon\). Telescoping the three factors of \(T\) with the bounds \(\norm{\Rbar},\norm{\Rtbar}\le1/s\), \(\Lw\le1\) and \(\norm{\EE\Gt}_\F\le C_\Sigma\),
\[ \sup_{s\in I}\norm{T^{(J)}(s)-T(s)}_\F\le C_I\Big(\epsilon+\sum_{j>J}\emaw j\Big)\qquad(J\ge J_0), \]
uniformly in the dimension. Splitting the integral (SC.3) at a cutoff \(\mathsf R\ge1\) as in the proof of Theorem SC.2, with \(\norm{T^{(J)}(s)}_\F,\norm{T(s)}_\F\le C_\Sigma/s\),
\[ \sup_B\sup_{\ridge\in[\ridge_-,\ridge_+]}\norm{\Gtildebar_\ridge-\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge}_\F \le\frac2\pi\Big[\mathsf R\,C_{I_{\mathsf R}}\Big(\epsilon+\sum_{j>J}\emaw j\Big)+\frac{2C_\Sigma}{\mathsf R}\Big], \]
and choosing first \(\mathsf R\) large, then \(\epsilon\) small and \(J\) large, proves (ii).
Part (iii) is the triangle inequality
\[ \norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(M^{(J)})}_\F \le\norm{\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)-\Gtildebar_\ridge}_\F+\norm{\Gtildebar_\ridge-\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge}_\F+\norm{\Gtildebar^{(J)}_\ridge-\EE\,\Qridge\ridge(M^{(J)})}_\F , \]
in which the first and third terms vanish in the dimension limit at fixed \(J\) by Theorem SC.2 and part (i), and the second is bounded uniformly in the dimension by a quantity vanishing as \(J\to\infty\) by part (ii).
□
