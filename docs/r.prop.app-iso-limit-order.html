Proposition SF.11 (Order of limits at positive ridge)
  1. (i)
    For fixed \(\gin,\gout>0\) and \(\ridge>0\), \(\lim_{\bmom\downarrow0}\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gin,\gout)=\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;0,\gin,\gout)\).
  2. (ii)
    For \(\gamma\ge2\) and \(\ridge\ge0\),
    \[ \Big|\gamma\,\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;0,\gamma,\gamma)-\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\Big|\le\frac{\sqrt2\,\big(1+\sqrt{2\pi}/2+\sqrt\ridge\big)}{\pi\gamma} . \]
  3. (iii)
    For every \(\ridge>0\), \(\lim_{\gamma\to\infty}\lim_{\bmom\downarrow0}\gamma\,\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gamma,\gamma)=\pi^{-1/2}\), whereas for every fixed \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\gamma\,\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{iso}}(\ridge;\bmom,\gamma,\gamma)\to\infty\) as \(\gamma\to\infty\).
Proof.
For (i), write \(q_\bmom(s)\) for the root at momentum \(\bmom\). The link functions converge pointwise: \(\emaw0=1-\bmom\to1\) and \(\Phi\) is continuous, and
\[ \sum_{j\ge1}\Phi(\emaw j^2q)\le q\sum_{j\ge1}\emaw j^2=q\big(\sw2-(1-\bmom)^2\big)\longrightarrow0, \]
so \(r_\bmom(q)\to\Phi(q)\). By Lemma SF.4(iii), \(\abs{\Jfun'}\le3\), \(\Jfun\le1\) and \(\sum_{j\ge1}\emaw j=\bmom\) give
\[ \abs{\Lw(q)-\Jfun(q)}\le\abs{(1-\bmom)\Jfun(\emaw0^2q)-\Jfun(q)}+\bmom\le3q\big(1-\emaw0^2\big)+2\bmom . \]
The roots converge. Fix \(s>0\) and \(\delta\in(0,q_0(s))\) with \(\Phi(q_0(s)+\delta)<\gamma_\wedge\). The function \(G\) of Lemma SF.5(i) at \(r=\Phi\) is negative at \(q_0(s)-\delta\) and positive at \(q_0(s)+\delta\); by the pointwise convergence the same holds at \(r=r_\bmom\) for small \(\bmom\), both points lying in \(D_{r_\bmom}\), so \(\abs{q_\bmom(s)-q_0(s)}<\delta\). With the bound on \(\Lw-\Jfun\) and \(\abs{\Jfun'}\le3\), the integrand of (SF.29) converges pointwise. It is at most \(q_\bmom(s)\le\gin\gout/s\le\min(\gin\gout/\ridge,\gin\gout/u^2)\) at \(s=\ridge+u^2\), by Lemma SF.4(v) and the proof of Proposition SF.5(i), and dominated convergence gives (i).
For (ii), apply Lemma SF.8 with \(\bmom=0\) and \(\gin=\gout=\gamma\ge1\), where \(-\mathsf W'=1\) and \(\mathsf W(1)=\gamma-1\), and write \(h_\ridge\defeq\psi(\ridge)<1\). For the lower bound, drop the integral term of \(\widetilde{\mathcal I}\):
\[ \int_0^\infty\mathsf G_0(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u\ge\tfrac12(\gamma-h_\ridge)\Lambda_0(Q_{h_\ridge})\ge\tfrac12(\gamma-1)\Lambda_0(Q_{h_\ridge}), \]
and use \(\sqrt{2\pi}-\Lambda_0(Q)\le2Q^{-1/2}\) (Lemma SF.4(iv)) with \(Q_{h_\ridge}=(\gamma-h_\ridge)^2/\ridge\ge(\gamma-1)^2/\ridge\); at \(\ridge=0\) the boundary term is \(\frac12(\gamma-1)\sqrt{2\pi}\) directly. This gives
\[ \int_0^\infty\mathsf G_0(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u\ge\frac{(\gamma-1)\sqrt{2\pi}}2-\sqrt\ridge . \]
For the upper bound, the integrand is nonincreasing in \(\ridge\), and at \(\ridge=0\) Lemma SF.7 at \(\bmom=0\) (\(\sqrt q\Jfun(q)\le1\)) and \(\Lambda_0\le\sqrt{2\pi}\) give
\[ \int_0^\infty\mathsf G_0(\ridge+u^2)\,\dif u\le\int_0^1\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}2\Big)\dif m+\frac{(\gamma-1)\sqrt{2\pi}}2 . \]
Multiplying by \(\sqrt2/(\pi\gamma)\), with \(\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi\gamma}\cdot\frac{(\gamma-1)\sqrt{2\pi}}2=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}-\frac1{\sqrt\pi\gamma}\) and \(\pi^{-1/2}\le\sqrt2(1+\sqrt{2\pi}/2)/\pi\), gives (ii).
For (iii), (i) at each \(\gamma\) and then (ii) give the first limit, and Corollary SF.2(ii) gives the second.
□
