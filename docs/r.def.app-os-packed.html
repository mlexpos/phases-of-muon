Definition SH.2 (Packed mode)
Let \(K\ge1\) and \(\delta\in(0,1]\). In the square case \(\Nout=\Nin=N\), an eigenvalue \(\mu_i>0\) of \(\Sigmaout\) is \((K,\delta)\)-packed if
\[ \#\{k\le N:\ \mu_k\le K\mu_i\}\ge\delta N . \]
