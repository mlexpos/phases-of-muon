Lemma SI.22 (Two-phase bound)
Assume the setting of Lemma SI.11. Let \(u_0>0\), and let \(h,w\ge0\) be continuous on \([0,u_0]\) and satisfy the three hypotheses of Lemma SI.20 there. Let \(G\) be as in that lemma and
\[ \Theta_0\defeq1+\frac{h(u_0)+w(u_0)}{\sqrt\epsilon} . \]
  1. (a)
    On \([0,u_0]\), \(Y\le(h+w+\sqrt\epsilon)^2\) and \(\sqrt Y\le\sqrt f+\epsilon^{1/4}G^{1/2}\).
  2. (b)
    If \(h(H)+w(H)\le\sqrt\epsilon\) for some \(H\in[0,u_0]\), then \(\hat u\le H\).
  3. (c)
    With \(\mathsf I\defeq\int_0^{\hat u}\sqrt Y\),
    \[ \begin{gathered} \mathsf I\le\int_0^{u_0}\sqrt Y\,\dif u+\big(\tilde{\mathsf J}^{1/2}+\tilde{\mathsf d}^{1/2}\big)^2,\\ \tilde{\mathsf J}\le\int_{u_0}^\infty\sqrt f\,\dif u+\frac2{b_{\min}}\sqrt\epsilon\,\Theta_0,\qquad \tilde{\mathsf d}\le\frac4{b_{\min}}\sqrt\epsilon\,\log\Theta_0 . \end{gathered} \]
Proof.
Part (a) is Lemma SI.20, together with \(Y-f=\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt Y\le\sqrt\epsilon\,G\) and \(\sqrt{a+b}\le\sqrt a+\sqrt b\).
For (b), part (a) gives \(Y(H)\le4\epsilon\le T\epsilon\).
For (c), the bound is immediate if \(\hat u\le u_0\). Otherwise \(Y>T\epsilon\) on \([0,u_0]\), and Lemma SI.21 applies with \(G_0\le G(u_0)\). By (a),
\[ Y(u_0)\le\big(h(u_0)+w(u_0)+\sqrt\epsilon\big)^2=\epsilon\,\Theta_0^2 , \]
which gives the bound on \(\tilde{\mathsf d}\). Expanding the square,
\[ \sqrt\epsilon\,\Theta_0^2=\sqrt\epsilon+2(h+w)(u_0)+\frac{(h+w)(u_0)^2}{\sqrt\epsilon}\ge G(u_0) , \]
so \(\epsilon^{1/4}G(u_0)^{1/2}\le\sqrt\epsilon\,\Theta_0\), which gives the bound on \(\tilde{\mathsf J}\).
□
