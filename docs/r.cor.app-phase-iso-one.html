Corollary SI.1 (The value at \(\gamma=1\))
The function \(\rho\) of Proposition SI.3 satisfies
\[ \rho(1)=\frac14\Big(1+\frac{\log(1+\sqrt2)}{\sqrt2}\Big)^2\approx0.6587 . \]
Proof.
By the definition of \(\rho\) at \(\gamma=1\), \(\rho(1)=\pi\,C(1)^2\). Theorem SF.6 evaluates the scalar coefficient at aspect ratio one,
\[ C(1)=\frac{1+\log(1+\sqrt2)/\sqrt2}{2\sqrt\pi}. \]
Squaring and multiplying by \(\pi\) gives the display.
□
