Theorem SI.12 (Accuracy-first ratio)
Fix \(\alpha>0\) and \(\texp\) with \(p=\alpha+\texp>1\). As \(N\to\infty\):
  1. (a)
    if \(\texp<1\), then \(\rho_N\sim D_NQ_AN^{\alpha/2}\); hence \(\rho_N\to K_A\) for \(0<\alpha<1\), while \(\rho_N\) is asymptotic to \(\frac\pi2Q_A\log N\), \(\pi(1-\frac\alpha2)\zeta(\alpha)Q_AN^{\alpha-1}\), \(\pi\zeta(2)Q_AN/\log N\) and \(\frac{\pi\zeta(\alpha)}{\zeta(\alpha/2)}Q_AN^{\alpha/2}\) for \(\alpha=1\), \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\alpha=2\) and \(\alpha>2\);
  2. (b)
    if \(\texp=1\), then \(\rho_N\sim D_N\frac{2\sqrt\pi}\alpha\,N^{\alpha/2}/\log N\); hence \(\rho_N\log N\to\pi^{3/2}(2-\alpha)/(\alpha(1-\alpha))\) for \(0<\alpha<1\), \(\rho_N\to\pi^{3/2}\) for \(\alpha=1\), and \(\rho_N\to\infty\) for \(\alpha>1\), with order \(N^{\alpha-1}/\log N\), \(N/\log^2N\) and \(N^{\alpha/2}/\log N\) for \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\alpha=2\) and \(\alpha>2\);
  3. (c)
    if \(1<\texp<\alpha+1\), then
    \[ \rho_N\sim D_N\,\frac{\mathcal K(s/2)}{\sqrt{8\alpha}\,J(p,\alpha/2)}\,N^{(\alpha+1-\texp)/2} ; \]
    hence \(\rho_N\to0\) for \(0<\alpha\le1\), with order \(N^{(1-\texp)/2}\) (times \(\log N\) at \(\alpha=1\)); \(\rho_N\asymp N^{(2\alpha-1-\texp)/2}\) for \(1<\alpha<2\), and on the chord \(\texp=2\alpha-1\), \(\rho_N\to K_c\); and \(\rho_N\to\infty\) for \(\alpha\ge2\);
  4. (d)
    if \(\texp=\alpha+1\), then \(\rho_N\sim D_N\sqrt{\alpha/8}\,\log N/J(p,\alpha/2)\); hence \(\rho_N\to0\) for \(0<\alpha<2\), \(\rho_N\to\pi\zeta(2)/(2J(5,1))\) for \(\alpha=2\), and \(\rho_N\to\infty\) with order \(\log N\) for \(\alpha>2\);
  5. (e)
    if \(\texp>\alpha+1\), then \(\rho_N\sim D_NJ(p,\alpha)/J(p,\alpha/2)\); hence \(\rho_N\to0\) for \(0<\alpha\le2\) (with order \(1/\log N\) at \(\alpha=2\)), and \(\rho_N\to\pi\zeta(\alpha)J(p,\alpha)/(\zeta(\alpha/2)J(p,\alpha/2))\in(0,\infty)\) for \(\alpha>2\).
Proof of Theorem SI.12.
The proof multiplies the asymptotics of three factors. For SignSVD, \(q=\alpha/2\), \(r=s\) and \(q(1-r)=(1-\texp)/2\); for SignSGD, \(q=\alpha\), \(r=s/2\) and \(q(1-r)=(\alpha+1-\texp)/2\). By (SI.43),
\[ J_N(p,\tfrac\alpha2)\ \begin{cases} \sim(4\alpha)^{-1/2}\mathcal K(s)\,N^{(1-\texp)/2},&\texp<1,\\ \sim\frac{\sqrt\alpha}4\log N,&\texp=1,\\ \to J(p,\alpha/2),&\texp>1, \end{cases} \]
and
\[ J_N(p,\alpha)\ \begin{cases} \sim(8\alpha)^{-1/2}\mathcal K(\frac s2)\,N^{(\alpha+1-\texp)/2},&\texp<\alpha+1,\\ \sim\sqrt{\alpha/8}\,\log N,&\texp=\alpha+1,\\ \to J(p,\alpha),&\texp>\alpha+1 . \end{cases} \]
At \(\texp=1\) one has \(s/2=\frac12\), and Proposition SI.8(iv) gives \(J_N(p,\alpha)\sim\frac12\sqrt{\pi/\alpha}\,N^{\alpha/2}\), whence
\[ \frac{J_N(p,\alpha)}{J_N(p,\alpha/2)}\sim\frac{2\sqrt\pi}\alpha\,\frac{N^{\alpha/2}}{\log N}\qquad(\texp=1). \]
In case (a) the quotient is
\[ \frac{(8\alpha)^{-1/2}\mathcal K(s/2)}{(4\alpha)^{-1/2}\mathcal K(s)}\,N^{\alpha/2}=Q_A\,N^{\alpha/2} . \]
Products and quotients of \(\sim\)-equivalent positive sequences are \(\sim\)-equivalent, so in each case \(\rho_N\) has the stated equivalent. Multiplying by (SI.31) gives the limits and orders. For \(0<\alpha<1\) in (a), \(D_NN^{\alpha/2}\to\pi(1-\frac\alpha2)/(1-\alpha)\), which gives \(K_A\). At \((\alpha,\texp)=(1,1)\),
\[ \frac\pi2N^{-1/2}\log N\cdot2\sqrt\pi\,\frac{N^{1/2}}{\log N}=\pi^{3/2} . \]
In (c) with \(1<\alpha<2\) the exponent of \(N\) is
\[ \frac\alpha2-1+\frac{\alpha+1-\texp}2=\frac{2\alpha-1-\texp}2 , \]
which vanishes on \(\texp=2\alpha-1\), where \(p=3\alpha-1\) and \(s=(3\alpha-2)/\alpha\); this gives \(K_c\). In (d) at \(\alpha=2\),
\[ \frac{\pi\zeta(2)}{\log N}\cdot\frac{\log N}2=\frac{\pi\zeta(2)}2 , \]
and \(J(p,\alpha/2)=J(5,1)\). Every remaining case is the same substitution, using that a positive power of \(N\) dominates every power of \(\log N\).
□
