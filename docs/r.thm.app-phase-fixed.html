Theorem SI.11 (Fixed arrays)
Assume (M1) with a finite index set and \(\mathcal S>0\), take \(\lr=2\sqrt\epsilon/\mathcal S\), and fix \(T\ge4\). As \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) with all other data fixed,
\[ t_{T\epsilon}\sim\frac{\mathcal S}{2\sqrt\epsilon}\int_0^\infty\sqrt{F(s)}\,\dif s,\qquad 0<\int_0^\infty\sqrt{F(s)}\,\dif s<\infty . \]
Proof of Theorem SI.11.
At the matched step, \(k=\sqrt\epsilon\,g\) with
\[ g(s)=\frac1{\mathcal S}\sum_i\mu_i\mathfrak v_ie^{-2\mathfrak d_is}=\sum_iw_i\,c_i\,e^{-c_is},\qquad c_i\defeq2\mathfrak d_i,\quad w_i\defeq\frac{\mu_i\mathfrak v_i}{2\mathfrak d_i\mathcal S}, \]
and \(\sum_iw_i=1\). The forcing has the rates \(2\mathfrak d_i\), and \(b_{\min}\defeq2\min_i\mathfrak d_i>0\) is fixed. For \(\epsilon<F_0/T\), Lemma SI.11 applies to \(Q=F+\sqrt\epsilon\,g*\sqrt Q\) in the intrinsic time \(s\). With \(\mathsf J=\int_0^\infty\sqrt F\) and \(\mathsf d_\epsilon=(2/b_{\min})\sqrt\epsilon\log(F_0/\epsilon)\) it gives
\[ \mathsf J-\frac{2\sqrt{T\epsilon}}{b_{\min}}\le\int_0^{H_Q(T\epsilon)}\sqrt Q\,\dif s\le\mathsf J+2\sqrt{\mathsf d_\epsilon\mathsf J}+\mathsf d_\epsilon . \]
Since \(\mathsf d_\epsilon\to0\), the clock integral tends to \(\mathsf J\in(0,\infty)\). By Theorem SI.3(i), \(t_{T\epsilon}=\lr^{-1}\int_0^{H_Q(T\epsilon)}\sqrt Q\) and \(\lr^{-1}=\mathcal S/(2\sqrt\epsilon)\).
□
