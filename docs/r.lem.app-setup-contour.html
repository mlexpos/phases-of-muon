Lemma SA.3 (Integral representation)
For every \(K\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\) and \(\ridge>0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.14} \Qridge{\ridge}(K)=\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\big(KK^\T+(\ridge+u^2)\Id_{\Nout}\big)^{-1}K\,du , \end{equation}
and \(\int_0^\infty\norm{(KK^\T+(\ridge+u^2)\Id)^{-1}K}_*\,du\le\tfrac\pi2\min(\Nout,\Nin)\).
Proof.
The identity is a scalar integral applied to each singular value. In the singular value decomposition of \(K\) the integrand is
\[ \big(KK^\T+(\ridge+u^2)\Id_{\Nout}\big)^{-1}K=\sum_k\frac{s_k}{s_k^2+\ridge+u^2}\,p_k\tensor r_k , \]
and for \(b>0\) the substitution \(u=\sqrt b\,v\) gives
\[ \int_0^\infty\frac{du}{b+u^2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt b}\int_0^\infty\frac{dv}{1+v^2}=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt b}. \]
Applying this with \(b=s_k^2+\ridge\) to each term and summing over \(k\) gives (SA.14). The nuclear norm of the integrand is the sum of its coefficients, so
\[ \int_0^\infty\norm{(KK^\T+(\ridge+u^2)\Id)^{-1}K}_*\,du=\frac\pi2\sum_{k:\,s_k>0}\frac{s_k}{\sqrt{s_k^2+\ridge}}\le\frac\pi2\min(\Nout,\Nin). \qedhere \]
□
