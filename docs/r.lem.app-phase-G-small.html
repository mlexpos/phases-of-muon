Lemma SI.24 (SignSGD kernel below the critical exponent)
Let \(\alpha>0\) and \(0<r<1\), and write \(g=g_{\text{s-SGD}}\). There are \(D_{\rm G}\ge1\), \(\tau_{\rm G}>0\) and \(N_{\rm G}\), depending only on \(\alpha\) and \(r\), such that for \(N\ge N_{\rm G}\), \(C>0\) and \(h(u)=C(1+u/D_{\rm G})^{-r}\),
\[ g*h\le2h\qquad\text{on }[0,\tau_{\rm G}N^\alpha] . \]
Proof.
Write \(g=g_1+g_2\) for the two terms of (SI.28). Integrating each exponential gives the exact masses
\[ m_{g_1}(u)=\frac{1-2/\pi}N\sum_{i=1}^N\big(1-e^{-2ui^{-\alpha}}\big),\qquad m_{g_2}(u)=\frac{2/\pi}{H_N(\alpha)}\sum_{i=1}^Ni^{-\alpha}\big(1-e^{-2ui^{-\alpha}}\big). \]
For \(\delta\in(0,1)\) split at \(J\defeq\lfloor\delta N\rfloor\). The terms \(i\le J\) contribute at most \((1-\frac2\pi)\delta\) and \(\frac2\pi H_J(\alpha)/H_N(\alpha)\), with \(H_0\defeq0\). For \(i>J\), \(i^{-\alpha}<(\delta N)^{-\alpha}\) and \(1-e^{-t}\le t\) give at most \(2u\delta^{-\alpha}N^{-\alpha}\) in total. Hence
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.61} m_{g_1}\le\Big(1-\frac2\pi\Big)\big[\delta+2u\delta^{-\alpha}N^{-\alpha}\big],\qquad m_{g_2}\le\frac2\pi\Big[\frac{H_J(\alpha)}{H_N(\alpha)}+2u\delta^{-\alpha}N^{-\alpha}\Big]. \end{equation}
Case \(0<\alpha<1\). By Lemma SI.8, \(H_J(\alpha)\le1+J^{1-\alpha}/(1-\alpha)\), and \(H_N(\alpha)\ge N^{1-\alpha}/(2(1-\alpha))\) when \(N^{1-\alpha}\ge2\); hence
\[ \frac{H_J(\alpha)}{H_N(\alpha)}\le2(1-\alpha)N^{-(1-\alpha)}+2\delta^{1-\alpha} . \]
Take \(\delta\defeq((1-r)/16)^{1/(1-\alpha)}\), \(\tau_{\rm G}\defeq(1-r)\delta^\alpha/8\), \(D_{\rm G}\defeq1\), and \(N_{\rm G}\) with \(N_{\rm G}^{1-\alpha}\ge\max\{2,32(1-\alpha)/(1-r)\}\). Since \(\delta\le\delta^{1-\alpha}=(1-r)/16\), on \([0,\tau_{\rm G}N^\alpha]\)
\[ m_g\le\delta+2\delta^{1-\alpha}+2(1-\alpha)N^{-(1-\alpha)}+2u\delta^{-\alpha}N^{-\alpha} \le(1-r)\Big(\frac1{16}+\frac18+\frac1{16}+\frac14\Big)=\frac{1-r}2 . \]
The kernel is nonincreasing, so Lemma SI.13 gives \(g*h\le\frac12h\).
Case \(\alpha=1\). Take \(\delta\defeq(1-r)/8\) and \(\tau_{\rm G}\defeq\delta(1-r)/16\). By (SI.61), \(m_{g_1}\le(1-r)/4\) on \([0,\tau_{\rm G}N]\), so \(g_1*h\le h/4\) by Lemma SI.13. For \(g_2\), Lemma SI.7(i) gives
\[ g_2(v)\le\frac{4E_{2,1}}{\pi H_N(1)}(1+v)^{-1} . \]
Take \(D_{\rm G}\defeq1\) and split at \(\delta_\circ(1+u)\) with \(\delta_\circ\defeq1-(2/3)^{1/r}\). The early part is at most \(\frac32\cdot\frac2\pi h(u)\), since \(g_2\) has mass \(\frac2\pi\). Since \(\int_0^uh\le(1+u)h(u)/(1-r)\), the late part is at most
\[ \frac{4E_{2,1}}{\pi H_N(1)\,\delta_\circ(1+u)}\int_0^uh \le\frac{4E_{2,1}}{\pi\delta_\circ(1-r)H_N(1)}\,h(u)\le\frac{h(u)}4 \]
once \(\log(N+1)\ge16E_{2,1}/(\pi\delta_\circ(1-r))\), which defines \(N_{\rm G}\); here \(H_N(1)\ge\log(N+1)\) by Lemma SI.8. In total \(g*h\le(\frac12+\frac3\pi)h<2h\).
Case \(\alpha>1\). By Lemma SI.7(i) and \(H_N(\alpha)\ge1\), the kernel satisfies (SI.60) for all \(v\ge0\) with \(q=\alpha\) and
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.62} K_f=\frac4\pi E_{2\alpha,\alpha},\qquad\upsilon=2-\frac1\alpha,\qquad K_s=2\Big(1-\frac2\pi\Big)E_{\alpha,\alpha},\qquad\mathsf b=1-\frac1\alpha , \end{equation}
because \(N^{-1}(1+v)^{-(\alpha-1)/\alpha}=N^{-\alpha}((1+v)/N^\alpha)^{-(\alpha-1)/\alpha}\). Apply Lemma SI.23 with \(r_0=r\) and \(D_{\rm G}\defeq\max\{1,[4K_f\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon}/(1-r)]^{1/(\upsilon-1)}\}\), where \(\delta_\circ=1-(2/3)^{1/r}\). Then \(\mathsf k_f\le\frac14\) and
\[ g*h\le\frac74h+\frac{K_s\delta_\circ^{-\mathsf b}}{1-r}\Big(\frac{D_{\rm G}+u}{N^\alpha}\Big)^{1-\mathsf b}h\le2h \quad\text{when }D_{\rm G}+u\le\tau'N^\alpha, \]
where
\[ \tau'\defeq\Big(\frac{(1-r)\delta_\circ^{\mathsf b}}{4K_s}\Big)^{1/(1-\mathsf b)} . \]
Take \(\tau_{\rm G}\defeq\tau'/2\) and \(N_{\rm G}\) with \(N_{\rm G}^\alpha\ge2D_{\rm G}/\tau'\).
□
