SignSVD wins, SignSGD wins, or the two are balanced according as \(\mathsf R\to\infty\), \(\mathsf R\to0\) or \(\mathsf R\asymp1\). For \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\texp<2\alpha-1\), \(\omega_c\) is the critical exponent of the main text: \(\omega_c=(2-\alpha)(p-1)/\alpha\) if \(\texp<1\) and \(\omega_c=(2-\alpha)(p-1)/(\alpha+1-\texp)\) if \(1\le\texp<2\alpha-1\), so that \(0<\omega_c<p-1\). The constants \(K_A\) and \(K_c\) are the explicit positive limits of \(\rho_N\) given by Theorem SI.12 for \(0<\alpha<1\), \(\texp<1\) and on the chord \(\texp=2\alpha-1\), \(1<\alpha<2\).
Corollary SI.7 (Phases of the accuracy-exponent map)
Under the hypotheses of Theorem SI.15:
  1. (a)
    Phase A, \(\alpha>2\), \(\texp\le\alpha+1\): SignSVD wins for every \(\omega\).
  2. (b)
    Phase B, \(0<\alpha<1\) and \(\texp\ge1\) with \((\alpha,\texp)\notin\mathsf E\), or \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\texp>2\alpha-1\): SignSGD wins for every \(\omega\).
  3. (c)
    Phase C, \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\texp<2\alpha-1\): SignSGD wins if \(\omega<\omega_c\) and SignSVD wins if \(\omega>\omega_c\); at \(\omega=\omega_c\) the two are balanced if \(\texp\ne1\), and SignSGD wins, with order \(1/\log N\), if \(\texp=1\).
  4. (d)
    Phase D, \(0<\alpha<1\), \(\texp<1\): SignSGD wins if \(\omega<p-1\), and the two are balanced if \(\omega\ge p-1\), with limit \(K_A\) if \(\omega>p-1\).
  5. (e)
    Tie, \(\alpha>2\), \(\texp>\alpha+1\): balanced for every \(\omega\).
  6. (f)
    Chord, \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\texp=2\alpha-1\): SignSGD wins if \(\omega<p-1\), and the two are balanced if \(\omega\ge p-1\), with limit \(K_c\) if \(\omega>p-1\).
  7. (g)
    \(\alpha=1\): for \(\texp<1\), SignSGD wins if \(\omega<p-1\) and SignSVD wins, with order \(\log N\), if \(\omega\ge p-1\); for \(\texp=1\), SignSGD wins if \(\omega<1\), and \(\mathsf R\to\pi^{3/2}\) if \(\omega>1\); for \(1<\texp<2\), SignSGD wins for every \(\omega\).
  8. (h)
    \(\alpha=2\): SignSVD wins for \(\texp<3\), the two are balanced for \(\texp=3\), and SignSGD wins for \(\texp>3\), for every \(\omega\).
This is the phase diagram of Figure 7 in exact form, with its boundary lines; the Tie is the main text’s Phase E. In Phase A Muon wins at every accuracy, in Phase B SignSGD does, in Phase C SignSGD wins at low accuracy and Muon once \(\epsilon\ll N^{-\omega_c}\), and in Phase D SignSGD wins above the accuracy scale and the two tie below it. The chord \(\texp=2\alpha-1\) between B and C is where \(\omega_c\) reaches \(p-1\), that is, where the critical accuracy meets the accuracy scale, so as \(\omega\to\infty\) the boundary between B and C becomes the chord. The level \(\omega_c=1\) is the curve \(\texp_c(\alpha)\) of Proposition SI.6, the cut of the map at \(\epsilon=N^{-1}\), where that proposition’s window ends. Not covered are the winner for \(\omega\le p-1\) on \(\{\alpha=1,\texp\ge2\}\cup\{\alpha=\frac12,\texp\ge\frac32\}\), and the limit constants for \(\omega\le p-1\) in the balanced cases; at \(\alpha=\texp=\omega=1\) only \(\mathsf R\lesssim1\) is proved.
Proof sketch.
Off \(\mathsf E\) the winner is the sign of \(S(\omega)\), and of \(L\) where \(S(\omega)=0\) (Theorem SI.15(ii)). Write \(\omega'=\min\{\omega,p-1\}\). For \(0<\alpha<1\), \(e_D=-\frac\alpha2\). If \(\texp<1\), then \(S(\omega)=\frac\alpha2(\omega'/(p-1)-1)\) and \(L=0\); this is negative below the accuracy scale and zero at and beyond it, which is Phase D, with the limit \(K_A\) of Theorem SI.15(i). If \(\texp\ge1\), then \(\chi(p-1)=\max\{\frac{\alpha+1-\texp}2,0\}\le\frac\alpha2\), so \(S(\omega)\le0\), with equality only at \(\texp=1\), \(\omega\ge p-1\), where \(L=-1\); this is the first part of Phase B. For \(1<\alpha<2\), \(e_D=\frac\alpha2-1<0\), and for \(1\le\texp\le\alpha+1\)
\[ S(\omega)=\frac\alpha2-1+\frac{\omega'}{p-1}\cdot\frac{\alpha+1-\texp}2 , \]
which vanishes at \(\omega'=\omega_c\). At \(\omega'=p-1\) it equals \((2\alpha-1-\texp)/2\), so \(\omega_c<p-1\) exactly when \(\texp<2\alpha-1\). Below the chord SignSGD wins for \(\omega<\omega_c\) and SignSVD for \(\omega>\omega_c\), which is Phase C; at \(\omega=\omega_c\), \(L=-[\texp=1]\) gives the balance or the win of order \(1/\log N\). On the chord \(S(\omega)<0\) for \(\omega<p-1\) and \(S=L=0\) beyond, with the limit \(K_c\); above it, and for \(\texp>\alpha+1\), \(S(\omega)<0\), which is the second part of Phase B. The case \(\texp<1\) is the same with \(\alpha\) in place of \(\alpha+1-\texp\).
For \(\alpha\ge2\) the dimension factor does not decay, \(e_D=0\), and only the accuracy factor and the logarithms count. For \(\alpha>2\), \(S(\omega)>0\) when \(\texp<\alpha+1\), which is Phase A; for \(\texp\ge\alpha+1\), \(S(\omega)=0\) and \(L=[\texp=\alpha+1]\), which gives SignSVD on the line and the Tie beyond it. At \(\alpha=2\), \(S(\omega)>0\) for \(\texp<3\), and \(S(\omega)=0\) with \(L=-1+[\texp=3]\) for \(\texp\ge3\). At \(\alpha=1\) and \(1\le\texp<2\) only the upper bound of Theorem SI.15(iii) is available. There \(S(\omega)=-\frac12+\frac{\omega'}{p-1}\cdot\frac{2-\texp}2<0\) unless \(\texp=1\) and \(\omega\ge p-1\), which forces \(\mathsf R\to0\); at \(\texp=1\), \(\omega>1\) the frozen limit \(\pi^{3/2}\) of \(\rho_N\) applies.
