Lemma SG.16 (Spectral pieces)
For intervals \(I,J\subset(0,\gamma)\), \(0\le\mathfrak v_i[I,J]\le\big(\mathfrak v_i[I,I]\,\mathfrak v_i[J,J]\big)^{1/2}\).
Proof.
Nonnegativity holds termwise. Let first \(I,J\) be compact. Under the change of variables of the proof of Proposition SG.4, they correspond to compact sets \(U_I,U_J\subset(0,\infty)\) of the variable \(u\) with \(u^2=s_N(m)\), and the filters \(f_I\defeq\frac2\pi\int_{U_I}\iota_{u^2}\,\dif u\), \(f_J\) are odd, bounded and Lipschitz. For real \(a,b\) the filter \(f\defeq af_I+bf_J\) has, in the independent model along the replications, row covariances whose finite-time sums \(\frac1{nT}\sum_k\EE\norm{\sum_{t'\le T}f^\sharp(M^{\mathrm g}_{t'})^\T(u_i\tensor e_k)}^2\) are nonnegative; the coupling of Lemma SG.3, with \(\operatorname{Lip}(f)\) in place of \(\ridge^{-1/2}\), gives the geometric lag envelope, so these sums converge to the integrated covariance as in the proof of Theorem SD.4(iii). In the limit of the replications, the integrated covariance of \(f\) is, by bilinearity and the integral form of the responses (proof of Proposition SG.4(iii)),
\[ a^2\mathfrak v_i[I,I]+2ab\,\mathfrak v_i[I,J]+b^2\mathfrak v_i[J,J]\ge0 . \]
A nonnegative quadratic form has a nonnegative determinant, which is the claim. For general intervals, exhaust them by compact intervals; the three integrals of nonnegative kernels increase to their limits.
□
