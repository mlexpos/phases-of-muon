Lemma SF.10 (The volatility factor)
\(\Omega(1)=1\), and for \(\bmom\in(0,1)\)
\[ V_\bmom=\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge0}k_n^2\,\frac{1+\bmom^{2n+1}}{1-\bmom^{2n+1}},\qquad 1<V_\bmom<\frac1{\sw2}=\frac{1+\bmom}{1-\bmom}, \]
\[ \lim_{\bmom\uparrow1}\sw2V_\bmom=\frac14+\frac1{\pi^2}+\frac{7\zeta(3)}{2\pi^2}=0.77759\ldots . \]
Proof.
The value at one is a sum over odd integers. With \(x=2n+1\), \(k_n^2=x^{-2}+2/(x(x+2))+(x+2)^{-2}\), and
\[ \sum_{n\ge0}k_n^2=\frac{\pi^2}8+2\cdot\frac12+\Big(\frac{\pi^2}8-1\Big)=\frac{\pi^2}4, \]
by \(\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}x^{-2}=\pi^2/8\) and the telescoping sum \(\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}\frac2{x(x+2)}=\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}\big(\frac1x-\frac1{x+2}\big)=1\). Hence \(\Omega(1)=1\).
The series form sums the geometric series in \(\ell\): \(1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\bmom^{\ell x}=(1+\bmom^x)/(1-\bmom^x)\), and the weights \(\frac4{\pi^2}k_n^2\) sum to \(1\). The map \(z\mapsto(1+z)/(1-z)\) is increasing, \(1<(1+\bmom^x)/(1-\bmom^x)\le(1+\bmom)/(1-\bmom)\), with strict inequality for \(n\ge1\), and \(k_1>0\). This gives \(1<V_\bmom<1/\sw2\).
For the limit, put \(x=2n+1\). Then
\[ \sw2\,\frac{1+\bmom^x}{1-\bmom^x}=\frac{1-\bmom}{1+\bmom}\cdot\frac{1+\bmom^x}{1-\bmom^x}\le\frac{2(1-\bmom)}{1-\bmom^x}\le2, \qquad \sw2\,\frac{1+\bmom^x}{1-\bmom^x}\longrightarrow\frac1x\quad(\bmom\uparrow1), \]
so dominated convergence over \(n\) gives \(\lim\sw2V_\bmom=\frac4{\pi^2}\sum_nk_n^2/x\). Partial fractions give
\[ \frac{k_n^2}x=\frac1{x^3}+\frac1{x^2}-\frac1{2x}+\frac1{2(x+2)}+\frac1{4x}-\frac1{4(x+2)}-\frac1{2(x+2)^2}, \]
and summing over odd \(x\), with \(\sum_{x\ \mathrm{odd}}x^{-3}=\frac78\zeta(3)\) and the two telescoping sums \(-\frac12\) and \(\frac14\),
\[ \sum_{n\ge0}\frac{k_n^2}{2n+1}=\frac78\zeta(3)+\frac{\pi^2}8-\frac12+\frac14-\frac12\Big(\frac{\pi^2}8-1\Big)=\frac78\zeta(3)+\frac{\pi^2}{16}+\frac14 . \]
Multiplying by \(4/\pi^2\) gives the limit.
□
