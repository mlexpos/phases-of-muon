Corollary SI.4 (Accuracy-first winners)
On \(\{(\alpha,\texp):\ \alpha>0,\ \alpha+\texp>1\}\), as \(N\to\infty\), \(\rho_N\to\infty\) exactly on
\[ \mathsf V\defeq\{\alpha=1,\ \texp<1\}\cup\{1<\alpha<2,\ \texp<2\alpha-1\}\cup\{\alpha=2,\ \texp<3\}\cup\{\alpha>2,\ \texp\le\alpha+1\} ; \]
\(\rho_N\) converges to a finite positive limit exactly on
\[ \mathsf T\defeq\{0<\alpha<1,\ \texp<1\}\cup\{(1,1)\}\cup\{1<\alpha<2,\ \texp=2\alpha-1\}\cup\{(2,3)\}\cup\{\alpha>2,\ \texp>\alpha+1\}, \]
with limits \(K_A\), \(\pi^{3/2}\), \(K_c\), \(\pi\zeta(2)/(2J(5,1))\) and \(\pi\zeta(\alpha)J(p,\alpha)/(\zeta(\alpha/2)J(p,\alpha/2))\) on the five parts; and \(\rho_N\to0\) exactly on
\[ \mathsf G\defeq\{0<\alpha<1,\ \texp\ge1\}\cup\{\alpha=1,\ \texp>1\}\cup\{1<\alpha<2,\ \texp>2\alpha-1\}\cup\{\alpha=2,\ \texp>3\} . \]
Proof of Corollary SI.4.
Collect the cases of Theorem SI.12. For \(1<\alpha<2\) the chord value \(2\alpha-1\) lies in \((1,\alpha+1)\), so (a), (b) and the part \(\texp<2\alpha-1\) of (c) give \(\mathsf V\), and the rest of (c) together with (d) and (e) gives \(\mathsf G\). For \(\alpha=2\), cases (a) to (c) give \(\texp<3\), case (d) gives the tie at \(\texp=3\), and case (e) gives \(\mathsf G\). The columns \(0<\alpha<1\), \(\alpha=1\) and \(\alpha>2\) are read off directly. The three sets are disjoint and cover \(\{p>1\}\).
□
