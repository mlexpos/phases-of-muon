Lemma SF.12 (Lag lower bound)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\Nin=\Nout=N\), \(\gamma=N/B\), \(\varepsilon\ge0\) and \(\ell\ge1\). Put \(m_\varepsilon\defeq\psi(\varepsilon)\) for \(\varepsilon>0\) and \(m_0\defeq\gamma\). Then
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(\varepsilon)\ge\frac{\mathsf k_\bmom}{\gamma^3}\int_{\mathsf c'_\bmom}^{\,m_\varepsilon-\ell-\mathsf c''_\bmom} \big(\gamma-\eta-\mathsf c''_\bmom\big)\big(\gamma-\eta-\ell-\mathsf c''_\bmom\big)\,\dif\eta, \]
where the right side is zero if the range is empty. For \(\varepsilon>0\) the right side is at least \(\frac{\mathsf k_\bmom}{\gamma^3}(\gamma-m_\varepsilon)^2\big(m_\varepsilon-\ell-\mathsf c''_\bmom-\mathsf c'_\bmom\big)_+\).
Proof.
The proof bounds the determinant by one, changes variables to the two roots, and integrates over a thin region where the participation count is effective. Write \(r=r_\bmom\) and \(Q\defeq r^{-1}(m_\varepsilon)\), so that \(Q=q(-\varepsilon)\) for \(\varepsilon>0\).
Change of variables. By Lemma SF.11,
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(\varepsilon)\ge\frac4{\pi^2\gamma^3}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty b_\ell\,q\,q'\,\dif x\,\dif y,\qquad q=q(-\varepsilon-x^2),\quad q'=q(-\varepsilon-y^2). \]
In the square case the closure (SF.7) inverts as \(s(q)=(\gamma-r(q))^2/q\), so each axis is parametrized by its root \(q\in(0,Q)\), with \(x=\sqrt{s(q)-\varepsilon}\). Since \(\sqrt{s(q)}=(\gamma-r(q))\,q^{-1/2}\) and \(r'>0\),
\[ \Big|\frac{\dif x}{\dif q}\Big|\ge\Big|\frac{\dif\sqrt{s(q)}}{\dif q}\Big|\ge\frac{\gamma-r(q)}{2\,q^{3/2}} . \]
By (SG.15), \(b_\ell=\bmom^\ell\big(r(q)-r(\bmom^{2\ell}q')\big)/\big(q-\bmom^{2\ell}q'\big)\). The substitution \(p=\bmom^{2\ell}q'\) in the second variable therefore gives
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(\varepsilon)\ge\frac1{\pi^2\gamma^3}\iint D(q,p)\,\big(\gamma-r(q)\big)\big(\gamma-r(p\,\bmom^{-2\ell})\big)\,\dif q\,\dif p, \qquad D(q,p)\defeq\frac{r(q)-r(p)}{(q-p)\sqrt{qp}}\ge0, \]
over the region \(r(q)<m_\varepsilon\), \(r(p\,\bmom^{-2\ell})<m_\varepsilon\).
Restriction. Keep only \(p\ge(1-\bmom)^{-2}\) and \(q=p\,e^\delta\) with \(\delta\in[0,2]\). By Lemma SF.6(ii), \(r'(x)\ge\mathsf e^-_\bmom/x\) for \(x\ge p\), so \(r(q)-r(p)\ge\mathsf e^-_\bmom\delta\), and with \((e^\delta-1)/\delta\le e^\delta\),
\[ D(q,p)\ge\frac{\mathsf e^-_\bmom\,\delta}{p^2(e^\delta-1)\,e^{\delta/2}}\ge\frac{\mathsf e^-_\bmom\,e^{-3}}{p^2} . \]
Since \(\mathsf n_\bmom(q)=\mathsf n_\bmom(p)+\delta/(2\log(1/\bmom))\) and \(\mathsf n_\bmom(p\bmom^{-2\ell})=\mathsf n_\bmom(p)+\ell\), Lemma SF.6(i) gives
\[ r(q)<r(p)+\mathsf c''_\bmom,\qquad r(p\,\bmom^{-2\ell})<r(p)+\ell+\mathsf c''_\bmom . \]
Put \(\eta\defeq r(p)\) and keep only \(\eta\le m_\varepsilon-\ell-\mathsf c''_\bmom\). Then both constraints of the region hold, and the two brackets are at least \(\gamma-\eta-\mathsf c''_\bmom\) and \(\gamma-\eta-\ell-\mathsf c''_\bmom\), which are nonnegative. Integrating over \(q=pe^\delta\), \(\dif q=pe^\delta\dif\delta\),
\[ \int_p^{pe^2}D(q,p)\,\dif q\ge\frac{\mathsf e^-_\bmom\,e^{-3}(e^2-1)}{p} . \]
Finally, by Lemma SF.6(ii), \(\dif\eta=r'(p)\,\dif p\le\mathsf e^+_\bmom\,\dif p/p\), so \(\dif p/p\ge\dif\eta/\mathsf e^+_\bmom\). The condition \(p\ge(1-\bmom)^{-2}\) holds when \(\eta\ge\mathsf c'_\bmom\), because \(r((1-\bmom)^{-2})\le\mathsf c'_\bmom\) (Lemma SF.6(i)). Collecting the factors gives the bound.
The case \(\varepsilon>0\). The integrand decreases in \(\eta\), and at the upper endpoint it is \((\gamma-m_\varepsilon+\ell)(\gamma-m_\varepsilon)\ge(\gamma-m_\varepsilon)^2\).
□
