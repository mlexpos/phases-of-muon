Lemma SA.8 (Rectangular resolvent intertwining)
Let \(\vec{A}\in\mathbb{R}^{n_1\times n_2}\) and \(\vec{B}\in\mathbb{R}^{n_2\times n_1}\). For \(z\in\mathbb{C}\setminus(\sigma(\vec{A}\vec{B})\cup\sigma(\vec{B}\vec{A}))\), define
\[ R_{AB}(z)\defeq(\vec{A}\vec{B}-z\,\Id_{n_1})^{-1},\qquad R_{BA}(z)\defeq(\vec{B}\vec{A}-z\,\Id_{n_2})^{-1}. \]
Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.38} R_{AB}\vec{A}=\vec{A}R_{BA},\qquad \vec{B}R_{AB}=R_{BA}\vec{B}, \end{equation}
and consequently
\begin{equation}\tag{SA.39} \vec{A}R_{BA}\vec{B}=\Id_{n_1}+z\,R_{AB},\qquad \vec{B}R_{AB}\vec{A}=\Id_{n_2}+z\,R_{BA}. \end{equation}
Proof.
The identities
\[ (\vec{A}\vec{B}-z\Id_{n_1})\vec{A}=\vec{A}(\vec{B}\vec{A}-z\Id_{n_2}),\qquad \vec{B}(\vec{A}\vec{B}-z\Id_{n_1})=(\vec{B}\vec{A}-z\Id_{n_2})\vec{B} \]
are immediate. Multiplying the first on the left by \(R_{AB}\) and on the right by \(R_{BA}\) gives \(R_{AB}\vec{A}=\vec{A}R_{BA}\); multiplying the second similarly gives \(\vec{B}R_{AB}=R_{BA}\vec{B}\). Then
\[ \vec{A}R_{BA}\vec{B}=(R_{AB}\vec{A})\vec{B}=R_{AB}(\vec{A}\vec{B}) =\Id_{n_1}+zR_{AB}, \]
and similarly
\[ \vec{B}R_{AB}\vec{A}=R_{BA}(\vec{B}\vec{A})=\Id_{n_2}+zR_{BA}. \]
□
