Power-law spectra and the large-batch regime · cited in the main text
Proved in LeanTheorem numbering in the paper: Corollary L.4
For a probability measure \(\nu\) with \(\nu([0,\mu_+])=1\) and a mode \(0<\mu\le\mu_+/2\), the acceleration of Corollary SH.4 compares the profile with the gradient-descent rate \(\mu/\mu_+\), and \(\varkappa\) is the condition number:
\[ \mathcal A=\frac{\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu)/\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu_+)}{\mu/\mu_+},\qquad \varkappa=\frac{\mu_+}\mu,\qquad C_*=\frac12+\frac{2\log2}\pi . \]
Corollary SH.5 (Power-law spectra)
Let \(\alpha>0\), \(N\ge1\) and \(c_N>0\), let \(\mu_k=c_Nk^{-\alpha}\) for \(1\le k\le N\), and put \(\nu_N\defeq N^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^N\delta_{\mu_k}\). For \(0<x\le1\) put
\[ \Xi_\alpha(x)\defeq\int_0^\infty\varpi(y)\Big(\int_0^1\frac{y\,u^\alpha}{y\,u^\alpha+x^\alpha}\,\dif u\Big)^{1/2}\dif y . \]
  1. (i)
    For every \(1\le i\le N\),
    \[ 0\le\chi_{\nu_N}(\mu_i)-\Xi_\alpha(i/N)\le N^{-1/2}. \]
  2. (ii)
    If \(i\le\varepsilon N\) with \(\varepsilon\in(0,1]\), then \(1-2\varepsilon^{\alpha/(1+\alpha)}\le\chi_{\nu_N}(\mu_i)\le1\). Consequently, for every sequence \(\varepsilon_N\to0\),
    \[ \max_{i\le\varepsilon_NN}\Big|\frac{\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu_N}(\mu_i)}{\sqrt{\mu_i/2}}-1\Big|\longrightarrow0 . \]
  3. (iii)
    The function \(\Xi_\alpha\) is nonincreasing on \((0,1]\), and
    \[ 1-\Xi_\alpha(x)\le2x^{\alpha/(1+\alpha)},\qquad \Xi_\alpha(x)\ge\frac{C_*}{\sqrt{2(1+\alpha)}} . \]
  4. (iv)
    For every \(1\le i\le N\),
    \[ \frac{C_*}{\sqrt{2(1+\alpha)}}\sqrt{\frac{\mu_i}2}\le\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu_N}(\mu_i)\le\sqrt{\frac{\mu_i}2}. \]
  5. (v)
    If \(N^\alpha\ge2\), the acceleration of Corollary SH.4 for \(\nu=\nu_N\), \(\mu_+=\mu_1\) and \(\mu=\mu_N\), for which \(\varkappa=N^\alpha\), satisfies
    \[ \frac{C_*}{\sqrt{2(1+\alpha)}}\,N^{\alpha/2}\le\mathcal A\le\frac{\sqrt2}{C_*}\,N^{\alpha/2}. \]
This corollary is the large-batch paragraph of Section 4.2. For a power law every mode keeps the square-root law up to the constant \(C_*/\sqrt{2(1+\alpha)}\), which depends only on \(\alpha\), by (iv); the modes \(i\ll N\) have efficiency tending to one, by (ii), which with the factor \(\vartheta_i\to1\) of the oversampled law as \(\epsilon_{\rm os}\to0\) gives \(\mathfrak d_i\sim\sqrt{\mu_iB_{\rm eff}/(2N)}\) (Theorem SH.1). By (v) the slowest mode contracts faster than under gradient descent, relative to the top mode, by a factor of order \(N^{\alpha/2}\), the square root of the condition number. The efficiency itself has the limit profile \(\Xi_\alpha(i/N)\) with error at most \(N^{-1/2}\), by (i).
Proof sketch.
After rescaling the spectrum, the soft fraction is a Riemann sum of a monotone function. The efficiency is unchanged by \(\mu_k\mapsto a\mu_k\) (Lemma SH.14), so one may take \(\mu_k=(k/N)^{-\alpha}\). Then
\[ 1-m_{\nu_N}(c)=\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^N\phi_c(k/N),\qquad \phi_c(u)=\frac{c\,u^\alpha}{c\,u^\alpha+1}, \]
and since \(\phi_c\) is nondecreasing with values in \([0,1)\) the sum exceeds \(\int_0^1\phi_c\) by at most \(1/N\). With \(\abs{\sqrt a-\sqrt b}\le\sqrt{\abs{a-b}}\) and \(\int\varpi=1\) this gives (i), and \(\Xi_\alpha(x)\) is the efficiency of the law of \(U^{-\alpha}\), \(U\) uniform on \((0,1)\), at the level \(x^{-\alpha}\).
The remaining parts are the head and packing bounds applied to this spectrum. The fraction of the spectrum above \(\delta\mu_i\) is at most \(\varepsilon\delta^{-1/\alpha}\) when \(i\le\varepsilon N\), and Proposition SH.1(i) with \(\delta=\varepsilon^{\alpha/(1+\alpha)}\) gives \(1-\chi_{\nu_N}(\mu_i)\le2\varepsilon^{\alpha/(1+\alpha)}\), which is (ii) and the first bound of (iii). For the limit law \(1-m(x^{-\alpha})=\int_0^1u^\alpha(u^\alpha+x^\alpha)^{-1}\dif u\ge1/(2(1+\alpha))\), and the bound \(\chi\ge C_*\sqrt{1-m}\) of Theorem SH.2(i) (sketch of Corollary SH.2) gives the lower bound of (iii) and, with (i), of (iv). Part (v) is Corollary SH.4(i), which brackets the acceleration by \(\chi_\nu(\mu)\sqrt\varkappa\) and \((\sqrt2/C_*)\chi_\nu(\mu)\sqrt\varkappa\), at the slowest mode, where \(\varkappa=N^\alpha\) and \(\chi_{\nu_N}(\mu_N)\) lies between \(C_*/\sqrt{2(1+\alpha)}\) and one by (iv).
