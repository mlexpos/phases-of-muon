Definition SH.1 (Oversampled objects)
Let \(\nu\) be a probability measure and \(r>0\).
  1. (i)
    The profile root \(b_0=b_0^{\nu,r}\) is the function of Lemma SH.1.
  2. (ii)
    For \(\mu\ge0\) and \(\hat\ridge\ge0\), the oversampled drift profile is
    \begin{equation}\tag{SH.6} \mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu,r}(\mu;\hat\ridge)\defeq\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\int_0^\infty \frac{b_0(\hat s)\,\mu}{\hat s+b_0(\hat s)\,\mu}\,\dif v,\qquad \hat s=\hat\ridge+v^2 . \end{equation}
    In the square case we write \(\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu\defeq\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu,1}\) and \(\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_\nu(\mu)\defeq\mathfrak d^{\rm os}_{\nu,1}(\mu;0)\).
  3. (iii)
    The density \(\varpi\) on \((0,\infty)\) and the square-root efficiency \(\chi_\nu\) are
    \begin{equation}\tag{SH.7} \varpi(y)\defeq\frac2\pi\,\frac{\sqrt y}{(1+y)^2},\qquad \chi_\nu(\mu)\defeq\int_0^\infty\varpi(y)\sqrt{1-m_\nu(\mu y)}\,\dif y\qquad(\mu>0). \end{equation}
  4. (iv)
    At a finite dimension, the empirical spectral probability measure of \(\Sigmaout\), the rescaled ridge and the small parameter are
    \begin{equation}\tag{SH.8} \begin{gathered} \hat\nu_{\mathrm{out}}\defeq\frac1{\Nout}\sum_{k=1}^{\Nout}\delta_{\mu_k}=\frac{\nu_{\mathrm{out}}}{\gout},\qquad \hat\ridge\defeq\frac{\ridge}{\sw2\gin}\quad(\ridge\ge0),\\ \epsilon_{\rm os}\defeq3(1-\bmom^2)\gin=3(1+\bmom)^2\frac{\Nin}{B_{\rm eff}} . \end{gathered} \end{equation}
