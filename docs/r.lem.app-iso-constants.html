Lemma SF.7 (Two constants)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\).
  1. (i)
    If \(y_0>0\) and \(y_j=y_0\bmom^j\), then \(\sum_{j\ge0}\min(y_j,1/y_j)<2/(1-\bmom)\).
  2. (ii)
    \(\displaystyle\mathsf g_\bmom\defeq\sup_{q>0}\sqrt q\,\Lw(q)<\frac2{1-\bmom}\).
  3. (iii)
    \(\displaystyle\mathsf g'_\bmom\defeq\sup_{q>0}\Big|\Lambda_\bmom(q)-\sqrt{2\pi}\,r_\bmom(q)\Big|<\frac{2\pi}{1-\bmom}\).
At \(\bmom=0\), \(\sqrt q\,\Jfun(q)\le1\) and \(\abs{\Lambda_0(q)-\sqrt{2\pi}\,\Phi(q)}\le\pi\) for every \(q>0\).
Proof.
Part (i) splits the sum at the last index with \(y_j\ge1\), and the bound is uniform in \(y_0\). If there is no such index, every \(y_j<1\) and the sum is \(y_0/(1-\bmom)<1/(1-\bmom)\). Otherwise let \(j_0\) be that index and \(y\defeq y_{j_0}\). Then \(1\le y<1/\bmom\), because \(y_{j_0+1}=\bmom y<1\), and
\[ \sum_{j\le j_0}\frac1{y_j}=\frac1y\sum_{k=0}^{j_0}\bmom^k\le\frac1{(1-\bmom)\,y},\qquad \sum_{j>j_0}y_j=\frac{\bmom y}{1-\bmom}. \]
The function \(y\mapsto1/y+\bmom y\) is convex and equals \(1+\bmom\) at both ends of \([1,1/\bmom]\). Hence
\[ \sum_{j\ge0}\min\Big(y_j,\frac1{y_j}\Big)\le\frac{1+\bmom}{1-\bmom}<\frac2{1-\bmom} \]
for every \(y_0>0\), and the suprema in (ii) and (iii) inherit the strict inequality.
Part (ii) is part (i) with \(y_j=\emaw j\sqrt q\). By Lemma SF.4(iii),
\[ \sqrt q\,\Lw(q)=\sum_jy_j\,\Jfun(y_j^2)\le\sum_j\min\Big(y_j,\frac1{y_j}\Big). \]
Part (iii) rests on a pointwise bound. Put \(\varphi\defeq\Lambda_0-\sqrt{2\pi}\,\Phi\). By Lemma SF.4(i),(iv),
\[ \Lambda_\bmom(q)-\sqrt{2\pi}\,r_\bmom(q)=\sum_j\varphi(\emaw j^2q). \]
We claim that \(\abs{\varphi(z)}\le\pi\min(\sqrt z,z^{-1/2})\). For \(z\le1\) the two terms \(\Lambda_0(z)\le2\sqrt z\) and \(\sqrt{2\pi}\,\Phi(z)\le\sqrt{2\pi}\,z\le\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sqrt z\) are nonnegative, so \(\abs{\varphi(z)}\le\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sqrt z<\pi\sqrt z\). For \(z\ge1\),
\[ \varphi(z)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,\big(1-\Phi(z)\big)-\big(\sqrt{2\pi}-\Lambda_0(z)\big) \]
is a difference of two nonnegative terms, bounded by \(\sqrt{2\pi}\sqrt{\pi/2}\,z^{-1/2}=\pi z^{-1/2}\) and by \(2z^{-1/2}\) (Lemma SF.4(ii),(iv)), so \(\abs{\varphi(z)}\le\pi z^{-1/2}\). Part (i) with \(y_j=\emaw j\sqrt q\) gives the bound.
At \(\bmom=0\) the same pointwise bounds give \(\sqrt q\,\Jfun(q)\le\min(\sqrt q,1/\sqrt q)\le1\) and \(\abs{\varphi(q)}\le\pi\).
□
