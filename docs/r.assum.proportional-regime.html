Assumption S2 (Proportional regime)
The dimensions \(\Nin\), \(\Nout\), \(B\) tend to infinity jointly, with aspect ratios converging to constants:
\[ \frac{\Nin}{B} \to \gin \in (0,\infty), \qquad \frac{\Nout}{B} \to \gout \in (0,\infty). \]
The covariance operator norms remain uniformly bounded: there exists a constant \(C > 0\), independent of \(\Nin\) and \(\Nout\), such that
\[ \|\Sigmain\|_{\mathrm{op}} \;\leq\; C, \qquad \|\Sigmaout\|_{\mathrm{op}} \;\leq\; C. \]
We refer to the joint limit as the proportional regime.
