Proposition SA.3 (Scaling, physical ridge and heavy ball)
Let \(K\in\R^{\Nout\times\Nin}\), \(\ridge\ge0\) and \(a>0\).
  1. (i)
    \(\Qridge{\ridge}(aK)=\Qridge{\ridge/a^2}(K)\).
  2. (ii)
    If \(\cR>0\) and \(\ridgephys\defeq2\cR\,\ridge\), then \(\Qridge{\ridgephys}\big(\sqrt{2\cR}\,K\big)=\Qridge{\ridge}(K)\).
  3. (iii)
    Let \((G_{t+1})_{t\ge0}\) be any sequence of matrices, \(\bmom\in[0,1)\), and let \(M_t=\bmom M_{t-1}+(1-\bmom)G_{t+1}\) and \(H_t=\bmom H_{t-1}+G_{t+1}\) for \(t\ge0\) with \(H_{-1}=M_{-1}/(1-\bmom)\). Then \(H_t=M_t/(1-\bmom)\) for all \(t\ge-1\), and \(\Qridge{\ridge}(H_t)=\Qridge{(1-\bmom)^2\ridge}(M_t)\).
Proof.
The singular values of \(aK\) are \(as_k\), with the same singular vectors as \(K\), so the singular-value filter of \(\Qridge{\ridge}(aK)\) is
\[ \frac{as_k}{\sqrt{a^2s_k^2+\ridge}}=\frac{s_k}{\sqrt{s_k^2+\ridge/a^2}}, \]
which is the filter of \(\Qridge{\ridge/a^2}(K)\); this proves (i). Part (ii) is (i) with \(a=\sqrt{2\cR}\) and \(\ridgephys\) in place of \(\ridge\), since \(\ridgephys/a^2=\ridge\).
Part (iii) is an induction. If \(H_{t-1}=M_{t-1}/(1-\bmom)\) then
\[ H_t=\bmom\frac{M_{t-1}}{1-\bmom}+G_{t+1}=\frac{\bmom M_{t-1}+(1-\bmom)G_{t+1}}{1-\bmom}=\frac{M_t}{1-\bmom}, \]
and the ridge conversion is (i) with \(a=(1-\bmom)^{-1}\).
□
