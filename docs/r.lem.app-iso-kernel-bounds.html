Lemma SF.15 (Kernel bounds)
Let \(y\ne z\), put \(\delta\defeq\abs{\log(y/z)}\) and \(r_\wedge\defeq\min(r(y),r(z))\), and write \(d_j\defeq\emaw jg\) with \(g\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\).
  1. (i)
    The two kernels are the series
    \[ \mathcal Z(y,z)=\sqrt{yz}\sum_{j\ge0}\EE\frac{d_j^2}{(1+yd_j^2)(1+zd_j^2)},\qquad \mathcal X(y,z)=yz\sum_{j\ge0}\EE\frac{d_j^4}{(1+yd_j^2)(1+zd_j^2)} . \]
  2. (ii)
    \(0\le\mathcal Z(y,z)\le\min\big(\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\,\delta/(2\sinh(\delta/2)),\ \sw2\sqrt{yz}\big)\), and \(\delta/(2\sinh(\delta/2))\le\min\big(1,(1+\delta)e^{-\delta/2}\big)\).
  3. (iii)
    \(\max(0,r_\wedge-\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom)\le\mathcal X(y,z)\le r_\wedge\).
  4. (iv)
    \(\mathcal X(y,z)+\mathcal Z(y,z)\le\sqrt{r(y)\,r(z)}\).
  5. (v)
    If \(\mathsf A(y)>0\) and \(\mathsf A(z)>0\), then, with \(\mathsf A_\vee=\mathsf A_\vee(y,z)\),
    \[ \gamma-\mathcal X\ge\mathsf A_\vee,\qquad \gamma-\mathcal X-\mathcal Z\ge\max\Big(\mathsf A_\vee-\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom,\ \frac{\mathsf A_\vee}2\Big)>0, \]
    \[ \mathsf A_\vee\max\Big(\mathsf A_\vee-\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom,\ \frac{\mathsf A_\vee}2\Big)\le\gamma^2\widehat\Delta(y,z)\le\big(\mathsf A_\vee+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\big)^2 . \]
By continuity, (ii)–(v) also hold at \(y=z\).
Proof.
Part (i) is partial fractions. By Lemma SF.4(i) and \(\Fw(y)=r(y)/y\),
\[ r(y)=\sum_j\EE\frac{yd_j^2}{1+yd_j^2},\qquad \Fw(y)=\sum_j\EE\frac{d_j^2}{1+yd_j^2}. \]
The difference quotient of the first series is the series of \(\mathcal Z/\sqrt{yz}\). The second form follows from \(z\,r(y)-y\,r(z)=yz\big(\Fw(y)-\Fw(z)\big)\) and
\[ \frac{d_j^2}{1+yd_j^2}-\frac{d_j^2}{1+zd_j^2}=\frac{(z-y)\,d_j^4}{(1+yd_j^2)(1+zd_j^2)} . \]
For (ii), nonnegativity is read from (i). The first bound uses \(\abs{r(y)-r(z)}\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\delta\) (Lemma SF.14(iv)) and \(\sqrt{yz}/\abs{y-z}=1/(2\sinh(\delta/2))\). The second bounds every factor \((1+\cdots)^{-1}\) in (i) by one, which leaves \(\sqrt{yz}\sum_j\emaw j^2\,\EE g^2=\sw2\sqrt{yz}\). The bound \(\delta/(2\sinh(\delta/2))\le1\) is \(\sinh x\ge x\). Since \(2\sinh(\delta/2)=e^{\delta/2}(1-e^{-\delta})\), the bound \(\delta/(2\sinh(\delta/2))\le(1+\delta)e^{-\delta/2}\) is equivalent to \((1+\delta)e^{-\delta}\le1\), that is to \(e^\delta\ge1+\delta\).
For (iii), take \(z<y\), so that \(y=ze^\delta\); the case \(y<z\) is symmetric. Then
\[ \mathcal X(y,z)=r(z)-\frac{r(y)-r(z)}{e^\delta-1},\qquad 0\le\frac{r(y)-r(z)}{e^\delta-1}\le\frac{\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\,\delta}{e^\delta-1}\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom , \]
and \(r(z)=r_\wedge\). Nonnegativity of \(\mathcal X\) is read from (i).
For (iv), put \(v(y)\defeq\big(yd^2,\sqrt y\,d\big)/(1+yd^2)\in\R^2\) for a real number \(d\). Then (i) reads
\[ \mathcal X(y,z)+\mathcal Z(y,z)=\sum_j\EE\big\langle v(y),v(z)\big\rangle\Big|_{d=d_j},\qquad \abs{v(y)}^2=\frac{yd^2}{1+yd^2}, \]
and the second identity summed over \(j\) and averaged over \(g\) is \(r(y)\). The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality in \(j\) and \(g\) gives (iv).
For (v), part (iii) gives \(\gamma-\mathcal X\ge\gamma-r_\wedge=\mathsf A_\vee\). With \(\mathcal Z\le\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) from (ii), this gives \(\gamma-\mathcal X-\mathcal Z\ge\mathsf A_\vee-\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\). By (iv) and the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality,
\[ \gamma-\mathcal X-\mathcal Z\ge\gamma-\tfrac12\big(r(y)+r(z)\big)=\tfrac12\big(\mathsf A(y)+\mathsf A(z)\big)\ge\frac{\mathsf A_\vee}2 . \]
The determinant factors as
\[ \gamma^2\widehat\Delta=(\gamma-\mathcal X-\mathcal Z)(\gamma-\mathcal X+\mathcal Z), \]
and \(\gamma-\mathcal X+\mathcal Z\ge\mathsf A_\vee\), which gives the lower bound. For the upper bound, (iii) gives \(0\le\gamma-\mathcal X\le\gamma-r_\wedge+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom=\mathsf A_\vee+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\), so that \(\gamma^2\widehat\Delta\le(\gamma-\mathcal X)^2\le(\mathsf A_\vee+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom)^2\).
□
