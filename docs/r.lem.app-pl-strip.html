Lemma SG.19 (Multiplicative diagonal strip)
For fixed \(R>1\), \(M\ge1\) and \(0<\eta<1/2\), let \(\mathcal D\defeq\{M<m_1,m_2<\eta\gamma,\ R^{-1}\le m_1/m_2\le R\}\). Along the row sequences above, the lag-summed integral of (SG.22) over \(\mathcal D\) is \(o((1+b)^2)\).
Proof.
On \(\mathcal D\), \(\vartheta_N\) and \(1/\Delta^N_\ell\) are bounded by Lemma SG.7 and Lemma SG.14, uniformly in lag and dimension, \(\Lambda_N(m_1)/\Lambda_N(m_2)\) is bounded by a constant depending on \((R,\alpha,\eta)\), and \(\sum_\ell\mathsf D_\ell\le C_\bmom\). Integrating over the second coordinate, whose range has length at most \(C_Rm_1\), bounds the strip by
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.49} C_{R,\eta}\int_M^{\eta\gamma}\frac{\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)}{(1+\mu_i\Lambda_N(m))^2}\,\dif m . \end{equation}
For \(\alpha<1\), \(\Lambda_N(m)\asymp\Lambda_N(1)m\) on this range, and with \(y\defeq\mu_i\Lambda_N(1)\) the integral is at most \(Cy^{-1}\log(1+y\gamma)\). By Proposition SG.6 with \(c_N=1\), \(y\gamma\asymp x^{-\alpha}\) and \(y^{-1}\asymp\gamma x^\alpha\), so the bound is \(O(b^2/T)=o(b^2)\).
For \(\alpha=1\) put \(\xi\defeq B/i\) and \(\mathcal L(m)\defeq\log(e\gamma/m)\). The identity \(S_1(V)=V\log(1+1/V)\) and Lemma SG.6(ii) give \(\mu_i\Lambda_N(m)\asymp\xi m/\mathcal L(m)\) on this range. Let \(m_0\) solve \(\xi m_0=\mathcal L(m_0)\); then \(m_0\le(1+\log(\gamma\xi))/\xi\) when \(\gamma\xi>1\). Bounding the integrand by one below \(m_0\) and by \(C\mathcal L(m)/(\xi m)\) above, the integral is at most \(C(1+\log(\gamma\xi))^2/\xi\). Since \(\gamma\xi=1/x\) and \(b^2\sim\frac49T^3/\xi\), this is \(O(b^2/T)\) as well.
□
