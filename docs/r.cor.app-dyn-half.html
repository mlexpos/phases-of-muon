Corollary SE.1 (Half-anisotropic input)
Assume the hypotheses of Theorem SE.1 with \(\Sigmain=\Id_{\Nin}\). Let \(\mathfrak d_i\defeq\mathfrak d(\mu_i,1)\) and let \(\mathfrak v_i\defeq\mathfrak v_i(\ridge)\) be the row coefficient of (SD.75). Then the row equations
\begin{equation}\tag{SE.13} \frac{\dif}{\dif\macrotime}\big(B\fQ_i(\macrotime)\big)=-\frac{2\macrostep\,\mathfrak d_i}{\sqrt{\fRisk(\macrotime)}}\,B\fQ_i(\macrotime)+\macrostep^2\mathfrak v_i,\qquad \fRisk(\macrotime)=\frac12\sum_i\mu_i\fQ_i(\macrotime),\qquad \fQ_i(0)=\norm{\Delta_0^\T u_i}^2, \end{equation}
have a unique solution on \([0,T_\star]\), given by \(\fQ_i=\sum_j\fQ_{ij}\) with \((\fQ_{ij})\) the solution of (SE.7). The row measures \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)\defeq\sum_i\CMscr Q_i(\lfloor B\macrotime\rfloor)\,\delta_{\mu_i}\) and \(\chi^{\mathrm{ode}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)\defeq\sum_i\fQ_i(\macrotime)\,\delta_{\mu_i}\) on \([0,C_\Sigma]\) satisfy \(\sup_{\macrotime\le T_\star}\norm{\chi^{\mathrm{act}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)-\chi^{\mathrm{ode}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)}_\BL\to0\) in probability, and the same holds for the measures weighted by \(\mu\).
Proof of Corollary SE.1.
When \(\Sigmain=\Id\), every \(\lambda_j\) equals \(1\), so \(\mathfrak d_{ij}=\mathfrak d_i\), and by Proposition SD.4 \(\mathfrak v_{ij}=\mathfrak v_i/\Nin\) with \(\sum_j\mathfrak v_{ij}=\mathfrak v_i\). Summing (SE.7) over \(j\) and dividing by \(B\), the functions \(\fQ_i\defeq\sum_j\fQ_{ij}\) satisfy (SE.13), with \(\fRisk=\frac12\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\fQ_{ij}=\frac12\sum_i\mu_i\fQ_i\) and \(\fQ_i(0)=\norm{\Delta_0^\T u_i}^2\) by Lemma SA.1. The row system is locally Lipschitz where its risk is positive, so its solution is unique by the Picard–Lindelöf theorem, as in the proof of Proposition SE.1.
The measures \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}(\macrotime)\) and \(\chi^{\mathrm{ode}}(\macrotime)\) are supported on the segment \(\{\lambda=1\}\) of \(\mathbb K\), and their images under \((\mu,\lambda)\mapsto\mu\) are \(\chi^{\mathrm{act}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)\) and \(\chi^{\mathrm{ode}}_{\mathrm{row}}(\macrotime)\), by \(\CMscr Q_i=\sum_j\CMscr Q_{ij}\). For a signed measure \(m\) on \(\mathbb K\) supported on that segment, with image \(\tilde m\), \(\norm m_\BL=\norm{\tilde m}_\BL\): a test \(\phi\) on \(\mathbb K\) restricts to the test \(\phi(\cdot,1)\) on \([0,C_\Sigma]\), and a test \(\psi\) on \([0,C_\Sigma]\) extends to the test \(\phi(\mu,\lambda)\defeq\psi(\mu)\) on \(\mathbb K\), which is \(1\)-Lipschitz for \(\abs\cdot_1\). Theorem SE.1(ii),(iii) therefore give the claims, since \(\mu\lambda=\mu\) on the segment.
□
