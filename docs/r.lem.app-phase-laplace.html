Lemma SI.6 (Convergent power sums)
Let \(p>1\), \(q>0\) and \(d\defeq(p-1)/q\). For every \(a>0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.21} \Big|\sum_{i\ge1}i^{-p}e^{-ai^{-q}}-\frac{\Gamma(d)}q\,a^{-d}\Big|\le2M_{p,q}a^{-p/q}, \end{equation}
and for every integer \(N\ge1\), \(0\le\sum_{i>N}i^{-p}\le N^{1-p}/(p-1)\).
Proof.
The series converges by comparison with \(\sum_ii^{-p}\). Letting \(N\to\infty\) in (SI.20) gives
\[ \int_0^\infty f_a(x)\,\dif x=\frac{a^{-d}}q\,\Gamma(d) , \]
and the error bound passes to the limit. The tail bound is the comparison \(\sum_{i>N}i^{-p}\le\int_N^\infty x^{-p}\,\dif x\).
□
