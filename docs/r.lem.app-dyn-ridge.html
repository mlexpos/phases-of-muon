Lemma SE.13 (Ridge susceptibility covariance)
Let \(D\) be deterministic with \(2\cR(D)=1\), consider the stationary chain at \((t,D)\) and let \(k,r\ge0\). Then
\[ \big|\EE\big[g_k\,\big\langle\Pi,\partial_\ell\Qridge\ell(N_r)\big|_{\ell=\ridge}\big\rangle\big]\big|\le C\big(1+B\delta\pi_\Pi\,\bmom^{\abs{r-k}}\big). \]
Proof.
The means are bounded by (SE.15): \(\abs{\EE g_k}\le C\) and \(\abs{\EE\langle\Pi,\partial_\ell\Qridge\ell(N_r)\rangle}\le c_\Pi\norm{\EE\,\partial_\ell\Qridge\ell(N_r)}_\F\le C\). For the covariance, a sample of relative time \(v\) changes \(\langle\Pi,\partial_\ell\Qridge\ell(N_r)\rangle\) by at most \(\pi_\Pi\frac12\ridge^{-3/2}\cdot14C_\Sigma\gamma_\star\emaw{r-v}\) in \(L^2\), by Lemma SE.4(iii) and trace duality, and changes \(g_k\) by at most \(C\delta\emaw{k-v}\) as in the previous proof. The shared samples are those with \(v\le\min(k,r)\), and Lemma SE.6 gives \(CB\delta\pi_\Pi\sum_{v\le\min(k,r)}\emaw{k-v}\emaw{r-v}\le CB\delta\pi_\Pi\bmom^{\abs{r-k}}\).
□
