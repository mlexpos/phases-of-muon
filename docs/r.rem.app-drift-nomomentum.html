Remark SC.1 (No momentum and the main-text kernel)
At \(\bmom=0\) the only weight is \(\emaw0=1\), \(\Lw=\Jfun\), and the candidate is
\[ T(s)=s\,\Jfun(q(-s))\,\Rbar(-s)\,\frac{\Sigmaout\Dt\Sigmain}{\sqrt{2\cR(t)}}\,\Rtbar(-s). \]
For the kernel, write for \(\mu,\lambda\ge0\)
\[ \mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(z)\defeq\frac{-z\,\Jfun(q(z))\,\mu\lambda}{\big(\tilde s_1(z)\mu-z\big)\big(s_1(z)\lambda-z\big)}, \]
so that, at \(\bmom=0\),
\[ \mathfrak d(\mu,\lambda)=\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\int_0^\infty\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(-\ridge-u^2)\,du . \]
The main-text formula (7) with the regularized filter \(\varphi(y)=(y+2\fRisk\ridge)^{-1/2}\) reads, after \(\sqrt{\fRisk}\,\varphi(2\fRisk x)=(2(x+\ridge))^{-1/2}\) and \(\xi^2f(\xi)=\Jfun(q)/2\),
\[ \mathfrak d_{ij}=\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt2}\int_0^\infty\frac{\Im\,\mathsf g_{\mu_i\lambda_j}(x+i0^+)}{\sqrt{x+\ridge}}\,dx . \]
Suppose that \(\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}\) extends analytically to \(\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)\), that it is the Stieltjes transform \(\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(z)=\int\frac{m(dx)}{x-z}\) of a finite signed measure \(m\) on \((0,\infty)\), and that it extends continuously from the upper half-plane to \((0,\infty)\). Then \(m\) has the density \(\frac1\pi\Im\,\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(x+i0^+)\) on \((0,\infty)\), by the Stieltjes inversion formula [80, Theorem 2.4.3] applied to the normalized positive and negative parts of \(m\), and the scalar integral of Lemma SA.3 gives
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\int_0^\infty\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(-\ridge-u^2)\,du &=\frac{\sqrt2}{\pi}\int m(dx)\int_0^\infty\frac{du}{x+\ridge+u^2} =\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\int\frac{m(dx)}{\sqrt{x+\ridge}}\\ &=\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt2}\int_0^\infty\frac{\Im\,\mathsf g_{\mu\lambda}(x+i0^+)}{\sqrt{x+\ridge}}\,dx , \end{aligned} \]
so the two expressions of the kernel are the same quantity for every \(\ridge>0\). The analytic continuation of the negative-axis branch and the Stieltjes representation are not proved in this appendix, and the unregularized kernels of the main text, obtained by setting \(\ridge=0\) in (7), are the formal specialization \(\ridge\downarrow0\) of Corollary SC.1: no statement of this appendix concerns the limit \(\ridge\downarrow0\), and the phase-comparison section adopts the \(\ridge=0\) kernels as assumptions.
