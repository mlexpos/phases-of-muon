Lemma SG.5 (Continuum participation)
  1. (i)
    As \(V\downarrow0\), \(S_\alpha(V)\sim V/(1-\alpha)\) for \(\alpha<1\), \(S_1(V)=V\log(1+1/V)\sim V\log(1/V)\), and \(S_\alpha(V)\sim K_\alpha V^{1/\alpha}\) for \(\alpha>1\). As \(V\to\infty\), \(1-S_\alpha(V)\sim((\alpha+1)V)^{-1}\).
  2. (ii)
    \(\mathsf T(V)\asymp S_\alpha(V)\big(1-S_\alpha(V)\big)\) for \(V>0\).
  3. (iii)
    As \(p\downarrow0\), \(V_\alpha(p)\sim(1-\alpha)p\) for \(\alpha<1\), \(V_1(p)\sim p/\log(1/p)\), and \(V_\alpha(p)\sim(p/K_\alpha)^\alpha\) for \(\alpha>1\); and \(V_\alpha(p)\asymp(1-p)^{-1}\) for \(p\ge1/2\).
Proof.
Part (i) is a scaling of the integral. For \(\alpha<1\), \(V^{-1}S_\alpha(V)=\int_0^1(V+t^\alpha)^{-1}\dif t\) increases to \(\int_0^1t^{-\alpha}\dif t=(1-\alpha)^{-1}\) as \(V\downarrow0\). For \(\alpha=1\) the integral is explicit. For \(\alpha>1\) the substitution \(t=V^{1/\alpha}u\) gives
\[ S_\alpha(V)=V^{1/\alpha}\int_0^{V^{-1/\alpha}}\frac{\dif u}{1+u^\alpha},\qquad \int_0^\infty\frac{\dif u}{1+u^\alpha}=\frac1\alpha\int_0^\infty\frac{t^{1/\alpha-1}}{1+t}\,\dif t=\frac{\pi}{\alpha\sin(\pi/\alpha)}=K_\alpha, \]
by the Beta integral and the reflection formula [21, (5.12.3), (5.5.3)]. As \(V\to\infty\), \(1-S_\alpha(V)=\int_0^1t^\alpha(V+t^\alpha)^{-1}\dif t\) and \(V(1-S_\alpha(V))\to\int_0^1t^\alpha\dif t\).
Part (ii) compares the ratio at the two ends. The same substitutions give \(\mathsf T/S_\alpha\to1\) as \(V\downarrow0\) for \(\alpha\le1\), \(\mathsf T/S_\alpha\to1/\alpha\) for \(\alpha>1\), and \(\mathsf T/(1-S_\alpha)\to1\) as \(V\to\infty\). Both \(\mathsf T\) and \(S_\alpha(1-S_\alpha)\) are continuous and positive on \((0,\infty)\), so their ratio is bounded above and below.
Part (iii) inverts (i).
□
