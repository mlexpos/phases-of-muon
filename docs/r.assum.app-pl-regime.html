Assumption S4 (Thin-batch power law)
The exponent \(\alpha>0\) and the momentum \(\bmom\in(0,1)\) are fixed. Along a sequence of positive integers \(N\) and \(B=B_N\),
\[ N\to\infty,\qquad B\to\infty,\qquad \gamma=\frac NB\to\infty, \]
and the spectrum is \(\mu_i=c_Ni^{-\alpha}\) for \(1\le i\le N\), with amplitudes \(c_N>0\), with \(\Sigmain=\Id_N\) and \(\Nout=\Nin=N\).
