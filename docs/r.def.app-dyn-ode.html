Definition SE.1 (Deterministic mode-energy equations)
For \(i\le\Nout\) and \(j\le\Nin\), the deterministic mode-energy equations are
\begin{equation}\tag{SE.7} \frac{\dif}{\dif\macrotime}\big(B^2\fQ_{ij}(\macrotime)\big)=-\frac{2\macrostep\,\mathfrak d_{ij}}{\sqrt{\fRisk(\macrotime)}}\,B^2\fQ_{ij}(\macrotime)+\macrostep^2B\,\mathfrak v_{ij},\qquad \fRisk(\macrotime)\defeq\frac12\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\,\fQ_{ij}(\macrotime), \end{equation}
with the initial values \(\fQ_{ij}(0)=\CMscr Q_{ij}(0)=(u_i^\T\Delta_0v_j)^2\). Their spectral energy measure is \(\chi^{\mathrm{ode}}(\macrotime)\defeq\sum_{i,j}\fQ_{ij}(\macrotime)\,\delta_{(\mu_i,\lambda_j)}\).
