Theorem SI.9 (Main covariance range)
Fix \(\alpha>1\), \(\texp\ge1\) and \(T\ge4\). There are \(N_*\), \(E_*\ge1\) and \(0<c_-\le c_+\), all depending only on \(\alpha\), \(\texp\) and \(T\), such that for all \(N\ge N_*\), \(B\ge N\) and \(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0\) with \(\epsilon N\ge E_*\), and for both \(j\), the first hit \(\hat u_j\) is attained and
\[ c_-\le\frac{t^j_{T\epsilon}}{\mathcal S_jT_0^j\,\Pi_{r_j}(\epsilon)}\le c_+ . \]
Along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) in this window, for each \(j\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SI.37} t^j_{T\epsilon}\sim\frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j}{4\sqrt{8q_j}}\,\epsilon^{-1/2}\log\frac1\epsilon\quad(r_j=1),\qquad t^j_{T\epsilon}\sim\frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j\,J(p,q_j)}{2\sqrt\epsilon}\quad(r_j>1). \end{equation}
If \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\), the second equivalent holds for \(j=\text{s-SVD}\) along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\), \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) and \(B\ge N\), with no relation between \(N\) and \(\epsilon\). Every sequence with \(N\to\infty\), \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) and \(\epsilon N\to\infty\) lies in the window for all large indices, uniformly over \(B\ge N\).
Proof of Theorem SI.9.
Here \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}=(p-1)/\alpha\ge1\) and \(r_{\text{s-SGD}}=r_{\text{s-SVD}}/2\ge\frac12\). For SignSGD put \(\upsilon_{\rm G}\defeq2-1/\alpha>1\), \(K_{\rm G}\defeq4(1-\frac2\pi)E_{\alpha,\alpha}+\frac4\pi E_{2\alpha,\alpha}\),
\[ r_{0,\text{s-SGD}}\defeq\begin{cases}r_{\text{s-SGD}},&r_{\text{s-SGD}}\le1,\\ \min\{r_{\text{s-SGD}},(1+\upsilon_{\rm G})/2\},&r_{\text{s-SGD}}>1,\end{cases}\qquad D_{\text{s-SGD}}\defeq D(K_{\rm G},r_{0,\text{s-SGD}},\upsilon_{\rm G}) . \]
For SignSVD put:
  • •
    if \(\alpha>2\): \(\upsilon_{\rm V}\defeq2-2/\alpha>1\), \(K_{\rm V}\defeq2E_{\alpha,\alpha/2}\), \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\defeq1\) if \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}=1\) and \(\min\{r_{\text{s-SVD}},(1+\upsilon_{\rm V})/2\}\) otherwise, \(D_{\text{s-SVD}}\defeq D(K_{\rm V},r_{0,\text{s-SVD}},\upsilon_{\rm V})\) and \(N_{\rm V}\defeq\lceil4^{\alpha/(\alpha-2)}\rceil\);
  • •
    if \(1<\alpha<2\): \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\) and \(N_{\rm V}\) of Lemma SI.16, and \(D_{\text{s-SVD}}\defeq1\);
  • •
    if \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp=1\): \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\defeq1\), \(D_{\text{s-SVD}}\defeq1\) and \(N_{\rm V}\defeq\lceil e^{64}\rceil\);
  • •
    if \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\): \(D_{\text{s-SVD}}\defeq1\) and \(N_{\rm V}\defeq\lceil e^{L_0}\rceil\) with \(L_0\) of Lemma SI.18.
Put \(N_*\defeq\lceil\max\{4,4^{\alpha/(\alpha-1)},N_{\rm V}\}\rceil\) and
\[ E_*\defeq\max\big\{1,\ D_{\text{s-SVD}}^{2r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}}C_{\text{s-SVD}}^2,\ D_{\text{s-SGD}}^{2r_{0,\text{s-SGD}}}C_{\text{s-SGD}}^2\big\}, \]
where the middle entry is \(C_{\text{s-SVD}}^2\) when \(D_{\text{s-SVD}}=1\). In every case \(r_{0,j}\le r_j\) and \(r_{0,j}<2\); moreover \(\frac12\le r_{0,\text{s-SGD}}\le\upsilon_{\rm G}\), \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\ge1\), \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\le\upsilon_{\rm V}\) when \(\alpha>2\), and \(r_{0,j}>1\) exactly when \(r_j>1\). Except for SignSVD when \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\), let \(h_j\defeq C_j(1+u/D_j)^{-r_{0,j}}\) and \(H_j\defeq D_j[(C_j/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r_{0,j}}-1]\).
The horizons lie below the cutoffs. Since \(D_j^{2r_{0,j}}C_j^2\le E_*\le\epsilon N\),
\[ H_j\le D_jC_j^{1/r_{0,j}}\epsilon^{-1/(2r_{0,j})}\le N^{1/(2r_{0,j})} . \]
Hence \(H_{\text{s-SGD}}\le N\), and \(H_{\text{s-SVD}}\le\sqrt N\) because \(r_{0,\text{s-SVD}}\ge1\). The choice of \(N_*\) gives \(N\le(N/4)^\alpha\) and \(\sqrt N\le(N/4)^{\alpha/2}\), and for \(\alpha>2\) also \(N\le(N/4)^{\alpha/2}\).
The convolution bound (a) holds on \([0,H_j]\). For SignSGD, Lemma SI.7(i) with \(a=\alpha>1\) and \(a=2\alpha\) gives
\[ g_{\text{s-SGD}}(u)\le\frac{2(1-\frac2\pi)E_{\alpha,\alpha}}N(1+u)^{1-\upsilon_{\rm G}}+\frac4\pi E_{2\alpha,\alpha}(1+u)^{-\upsilon_{\rm G}}\le K_{\rm G}(1+u)^{-\upsilon_{\rm G}}\qquad(0\le u\le N), \]
using \(H_N(\alpha)\ge1\) and \((1+u)/N\le2\); Lemma SI.14 with \(H_*=N\) applies. For SignSVD with \(\alpha>2\) the envelope \(g\le K_{\rm V}(1+u)^{-\upsilon_{\rm V}}\) holds for all \(u\) and Lemma SI.14 applies. For \(1<\alpha<2\), Lemma SI.16 applies, since \(\epsilon N\ge E_*\ge C_{\text{s-SVD}}^2\). For \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp=1\), Lemma SI.17(iii) gives, for \(u\le H_{\text{s-SVD}}\le\sqrt N-1\),
\[ \frac{(g*h)(u)}{h(u)}\le\frac{2\log(1+u)+64}{H_N(1)}\le2 , \]
because \(2\log(1+u)\le\log N\le H_N(1)\) and \(H_N(1)\ge\log(N+1)\ge64\).
By Lemma SI.9, each hit is attained with \(\hat u_j\le H_j\), and (SI.51) gives \(\mathcal E_j\le D_jC_j^{1/r_{0,j}}[\sqrt2/(1-r_{0,j}/2)+1]\epsilon^{(r_{0,j}-1)/(2r_{0,j})}\). For SignSVD when \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\), Lemma SI.18 gives the hit and a bound on \(\mathcal E\) that is fixed for \(\epsilon<1\) and tends to zero along every sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). Now apply Lemma SI.10 with \(H=H_j\), in its three cases.
If \(r_j<1\), then \(j=\text{s-SGD}\), \(r_{0,j}=r_j\) and \(H_j\le N\le(N/4)^\alpha\); the argument of the proof of Theorem SI.8 gives the order \(\epsilon^{-(1-r_j)/(2r_j)}\) for the clock integral.
If \(r_j=1\), then \(r_{0,j}=1\), \(H_j\le\sqrt N\le(N/4)^{q_j}\), and \((1+H_j)\sqrt\epsilon\le D_jC_j\). Since \(q_j\ge\alpha/2>\frac12\), \(H_jN^{-q_j}\le N^{1/2-q_j}\le1\), and \(\mathcal E_j\le(2\sqrt2+1)D_jC_j\). Part (ii) of Lemma SI.10 gives
\[ \Big|\int_0^{\hat u_j}\sqrt{Y_j}\,\dif u-\frac{a_{q_j}}2\log\frac1\epsilon\Big|\le K' \]
with \(K'\) fixed, together with the lower bound \(\frac{\sqrt A}4\log\frac1\epsilon\). Since \(\log(1/\epsilon)\ge\log4\), the clock integral is between two fixed multiples of \(\log(1/\epsilon)\), and it is \(\sim\frac{a_{q_j}}2\log(1/\epsilon)\) as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).
If \(r_j>1\), part (iii) of Lemma SI.10 gives the lower bound \(1/(2\sqrt2)\) and the upper bound \(J(p,q_j)+\mathcal E_j\), which is fixed because \(\epsilon<1\). Along a sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), the three error terms of part (iii) tend to zero, so the clock integral tends to \(J(p,q_j)\). For SignSVD when \(\alpha=2\) and \(\texp>1\) this uses only Lemma SI.18, \(N\ge4\) and \(\epsilon<\epsilon_0\), which hold for large indices of every sequence with \(N\to\infty\) and \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).
Multiplying by \(\mathcal S_jT_0^j/(2\sqrt\epsilon)\) gives the orders \(\mathcal S_jT_0^j\Pi_{r_j}(\epsilon)\) and, with \(a_q=(8q)^{-1/2}\),
\[ \frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j}{2\sqrt\epsilon}\cdot\frac{a_{q_j}}2\log\frac1\epsilon=\frac{\mathcal S_jT_0^j}{4\sqrt{8q_j}}\,\epsilon^{-1/2}\log\frac1\epsilon . \]
The thresholds are fixed, which proves the last assertion.
