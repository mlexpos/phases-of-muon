Proposition SC.2 (One-step risk drift in the frozen state)
Under Assumption S3, let \(\lr\ge0\) be deterministic and put \(\Delta^+\defeq\Dt-\lr\,\Gtilde\) with \(\Gtilde=\Qridge\ridge(\Mt)\). Then
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.15} \EE\big[\cR(\Delta^+)\big]-\cR(t)=-\lr\,\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\,\big\langle\EE\Gt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle+\frac{\lr^2}{2}\,\EE\norm{\Gtilde}_\Sigma^2 , \end{equation}
and the linear term satisfies
\begin{equation}\tag{SC.16} \Big|\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\,\big\langle\EE\Gt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}}\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\,\mathfrak d_{ij}\,\CMscr Q_{ij}(t)\Big| \le\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\,C_\Sigma\,\varepsilon_B . \end{equation}
Proof of Proposition SC.2.
The identity (SC.15) is the total-risk line of Proposition SA.4 in the frozen state. In the frozen-state model the state \(\Dt\) is deterministic, the update \({\mathsf S}=\Gtilde\) has \(\EE\norm{{\mathsf S}}_\F^2\le\min(\Nout,\Nin)\) by Lemma SA.2(i), and the conditional expectations of that proposition are plain expectations; its identity (SA.22) with the contractions (SA.23) and \(\cR=\tfrac12\norm\cdot_\Sigma^2\) reads
\[ \EE\big[\cR(\Delta^+)\big]-\cR(t)=-\lr\,\big\langle\Dt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle_\Sigma+\frac{\lr^2}{2}\,\EE\norm{\Gtilde}_\Sigma^2 . \]
The pairing is rewritten through the mean gradient:
\[ \begin{aligned} \big\langle\Dt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle_\Sigma&=\Tr\big(\Sigmaout\Dt\Sigmain\,(\EE\Gtilde)^\T\big) =\big\langle\Sigmaout\Dt\Sigmain,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle\\ &=\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\,\big\langle\EE\Gt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle , \end{aligned} \]
by (SA.11), which gives (SC.15).
The deterministic equivalent of the pairing is read in the eigenbases. By (SC.9) with \(V=\EE\Gt\) and \(\norm{\EE\Gt}_\F\le C_\Sigma\),
\[ \big|\big\langle\EE\Gt,\EE\Gtilde\big\rangle-\big\langle\EE\Gt,\Gtildebar_\ridge\big\rangle\big|\le C_\Sigma\varepsilon_B , \]
and, expanding both matrices in the bases \((u_i)\), \((v_j)\), with \(u_i^\T\EE\Gt v_j=\mu_i\lambda_j(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)/\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\) and (SC.11),
\[ \big\langle\EE\Gt,\Gtildebar_\ridge\big\rangle=\sum_{i,j}\frac{\mu_i\lambda_j(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)}{\sqrt{2\cR(t)}}\cdot\frac{\mathfrak d_{ij}(u_i^\T\Dt v_j)}{\sqrt{\cR(t)}} =\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\cR(t)}\sum_{i,j}\mu_i\lambda_j\,\mathfrak d_{ij}\,\CMscr Q_{ij}(t). \]
Multiplying by \(\sqrt{2\cR(t)}\) gives (SC.16).
□

    (hDt : ∀ k, (R.F k).A = Ahat (R.F k).C (Dt k)) (hRt : ∀ k, 0 < riskSigma (R.F k).C (Dt k))

      Integrable (fun ω => pairSigma (R.F k).C (Q ϱ (WeightedBuf (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ω))
        (Q ϱ (WeightedBuf (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ω))) (P k) ∧
      (∫ ω, riskSigma (R.F k).C (Dt k - lr k • Q ϱ (WeightedBuf (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ω)) ∂(P k)) -
          riskSigma (R.F k).C (Dt k) =
        -(lr k * Real.sqrt (2 * riskSigma (R.F k).C (Dt k)) * (EG (R.F k) * (EQ (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ϱ)ᵀ).trace) +
          (lr k ^ 2 / 2) * (∫ ω, pairSigma (R.F k).C (Q ϱ (WeightedBuf (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ω))
            (Q ϱ (WeightedBuf (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ω)) ∂(P k))) ∧
    (∀ k, BddAbove ((fun ϱ => fro (EQ (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (S k) t ϱ -
        Gbar (emaW R.β) (R.F k) (specOutFM (R.F k)) (specInFM (R.F k)) ϱ)) '' Icc R.ϱlo R.ϱhi)) ∧
