Power-law spectra and the large-batch regime · cited in the main text
Proved in LeanTheorem numbering in the paper: Definition L.1
For participations \(m_1,m_2>0\), with \(q_k=Q_{m_k}\), an integer lag \(\ell\ge0\) and a standard Gaussian \(g\), the functions referred to in the definition as those of (SG.20) are
\[ \mathsf D_\ell(m_1,m_2)=\sum_{a\ge0}\EE\frac{\bmom^\ell\sqrt{q_1q_2}\,\emaw a^2g^2}{(1+q_1\emaw a^2g^2)(1+\bmom^{2\ell}q_2\emaw a^2g^2)}, \]
\[ \mathsf M_\ell(m_1,m_2)=\sum_{a\ge0}\EE\frac{\bmom^{2\ell}q_1q_2\,\emaw a^4g^4}{(1+q_1\emaw a^2g^2)(1+\bmom^{2\ell}q_2\emaw a^2g^2)}, \]
and for a negative lag \(-\ell\) they are those of the lag \(\ell\) at \((m_2,m_1)\).
Definition SG.3 (Profiles)
Let \(S_\alpha(V)\defeq\int_0^1V(V+t^\alpha)^{-1}\dif t\) for \(V>0\), a strictly increasing bijection onto \((0,1)\), and \(V_\alpha\defeq S_\alpha^{-1}\). For \(0<x\le1\) and \(0<p<1\) put
\[ f_x(p)\defeq\frac{V_\alpha(p)}{V_\alpha(p)+x^\alpha},\qquad U_\alpha(p)\defeq\frac{V_\alpha(p)}{p(1-p)} . \]
The bulk profiles are
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.24} \begin{gathered} \Psi_\alpha(x)\defeq c_0\int_0^1\sqrt{\frac{1-p}{p\,V_\alpha(p)}}\;f_x(p)\,\dif p,\\ C_{\alpha,x}(\tau)\defeq\int_0^{1-\tau}\frac{f_x(p+\tau)-f_x(p)}{\tau}\sqrt{\frac{U_\alpha(p)}{U_\alpha(p+\tau)}}\,\dif p\quad(0<\tau<1),\\ \Gamma_\alpha(x)\defeq2\int_0^1C_{\alpha,x}(\tau)\,\dif\tau . \end{gathered} \end{equation}
For \(\alpha>1\), the crossover profiles are, for \(y,X>0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.25} \begin{aligned} \Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}(y)\defeq\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty&\sqrt q\,\Lw(q)\,r_\bmom(q)^{-\frac{\alpha+1}2}\\ &\times\frac{y\,r_\bmom(q)^\alpha}{1+y\,r_\bmom(q)^\alpha}\Big(1+(\alpha-1)\frac{q\,r_\bmom'(q)}{r_\bmom(q)}\Big)\frac{\dif q}{q}, \end{aligned} \end{equation}
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.26} \begin{aligned} G_{\alpha,\bmom}(X)\defeq\frac1{\pi^2}\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty &\frac{\sqrt{\zeta_X(u)\big(1-\zeta_X(u)\big)\zeta_X(v)\big(1-\zeta_X(v)\big)}}{\sqrt{uv}}\\ &\times\frac{\mathsf D_\ell(u,v)\,\vartheta_\infty(u)\vartheta_\infty(v)}{\Delta_\ell^\infty(u,v)}\,\dif u\,\dif v, \end{aligned} \end{equation}
where \(\mathsf D_\ell\) and \(\mathsf M_\ell\) are the functions of (SG.20), which depend on \((m_1,m_2)=(u,v)\) only through \((Q_u,Q_v)\), and
\[ \zeta_X(u)\defeq\frac{u^\alpha}{X^\alpha+u^\alpha},\qquad \vartheta_\infty(u)\defeq\frac1{E(u)}+\frac{\alpha-1}u,\]
\[ t_\alpha(u,v)\defeq\frac{v^\alpha u-u^\alpha v}{v^\alpha-u^\alpha},\qquad \Delta_\ell^\infty\defeq1-\frac{t_\alpha(u,v)\,\mathsf M_\ell(u,v)}{uv}, \]
with \(t_\alpha(u,u)\defeq(\alpha-1)u/\alpha\), and with the convention of (SG.20) for negative lags.
These are the profiles of Table 1. The function \(S_\alpha\) is the continuum limit of the participation of a power law: a participation fraction \(p=m/\gamma\) corresponds to the level \(V_\alpha(p)\), and \(f_x(p)\) is the share of the row at position \(x=i/N\) that is whitened at that level (Section G.5). The drift profile \(\Psi_\alpha\) integrates this share against the weight \(c_0\sqrt{(1-p)/(pV_\alpha)}\) that the averaged momentum weights produce, and \(C_{\alpha,x}(\tau)\) is the limiting correlation of two updates of row \(x\) that are \(\tau\gamma\) steps apart, so that \(\Gamma_\alpha\) is the integrated volatility per unit of \(\gamma\). Neither depends on \(\bmom\). For \(\alpha>1\) the crossover profiles \(\Phi_{\alpha,\bmom}\) and \(G_{\alpha,\bmom}\) describe the rows near the crossover index \(i_{\mathrm c}=B/K_\alpha\), and they keep the full momentum weight functions.
