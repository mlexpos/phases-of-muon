Lemma SJ.9 (Deletion estimates)
In the Haar model put
\[ Q_0\defeq105\Big(\frac{4A_0}{P_-}\Big)^4,\qquad k_*\defeq\max\{4,\lceil4A_2/P_-\rceil\}, \]
and let \(k\ge k_*\). On the event \(\mathcal A_p\defeq\{R_p\le P_-/4\}\), the deleted block of Lemma SJ.8 has squared norm at least \(\cR\). For \(1\le q\le4\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SJ.26} \EE R_p^q\le\frac{(2q-1)!!\,A_0^q}{\Nout^q},\qquad \EE\abs{\mu_{pj}}^{2q}\le\frac{(2q-1)!!\,A_1^q}{\Nout^qk^q},\qquad \PP(\mathcal A_p^c)\le\frac{Q_0}{\Nout^4}. \end{equation}
Proof.
Ellipticity removes the random denominator: \(v_p\ge c\). Then (SJ.22) with \(T=T_D\), \(\Tr T_D\le H_0\), and with \(a=\Lambda D_{\cdot j}\), \(\norm a^2\le L\,C_j\le L^2C_{\rm col}/k\), together with \(d_q(\Nout)\ge\Nout^q\), gives the two moment bounds. On \(\mathcal A_p\), \(R_p+C_j\le P_-/4+A_2/k\le P_-/2\le\cR\), so the deleted block has squared norm \(2\cR-R_p-C_j+\mu_{pj}^2\ge\cR\). Markov’s inequality for \(R_p^4\) gives the probability bound.
□
