Lemma SC.8 (Uniform Gaussian limit of the derivative functional)
With \(d=z^\T\Ahat w\) as in Lemma SB.13,
\[ r_B'\defeq\sup_{0\le x\le q_I}\abs{\EE f_x'(d)-\Jfun(x)}\longrightarrow0 . \]
Proof.
Pointwise convergence holds at each \(x\). The function \(f_x'\) is continuous and bounded by \(1\), so Lemma SA.7(iii) gives \(\EE f_x'(d)\to\EE f_x'(g)\) for \(g\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\), and Stein’s lemma (SB.24) with \(\phi=f_x\) identifies the limit:
\[ \EE f_x'(g)=\EE\big[g\,f_x(g)\big]=\EE\frac{g^2}{1+xg^2}=\Jfun(x). \]
The family is uniformly Lipschitz in \(x\). By Lemma SC.7 and \(\EE d^2=1\),
\[ \abs{\EE f_x'(d)-\EE f_{x'}'(d)}\le3\abs{x-x'},\qquad \abs{\Jfun(x)-\Jfun(x')}\le3\abs{x-x'}, \]
the second because \(\abs{\Jfun'}\le\EE g^4=3\) by Lemma SB.1(i).
Pointwise convergence of a uniformly Lipschitz family on the compact interval \([0,q_I]\) is uniform, by the finite-net argument in the proof of Lemma SB.13 with the constants \(3\) and \(3\) in place of \(9\) and \(3\).
□
