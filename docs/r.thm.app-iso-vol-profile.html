Theorem SF.10 (Lag profile at zero ridge)
Let \(\bmom\in(0,1)\), \(\Nin=\Nout=N\) and \(\gamma=N/B\ge\gamma^\circ_\bmom\). For every integer \(\ell\ge1\),
\[ -\frac{4\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(\mathsf M_1+1)}\gamma\Big(1+\log^+\frac\gamma{4\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom}\Big)\le\overline C_{1,\ell}(0)-\mathcal G\Big(\frac\ell\gamma\Big)\le\frac{\mathsf K^{\rm pr}_\bmom(1+\log\gamma)}\gamma, \]
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(0)\le\mathsf K^{\rm dec}_\bmom\,e^{-\delta_\bmom(\ell-\gamma)} . \]
Proof.
The proof is the unsummed version of the proof of Theorem SF.9: the same pointwise bounds, with \(\mathsf T(z)\) replaced by a single weight, compare the integrand with \((1-\ell/x)_+\), whose integral over the level is exactly \(\mathcal G(\ell/\gamma)\).
Step 1: representation. By Proposition SF.10 at \(\ridge=0\) and Lemma SF.16(i),
\[ \overline C_{1,\ell}(0)=\frac1\gamma\int_0^\gamma\EE_\star[\Psi_\ell]\,\dif\eta,\qquad \Psi_\ell\defeq\mathbf 1\{y<Q_\gamma,\ z_\ell<Q_\gamma\}\,\frac{\mathsf j(y)\,\mathsf j(z_\ell)}{\gamma^2\,\widehat\Delta(y,z)}, \]
with \((y,z)=\Pi(Q_\eta;\cdot)\). With \(x=\gamma-\eta\), direct integration gives
\[ \frac1\gamma\int_0^\gamma\Big(1-\frac\ell x\Big)_+\dif\eta=\frac1\gamma\int_\ell^\gamma\Big(1-\frac\ell x\Big)\dif x=\mathcal G\Big(\frac\ell\gamma\Big). \]
One more integral recurs below. For \(Y>0\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SF.61} \frac1\gamma\int_0^\gamma\min\Big(1,\frac Yx\Big)\dif\eta\le\frac Y\gamma\Big(1+\log^+\frac\gamma Y\Big), \end{equation}
and the right side is nondecreasing in \(Y\).
Step 2: upper bound. As in Steps 2 and 3 of the proof of Theorem SF.9, \(\mathsf A_\vee\ge\Phi(1)\) on \(\{\Psi_\ell>0\}\), and
\[ \mathsf j(z_\ell)\,\mathbf 1\{z_\ell<Q_\gamma\}\le(\mathsf A_\vee+E_z-\ell)_++2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom , \]
so that
\[ \Psi_\ell\le\frac{(\mathsf A_\vee+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom)\big((\mathsf A_\vee+E_z-\ell)_++2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\big)}{\mathsf A_\vee\max(\mathsf A_\vee-\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom,\mathsf A_\vee/2)} . \]
If \(\mathsf A_\vee\ge2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\), this is at most
\[ \Big(1+\frac{6\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom}{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big)\Big[\Big(1-\frac\ell{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big)_++\frac{E_z+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom}{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big]\le\Big(1-\frac\ell{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big)_++\frac{14\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom+4E_z}{\mathsf A_\vee} . \]
If \(\mathsf A_\vee<2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\), it is at most \(8\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(4\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom+E_z)/(\Phi(1)\mathsf A_\vee)\). Hence
\[ \Psi_\ell\le\Big(1-\frac\ell{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big)_++\frac{\mathsf K_3+\mathsf K_4E_z}{\mathsf A_\vee} . \]
The first term is compared with its value at \(x\). The function \(A\mapsto(1-\ell/A)_+\) is nondecreasing, and \((1-\ell/(x+\delta))_+-(1-\ell/x)_+\le\min(1,\delta/x)\) for \(\delta\ge0\); with \(\mathsf A_\vee\le x+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau\) this gives
\[ \Big(1-\frac\ell{\mathsf A_\vee}\Big)_+\le\Big(1-\frac\ell x\Big)_++\min\Big(1,\frac{1+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau}x\Big). \]
The second term is controlled by
\[ \frac1{\mathsf A_\vee}\le\frac1{\Phi(1)}\min\Big(1,\frac{2(\Phi(1)+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau)}x\Big): \]
if \(\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau\le x/2\), then \(\mathsf A_\vee\ge\mathsf A(y)\ge x/2\), and otherwise the minimum is one. Hence
\[ \Psi_\ell\le\Big(1-\frac\ell x\Big)_++\min\Big(1,\frac{1+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau}x\Big)+\frac{\mathsf K_3+\mathsf K_4\mathsf c^+_\bmom}{\Phi(1)}\min\Big(1,\frac{2(\Phi(1)+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau)}x\Big)+\frac{\mathsf K_4\,\mathsf n^-_\bmom(z)}{\Phi(1)} . \]
Integrate with (SF.61). For \(Y=1+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau\ge1\) the logarithm is at most \(\log\gamma\). For \(Y=2(\Phi(1)+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau)\ge2\Phi(1)\) it is at most \(\log\gamma+\log(1/(2\Phi(1)))\), and \(1+a+b\le(1+a)(1+b)\) for \(a,b\ge0\). Then take \(\EE_\star\), and bound the last term by Lemma SF.17.
Step 3: lower bound. If \(\mathsf A(z)>\ell\), then \(r(z_\ell)\le r(z)+\ell<\gamma\) and \(\mathsf j(z_\ell)\ge\mathsf A(z)-\ell\). With Lemma SF.15(v),
\[ \Psi_\ell\ge\mathbf 1\{y<Q_\gamma\}\,\frac{\mathsf A(y)\,(\mathsf A(z)-\ell)_+}{(\mathsf A_\vee+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom)^2} . \]
With \(\upsilon=\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(\tau+1)\) as before, \(\mathsf A(y)\ge x-\upsilon\), \(\mathsf A(z)-\ell\ge x-\ell-\upsilon\) and \(\mathsf A_\vee+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\le x+\upsilon\). If \(x-\ell>4\upsilon\), put \(t\defeq\upsilon/x<\frac14\). Then \(y<Q_\gamma\), and
\[ \Psi_\ell\ge\frac{1-t}{(1+t)^2}\Big(1-\frac\ell x-t\Big)\ge(1-2t)(1-t)\Big(1-\frac\ell x-t\Big)\ge(1-3t)\Big(1-\frac\ell x-t\Big)\ge1-\frac\ell x-4t . \]
If \(x-\ell\le4\upsilon\), then \((1-\ell/x)_+\le\min(1,4\upsilon/x)\). Hence
\[ \Psi_\ell\ge\Big(1-\frac\ell x\Big)_+-\min\Big(1,\frac{4\upsilon}x\Big) \]
in all cases. Integrate with (SF.61), using \(4\upsilon\ge4\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) in the logarithm and \(\EE_\star[4\upsilon]=4\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom(\mathsf M_1+1)\).
Step 4: decay. Keeping the indicator \(\mathbf 1\{\ell<L_z\}\) in Step 2, and using \(L_z-\ell+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\le\mathsf A_\vee+E_z+2\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\) in both cases of \(\mathsf A_\vee\), gives
\[ \Psi_\ell\le\mathsf K_5\,(1+E_z)\,\mathbf 1\{\ell<L_z\} . \]
Also \(L_z\le\mathsf A_\vee+E_z\le\gamma+\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\tau+\mathsf c^+_\bmom+\mathsf n^-_\bmom(z)\). With \(\delta=\delta_\bmom\), the bounds \(\mathbf 1\{\ell<L_z\}\le e^{\delta(L_z-\ell)}\) and \((1+\mathsf c^+_\bmom+n)e^{\delta n}\le(1+\mathsf c^+_\bmom+\delta^{-1})e^{2\delta n}\) for \(n\ge0\) give
\[ \Psi_\ell\le\mathsf K_5\Big(1+\mathsf c^+_\bmom+\frac1\delta\Big)e^{\delta\mathsf c^+_\bmom}\,e^{-\delta(\ell-\gamma)}\,e^{\tau/8}\,e^{(\tau-\sigma)_+/8}, \]
because \(\delta\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\le\frac18\) and \(2\delta\,\mathsf n^-_\bmom(z)\le(\tau-\sigma)_+/8\), with \(\sigma=\log((1-\bmom)^2Q_\eta)\) (Lemma SF.17). Finally, with \(\dif\eta=\mathsf e_\bmom\,\dif\sigma\) and the two bounds on \(\mathsf e_\bmom\) of the proof of Lemma SF.17,
\[ \begin{aligned} \int_0^\gamma\EE_\star\big[e^{\tau/8}e^{(\tau-\sigma)_+/8}\big]\dif\eta &\le\gamma\,\mathsf M_e+\EE_\star\Big[e^{\tau/8}\int_\R\big(e^{(\tau-\sigma)_+/8}-1\big)\mathsf e_\bmom\,\dif\sigma\Big]\\ &\le\mathsf M_e\Big(\gamma+\frac8{7(1-\bmom^2)}+8\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom\Big), \end{aligned} \]
where the part \(\sigma<0\) of the inner integral is at most \(8e^{\tau/8}/(7(1-\bmom^2))\) and the part \(0\le\sigma<\tau\) is at most \(8\bar{\mathsf e}_\bmom e^{\tau/8}\). Dividing by \(\gamma\ge1\) gives the decay bound.
□
