Lemma SG.8 (Drift in the participation coordinate)
For every finite positive spectrum,
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.44} \sqrt2\,\mathfrak d_i(0)=\frac1{\pi\gamma}\int_0^\gamma\mathcal A(m)\,\mathcal B_i(m)\,E(m)\vartheta_N(m)\,\dif m . \end{equation}
Under Assumption S4, with \(x=i/N\) and \(m=\gamma p\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SG.45} \mathcal B_i(\gamma p)=a_N\sqrt{\frac{1-p}{p\,V_N(p)}}\;\frac{V_N(p)}{V_N(p)+x^\alpha}. \end{equation}
Proof.
In (SG.12) substitute \(u^2=s_N(m)\). By Proposition SG.1(i), \(\dif u=\frac12\sqrt{s_N(m)}\,\vartheta_N(m)\,\dif m\) in absolute value, and with \(\Fw(Q_m)=m/Q_m\),
\[ \frac{\Lw(Q_m)}{\Fw(Q_m)}\sqrt{s_N(m)}=\frac{Q_m\Lw(Q_m)}{m}\sqrt{\frac{(\gamma-m)m}{Q_m\Lambda_N(m)}}=\sqrt{Q_m}\,\Lw(Q_m)\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-m}{m\Lambda_N(m)}}, \]
which gives (SG.44), since \(\sqrt{Q_m}\Lw(Q_m)=\mathcal A(m)E(m)\). For the second identity, (SG.42) gives \(\Lambda_N\mu_i=V_N(p)x^{-\alpha}\), so
\[ \frac{f_i}{\sqrt{\Lambda_N}}=\frac{\sqrt{\Lambda_N}\,\mu_i}{1+\Lambda_N\mu_i}=\frac{\sqrt{c_N}N^{\alpha/2}\sqrt{V_N}\,i^{-\alpha}}{1+V_Nx^{-\alpha}}=a_N\frac{\sqrt{V_N}}{V_N+x^\alpha}, \]
and \(\sqrt{(\gamma-m)/m}=\sqrt{(1-p)/p}\).
□
