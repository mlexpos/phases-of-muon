Lemma SI.16 (SignSVD kernel, \(1<\alpha<2\), \(\texp\ge1\))
Let \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\texp\ge1\), so that \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}\ge1\). Put \(r_0\defeq1\) if \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}=1\) and \(r_0\defeq\min\{r_{\text{s-SVD}},(1+2/\alpha)/2\}\) if \(r_{\text{s-SVD}}>1\). Put \(\upsilon_\circ\defeq2-2/\alpha\in(0,1)\), \(K_{\rm V}\defeq2E_{\alpha,q}\), \(\delta_\circ\defeq1-(2/3)^{1/r_0}\), \(d_\alpha\defeq(\alpha-1)(2-\alpha)/(2\alpha)\),
\[ (J_{\rm V},d_{\rm V})\defeq\begin{cases}\big(K_{\rm V}\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon_\circ}/(e\,d_\alpha),\ d_\alpha/2\big),&r_0=1,\\[2pt] \big(K_{\rm V}\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon_\circ}/(r_0-1),\ 1-q-(r_0-\upsilon_\circ)/(2r_0)\big),&r_0>1,\end{cases} \]
\[ N_{\rm V}\defeq\big\lceil\max\{1,(2J_{\rm V})^{1/d_{\rm V}}\}\big\rceil . \]
Then \(d_{\rm V}>0\). Let \(C=C_{p,q}\), \(N\ge N_{\rm V}\), \(0<\epsilon<C^2\) with \(\epsilon N\ge C^2\), \(h(u)=C(1+u)^{-r_0}\) and \(H=(C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r_0}-1\). Then \(1+H\le\sqrt N\) and \((g*h)(u)\le2h(u)\) on \([0,H]\).
Proof.
The quantity \(1-q-(r_0-\upsilon_\circ)/(2r_0)=\frac12-q+\upsilon_\circ/(2r_0)\) decreases in \(r_0\) and vanishes at \(r_0=\upsilon_\circ/(2q-1)=2/\alpha\). Since \(r_0\le(1+2/\alpha)/2<2/\alpha\) for \(\alpha<2\), \(d_{\rm V}>0\). The window gives \(1+H=(C/\sqrt\epsilon)^{1/r_0}\le N^{1/(2r_0)}\le\sqrt N\), because \(r_0\ge1\).
By Lemma SI.7(i) with \(a=\alpha>1\),
\[ g(v)=\frac2{H_N(q)}L_{N,\alpha,q}(v)\le\frac{K_{\rm V}}{H_N(q)}(1+v)^{-\upsilon_\circ} . \]
Split the convolution at \(v=\delta_\circ(1+u)\). As in the proof of Lemma SI.14, the early part is at most \(\frac32h(u)\). On the late part \(g(v)\le K_{\rm V}\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon_\circ}(1+u)^{-\upsilon_\circ}/H_N(q)\), and
\[ \frac1{h(u)}\int_{\delta_\circ(1+u)}^ug(v)h(u-v)\,\dif v\le\frac{K_{\rm V}\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon_\circ}}{H_N(q)}(1+u)^{r_0-\upsilon_\circ}I_{r_0}(u) . \]
By Lemma SI.8, \(H_N(q)\ge N^{1-q}\). If \(r_0=1\), then \((1+u)^{1-\upsilon_\circ}\log(1+u)\le N^{(1-\upsilon_\circ)/2}\cdot\frac12\log N\) for \(u\le H\), and \((1-\upsilon_\circ)/2-(1-q)=-d_\alpha\); with \(\sup_{x\ge1}x^{-d_\alpha/2}\log x=2/(e\,d_\alpha)\) the late part is at most
\[ \frac{K_{\rm V}\delta_\circ^{-\upsilon_\circ}}2N^{-d_\alpha}\log N\le J_{\rm V}N^{-d_{\rm V}} . \]
If \(r_0>1\), then \(I_{r_0}\le1/(r_0-1)\) and \((1+u)^{r_0-\upsilon_\circ}\le(1+H)^{r_0-\upsilon_\circ}\le N^{(r_0-\upsilon_\circ)/(2r_0)}\), so the late part is at most \(J_{\rm V}N^{-d_{\rm V}}\). In both cases \(N\ge N_{\rm V}\) makes the late part at most \(\frac12\), and the sum is at most \(2\).
□
