Remark SI.4 (The adopted SignSGD array and the corrected modes)
The SignSGD section computes the raw annealed row volatility of the actual sign update, \(\mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}\), for a Haar eigenbasis independent of the batch, comparable dimensions and a covariance bounded below (Theorem SJ.4). At \(\Nout=\Nin=N\), with \(\Sigmaout=U\Lambda U^\T\), the state \(\Delta=UD\) and \(\cR=\frac12\Tr(\Lambda DD^\T)\), its difference from the adopted array is
\[ \mathfrak v_i^{\rm corr}-\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=\frac2\pi\,N\Big[\frac{2\mu_i}{\Tr\Sigmaout}+\frac{(B+2N+3)\,(\Lambda DD^\T\Lambda)_{ii}}{2\cR\,\Tr\Sigmaout}\Big]\ \ge0 , \]
where \(\mathfrak v_i^{\text{s-SGD}}=N(1-\frac2\pi)+\frac2\pi N^2\mu_i/\Tr\Sigmaout\). The corrected array depends on the state, its total exceeds \(N^2\), and the power-law family has no covariance lower bound fixed in \(N\). The phase theorems of this subsection concern the adopted array (SI.25). Replacing it by another nonnegative array with total \(N^2\) and the same drift leaves \(\mathcal S_{\text{s-SGD}}\) unchanged, but not the kernel \(g_{\text{s-SGD}}\) on which the joint estimates rest.
