Lemma SE.8 (Causal tangent)
The derivatives at \(\varepsilon=0\) exist, and with \(\ell=\ridgephys(D)\),
\begin{equation}\tag{SE.18} \begin{gathered} \dot\Delta_r=-\sum_{k<r}V^{m_0}_k,\qquad \dot M_r=-\sum_{0\le k<j\le r}\emaw{r-j}\,G^\circ_{t+j+1}\big[V^{m_0}_k\big],\\ \dot {\mathsf S}_r=D\Qridge\ell(N^{m_0}_r)\big[\dot M_r\big]-2\ridge\Big\langle\Sigmaout D\Sigmain,\sum_{k<r}V^{m_0}_k\Big\rangle\,\partial_\ell\Qridge\ell(N^{m_0}_r). \end{gathered} \end{equation}
Proof.
The map \((K,\ell)\mapsto\Qridge\ell(K)\) is smooth on \(\ell>0\), so the chain rule applies at each step. The state is \(\Delta^\varepsilon_r=D-\varepsilon\sum_{k<r}{\mathsf S}^\varepsilon_k\), whose derivative at \(0\) is \(-\sum_{k<r}V^{m_0}_k\). Unrolling the buffer, \(M^\varepsilon_r=\bmom^{r+1}m_0+\sum_{j\le r}\emaw{r-j}G^\circ_{t+j+1}[\Delta^\varepsilon_j]\), and the linearity of \(G^\circ_{t+j+1}\) gives \(\dot M_r=\sum_{j\le r}\emaw{r-j}G^\circ_{t+j+1}[\dot\Delta_j]\), which is the second formula. The physical ridge is \(\ridgephys(\Delta)=\ridge\norm\Delta_\Sigma^2\), whose derivative along \(\Delta^\varepsilon_r\) at \(0\) is \(2\ridge\langle D,\dot\Delta_r\rangle_\Sigma=2\ridge\langle\Sigmaout D\Sigmain,\dot\Delta_r\rangle\). The chain rule for \({\mathsf S}^\varepsilon_r=\Qridge{\ridgephys(\Delta^\varepsilon_r)}(M^\varepsilon_r)\) gives the third formula.
□
