Corollary SJ.2 (One step of the risk)
In the Haar model with \(k\ge k_*\), for every deterministic \(\lr\ge0\),
\[ \EE\,\cR(\Delta-\lr S)=\cR-\lr L_B\norm{\Lambda D}_\F^2+\frac{\lr^2}2\,\mathcal Q_{\rm print}+\mathcal E_\lr,\qquad \abs{\mathcal E_\lr}\le\lr Lf_+\sqrt{C_*}+\frac{\lr^2}2\,C_{\rm vol}\frac{\Nout\Nin}{\sqrt k}. \]
Proof.
Given \(U\), the data satisfy Definition SA.1 with covariance \(U\Lambda U^\T\), because the whitened batch is independent of \(U\). The identity (SJ.2) applied given \(U\) and averaged over \(U\) gives
\[ \EE\,\cR(\Delta-\lr S)=\cR-\lr\,\EE\langle\Delta,S\rangle_\Sigma+\frac{\lr^2}2\,\mathcal Q . \]
Corollary SJ.1 and Theorem SJ.3 give the two error terms.
□
